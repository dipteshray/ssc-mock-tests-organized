13. Which two signs need to be interchanged to make the following equation correct? 72 × 18 ÷ 9 + 19 − 39 = 16

A. − and ×

B. + and ×

C. − and +

D. ÷ and ×

Answer D

Solution

The logic followed here is :- By using BODMAS RULE : Option 1) : − and × Given equation : 72 × 18 ÷ 9 + 19 − 39 = 16 After interchanging the symbols in given equation the equation is as follows : 72 - 18 ÷ 9 + 19 × 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 - 18 ÷ 9 + 19 × 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 - 2 + 19 × 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 - 2 + 741 = 16 ⇒ 813 - 2 = 16 ⇒ 811 ≠ 16 LHS ≠ RHS Option 2) : + and × Given equation : 72 × 18 ÷ 9 + 19 − 39 = 16 After interchanging the symbols in given equation the equation is as follows : 72 + 18 ÷ 9 × 19 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 + 18 ÷ 9 × 19 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 + 2 × 19 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 + 38 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 110 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 71 ≠ 16 LHS ≠ RHS Option 3) : − and + Given equation : 72 × 18 ÷ 9 + 19 − 39 = 16 After interchanging the symbols in given equation the equation is as follows : 72 × 18 ÷ 9 - 19 + 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 × 18 ÷ 9 - 19 + 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 × 2 - 19 + 39 = 16 ⇒ 144 - 19 + 39 = 16 ⇒ 183 - 19 = 16 ⇒ 164 ≠ 16 LHS ≠ RHS Option 4) : ÷ and × Given equation : 72 × 18 ÷ 9 + 19 − 39 = 16 After interchanging the symbols in given equation the equation is as follows : 72 ÷ 18 × 9 + 19 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 72 ÷ 18 × 9 + 19 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 4 × 9 + 19 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 36 + 19 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 55 - 39 = 16 ⇒ 16 = 16 LHS = RHS Hence, the correct answer is "÷ and ×".
