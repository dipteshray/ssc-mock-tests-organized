93. Select the correct indirect narration of the given sentence. She said, "We visited Uttarakhand in the summer vacation."

C. She said that they have visited Uttarakhand in the summer vacation.

D. She said that we visited Uttarakhand in the summer vacation.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Option 1. The given sentence is an assertive sentence in Direct speech. It needs to be changed into Indirect speech. When we convert sentence from direct to indirect speech, the following changes take place: i) The personal pronoun 'we' changes to 'they'. ii) Tense of the reporting speech changes from past tense to past perfect. 'visited' => 'had visited' iii) Determiner 'that' is added, while the inverted commas are omitted. Thus the sentence in the indirect speech is: She said that they had visited Uttarakhand in the summer vacation.
