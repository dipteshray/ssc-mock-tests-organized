6. A private taxi company charges a fixed charge along with a per kilometre charge based on the distance covered. For a journey of 24 km, the charges paid are Rs. 368 and for a journey of 32 km, the charges paid Rs. 464. How much will a person have to pay for travelling a distance of 15 km?

A. Rs. 180

B. Rs. 260

C. Rs. 290

D. Rs. 280

Answer B

Solution

A private taxi company charges a fixed charge along with a per kilometre charge based on the distance covered. For a journey of 24 km, the charges paid are Rs. 368 and for a journey of 32 km, the charges paid Rs. 464. Let, the fixed charge be ‘a’ and charge per km be ‘b’. According to question: a + 24b = 368 ---- (I) a + 32b = 464 ---- (II) (II) - (I), we get: 32b - 24b = 96 8b = 96 b = 12 If we put the value of ‘b’ in equation (I), we get: a + 24 × 12 = 368 a = 368 - 288 a = 80 For travelling a distance of 15 km, a person will have to pay Rs. (a + 15b) = Rs. (80 + 15 × 12) = Rs. (80 + 180) = Rs. 260. Hence, ‘260’ is the correct answer.

Solution

88. Select the most appropriate option to fill in blank number (3).

A. off

B. on

C. at

D. in

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Switch on is a phrasal verb. It means to turn on (something) by turning or pushing a button. No other option fits in blank 3 except on. Other options are incorrect.
