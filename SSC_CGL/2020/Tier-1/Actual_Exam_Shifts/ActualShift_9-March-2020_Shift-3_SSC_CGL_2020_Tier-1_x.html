17. Select the option in which the words share the same relationship as that shared by the given pair of words. Arteries : Circulatory system

A. Kidney : Respiratory system

B. Eyes : Endocrine system

C. Lungs : Olfactory system

D. Nerves : Nervous system

Answer D

Solution

The relation is: Arteries : Circulatory system Arteries – vessel that carries blood high in oxygen content away from the heart to the farthest reaches of the body. Since blood in arteries is usually full of oxygen, the haemoglobin in the red blood cells is oxygenated. Circulatory system – The system that circulates blood and lymph through the body, consisting of the heart, blood vessels, blood, lymph, and the lymphatic vessels and glands. So, the relation is that arteries forms the Circulatory system of our body. 1) Kidney : Respiratory system Kidney – The kidneys are two bean-shaped organs in the renal system. They help the body pass waste as urine. They also help filter blood before sending it back to the heart, creating hormones that help produce red blood cells, promote bone health, and regulate blood pressure. Respiratory system – The human respiratory system is a series of organs responsible for taking in oxygen and expelling carbon dioxide. The primary organs of the respiratory system are the lungs, which carry out this exchange of gases as we breathe. The kidneys forms the excretory system of our body and not the Respiratory system. 2) Eyes : Endocrine system Eyes – Eyes are organs of the visual system. They provide animals with vision, the ability to receive and process visual detail, as well as enabling several photo response functions that are independent of vision. Eyes detect light and convert it into electro-chemical impulses in neurons. Endocrine system - The endocrine system is the collection of glands that produce hormones that regulate metabolism, growth and development, tissue function, sexual function, reproduction, sleep, and mood, among other things. Eyes belong to the sensory system and not the endocrine system. 3) Lungs : Olfactory system Lungs – The lungs are a pair of spongy, air-filled organs located on either side of the chest (thorax). The trachea (windpipe) conducts inhaled air into the lungs through its tubular branches, called bronchi. The lungs are covered by a thin tissue layer called the pleura. Olfactory system – The olfactory system, or sense of smell, is the sensory system used for smelling (olfaction). Olfaction is one of the special senses, that have directly associated specific organs. Most mammals and reptiles have a main olfactory system and an accessory olfactory system. Lungs are related to respiratory system, and not olfactory system. 4) Nerves : Nervous system Nerves – Bundles of fibres or axons, in the peripheral nervous system are called nerves, and bundles of afferent fibers are known as sensory nerves. An efferent nerve fiber conducts signals from a motor neuron in the central nervous system to muscles. Nervous system – The nervous system is a highly complex part of an animal that coordinates its actions and sensory information by transmitting signals to and from different parts of its body. The nervous system detects environmental changes that impact the body, then works in tandem with the endocrine system to respond to such events. The nerves forms the nervous system. Hence, ‘Nerves : Nervous system’ is the correct answer.

Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, which of the given conclusions logically(s) follow. From the statements. Statements: 1. Some bottles are glasses. 2. Some glasses are bowls. Conclusions: I. Some bowls are bottles. II. Some glasses are bottles III. No bowl is a bottle

A. All Conclusions I, II and III follow.

B. Only Conclusions II and III follow.

C. Either Conclusion II or III follows.

D. Only Conclusions II and either Conclusion I or III follow.

Answer D

Solution

Concept: 1) We have to check the conclusions are 100% match with the statement. If the conclusion is 100% match with the given statement then the conclusion is true. If there is a 1% chance of not following the statement then the conclusion is considered to be false. 2) The possibility case can be considered to be "false" in "definite case". 3) In Possibility case, either "can" or "possible" word must be present. The least possible venn diagram is: I. Some bowls are bottles. → False (It can be possible, but it is not definite) II. Some glasses are bottles → True (It is definite) III. No bowl is a bottle → False (It can be possible, but it is not definite) Conclusion I and III forms a complementary pair. Hence, only Conclusions II and either Conclusion I or III follow. Note: Conditions of Either-or case: 1. The elements should be the same in both the statements. 2. Individually both the conclusions should be false. 3. One conclusion should be positive and one conclusion should be negative (Complementary pair) The two complementary pairs are: 1) All + Some not 2) No + Some Additional Information

Answer A

Solution

• In the indirect form, the reporting verb "said" changes to "asked," and the noun "Johnny" changes to "him" as the subject becomes the object.

• Option 4 is incorrect because it changes the roles of the speaker and the listener. In the original sentence, Johnny’s mother is the speaker who is making the request, while Johnny is the listener.
