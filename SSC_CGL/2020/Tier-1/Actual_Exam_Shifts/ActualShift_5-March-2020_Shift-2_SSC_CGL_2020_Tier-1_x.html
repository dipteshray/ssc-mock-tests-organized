The difference ∠P − ∠Q = (180° − ∠A − ∠B) − (180° − ∠A − ∠D) = ∠D − ∠B. ∴ ∠DPC − ∠BQC = 108° − 72° = 36°. ∴ The correct answer is 36°. Additional Information Cyclic Quadrilateral Properties The sum of opposite angles is always 180°. If one side is produced, the exterior angle formed is equal to the interior opposite angle.
