22. Which of these is the energy conversion that happens in the process called photosynthesis?

A. Potential energy to chemical energy

B. Heat energy to light energy

C. Heat energy to chemical energy

D. Light energy to chemical energy

Answer D

Solution

Photosynthesis is a process in which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy from the sun into chemical energy stored in glucose molecules. Chlorophyll, the green pigment in chloroplasts, absorbs sunlight and uses it to drive the synthesis of glucose from carbon dioxide and water. During this process, light energy is transformed into chemical energy in the form of bonds within glucose molecules. This stored chemical energy serves as a fuel source for the plant and other organisms that consume it.
