10. Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements; Statements: 1. All toys are pens. 2. All games are pens. Conclusions: I. No toy is a game. II. No pen is a game. III. Some toys are games.

A. Only conclusions II and III follow

B. All conclusions I, II and III follow

C. Only conclusions I and II follow

D. Either conclusion I or III follows

Answer D

Solution

Drawing the Venn diagram, Conclusion 1: No toy is a game → False (Possible but Not Definite) Conclusion 2: No pen is a game → False (This is definitely false) Conclusion 3: Some toys are games → False (Possible but Not Definite) But, conclusion 1 and conclusion 2 form a complimentary pair. Hence, “either conclusion 1 or conclusion 3” follows.

18. In a certain code language, ‘SOLID’ is coded as ‘64’ and ‘POUR’ IS coded as ‘74’ How will ‘TON’ be coded as in that language?

A. 60

B. 85

C. 52

D. 49

Answer C

Solution

The pattern followed is, For SOLID, The place value of S is 19 The place value of O is 15 The place value of L is 12 The place value of I is 9 The place value of D is 4 SOLID → 19 + 15 + 12 + 9 + 4 = 59 59 + 5 = 64( we added 5 because there are 5 letters in SOLID) SOLID is coded as 64 For POUR, The place value of P is 16 The place value of O is 15 The place value of U is 21 The place value of R is 18 POUR → 16 + 15 + 21 + 18 = 70 70 + 4 = 74( we added 4 because there are 4 letters in POUR) POUR is coded as 74 Similarly, For TON, The place value of T is 20 The place value of O is 15 The place value of N is 14 TON → 20 + 15 + 14 = 49 49 + 3 = 52 TON is coded as 52 Hence, “52” is the correct answer.

C. Morbidity

D. Default

Answer B

Solution

Option 2 is correct, i.e. Deficit Financing. Key Points

• A situation where the expenditure of the government exceeds its revenue is known as Deficit Financing.

• In the case of Deficit Financing, the government has to either borrow or look for other means to cover the expenditure.

• As per the National Planning Commission of India, ‘deficit financing’ term is used to denote the direct addition to gross national expenditure through budget deficits.

90. Select the correct passive form of the given sentence. He incurred heavy losses in business.

A. Heavy losses are incurred in business by him.

B. Heavy losses were incurred in business by him.

C. Heavy losses are being incurred in business by him.

D. Heavy losses was incurred in business by him.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. The given sentence is an assertive statement in Active Voice. Its structure is: Subject + simple past tense + Indirect object + Direct Object. When we convert the sentence in Passive Voice, its structure will be: Indirect Object + were + simple past tense + Direct object + by + Subject (Objective case). Thus the correct Passive Voice sentence will be: Heavy losses were incurred in business by him.
