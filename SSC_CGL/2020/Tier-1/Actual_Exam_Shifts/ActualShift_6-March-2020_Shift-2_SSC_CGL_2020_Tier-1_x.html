1. A group of three friends, K, L and M, are sitting in a café. Their average age is 24 years. Another friend ‘N’ joins the group and the new average age of the group becomes 23 years. If another friend ‘R’, whose age is 2 years more than that of ‘N’, replace ‘K’, then the average age of L, M, N and R becomes 22.5 years. What is the age of K?

A. 22 years

B. 24 years

C. 20 years

D. 25 years

Answer B

Solution

According to the given information, Average of K, L and M is 24 K + L + M/3 = 24 K + L + M = 72 ...1) Average of K, L, M and N is 23 K + L + M + N/4 = 23 K + L + M + N = 92 ...2) Substituting (1) in (2) 72 + N = 92 N = 20 R who is two years older than N replace K R = 22 Average of R, L, M and N is 22.5 R + L + M + N/4 = 22.5 R + L + M + N = 90 22 + L + M + 20 = 90 L + M = 48 Substituting this in (1) K + 48 = 72 K = 24 K is 24 years old. Hence, “24” is the correct answer.

D. 8/25%

Answer A

Solution

Additional Information Difference for 3 Years The difference between CI and SI for 3 years is given by: D = P × (R/100)2 × (3 + R/100). Successive Interest Rates For Compound Interest, if the rate for the 1st year is R1 and for the 2nd year is R2, the effective rate is (R1 + R2 + R1×R2/100)%.
