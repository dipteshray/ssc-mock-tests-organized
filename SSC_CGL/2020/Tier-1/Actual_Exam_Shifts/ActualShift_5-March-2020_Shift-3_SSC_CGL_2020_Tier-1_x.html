65. The distance between two stations, A and B, is 428 km. a train starts from station ‘A’ at 6:00 a.m and moves towards station ‘B’ at an average speed of 48 km/h. Another train starts from station ‘B’ at 6:20 a.m and moves towards statin ‘A’ at an average speed of 55 km/h. At what time will the trains meet?

A. 10:00 a.m.

B. 10.:40 a.m.

C. 10:20 a.m.

D. 9:40 a.m.

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Train A travels alone for 20 minutes (1/3 hour) → Distance = 48 × (1/3) = 16 km. Remaining distance at 6:20 a.m. = 428 − 16 = 412 km. Relative speed = 48 + 55 = 103 km/h. Time to meet = 412 ÷ 103 = 4 hours after 6:20 a.m. ∴ The correct answer is 10:20 a.m. Alternate Method Given: Total Distance = 428 km, Speed of Train A = 48 km/h (starts 6:00 a.m.), Speed of Train B = 55 km/h (starts 6:20 a.m.). Formula Used: Distance = Speed × Time; Relative Speed (Opposite direction) = S1 + S2. ⇒ Distance covered by Train A in 20 mins = 48 × (20/60) = 16 km. ⇒ Distance between trains at 6:20 a.m. = 428 − 16 = 412 km. ⇒ Relative speed = 48 + 55 = 103 km/h. ⇒ Time taken to meet = 412 ÷ 103 = 4 hours. ⇒ Meeting time = 6:20 a.m. + 4 hours = 10:20 a.m. ∴ The correct answer is 10:20 a.m. Additional Information Relative Speed Concept When two objects move toward each other, their relative speed is the sum of their individual speeds: Srel = S1 + S2. Meeting Time Rule To calculate meeting time, ensure both objects start moving from their respective positions at the same reference time by calculating initial distance covered. Unit Conversion Always convert time into hours when speed is in km/h: 1 minute = 1/60 hour.
