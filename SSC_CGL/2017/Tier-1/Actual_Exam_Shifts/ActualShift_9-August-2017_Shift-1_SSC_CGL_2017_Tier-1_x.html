Solution

The correct answer is Hydrogen bond. Concept: Chemical bond: Explanation: triple bond > double bond > single bond > H-Bond. Hence, the weakest among the bonds is the Hydrogen bond.

• A chemical bond is a force or linkage which holds two or more atoms together in a stable molecule.

• The driving force for atoms to combine is related to their tendency to attain stable electronic configuration.

• A chemical bond may be formed either by electron transfer or electron sharing.

• Ionic Bonds: Ionic bond formation occurs via electrostatic attraction between two ions.

• One ion is positively charged and the other is negatively charged like in NaCl.

• Covalent bonds: Covalent bond formation occurs when there is electron sharing occurs between two atoms.

• The electron pair is shared equally between the atoms forming the bond-like in Cl2.

• When there exists a single bond between two atoms, there is only a sigma bond.

• When there exist multiple bonds between atoms, there is only one sigma bond and others are pi bond.

• Whether a bond is weak or strong depends on the extent of linkage or interaction among the molecules or atoms.

• Besides the chemical bond, there exist interactions between the molecules, which are not actual bonds, which means that there is no sharing or transfer of electrons but only interaction between the molecules is there.

• The types of interactions are: Electrostatic: They are interactions between charged molecules.

• Induction: These types of interactions arise when there is induction of dipole in the molecule.

• Dispersion: These are also called London forces or Van der Waals forces and exists mostly in covalent molecules.

• Hydrogen bonds: Hydrogen bonds are the dipole-dipole interactions that occur when a Hydrogen is bonded to a very electronegative atom like oxygen, Nitrogen. It is not a covalent bond.

• So, the Hydrogen bond is just an interaction between the Hydrogen and an electronegative element where there is no electron exchange leading to weak bonding between molecules whereas a single bond, double bond, and triple bond are covalent bonds and not just interactions.

• Chemical bonds are much much stronger than chemical interactions.

• It is difficult to break chemical bonds whereas Hydrogen bonds are easily and rapidly formed and broken under normal biological conditions.

87. In the following question, a sentence has been given in Direct/Indirect speech. Out of the four alternatives suggested, select the one which best expresses the same sentence in Indirect/Direct speech. Nisha said to Swati, " Will you help me in my project just now?"

A. Nisha told Swati whether she will help her in her project just now.

B. Nisha asked Swati if she would help her in her project just then.

C. Nisha questioned to Swati that will you help me in my project just now.

D. Nisha asked to Swati that will she help her in her work just now.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is- Nisha asked Swati if she would help her in her project just then. Key Points

• Let's look at the steps to change the sentence from direct to indirect speech:

• The tense of direct speech is changed only when the reporting verb is in the past tense.

• When the direct speech is in an interrogative form, we follow the steps given below: The reporting verb said to is converted into asked.

• Connectors 'whether' or 'if' are used in place of comma and inverted commas.

• Change the reported speech from interrogative to assertive.

• The personal pronouns will be changed according to the subject and object of the reporting verb.

• Here the 2nd person 'you' will change into the 3rd person 'she' as per the object.

• Similarly 'my' is converted into 'her'.

• The auxiliary verb (will) in direct speech changes to (would).

• Words showing time and place are changed accordingly. (now- then)

• At last line up the remaining part.

• Following these steps, we finally get- Nisha asked Swati if she would help her in her project just then.
