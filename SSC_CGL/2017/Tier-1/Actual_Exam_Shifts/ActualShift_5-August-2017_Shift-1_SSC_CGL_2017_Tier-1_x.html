25. Which type of rock is Sandstone?

A. Calcareous Rock

B. Igneous Rock

C. Metamorphic Rock

D. Sedimentary Rock

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Sedimentary Rock. Key Points

• Calcareous rock: Composed primarily of calcium carbonate

• Often formed from the accumulation of marine organisms such as coral and shellfish

• Examples include limestone and marble

• Igneous rock: Formed from the solidification of molten magma or lava

• Can be classified as intrusive (formed underground) or extrusive (formed on the surface)

• Examples include granite, basalt, and obsidia

• Metamorphic rock: Formed from the alteration of existing rock through heat, pressure, or chemical processes

• Often have a layered or banded appearance

• Examples include marble, slate, and gneiss

• Sedimentary rock: Formed from the accumulation and compression of sediment such as sand, mud, and organic material

• Often contain fossils and other evidence of past environments

• Examples include sandstone, shale, and limestone

43. If X and Y are the two digits of the number 347XY such that the number is completely divisible by 80, then what is the minimum value of X + Y?

A. 2

B. 4

C. 6

D. 8

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick For a number to be divisible by 80, it must be divisible by both 8 and 10. Divisibility by 10 ⇒ Last digit Y = 0. Divisibility by 8 ⇒ Last three digits 7X0 must be divisible by 8. For 7X0, if X = 2, then 720 ÷ 8 = 90 (perfectly divisible). Minimum X + Y = 2 + 0 = 2. ∴ The correct answer is 2. Alternate Method Given: The number is 347XY and it is divisible by 80. Formula: A number is divisible by 80 if it is divisible by 8 and 10 (where 80 = 8 × 10). Step 1: Determine Y ⇒ For 347XY to be divisible by 10, the unit digit must be 0. ⇒ Y = 0. Step 2: Determine X ⇒ The number is now 347X0. For divisibility by 8, the last three digits 7X0 must be divisible by 8. ⇒ Possible values for 7X0: 700, 710, 720, 730, 740, 750, 760, 770, 780, 790. ⇒ 720 ÷ 8 = 90 (Divisible, so X = 2) ⇒ 760 ÷ 8 = 95 (Divisible, so X = 6) Step 3: Minimum Value ⇒ For minimum X + Y, we take X = 2 and Y = 0. ⇒ X + Y = 2 + 0 = 2. ∴ The correct answer is 2. Additional Information Divisibility Rule of 8 A number is divisible by 8 if its last three digits are divisible by 8 or if the last three digits are 000. Divisibility Rule of 10 A number is divisible by 10 if its units digit is 0. Composite Divisibility If a number is divisible by two co-prime numbers a and b, then it is also divisible by their product a × b.

44. A, B and C can complete a work in 20, 24 and 30 days respectively. All three of them starts together but after 4 days A leaves the job and B left the job 6 days before the work was completed. C completed the remaining work alone. In how many days was the total work completed?

A. 10

B. 12

C. 14

D. 16

Answer C

Solution

Given: Time is taken by A to complete a work = 20 days Time is taken by B to complete a work = 24 days Time is taken by C to complete a work = 30 days Formula Used: Total work = Efficiency × Total time taken Calculation: The LCM for 20, 24, 30 is (120) Total work = 120 units The efficiency of A = 120/20 ⇒ 6 units/day The efficiency of B = 120/24 ⇒ 5 units/day The efficiency of C = 120/30 ⇒ 4 units/day The efficiency of A + B + C = 6 + 5 + 4 ⇒ 15 units/day The efficiency of B + C = 5 + 4 ⇒ 9 units/day A, B, C work together for first 4 days = 15 × 4 = 60 units Remaining work = 120 - 60 ⇒ 60 units B left the work 6 days before completion so,C completed the remaining work in last 6 days = 4 × 6 = 24 unit Middle work = Total work - First work - last work The B and C complete the work in middle days = 120 - 60 - 24 = 36 units Time taken by B and C to do the middle days work = 36/9 ⇒ 4 days Total Time taken to complete the work = 4 + 4 + 6 ⇒ 14 days ∴ In 14 days the total work completed was completed
