52. What is the value of tan 6° tan 36° tan 84° tan 54° tan 45°?

A. 1/2

B. 1/√2

C. 1

D. 1/3

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Use the property: If θ1 + θ2 = 90°, then tan θ1 × tan θ2 = 1. Pairing terms: (tan 6° × tan 84°) × (tan 36° × tan 54°) × tan 45°. As 6 + 84 = 90 and 36 + 54 = 90, both pairs become 1. Final calculation: 1 × 1 × 1 = 1. ∴ The correct answer is 1. Alternate Method Given: tan 6°, tan 36°, tan 84°, tan 54°, tan 45° Formula: tan (90° − θ) = cot θ and tan θ × cot θ = 1 ⇒ Expression = tan 6° × tan 36° × tan (90° − 6°) × tan (90° − 36°) × tan 45° ⇒ Expression = tan 6° × tan 36° × cot 6° × cot 36° × tan 45° ⇒ Expression = (tan 6° × cot 6°) × (tan 36° × cot 36°) × 1 ⇒ Expression = 1 × 1 × 1 = 1 ∴ The correct answer is 1. Additional Information Complementary Angle Identities For any angle θ, sin (90° − θ) = cos θ, tan (90° − θ) = cot θ, and sec (90° − θ) = cosec θ. Standard Values Value of tan 45° = 1 and cot 45° = 1 are frequently used to simplify trigonometric products. Product Rule If A + B = 90°, then tan A × tan B = 1 and cot A × cot B = 1.
