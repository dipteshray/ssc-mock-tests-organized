44. A is 1.5 times efficient as B therefore takes 8 days less than B to complete a work. If A and B work on alternate days and A works on first day, then in how many days the work will be completed?

A. 17

B. 19

C. 19.5

D. 21

Answer B

Solution

Given: A is 1.5 times efficient as B. A is therefore takes 8 days less than B to complete a work. A and B work on alternate days and A works on the first day. Formula used: W = E × T Where, W = Work, E = Efficiency, and T = Time Calculation: Let the days taken by B to complete the work = c days So, days taken by A to complete the work = c - 8 days Now, it is given that A is 1.5 times efficient as B ⇒ c = 1.5 x (c – 8) ⇒ 0.5c = 12 ⇒ c = 24 Work done by B in 1 day = 1/24 Work done by A in 1 day = 1/16 Hence, the series becomes: 1/16 + 1/24 + 1/16 + ……. = 1 ⇒ Work done in 2 consecutive days = 1/24 + 1/16 ⇒ 5/48 ⇒ 1/9.6 Work done in 18 consecutive days = 9/9.6 Work left after 18 days = 1 – (9/9.6) ⇒ 0.6/9.6 ⇒ 1/16 Since A will work on the 19th day and will complete 1/16 of the work Total days = 18 + 1 ⇒ 19 ∴ The work will be completed in 19 days.

A. 20

B. 30

C. 35

D. 40

Answer A

Solution

Let the Cost price of the table be a Since he gains a profit of 60%, the selling price = a + (60/100) x a = 1.6a Let the Marked price of the table be b ⇒ b – (20/100) x b = 1.6a ⇒ b = 2a Now, if he sells the table at 40% discount ⇒ New selling price will be = 2a – (2a) x (40/100) ⇒ New selling price = 1.2a ∴ New profit percent = [(1.2a – a)/a] x 100 = 20%
