19. For which of the following game, players must have the knowledge of Pascal's law?

A. Climbing

B. Paragliding

C. Rafting

D. Scuba diving

Answer D

Solution

CONCEPT: Pascal's Law: EXPLANATION:

• It states that if the gravity effect is neglected, the pressure at every point of liquid in the equilibrium of rest is the same.

• The increase in pressure at one point of the enclosed liquid in an equilibrium of rest is transmitted equally to all other points of the liquid and also to the walls of the container, provided the effect of gravity is neglected.

• Pascal's principle states that pressure applied to fluids is equally transmitted in all directions, to all parts of the fluid and to all parts of a container.

• Scuba divers must also understand this principle as at a depth of 10 meters underwater, the pressure is twice the atmospheric pressure at the sea surface, and increases by about 100 kPa for each increase of 10 m depth. Therefore option 4 is correct.

92. Improve the bracketed part of the sentence. The (reason of) her outburst at the party is yet unknown.

A. reason for

B. reason to

C. reason on

D. No improvement

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is ‘reason for’. The preposition used with reason is for, not of, as for denotes the linkage. Hence, the correct sentence is: ’The reason for her outburst at the party is yet unknown.’
