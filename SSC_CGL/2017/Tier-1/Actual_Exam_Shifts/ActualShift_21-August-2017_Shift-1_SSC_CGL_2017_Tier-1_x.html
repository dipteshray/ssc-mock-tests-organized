25. Aurelia (Medusa) and Adamsia (Polyp) are examples of which Phylum?

A. Ctenophora

B. Annelida

C. Coelenterata

D. Aschelminthes

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Coelenterata Key Points Aurelia (Medusa) and Adamsia (Polyp) belong to the Phylum Coelenterata (also known as Cnidaria).

• Body Forms: Coelenterates/Cnidarians showcase two primary body forms— 'Polyp' and 'Medusa.' Polyps, like Adamsia, are typically sessile and adhere to a substrate. On the other hand, Medusae, like Aurelia, are generally free-floating or free-swimming. This diversity of body forms represents the characteristic feature of this phylum.

• Cellular Specialization: A most notable feature of Coelenterates/Cnidarians is their specialized cells known as cnidocytes, which are used primarily for capturing prey and defense. They contain harpoon-like structures that can be explosively discharged for both purposes. Both Aurelia and Adamsia exhibit these cnidocytes.

33. _________ is the breaking up of the rocks on the earth's surface.

A. Erosion

B. Weathering

C. Attrition

D. Abrasion

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Weathering NOTE:

• Weathering is the breaking down of rocks, soil, and minerals as well as wood and artificial materials through contact with the Earth's atmosphere, waters, and biological organisms.

• Weathering and Erosion are geological processes that act together to shape the surface of the Earth. Erosion is the displacement of solids (soil, mud, rock and other particles) usually by the agents of currents such as wind, water, or ice by downward or down-slope movement in response to gravity or by living organisms. Weathering is the decomposition of rocks, soils, and minerals through direct contact with the Earth's atmosphere.

• Denudation refers to the wearing down, stripping, and leveling of the earth's surface. This is caused by rivers, glaciers, waves, and wind. there are three denudation processes: Erosion, Weathering, and mass wasting.

• The word ‘denude’ means to strip off or to uncover. All the exogenic geomorphic processes are covered under a general term called Denudation.

• Weathering, mass wasting/movements, erosion, and transportation are included in denudation.

• Hydration is a form of chemical weathering that involves the rigid attachment of H+ and OH- ions to the atoms and molecules of a mineral.

• The processes of Decomposition (or chemical weathering) transform rocks and minerals exposed to water and atmospheric gases into new chemical compounds (different rocks and minerals), some of which can be dissolved away.

• Carbonation: When carbon dioxide is added to the rocks and minerals it may result in their dissolution.
