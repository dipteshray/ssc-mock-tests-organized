25. Which one of the following is not a Major Abiotic Factors?

A. Temperature

B. Water

C. Light

D. Plant

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4 i.e. Plants. Plants are considered a biotic factor - they are living organisms Temperature, Water, Light, and Air - are all major abiotic factors. Abiotic factors refer to the non-living physical and chemical elements in the ecosystem that can influence the behavior and survival of organisms. These factors can significantly affect the ability of organisms to survive and reproduce.

• Temperature affects the metabolic rates of organisms and influences their geographical distribution.

• Water is vital for life and its availability can limit the type of organisms that inhabit a particular area.

• Light provides the energy that drives photosynthesis, and its availability can also limit which organisms can live in a certain area.

• Air, specifically the availability of different gases such as oxygen, carbon dioxide, and nitrogen, can greatly influence the types of organisms that can survive in specific environments.
