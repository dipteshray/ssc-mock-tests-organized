B. 324

C. 210

D. 102

Answer D

Solution

1st Equation: 4 * 7 * 2 = 361 (4 - 1) = 3 (7 - 1) = 6 (2 - 1) = 1 When concatenating these results (3, 6,1) gives 361. 2nd Equation: 5 * 9 * 1 = 480 (5 - 1) = 4 (9 - 1) = 8 (1 - 1) = 0 When concatenating these results (4, 8, 0) gives 480. Similarly, 2 * 1 * 3 = ? (2 - 1) = 1 (1 - 1) = 0 (3 - 1) = 2 So, when concatenating these results (1, 0, 2) gives 102. Hence, 102 is the correct answer.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is ‘He was seen conducting the seminar on personality development.’ Key Points

• Let's understand this we the below example and rule-application:

• When there are two objects in an active sentence, there are two possible active sentences and two possible passive sentences.

• Sentence 1: The professor gave the students the books.

• Sentence 2: The professor gave the books to the students.

• There are two objects in each of the above sentences: Object 1 = indirect object → the students

• Object 2 = direct object → the books

• An indirect object is very often a person, a direct object a thing. When a direct object is followed by an indirect one, we put to in front of the indirect object.

• Active sentence 1: "The professor (sub) + gave (verb) + the students (indirect object) + the books (direct object)"

• Active sentence 2: "The professor (sub) + gave (verb) + the book (direct object) + to the students (indirect object)"

• Each of the objects (books/students) in the active sentences can become a subject in the passive sentence.

• Passive sentence 1 (Personal Passive): "The students (sub) + were given (verb) + the books (object) (by the professor) (by-agent)"

• Passive sentence 2: The books (sub) + were given (verb) + to the students (object) (by the professor) (by-agent)
