8. Eshan's birthday is on Friday 10th March. On what day of the week will be Shlok's Birthday in the same year if Shlok was born on 25th September?

A. Monday

B. Wednesday

C. Friday

D. Thursday

Answer A

Solution

According to the question, 10th March is Friday. Number of days left in March = 31 – 10 = 21. Similarly, odd days from 10th March till 25th September = (21 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 31 + 25) % 7 = 0 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 4 = 17/7. Remainder = 3. Thus, day on 25th September = Friday (+3) = Monday. Hence, Shlok's birthday will be on Monday.
