A. 6

B. 40

C. 45

D. 50

Answer A

Solution

The pattern followed is that the sum of digits of second number is subtracted from the sum of digits of first number. 45% 11 = (4 + 5) – (1 + 1) = 9 – 2 = 7 and 59% 34 = (5 + 9) – (3 + 4) = 14 – 7 = 7 Similarly, 55% 4 = (5 + 5) – (4) = 10 – 4 = 6 Hence, 6 is the correct answer.
