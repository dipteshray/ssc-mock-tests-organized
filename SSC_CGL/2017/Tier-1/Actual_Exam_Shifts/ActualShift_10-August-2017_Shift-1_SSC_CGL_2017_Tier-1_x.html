Trick: According to the question, 4 : 5 (+3)↓ ↓(+3) (This ratio changes by 3 after 3 years) 7 : 8 In implies, 3 values = 3 years so, 1 values = 1 year So, Anil's age (at present) = 4 × 1 = 4 years Aakash's age = 5 × 1 = 5 years Hence, after 3 years, their ages will be 7 and 8 years respectively.
