47. ABCD is an isoceles trapezium such that AD||BC, AB = 5 cm, AD = 8 cm and BC = 14 cm. What is the area (in cm2) of trapezium?

A. 36

B. 44

C. 88

D. 144

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick In an isosceles trapezium, the projection of the non-parallel side on the base is given by: projection = (BC − AD) ÷ 2 = (14 − 8) ÷ 2 = 3 cm. Using the Pythagorean triplet (3, 4, 5) for the right-angled triangle formed by the height, projection, and slant side (5 cm), the height (h) is directly found to be 4 cm. Area of the trapezium = Average of parallel sides × height = ((8 + 14) ÷ 2) × 4 = 11 × 4 = 44 cm2. ∴ The correct answer is 44 cm2. Alternate Method Given: Parallel sides: AD = 8 cm, BC = 14 cm Non-parallel sides: AB = CD = 5 cm Formula Used: Area of trapezium = (1 ÷ 2) × (sum of parallel sides) × height By Pythagoras theorem: Hypotenuse2 = Base2 + Height2 Calculations: Draw perpendiculars AP and DQ from A and D to BC respectively. ⇒ PQ = AD = 8 cm ⇒ BP = QC = (BC − PQ) ÷ 2 ⇒ BP = (14 − 8) ÷ 2 = 3 cm In right-angled triangle APB: ⇒ AB2 = AP2 + BP2 ⇒ 52 = AP2 + 32 ⇒ 25 = AP2 + 9 ⇒ AP2 = 16 ⇒ AP = 4 cm (height, h) Now, calculate the area of the trapezium ABCD: ⇒ Area = (1 ÷ 2) × (AD + BC) × AP ⇒ Area = (1 ÷ 2) × (8 + 14) × 4 ⇒ Area = (1 ÷ 2) × 22 × 4 ⇒ Area = 11 × 4 = 44 cm2 ∴ The correct answer is 44 cm2. Additional Information Isosceles Trapezium Properties The non-parallel sides are equal in length (AB = CD), the base angles are equal (∠B = ∠C), and the diagonals are equal in length (AC = BD).
