39. Which of the following pair is INCORRECT? I. Parsec - Distance II. Barrel - Liquid III. Light year – Time

Solution

Parsec is used to measure distance, the barrel is used to measure liquid and a light year is a unit of astronomical distance equivalent to the distance that light travels in one year. Key Points

• Parsec - Distance: Parsec is a unit of distance used in astronomy. It is equivalent to about 3.26 light-years, or approximately 3.086 × 10¹³ kilometers. It measures the distance at which one astronomical unit subtends an angle of one arcsecond.

• Correct: A parsec is a measure of distance, so this pair is correct.

• Barrel - Liquid: A barrel is a unit of volume, commonly used to measure liquids like oil. For example, in the oil industry, one barrel is typically equal to 42 U.S. gallons or about 159 liters.

• Correct: Since a barrel measures liquid volume, this pair is correct.

• Light year – Time: A light-year is a unit of distance, not time. It represents the distance that light travels in a vacuum in one year, which is approximately 9.46 trillion kilometers (5.88 trillion miles).

• Incorrect: A light-year measures distance, not time, so this pair is incorrect.
