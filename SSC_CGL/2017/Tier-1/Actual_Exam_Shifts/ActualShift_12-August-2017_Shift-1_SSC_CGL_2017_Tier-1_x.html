A. A car was being bought by him.

B. A car was bought by him.

C. A car will have been bought by him.

D. A car would have been bought by him.

Answer C

Solution

Correct Answer: A car will have been bought by him. The passive voice should use the Future Perfect Tense, as the active voice uses. This is seen only in Sentence 3, which uses ‘will have been’. The other sentences are incorrect due to their wrong tense usage.
