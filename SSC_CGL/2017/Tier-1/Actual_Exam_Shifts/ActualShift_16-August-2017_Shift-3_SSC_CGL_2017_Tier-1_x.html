19. In which of the following market forms a firm does not exercise control over price?

A. Mixed Competition

B. Monopoly

C. Oligopoly

D. Perfect Competition

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Perfect Competition Key Points Important Points Additional Information

• Perfect Competition: In a market form characterized by perfect competition, firms do not have control over the price of the products they sell. Prices are determined by the forces of supply and demand in the market. Since there are many sellers offering identical products, no single firm can influence the market price.

• Price Takers: Firms in a perfect competition market are known as "price takers," meaning they must accept the prevailing market price and cannot set prices above or below it. Any attempt to change the price would result in losing customers to competitors.

• Hence, option 4 (Perfect Competition) is correct.

• Characteristics of Perfect Competition: Perfect competition is characterized by a large number of buyers and sellers, homogenous products, free entry and exit of firms, and perfect information. These factors contribute to the inability of individual firms to influence prices.

• Market Equilibrium: In perfect competition, the market reaches equilibrium where the quantity supplied equals the quantity demanded at the prevailing price, ensuring that the market clears without any excess supply or demand.

• Economic Efficiency: Perfect competition leads to economic efficiency, as firms produce at the lowest possible cost, and consumers pay the lowest possible price, resulting in optimal allocation of resources.

• Comparison with Other Market Forms: In contrast to perfect competition, market forms like monopoly and oligopoly allow firms to exert some control over prices due to the lack of competition or the presence of few sellers.

• Real-World Examples: While perfect competition is a theoretical construct, some agricultural markets, where many farmers sell identical products like wheat or corn, come close to this model.

• Consumer Benefit: Consumers benefit from perfect competition as it typically results in lower prices and a higher level of product availability.

48. Price of a diamond is directly proportional to square of its weight. A man broke the diamond accidently in three pieces in the ratio of 3 : 5 : 7 and thus loses Rs 42600. What was the original price (in Rs) of the diamond?

A. 11786

B. 60000

C. 67500

D. 75000

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total Weight Ratio = 3 + 5 + 7 = 15 units Original Price ∝ 152 = 225 units Price after breaking ∝ (32 + 52 + 72) = 9 + 25 + 49 = 83 units Loss = 225 − 83 = 142 units → ₹42600 1 unit = 42600 ÷ 142 = 300 Original Price = 225 × 300 = ₹67500 ∴ The correct answer is ₹67500. Alternate Method Given: Ratio of pieces = 3 : 5 : 7, Loss = ₹42600. Formula: Price (P) = k × W2, where W is the weight. ⇒ Let the weights of pieces be 3x, 5x, and 7x. ⇒ Original weight (W) = 3x + 5x + 7x = 15x. ⇒ Original Price (P1) = k × (15x)2 = 225kx2. ⇒ Price of pieces (P2) = k × [(3x)2 + (5x)2 + (7x)2] = k × [9x2 + 25x2 + 49x2] = 83kx2. ⇒ Loss = P1 − P2 = 225kx2 − 83kx2 = 142kx2. ⇒ 142kx2 = 42600 ⇒ kx2 = 42600 ÷ 142 = 300. ⇒ Original Price = 225 × kx2 = 225 × 300 = ₹67500. ∴ The correct answer is ₹67500. Additional Information Variation Principles When a quantity y is directly proportional to the square of x, the relation is y = kx2. If a whole is divided into parts, (a+b+c)2 is always greater than (a2+b2+c2), which explains the loss in such breaking problems. Ratio and Sum of Squares The ratio of the original price to the broken price is (Sum of parts)2 : (Sum of squares of parts). For a ratio a:b:c, the ratio is (a+b+c)2 : (a2+b2+c2). Loss Calculation Loss Percentage = [(Original Price − Broken Price) ÷ Original Price] × 100.
