18. Which among the following is not an instrument of fiscal policy?

A. Taxation

B. Public expenditure

C. Public debt

D. Credit Rationing

Answer D

Solution

Fiscal policy is the use of government spending, taxation, and transfer payments to influence aggregate demand. The major instruments of fiscal policy include the budget, taxation, public expenditure, public works, and public debt. The budget involves planning government spending and revenue. Taxation involves collecting taxes from citizens and businesses. Public expenditure includes government spending on goods and services. Public works involve government-funded construction projects. Public debt involves borrowing money to finance government spending. These instruments are used to stimulate or slow down the economy, depending on the prevailing economic conditions.

29. When a ball is thrown vertically upwards, which of the following quantities remains constant during its motion?

A. Energy

B. Displacement

C. Velocity

D. Kinetic Ennergy

Answer A

Solution

CONCEPT: EXPLANATION:

• The capacity to do work is called energy.

• According to the energy conservation principle, the total energy of the system always remains constant.

• Kinetic energy: The energy of a body due to its motion is called kinetic energy.

• Potential energy: The energy of a body due to its position is called potential energy.

• The total energy is the sum of kinetic energy and potential energy.

• When a ball is thrown vertically upwards, the kinetic energy of the ball converted into potential energy of the ball. But the total energy of the ball remains constant during its motion. So option 1 is correct.

• It is because at the beginning the ball has high kinetic energy and no potential energy. As it moves up its kinetic energy gets converted to potential energy. At the peak its kinetic energy is nil and it has the highest potential energy.

• From now onwards its potential energy begins to convert into kinetic energy as it falls to the ground.

• As the ball goes in the upward direction, the displacement, and velocity changes. When the velocity changes the kinetic energy also changes. So options 2, 3, and 4 are wrong.

58. In triangle ABC, ∠ABC = 90°. BP is drawn perpendicular to AC. If ∠BAP = 30°, then what is the value (in degrees) of ∠PBC?

A. 30

B. 36

C. 45

D. 60

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick In a right-angled triangle, the altitude drawn to the hypotenuse creates two smaller triangles that are similar to the original triangle. A key property of this configuration is that the angle between the altitude and a leg is equal to the interior angle at the opposite vertex. Specifically, in ▵ABC right-angled at B with altitude BP, ∠PBC = ∠BAP. Given ∠BAP = 30°, it follows that ∠PBC = 30°. ∴ The correct answer is 30°. Alternate Method Given: In ▵ABC, ∠ABC = 90°, BP ⊥ AC, and ∠BAP = 30°. Formula Used: Angle Sum Property of a triangle (sum of angles = 180°). In ▵ABP: ⇒ ∠APB = 90° (Since BP ⊥ AC) ⇒ ∠BAP = 30° (Given) ⇒ ∠ABP = 180° − (∠APB + ∠BAP) ⇒ ∠ABP = 180° − (90° + 30°) = 60° Now, considering the right angle at vertex B: ⇒ ∠ABC = 90° ⇒ ∠ABP + ∠PBC = 90° ⇒ 60° + ∠PBC = 90° ⇒ ∠PBC = 90° − 60° = 30° ∴ The correct answer is 30°. Additional Information Similarity in Right Triangles The altitude to the hypotenuse of a right triangle divides it into two triangles that are similar to each other and to the original triangle (▵APB ∼ ▵BPC ∼ ▵ABC). Geometric Mean Theorem The length of the altitude BP is the geometric mean of the lengths of the segments of the hypotenuse: BP2 = AP × PC. Angle Relationships In this configuration, the angles are related as ∠ABP = ∠BCP and ∠PBC = ∠BAP.
