40. The short run average cost curve is ____ shaped.

A. U

B. V

C. X

D. W

Answer A

Solution

A cost curve maps the costs incurred by an enterprise at different levels of output. In the short term, the average cost curve is U-shaped. The reason is that, in the beginning of production, fixed factors of a firm remain the same and the change only takes place in variable factors (like labour, raw material, etc). As the output increases, the cost per unit gets distributed more and more. Thus, the average cost begins to fall and reaches a minimum when the firm is operating at its optimum capacity. Now, if the firm expands its scale of production further with the same fixed resources, the average cost starts rising.

41. An image formed by a plane mirror, that cannot be obtained on a screen is called _______.

A. Virtual image

B. Real image

C. Inverted image

D. Erect image

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is option 1 i.e Virtual Image, A virtual image is one which is formed by the apparent divergence of light rays from a point rather than their actual divergence. Such an image cannot be projected on a screen because the light rays do not actually converge at a point. Images formed by mirrors are always virtual. A real image can be viewed when a screen is placed in the plane of convergence, alternatively, one may also view the real image directly. A real image is the collection of focus points actually made by converging rays, while a virtual image is the collection of focus points made by extensions of diverging rays.
