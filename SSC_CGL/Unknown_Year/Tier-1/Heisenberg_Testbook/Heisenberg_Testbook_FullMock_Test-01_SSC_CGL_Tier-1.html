2. How many natural numbers are in between 120 and 519 which are exactly divisible by both 3 and 7 ?

A. 19

B. 17

C. 15

D. 21

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Numbers divisible by both 3 and 7 must be divisible by their LCM(3, 7) = 21. Identify count up to 518: 518 ÷ 21 = 24.66 → 24 multiples. Identify count up to 120: 120 ÷ 21 = 5.71 → 5 multiples. Total numbers in between = 24 − 5 = 19. ∴ The correct answer is 19. Alternate Method Given: Range: Numbers between 120 and 519 (excluding 120 and 519). Divisors: 3 and 7. Formula used: A number divisible by both 3 and 7 is divisible by LCM(3, 7) = 21. Number of terms in Arithmetic Progression (AP): n = [(Last term − First term) ÷ Common Difference] + 1 ⇒ LCM of 3 and 7 = 21. ⇒ First multiple of 21 after 120: 21 × 6 = 126. ⇒ Last multiple of 21 before 519: 21 × 24 = 504. ⇒ In the sequence 126, 147, 168, ..., 504: ⇒ a = 126, l = 504, d = 21. ⇒ n = [(504 − 126) ÷ 21] + 1 ⇒ n = [378 ÷ 21] + 1 ⇒ n = 18 + 1 ⇒ n = 19. ∴ The correct answer is 19. Additional Information LCM Rule for Divisibility If a number must be divisible by multiple divisors (e.g., a and b), it must be divisible by their Least Common Multiple, LCM(a, b). Counting terms in a Range The number of terms in an Arithmetic Progression is given by (Last Term − First Term) ÷ Difference + 1. Between vs. From-To 'Between x and y' excludes both x and y. 'From x to y' includes both x and y.

3. Chords PR and QS of a circle with centre O, are produced to meet at a point T and PQ is the diameter of the circle. If ∠ROS = 48°, then what is the measure of ∠PTQ?

A. 66°

B. 52°

C. 62°

D. 56°

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick For a circle where diameter PQ and produced chords PR and QS meet at T: The direct relation is ∠PTQ = 90° − (∠ROS / 2) Given ∠ROS = 48° ∠PTQ = 90° − (48° / 2) = 90° − 24° = 66° ∴ The correct answer is 66°. Alternate Method Given: PQ is the diameter of the circle with centre O. Chords PR and QS meet at T when produced. ∠ROS = 48° Formula Used: Angle subtended by an arc at the centre is double the angle at the circumference: ∠Central = 2 × ∠Circumference Angle formed by two secants outside a circle: ∠T = 1/2 × (Measure of Major Arc − Measure of Minor Arc) Calculations: ⇒ Arc PQ is a semi-circle, so its measure = 180°. ⇒ The angle subtended by arc RS at the centre is ∠ROS = 48°, so the measure of Arc RS = 48°. ⇒ According to the exterior angle theorem for secants: ⇒ ∠PTQ = 1/2 × (Arc PQ − Arc RS) ⇒ ∠PTQ = 1/2 × (180° − 48°) ⇒ ∠PTQ = 1/2 × 132° ⇒ ∠PTQ = 66° ∴ The correct answer is 66°. Additional Information Angle in a Semicircle Any angle subtended by the diameter at the circumference of the circle is always a right angle (90°). Inscribed Angle Theorem The measure of an inscribed angle is half the measure of its intercepted arc. (∠Circumference = 1/2 × Arc measure). Angles Outside the Circle If two secants, two tangents, or a secant and a tangent intersect outside a circle, the angle formed is half the positive difference of the intercepted arcs.

6. Four cows are tied at 4 corners (one at each corner) of a rectangular field of length 30 m and width 20 m. The length of the rope is 7 m. What is the area of the field that cows CANNOT graze? (Use π = 22/7)

A. 446 m2

B. 434 m2

C. 424 m2

D. 414 m2

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Area of rectangular field = 30 × 20 = 600 m2 Each corner of a rectangle is 90°, so 4 corners = 360° = 1 complete circle. Grazed Area = Area of circle with radius 7 m = πr2 Grazed Area = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 154 m2 Ungrazed Area = Total Area − Grazed Area = 600 − 154 = 446 m2 ∴ The correct answer is 446 m2. Alternate Method Given: Length of rectangular field (L) = 30 m Width of rectangular field (W) = 20 m Length of rope (radius, r) = 7 m Formula Used: Area of Rectangle = Length × Width Area of a Sector = (θ/360) × πr2 Calculations: ⇒ Total area of the field = 30 × 20 = 600 m2 ⇒ In a rectangle, the interior angle at each corner is 90°. ⇒ Area grazed by one cow = (90/360) × π × (7)2 ⇒ Area grazed by one cow = (1/4) × (22/7) × 49 = 38.5 m2 ⇒ Total grazed area by 4 cows = 4 × 38.5 = 154 m2 ⇒ Area that cows cannot graze = Total area − Total grazed area ⇒ Area that cows cannot graze = 600 − 154 = 446 m2 ∴ The correct answer is 446 m2. Additional Information Area of Circle and Sectors The area of a circle is πr2. Since the sum of the angles at the corners of a rectangle is 360°, the four quadrants at the corners combine to form exactly one full circle. General Polygon Case If cows are tied at the corners of any quadrilateral (not just a rectangle), the sum of the interior angles is always 360°, so the total grazed area will still be πr2 if the ropes are of equal length. Perimeter of Grazed Region The total boundary length of the grazed area inside the field is equivalent to the circumference of one circle, which is 2πr.

8. The amount of a certain sum in 2 years at 20% p.a is Rs. 7776, interest compounded yearly. What will be the amount of the same sum in 1(1/2) years at the same rate of interest, compounded half yearly? (Nearest to a Rs.)

Solution

Formula Used: A = P(1 + r/100)n; for half-yearly rate = R/2 and n = 2×T. ⇒ Case 1 (Compounded Yearly): ⇒ 7776 = P(1 + 20/100)2 ⇒ 7776 = P(6/5)2 ⇒ 7776 = P(36/25) ⇒ P = (7776 × 25) ÷ 36 = 216 × 25 = ₹5400 ⇒ Case 2 (Compounded Half-yearly for 1.5 years): ⇒ Rate = 20% ÷ 2 = 10%, Time = 1.5 × 2 = 3 periods ⇒ Amount = 5400(1 + 10/100)3 ⇒ Amount = 5400 × (1.1)3 = 5400 × 1.331 ⇒ Amount = ₹7187.4 ∴ The correct answer is ₹7187. 9. A hollow metallic pipe with an external radius of 41 cm and thickness of 1 cm is melted and recast to form a cylinder of length equal to the length of the pipe. The radius of the cylinder will be.

A. 6 cm

B. 7 cm

C. 8 cm

D. 9 cm

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Radius of solid cylinder (r) = √(External Radius2 − Internal Radius2) Given: External Radius (R) = 41 cm, Thickness = 1 cm → Internal Radius (ri) = 40 cm r = √(412 − 402) r = √((41 − 40) × (41 + 40)) = √(1 × 81) = 9 cm ∴ The correct answer is 9 cm. Alternate Method Given: External radius of pipe (R) = 41 cm Thickness of pipe = 1 cm Internal radius of pipe (ri) = 41 − 1 = 40 cm Length of pipe = Length of cylinder = h Formula Used: Volume of hollow cylinder = π(R2 − ri2)h Volume of solid cylinder = πr2h Calculations: ⇒ Volume of pipe = Volume of cylinder ⇒ π × (R2 − ri2) × h = π × r2 × h ⇒ R2 − ri2 = r2 ⇒ 412 − 402 = r2 ⇒ (41 − 40) × (41 + 40) = r2 ⇒ 1 × 81 = r2 ⇒ r = √81 ⇒ r = 9 cm ∴ The correct answer is 9 cm. Additional Information Volume of Hollow Cylinder The volume of a hollow cylinder or pipe is given by π(R2 − r2)h, where R is the external radius, r is the internal radius, and h is the height. Conservation of Volume When one solid shape is melted and recast into another, the total volume remains constant, provided there is no wastage during the process. Average Speed = (30 ÷ 200) × 60 = 9 km/hr. ∴ The correct answer is 9 km/hr. A. 1635 4

B. 3375 8

C. 3105 8

D. 1625 4

Answer C

Solution

Alternate Method Given: 2x + 1/(3x) = 5 Formula Used: (a + b)3 = a3 + b3 + 3ab(a + b) Calculation: ⇒ Multiply the given equation by 3/2 to adjust the coefficients. ⇒ (3/2) × [2x + 1/(3x)] = (3/2) × 5 ⇒ 3x + 1/(2x) = 15/2 ⇒ Cubing both sides of the equation: ⇒ (3x + 1/(2x))3 = (15/2)3 ⇒ (3x)3 + (1/(2x))3 + 3 × (3x) × (1/(2x)) × (3x + 1/(2x)) = 3375/8 ⇒ 27x3 + 1/(8x3) + (9/2) × (15/2) = 3375/8 ⇒ 27x3 + 1/(8x3) + 135/4 = 3375/8 ⇒ 27x3 + 1/(8x3) = 3375/8 − 270/8 ⇒ 27x3 + 1/(8x3) = 3105/8 ∴ The correct answer is 3105/8. A. 15 hours

D. 8 hours

Answer A

Solution

The rate of A = 1/6 returns per hour, the rate of B = 1/10 returns per hour, and the rate of A, B, and C together = 1/3 returns per hour. 18. A takes twice as long as B and C together take to complete a work, and B takes twice as long as A and C together. However, A, B and C together can complete the work in 30 days. How long would C alone take to complete the work?

A. 50 days

B. 72 days

C. 63 days

D. 90 days

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Ratio of Efficiency of A : (B + C) = 1 : 2 (Sum = 3 parts) Ratio of Efficiency of B : (A + C) = 1 : 2 (Sum = 3 parts) Since total parts are equal, Eff(A) = 1, Eff(B) = 1, and Eff(C) = 3 − (1 + 1) = 1 part. Total Work = Total Efficiency × Days = 3 × 30 = 90 units. Time for C alone = Total Work ÷ Eff(C) = 90 ÷ 1 = 90 days. ∴ The correct answer is 90 days. Alternate Method Given: Time for A = 2 × (B + C) Time for B = 2 × (A + C) Time for (A + B + C) = 30 days Formula Used: Efficiency = Total Work ÷ Time Person Days Total Work Efficiency A + B + C 30 90 units 3 A 90 1 B 90 1 C 90 1 Calculations: ⇒ Efficiency is inversely proportional to time. ⇒ Eff(A) : Eff(B+C) = 1 : 2 ⇒ Eff(B) : Eff(A+C) = 1 : 2 ⇒ Let total efficiency of A + B + C = 3 units. ⇒ From ratio 1: Eff(A) = 1 unit. ⇒ From ratio 2: Eff(B) = 1 unit. ⇒ Eff(C) = Total Efficiency − Eff(A) − Eff(B) = 3 − 1 − 1 = 1 unit. ⇒ Total work = Total Efficiency × Total Days = 3 × 30 = 90 units. ⇒ Time for C alone = Total Work ÷ Eff(C) = 90 ÷ 1 = 90 days. ∴ The correct answer is 90 days. Additional Information Efficiency and Time Relationship Efficiency is inversely proportional to the time taken to complete a task. If the ratio of time taken is x : y, the ratio of efficiency is y : x. Combined Work Rule If A can do a work in 'a' days and B in 'b' days, together they complete it in (a × b) ÷ (a + b) days.
