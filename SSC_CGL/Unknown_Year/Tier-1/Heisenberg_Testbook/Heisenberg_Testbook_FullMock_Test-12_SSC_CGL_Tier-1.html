35. Modern periodic law states that the properties of elements are a periodic function of which fundamental quantity?

A. Atomic mass

B. Atomic number

C. Electronegativity

D. Atomic volume

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Atomic number. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• Modern Periodic Law: The Modern Periodic Law states that the properties of elements are a periodic function of their atomic number, not their atomic mass. This was established after the discovery of protons and the importance of atomic number in determining chemical behavior.

• Atomic Number: The atomic number refers to the number of protons in the nucleus of an atom, which uniquely identifies an element. It is a fundamental property that determines the chemical properties and periodic trends of elements.

• Periodic Table Arrangement: The elements in the modern periodic table are arranged in order of increasing atomic number, which allows elements with similar properties to appear in the same group or column.

• Mendeleev's Contribution: While Dmitri Mendeleev's periodic table was based on atomic mass, certain discrepancies arose. The modern periodic table, based on atomic number, resolved these issues, such as the placement of iodine and tellurium.

• Periodic Trends: Properties such as electronegativity, ionization energy, atomic radius, and chemical reactivity exhibit periodic trends because they depend on the atomic number and the arrangement of electrons in shells.

• Atomic Mass vs. Atomic Number: Earlier periodic tables were based on atomic mass. However, elements like cobalt and nickel, as well as iodine and tellurium, did not align correctly when ordered by atomic mass. Ordering by atomic number resolved these inconsistencies.

• Moseley's Discovery: In 1913, Henry Moseley demonstrated that the atomic number is more fundamental than atomic mass. Using X-ray spectroscopy, he showed that atomic number corresponds to the number of protons in an atom, which is a unique identifier for each element.

• Periodic Trends Explained: The modern periodic table explains trends in chemical properties, such as electronegativity, ionization energy, and atomic radius. These trends arise due to the arrangement of electrons and the atomic number.

• Applications of Atomic Number: The concept of atomic number is used in nuclear chemistry, quantum mechanics, and material science to predict the behavior of elements and their isotopes.

• Periodic Table Groups and Periods: Elements are categorized into groups (vertical columns) and periods (horizontal rows) based on their atomic number. This arrangement helps in understanding their chemical reactivity and bonding behavior.

• Electronegativity: While electronegativity helps determine how atoms attract electrons during bonding, it is not the basis for the arrangement of the periodic table.

• Atomic Volume: Atomic volume refers to the space occupied by an atom, but it does not determine periodic trends or table organization.

• Significance of Atomic Number: The atomic number is crucial for defining isotopes of elements, as isotopes have the same atomic number but different atomic masses.

• Noble Gases: Noble gases (Group 18) exhibit periodicity based on atomic number, as their outer electron shells are completely filled.

• Periodic Table Evolution: The shift from atomic mass to atomic number in the periodic table marked a significant advancement in chemistry, aligning chemical properties with electron configurations.
