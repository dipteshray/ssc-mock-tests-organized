31. What is the term used to describe the maximum displacement of an oscillating object from its equilibrium position?

A. Frequency

B. Phase

C. Period

D. Amplitude

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Amplitude. Key Points Additional Information

• Amplitude refers to the maximum displacement of an oscillating object from its equilibrium position.

• It is a measure of the extent of vibration or oscillation, typically represented in units of distance (meters) or angular displacement (radians).

• The amplitude is directly related to the energy of oscillation, as greater amplitude signifies higher energy.

• Graphically, amplitude is represented as the height of the crest or the depth of the trough of a wave relative to the equilibrium position.

• Amplitude plays a crucial role in determining the intensity of oscillatory motion, such as sound waves (loudness) or light waves (brightness).

• Frequency: Frequency is the number of complete oscillations or cycles a wave undergoes per unit of time.

• It is measured in hertz (Hz), where 1 Hz equals 1 cycle per second.

• Frequency is inversely proportional to the period of the wave, meaning higher frequency corresponds to shorter periods.

• It is a key parameter in wave mechanics, influencing the pitch of sound or the color of light waves.

• Phase: Phase refers to a specific stage in the cycle of a wave at a given point in time, often measured in degrees or radians.

• It is used to describe the relative position of one wave with respect to another.

• A phase difference of 0° implies that the waves are in sync, while 180° implies they are out of phase.

• Phase is crucial in understanding wave interference, such as constructive and destructive interference.

• Period: The period is the time taken by a wave to complete one full oscillation or cycle.

• It is measured in seconds (s) and is the reciprocal of frequency (Period = 1/Frequency).

• The period determines the duration of one cycle and is an important factor in time-dependent wave phenomena.

• Equilibrium Position: The equilibrium position is the point where the net force acting on the oscillating object is zero.

• It represents the stable state of the system in the absence of oscillation or external disturbance.

• It serves as the baseline from which displacement (amplitude) is measured.
