1. Find the LCM of (2/5), (3/5) and (1/2).

A. 1

B. (1/6)

C. 6

D. (6/10)

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick LCM of fractions = LCM(Numerators) ÷ HCF(Denominators) Numerators: 2, 3, 1 → LCM = 6 Denominators: 5, 5, 2 → HCF = 1 Required LCM = 6 ÷ 1 = 6 ∴ The correct answer is 6. Alternate Method Given Fractions: 2/5, 3/5, 1/2 Formula used: LCM of Fractions = LCM(Numerators) ÷ HCF(Denominators) LCM of Numerators (2, 3, 1) = 6 HCF of Denominators (5, 5, 2) = 1 ⇒ Identify the numerators: 2, 3, and 1. ⇒ Multiples of 2: 2, 4, 6, 8, ... ⇒ Multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, ... ⇒ Multiples of 1: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, ... ⇒ Least Common Multiple (LCM) of (2, 3, 1) = 6 ⇒ Identify the denominators: 5, 5, and 2. ⇒ Factors of 5: 1, 5 ⇒ Factors of 2: 1, 2 ⇒ Highest Common Factor (HCF) of (5, 5, 2) = 1 ⇒ LCM of Fractions = 6 ÷ 1 = 6 ∴ The correct answer is 6. Additional Information HCF of Fractions To find the Highest Common Factor of fractions, use the formula: HCF of Fractions = HCF(Numerators) ÷ LCM(Denominators). Simplest Form Requirement Before applying LCM/HCF formulas, always ensure all given fractions are reduced to their lowest (simplest) terms. Relationship for Two Numbers For any two numbers a and b, the product of the numbers is equal to the product of their HCF and LCM: a × b = HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b).

2. The speed of a boat in still water is 30 km/h and the rate of the current is 6 km/h. The distance (in km) travelled upstream in 5 minutes is:

A. 3

B. 4

C. 2

D. 2.5

Answer C

Solution

Distance = Speed × Time Upstream speed = Speed of boat - Speed of current Upstream speed = (30 - 6)km/h = 24 km/h Distance covered in 1 hour (60 minutes) = 24 km Distance covered in 5 minutes travelled Upstream = (24/60) × 5 km = 2 km

3. A number is increased by 30% and then again increased by 30%. By what percentage should the increased number be reduced so as to get back the original number?

A. 40.82%

B. 48.20%

C. 42.80%

D. 38.28%

Answer A

Solution

After two increases, the final value = Original Value × (1 + 30/100) × (1 + 30/100) = 1.69 × Original Value. To return to the original value, the percentage decrease required = (1 - 1/1.69) × 100 = 40.82%.

4. A moneylender lent Rs. 13000 at 30% compound interest per annum for 3 years. What is the difference between the interest earned by the man in the 2nd year only and the interest earned by the man in the 3rd year only?

A. Rs. 2000

B. Rs. 1521

C. Rs. 1444

D. Rs. 1500

Answer B

Solution

Given: Principal = Rs. 13000 Rate % = 30% Time = 3 years Concept used: The effective rate of interest for two years at the rate of R% per annum compounded yearly, ⇒ R + R + R2/100 Similarly, effective rate of interest for three years = 3R + 3R2/100 + R3/10000 Calculation: The effective rate of interest for two years at the rate of 30% per annum ⇒ 30 + 30 + (30 × 30)/100 ⇒ 60 + 9 = 69% ∴ Rate of interest for 2nd year only = 69 - 30 = 39% The effective rate of interest for three years at the rate of 30% per annum ⇒ 69 + 30 + (69 × 30)/100 ⇒ 99 + 20.7 = 119.7 % Rate of interest for only 3rd year = 119.7 - 69 = 50.7% ∴ The difference between the rate of interest for the 2nd year only and the rate of interest for the 3rd year only = 50.7 - 39 ⇒ 11.7% ∴ Interest earned = 13000 × 11.7/100 = Rs. 1521 ∴ The difference between the interest earned by the man in the 2nd year only and the interest earned by the man in the 3rd year only is Rs. 1521. Alternate Method Interest earned in 2nd year only = 3900 + 1170 Interest earned in 3rd year only = 3900 + 2 × 1170 + 351 The difference between the interest earned in the 3rd year only and in the 2nd year only = 3900 + 2 × 1170 + 351 - 3900 - 1170 ⇒ 1170 + 351 = Rs. 1521 ∴ The difference between the interest earned by the man in the 2nd year only and the interest earned by the man in the 3rd year only is Rs. 1521.

5. A sum of money doubles itself at compound interest in 10 years. In how many years will it be four times at the same rate of interest?

A. 10

B. 20

C. 15

D. 5

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick In Compound Interest, if a sum becomes 'n' times in 'T' years, it becomes 'nk' times in 'k × T' years. Here, the sum doubles (2 times) in 10 years (n = 2, T = 10). To become four times (4 = 22), the power k = 2. Time required = k × T = 2 × 10 = 20 years. ∴ The correct answer is 20 years. Alternate Method Given: Initial Sum = P Amount after 10 years = 2P Target Amount = 4P Formula Used: A = P(1 + r/100)t Calculations: ⇒ 2P = P(1 + r/100)10 ⇒ 2 = (1 + r/100)10 ---- (i) ⇒ Let the required time be 't' years for the sum to become 4P. ⇒ 4P = P(1 + r/100)t ⇒ 4 = (1 + r/100)t ⇒ (2)2 = (1 + r/100)t ⇒ Substituting value of 2 from (i): ⇒ [(1 + r/100)10]2 = (1 + r/100)t ⇒ (1 + r/100)20 = (1 + r/100)t ⇒ By comparing powers, t = 20 years. ∴ The correct answer is 20 years. Additional Information Effective Rate of Interest When interest is compounded more than once a year, the effective annual rate is (1 + r/n)n − 1, where n is the frequency of compounding. Rule of 72 A quick way to estimate how long it takes to double money at CI: Time ≈ 72 ÷ Interest Rate. SI vs CI for Doubling In Simple Interest, if a sum doubles in T years, it becomes n times in (n−1) × T years. For 4 times, it would take (4−1) × 10 = 30 years.

6. A is half as efficient as B who is thrice as efficient as C. In how many days can all 3 of them complete a task if B can complete the task alone in 6 days?

A. 3.13

B. 3.27

C. 3.33

D. 3.36

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Ratio of efficiency of A : B : C = 3 : 6 : 2. Total efficiency of A, B, and C = 3 + 6 + 2 = 11 units/day. Total work = Efficiency of B × Days taken by B = 6 × 6 = 36 units. Time taken by A, B, and C together = Total Work ÷ Total Efficiency = 36 ÷ 11 ≈ 3.27 days. ∴ The correct answer is 3.27 days. Alternate Method Given: A is half as efficient as B. B is thrice as efficient as C. B can complete the task alone in 6 days. Calculations: ⇒ Let the efficiency of C be 2 units/day. ⇒ Efficiency of B = 3 × 2 = 6 units/day. ⇒ Efficiency of A = 1/2 × 6 = 3 units/day. ⇒ Total Work = B's efficiency × Days taken by B = 6 × 6 = 36 units. ⇒ Time taken by A = Total Work ÷ A's efficiency = 36 ÷ 3 = 12 days. ⇒ Time taken by C = Total Work ÷ C's efficiency = 36 ÷ 2 = 18 days. Person Days Total Work Efficiency A 12 36 36 ÷ 12 = 3 B 6 36 ÷ 6 = 6 C 18 36 ÷ 18 = 2 ⇒ Total efficiency of A, B, and C = 3 + 6 + 2 = 11 units/day. ⇒ Time taken by all 3 together = Total Work ÷ Total Efficiency = 36 ÷ 11 ≈ 3.27 days. ∴ The correct answer is 3.27 days. Additional Information Efficiency & Time Relation The efficiency of a person is inversely proportional to the time taken to complete a task. Hence, Ratio of Time = 1 ÷ Ratio of Efficiency. Work & Wages When multiple people work together for the exact same number of days, their total wages are distributed in the direct proportion of their working efficiencies. Pipes & Cisterns Concept This concept operates identically to Time & Work problems. A filling pipe represents positive efficiency, whereas an emptying pipe or a leak denotes negative efficiency.

10. If sinθ - cosθ = 0, then what is sin4θ + cos4θ equal to?

A. 1

B. (3/4)

C. (1/2)

D. (1/4)

Answer C

Solution

Required value = (1/√2)4 + (1/√2)4 = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2. ∴ The correct answer is 1/2. Additional Information Trigonometric Identity The fundamental identity sin2θ + cos2θ = 1 is valid for all values of θ. Power Reduction Formula For any angle, sin4θ + cos4θ = 1 − 2sin2θcos2θ or 1 − (1/2)sin2(2θ). Values of Standard Angles At θ = 45°, sinθ = cosθ = 1/√2. At θ = 0°, sinθ=0, cosθ=1.
