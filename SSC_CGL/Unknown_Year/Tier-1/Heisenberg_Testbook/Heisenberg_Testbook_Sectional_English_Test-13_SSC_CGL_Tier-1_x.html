1. Select the most appropriate option that rectifies the underlined group of words in the given sentence. If the pressure in the reactor increase beyond the safety threshold, the cooling system is designed to activate automatically to prevent a meltdown.

B. Were the pressure in the reactor to have

C. If the pressure in the reactor shall

D. If the pressure in the reactor should have

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is '1' i.e. Should the pressure in the reactor . Key Points Correct sentence: Should the pressure in the reactor increase beyond the safety threshold, the cooling system is designed to activate automatically to prevent a meltdown. Additional Information

• The given sentence refers to a hypothetical situation where the pressure in the reactor increases beyond a safety threshold.

• The correct form for this conditional sentence is the inversion of "if" with "should," which is a formal way to express conditional situations.

• When "should" is used in place of "if," the sentence structure changes to: Should + subject + base verb. This is grammatically correct and concise.

• Conditional Sentence Types: Second Conditional: Used for hypothetical situations in the present or future. Example: If I were rich, I would travel the world.

• Inverted Conditional (Formal): Uses "should" to replace "if." Example: Should you need assistance, please call customer service.

• The other options: Option 2 ("Were the pressure..."): Incorrect as it implies an unreal past situation.

• Option 3 ("If the pressure... shall"): Incorrect as "shall" is not used in hypothetical conditional sentences.

• Option 4 ("If the pressure... should have"): Incorrect as it refers to an unreal past scenario.

3. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. People generally suppose that the CEO is planning to resign.

A. It is generally supposed by people that the CEO is planning to resign.

B. The CEO is generally supposed that he is planning to resign.

C. To resign is generally supposed to be planned by the CEO.

D. The CEO is generally supposed to be planning to resign.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is '4' i.e. The CEO is generally supposed to be planning to resign. Key Points Correct sentence: The CEO is generally supposed to be planning to resign. Additional Information

• The given sentence is in the active voice, where the subject (people) performs the action of supposing.

• In the passive voice, the object of the active sentence (the CEO) becomes the subject of the passive sentence.

• The correct transformation is: People generally suppose that the CEO is planning to resign → The CEO is generally supposed to be planning to resign.

• The structure for sentences of this type in the passive voice is: Subject + is/are + supposed + infinitive (to + base verb).

• Active Voice: The subject performs the action. Example: People suppose that the CEO is planning to resign.

• Passive Voice: The subject receives the action. Example: The CEO is generally supposed to be planning to resign.

• Passive voice is often used to focus on the action or the recipient of the action rather than the doer.
