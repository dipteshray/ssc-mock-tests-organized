15. Change the following from active to passive: Someone must have tempered with the encryption keys.

A. The encryption keys must have been tempered with.

B. The encryption keys must have tempered by someone.

C. The encryption keys must be tempered with by someone.

D. The encryption keys must had been tempered with.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: The encryption keys must have been tempered with. The conversion is from Active to Passive. Explanation of the change in form: The Active voice in the question is in Present Perfect Modal (Subject + must + have + V3 + Object). Thus, the Passive voice is formed by shifting the object of the active sentence to the subject position. The structure in Passive becomes: Object of active voice + must + have + been + V3 + by + subject of active voice (optional). Here, "Someone" is the implied subject, which is omitted in the Passive voice, and "the encryption keys" becomes the subject in the Passive form.
