Additional Information

• Example: By the time the train arrived, they had already left the station.

• Past Simple Tense: Structure: Subject + verb (past form).

• Example: They left the station at 5 PM.

• Usage: The past simple is used to describe actions that happened at a specific time in the past.

Solution

• The given sentence is in Passive Voice. As per the given question we have to change it into Active Voice.

• The process of transformation is as follows: The structure of the given sentence is as follows: Object + shall/will not + have + been + V3 + by + subject. (Passive Voice)

• Subject + shall/will not + have + V3 + object. (Active Voice)

• The subject of the given sentence is 'The message'.

• The object of the given sentence is 'Hansa'.

• The subject will be put in place of the object and the object will be put in place of the subject.

• 'will not have been received' will be changed into 'will not have received'.

• Lastly, the conjunction 'by' will be removed.

• Therefore, as per the points mentioned above, we find that the correct answer is Option 1.

• Transformation of Infinitives: The structure of it is as follows:

• Subject + is/am/are/was/were/has/have/had + to+ V1+ Object.

• Subject(Objective case) + is/am/are/was/were/has/have/had+to+be+ V3 + by+ object(subjective case). Example: Your child is to learn chess. (Active Voice)

25. Direction: Choose the option that is in the direct form of the sentence. Hitesh told me that he couldn’t pick my call as he had been driving.

A. Hitesh said to me, "He couldn't pick my call as I was driving."

B. Hitesh told to me, "I couldn't pick my call as I had been driving."

C. Hitesh said to me, "I couldn't pick your call as I was driving."

D. Hitesh told me, "I couldn't pick your call as he was driving."

Answer C

Solution

The direct form of the given sentence is 'Hitesh said to me, "I couldn't pick your call as I was driving." Key Points The final sentence- Hitesh said to me, "I couldn't pick your call as I was driving."

• While changing a sentence from indirect speech to direct speech, we need to follow these steps- The conjunction 'that' is replaced with a comma (,) and inverted commas (" ").

• 'Told' remains either unchanged or changed into 'said to'.

• The past perfect continuous tense (had been driving) is either remains unchanged or changed into the past continuous tense (was driving).

• The third-person (he) is changed into 'I' i.e. according to the subject of the reporting verb (Hitesh).

• The possessive adjective 'my' is changed into 'your' i.e. according to the object of the reporting verb (me).
