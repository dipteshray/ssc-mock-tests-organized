1. In a row of boys, Rahul is 33rd from the left end. Rajat is 17 ranks to the right of Rahul. If Rajat is 35th from right end, then what will be the rank of Rahul from the right end?

A. 49

B. 50

C. 51

D. 52

Answer D

Solution

Rahul’s rank from the left = 33 Rajat’s rank from the right = 35 Rajat’s rank = 17 to the right of Rahul So, Rajat’s rank from the left = 33 + 17 → 50 Total students = Rajat’s rank from left + Rajat’s rank from the right – 1 → 50 + 35 – 1 → 84 Rahul’s rank from the right = Total students – Rahul’s rank from the left – 1 → 84 – 33 + 1 = 52 Hence, Rahul’s rank from the right is 52.
