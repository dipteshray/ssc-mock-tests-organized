Solution

Kathakali is a classical dance form from Kerala, distinct from the Odia culture. 11. Which Article of the Indian Constitution mentions that ‘The Council of Ministers shall be collectively responsible to the Legislative Assembly of the state’?

A. Article 164(3)

B. Article 164(4)

C. Article 164(2)

D. Article 164(1)

Answer C

Solution

Article 164(2) of the Indian Constitution mandates that the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Legislative Assembly of the state. This implies that the entire Council, including the Chief Minister, must answer to the Assembly for its actions. A vote of no confidence by the Assembly necessitates the resignation of the whole Council. This ensures the executive's accountability and reflects parliamentary democracy principles. The Indian Constitution, effective from 26th January 1950, is the world's longest written constitution, detailing political principles, structures, powers, and citizen duties, and emphasizing justice, equality, and liberty.

25. Which of the following is NOT correct about Directive Principles of State Policy?

A. They ensure social and economic development.

B. They are justiciable in nature.

C. They provide guidelines for the governance of the country.

D. They are morally binding on the government.

Answer B

Solution

The Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) are guidelines enshrined in Part IV of the Indian Constitution, aimed at promoting social and economic justice. They ensure social and economic development by guiding the state in formulating policies for the welfare of the people. The DPSPs provide a framework for governance, directing the government in law and policy-making to promote public welfare.
