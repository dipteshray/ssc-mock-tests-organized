1. Fill in the blank with most appropriate tense: If you study hard, you ___ the exam.

A. pass

B. passed

C. will pass

D. passing

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Option 3. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 3. Correct Sentence: If you study hard, you will pass the exam. Additional Information

• The sentence provided is an example of the first conditional, which is used to describe real or possible situations in the future.

• In the first conditional sentence structure, the if-clause uses the present simple tense, and the result clause uses "will + base verb."

• The correct structure for this sentence is: If + Present Simple, will + base verb.

• Among the options provided, "will pass" fits the required structure and conveys the correct meaning.

• 1st Conditional Sentence: Structure: If + Present Simple, will + base verb.

• Example: If it rains, we will cancel the picnic.

• Usage: Describes real or possible situations in the future with likely outcomes.

• 2nd Conditional Sentence: Structure: If + Past Simple, would + base verb.

• Example: If I were rich, I would travel the world.

• Usage: Refers to hypothetical or imaginary situations in the present or future.

• 3rd Conditional Sentence: Structure: If + Past Perfect, would have + past participle.

• Example: If she had studied harder, she would have passed the exam.

• Usage: Refers to unreal past situations and their imagined outcomes.

8. Find the part of the sentence that contains an error: Of the two brothers (1) / Ramesh is more tall (2) / than Suresh (3) / by two inches (4).

A. Of the two brothers (1)

B. Ramesh is more tall (2)

C. than Suresh (3)

D. by two inches. (4)

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is 2. Correct Sentence: "Of the two brothers, Ramesh is taller than Suresh by two inches." Additional Information

• The error is located in part 2 of the sentence, specifically in the formation of the comparative adjective.

• In English, most one-syllable adjectives (like tall, fast, short) form their comparative degree by adding the suffix "-er" rather than using the word "more."

• The phrase "more tall" is grammatically incorrect and should be replaced with "taller."

• "More" is generally used for adjectives with three or more syllables (e.g., "more beautiful") or certain two-syllable adjectives.

• (1) "Of the two brothers" – This is a correct way to introduce a comparison between two specific entities. In such cases, the comparative degree is used.

• (3) "than Suresh" – This part is correct; "than" is the standard conjunction used to introduce the second element in a comparison.

• (4) "by two inches." – This correctly uses the preposition "by" to indicate the margin or degree of difference between the two subjects.

• Comparison Rule: When comparing two things, use the comparative degree (taller). When comparing three or more, use the superlative degree (tallest).

12. Choose the correct active voice transformation of the sentence: The new environmental policy will be implemented by the government with immediate effect.

A. The government will implement the new environmental policy with immediate effect.

B. The government will have implemented the new environmental policy with immediate effect.

C. The government has implemented the new environmental policy with immediate effect.

D. The government is implementing the new environmental policy with immediate effect.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Option 1. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 1. Correct Sentence: "The government will implement the new environmental policy with immediate effect." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The subject of the active voice sentence becomes the object of the passive voice sentence, and the object becomes the subject.

• The given sentence is already in the passive voice: "The new environmental policy will be implemented by the government with immediate effect."

• To convert this to active voice, the subject becomes "The government," and the object becomes "the new environmental policy."

• The correct active voice structure is: "The government will implement the new environmental policy with immediate effect."

• The verb "will be implemented" changes to "will implement" to match the active voice construction.

• Option 2: "The government will have implemented the new environmental policy with immediate effect." – Incorrect tense (future perfect).

• Option 3: "The government has implemented the new environmental policy with immediate effect." – Incorrect tense (present perfect).

• Option 4: "The government is implementing the new environmental policy with immediate effect." – Incorrect tense (present continuous).
