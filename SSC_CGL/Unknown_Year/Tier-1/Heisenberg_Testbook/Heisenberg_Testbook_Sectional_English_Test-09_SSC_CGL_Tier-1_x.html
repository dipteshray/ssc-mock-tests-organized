C. flabby

D. valiant

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is "valiant" Key Points Let's see the meanings of the given words According to the meanings of the given words, the correct answer is Option 4, i.e., valiant.

• Doughty means being brave and resolute, often in the face of challenges or adversity. It can also refer to something that is strong and sturdy. (डौटी का अर्थ है बहादुर और दृढ़ होना, अक्सर चुनौतियों या विपरीत परिस्थितियों का सामना करना। यह किसी ऐसी चीज का भी उल्लेख कर सकता है जो मजबूत हो।) Example: The doughty explorer braved the treacherous jungle, determined to discover the lost city.

(धोखेबाज का अर्थ है गुमराह करना या धोखा देना। यह किसी ऐसी चीज़ का भी उल्लेख कर सकता है जो ऐसा नहीं लगता है या जिसका उद्देश्य गुमराह करना या धोखा देना है।) Example: The deceptive salesman tried to convince the customer to buy the car by pointing out its supposed advantages, even though it was actually a lemon.

• Flabby means being soft, loose, or lacking firmness or toned muscles. It can also refer to something that is weak or lacking in strength or vigor. (फ्लैबी का अर्थ है नरम, ढीला होना, या दृढ़ता या टोंड मांसपेशियों की कमी। जो कमजोर है या ताक़त में कमी है।) Example: After months of inactivity, he had become flabby and out of shape.

• Valiant means being brave, courageous, or noble in character. It can also refer to something that is worthy of respect or admiration. (बहादुर का अर्थ है बहादुर, साहसी या चरित्र में महान होना। यह किसी ऐसी चीज का भी उल्लेख कर सकता है जो सम्मान या प्रशंसा के योग्य हो।)

• Example: The valiant firefighter risked her own life to save the trapped child from the burning building.

12. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in indirect speech. The critic remarked, "What a magnificent performance the lead actor gave! Rarely have I seen such raw emotion on this stage."

A. The critic exclaimed that the lead actor gave a magnificent performance and that rarely he had seen such raw emotion on that stage.

B. The critic exclaimed that the lead actor had given a very magnificent performance and remarked that he had rarely seen such raw emotion on that stage.

C. The critic exclaimed with surprise that what a magnificent performance the actor had given and that rarely had he seen such raw emotion on that stage.

D. The critic remarked with wonder that the lead actor had given a magnificent performance and added that rarely had he seen such raw emotion on that stage.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is '4' i.e. The critic remarked with wonder that the lead actor had given a magnificent performance and added that rarely had he seen such raw emotion on that stage . Key Points Correct sentence: The critic remarked with wonder that the lead actor had given a magnificent performance and added that rarely had he seen such raw emotion on that stage. Additional Information

• The given sentence is an example of indirect speech, where a direct quotation is converted into a report without changing the meaning.

• When converting a sentence into indirect speech: The tense of the verbs usually shifts back (e.g., past simple to past perfect).

• Expressions like "What a magnificent performance!" are rewritten to maintain their emotional tone but in an indirect way.

• Words like "this" and "here" change to "that" and "there."

• Option 4 is correct because: The direct speech "What a magnificent performance" is correctly converted into "the lead actor had given a magnificent performance."

• The phrase "Rarely have I seen" is correctly transformed into "rarely had he seen."

• The emotional tone is preserved with "remarked with wonder" and "added."

• Rules for Converting Direct to Indirect Speech: Tense Shift: Present tense changes to past tense; past tense changes to past perfect.

• Pronoun Change: Pronouns must be adjusted to reflect the perspective of the reporting speaker.

• Time and Place Words: Words like "now," "today," "this," and "here" change to "then," "that day," "that," and "there."

• Example: Direct: He said, "I am happy today."

• Indirect: He said that he was happy that day.
