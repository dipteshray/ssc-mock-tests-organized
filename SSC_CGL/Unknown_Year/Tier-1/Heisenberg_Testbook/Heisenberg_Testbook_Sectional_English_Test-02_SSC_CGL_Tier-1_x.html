1. Which of the following acts gave legislative councils the power to discuss the budget?

A. Government of India Act 1919

B. Indian Councils Act of 1892

C. Government of India Act 1935

D. Pitt's India Act of 1784

Answer B

Solution

The Indian Councils Act of 1892 was significant for increasing the number of additional (non-official) members in the Central and provincial legislative councils while maintaining an official majority. It expanded the functions of these councils by allowing them to discuss the budget and address questions to the executive. Additionally, the Act provided for the nomination of some non-official members to the Central Legislative Council by the viceroy based on recommendations from provincial councils and the Bengal Chamber of Commerce, and to the Provincial Legislative Councils by the Governors based on recommendations from various local bodies and associations.

7. 'Ijara' revenue system was started during the reign of

A. Bahadur Shah Zafar

B. Farrukhsiyar

C. Jahandar Shah

D. Mohd Shah

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Jahandar Shah. Key Points Additional Information

• Ijarah system was a revenue farming system introduced by Jahandar Shah.

• When peasants lacked the means to cultivate their land or cultivation was impossible due to a calamity, the lands were farmed out on ijara to a third party known as revenue farmers.

• It was forbidden for revenue officials or their families to take land on ijara.

• It was anticipated that revenue farmers would not take more from peasants than the stipulated land revenue.

• The revenue farmer paid nine-tenths of the total collection to the government and retained the remainder as collection charges.

• Jahandar Shah was a Mughal Emperor who ruled for a brief period in 1712–1713.

• Farrukhsiyar was the Mughal emperor from 1713 to 1719 after he murdered Jahandar Shah. Farrukh Siyar Farman in 1717 was a grant-giving concession to the English East India company and allowed them tax-free monopoly trade especially in the Mughal subah of Bengal, Bihar, and Orissa.

• Muḥammad Shah was the thirteenth Mughal emperor, who reigned from 1719 to 1748. He was the son of Khujista Akhtar, the fourth son of Bahadur Shah I.

• He ascended the throne with the help of the Sayyid brothers at the young age of 17 and later got rid of them.

• His pen name was Sada Rangila (Ever Joyous) and was often referred to as Muhammad Shah Rangila.

• Bahadur Shah II (Bahadur Shah Zafar) was the last Mughal Emperor. He was deposed by the British and exiled to Burma (Rangoon) following the Indian Rebellion of 1857.

8. If there were no organic life on the Earth, the amount of oxygen in the atmosphere will

A. Remain unchanged.

B. Be approximately hundred per cent.

C. Be almost nil.

D. Be approximately fifty per cent.

Answer C

Solution

Oxygen is a crucial element for sustaining human life and is found in the Earth's atmosphere, making up approximately 20%. Its presence in the atmosphere is a strong signal of possible life on other planets if discovered. However, it is important to note that oxygen will react with compounds to oxidize them, meaning without replenishment from organic life, the amount of oxygen in the atmosphere will be almost nil. Therefore, the correct answer is that the amount of oxygen in the atmosphere will be almost nil.

18. Which one of the following statements is not correct?

A. When total utility is maximum, marginal utility is zero.

B. When total utility is decreasing, marginal utility is negative.

C. When total utility is increasing, marginal utility is positive.

D. When total utility is maximum, marginal and average utility is equal to each other.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4. Key Points Total utility Marginal utility Decreasing Negative Maximum Zero Increasing Positive

• Total Utility is the sum of all utilities derived by a consumer from all units of commodity consumed by him. It rises when more consumption is done.

• It suffers from reduced returns.

• Marginal utility is the addition to the total utility derived by consuming an extra or additional unit of the commodity. It drops with an increase in total utility.

• It reduces the consumption of each additional unit.

• Unlike marginal utility, average utility is always positive, as it is a ratio of two non-negative values.

• So, the graph of average utility always remains above the X-axis.

• When average utility attains maximum value, it is equal to marginal utility. Like the marginal utility curve, the average utility curve is also downward sloping.

22. Who among the following coined the name oxygen for the element released by mercury oxide in 1779?

A. Antoine Lavoisier

B. Martin Klaproth

C. Jons Jacob Berzelius

D. John Dalton

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Antoine Lavoisier. Key Points Additional Information

• Antoine Lavoisier is known as the "Father of Modern Chemistry".

• He coined the term "oxygen" in 1779 after conducting experiments with mercury oxide.

• Lavoisier demonstrated that oxygen is a key component in combustion and respiration.

• He also named hydrogen and helped reform chemical nomenclature.

• Lavoisier's work laid the foundation for the law of conservation of mass.

• Mercury Oxide Experiment: Lavoisier heated mercury oxide and observed the release of a gas, which he identified as oxygen.

• This experiment debunked the phlogiston theory, which was the prevailing theory of combustion at the time.

• Law of Conservation of Mass: Lavoisier established that mass is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.

• This principle is fundamental to modern chemical equations and stoichiometry.

• Chemical Nomenclature: Lavoisier collaborated with other chemists to create a systematic method for naming chemical substances.

• This new nomenclature helped standardize chemical language and improve communication among scientists.

• Lavoisier's Publications: His notable works include "Traité Élémentaire de Chimie" (Elementary Treatise of Chemistry) published in 1789.

• This book is considered one of the first comprehensive chemistry textbooks and outlines the principles of the new chemistry.
