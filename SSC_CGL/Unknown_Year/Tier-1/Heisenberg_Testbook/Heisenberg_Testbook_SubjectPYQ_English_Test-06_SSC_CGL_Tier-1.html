7. Change the following from active to passive: The tourists had taken many photographs.

A. Many photographs have been taken by the tourists.

B. Many photographs are taken by the tourists.

C. Many photographs had been taken by the tourists.

D. Many photographs were being taken by the tourists.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is '3' i.e. Many photographs had been taken by the tourists. Key Points Correct sentence: Many photographs had been taken by the tourists. Additional Information

• The given sentence is in the past perfect tense in active voice: "The tourists had taken many photographs."

• To convert a sentence from active to passive voice, the object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence.

• The structure of the passive voice for past perfect tense is: Object + had been + past participle + by + subject.

• Following this structure, the correct passive voice form is: Many photographs had been taken by the tourists.

• Common tense transformations for passive voice: Present Simple: Active: "The tourists take photographs." Passive: "Photographs are taken by the tourists."

• Past Simple: Active: "The tourists took photographs." Passive: "Photographs were taken by the tourists."

• Future Simple: Active: "The tourists will take photographs." Passive: "Photographs will be taken by the tourists."

• Present Perfect: Active: "The tourists have taken photographs." Passive: "Photographs have been taken by the tourists."

• Past Perfect: Active: "The tourists had taken photographs." Passive: "Photographs had been taken by the tourists."
