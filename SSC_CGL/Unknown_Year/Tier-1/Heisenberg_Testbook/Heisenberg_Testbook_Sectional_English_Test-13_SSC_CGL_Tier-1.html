3. If 17 December 2012 was Monday, then what was the day of the week on 18 November 2020?

A. Tuesday

B. Wednesday

C. Thursday

D. Sunday

Answer B

Solution

Given: 17 December 2012 was Monday. Now, 2012 to 2020 = 8 Years (6 Ordinary Years and 2 Leap Years). And, In 8 Years = 2 Leap Years and 6 Ordinary Years. So, 2 Leap Years have 4 odd days, And, 6 Ordinary Years have 6 odd days. Total odd days = 4 + 6 = 10. 10 odd days → 10/7 = 1 week and 3 odd days. then, 17 December 2020 will be Monday + 3 = Thursday. Now, the number of days between 18 November 2020 to 17 December 2020 = 12 + 17 = 29 days. = 29 days → 29/7 = 4 weeks and 1 odd days. The dates are in past, so subtract 1 days from the day on 17 December 2020 which is Thursday So, 18 November 2020 will be Thursday - 1 = Wednesday. Hence, the correct answer is "Option 2".
