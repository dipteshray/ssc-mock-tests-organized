32. Which of the following properties of light waves remains unchanged when light travels from one medium to another (e.g., from air to water)?

A. Wavelength

B. Speed

C. Frequency

D. Amplitude

Answer C

Solution

When a light wave passes from one medium to another, its speed changes (e.g., it slows down in water compared to air), which in turn causes its wavelength to change (λ = v/f). The amplitude also generally changes due to reflection and absorption at the boundary. However, the frequency of the light wave remains constant. This is because the frequency is determined by the source of the light and does not change as the wave propagates through different media.
