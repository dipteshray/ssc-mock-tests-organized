1. How does the IMEC Alliance impact global supply chains?

A. Disrupts existing supply chains

B. Strengthens supply chain efficiency

C. Limits trade to specific regions

D. Reduces supply chain reliability

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Strengthens supply chain efficiency. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• The India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) is a multi-modal infrastructure project designed to strengthen supply chain efficiency by creating a seamless ship-to-rail transit network connecting India, the Arabian Gulf, and Europe.

• One of the primary objectives of the IMEC Alliance is to reduce transit time for goods by approximately 40% compared to existing maritime routes through the Suez Canal.

• The corridor aims to achieve a 30% reduction in transit costs, thereby making global trade more cost-effective and accessible for participating nations.

• By diversifying trade routes, the alliance enhances global supply chain resilience, ensuring that logistics remain functional even during geopolitical tensions or maritime disruptions.

• The project is not limited to transport; it also integrates digital connectivity through undersea cables and energy security via clean hydrogen pipelines, which boosts the overall efficiency of resource distribution.

• Launch and Participants: The IMEC was officially announced on September 9, 2023, during the G20 Summit held in New Delhi. The signatory countries and entities include India, the United States, the United Arab Emirates (UAE), Saudi Arabia, France, Germany, Italy, and the European Union.

• Corridor Structure: The project is divided into two main segments: the East Corridor, which connects Indian ports to the Arabian Gulf, and the Northern Corridor, which connects the Gulf to European ports.

• Infrastructure Components: It involves the construction of a high-speed railway line, electricity grids, high-speed data cables, and pipelines for renewable energy, specifically green hydrogen.

• Strategic Importance: IMEC is often viewed as a strategic alternative to China's Belt and Road Initiative (BRI), emphasizing transparency, sustainability, and sovereign equality among partners.

• Economic Impact: The alliance is expected to stimulate economic growth, facilitate cross-border trade, and create significant employment opportunities across Asia, the Middle East, and Europe.

• Logistics and Transit: The use of railway systems to supplement maritime transport helps in bypassing congested chokepoints, thereby increasing the reliability of global logistics.

• Sustainability: The IMEC aligns with global climate goals by promoting clean energy trade and reducing the carbon footprint of international shipping through shorter transportation distances.

• Geopolitics: This alliance fosters closer diplomatic ties and economic integration between Israel, Arab nations, India, and the West, contributing to regional stability.

• Trade Volumes: The corridor is anticipated to handle a significant volume of container traffic, which will strengthen India’s position as a global manufacturing hub and a key exporter to European markets.

3. The first-ever Export Preparedness Index in India was launched in ______.

A. 2018

B. 2005

C. 2015

D. 2020

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is 2020. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• The first-ever Export Preparedness Index (EPI) in India was launched in the year 2020 by the NITI Aayog, in collaboration with the Institute of Competitiveness.

• The Export Preparedness Index aims to evaluate the export readiness of Indian states and Union Territories (UTs).

• The index is structured around four pillars: Policy, Business Ecosystem, Export Ecosystem, and Export Performance.

• Gujarat topped the rankings in the inaugural edition of the EPI 2020, followed by Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu.

• The goal of the index is to foster a competitive and conducive export environment across all states and UTs, thereby helping them identify areas of improvement.

• Objectives of the Export Preparedness Index: To examine the performance of Indian states and UTs in enhancing export competitiveness.

• To identify challenges and opportunities to improve state-wise and national export performance.

• To provide a framework for policy-makers to design and implement export strategies at the sub-national level.

• Four Pillars of the Index: Policy: Examines the export-related policies of states and their effectiveness in promoting exports.

• Business Ecosystem: Evaluates the ease of doing business, infrastructure, and the availability of a supportive ecosystem for industries.

• Export Ecosystem: Assesses the export infrastructure and the specific strategies implemented to boost exports.

• Export Performance: Analyzes the export trends and growth from the respective states and UTs.

• Top Performers of EPI 2020: Gujarat: Ranked first due to strong policies and robust infrastructure for exports.

• Maharashtra: Secured the second position owing to its diverse industrial base and export-oriented policies.

• Tamil Nadu: Ranked third, benefiting from its strong manufacturing sector and export-friendly environment.

• Significance of the Index: Helps identify bottlenecks in state-level export promotion strategies.

• Encourages states to develop export-oriented policies tailored to their unique strengths.

• Supports the central government's vision of making India a global export hub.

• NITI Aayog: The National Institution for Transforming India (NITI Aayog) was established in 2015 to replace the Planning Commission.

• It serves as a policy think tank for the Government of India, focusing on sustainable development and inclusive growth.

• India’s Export Performance: India is one of the world's largest exporters in sectors like textiles, pharmaceuticals, and IT services.

• The government aims to increase India's share in global trade through initiatives like Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat.

• Export Promotion Schemes: Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS): A scheme to incentivize exporters by providing duty credit scrips.

• Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products (RoDTEP): Aims to refund embedded taxes and duties incurred during the production process.

• ISRO and JAXA Collaboration: ISRO will provide the rover for the mission, while JAXA will contribute the lander and launch services.

Solution

• The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023, which replaced the Indian Penal Code (IPC) of 1860, contains a dedicated chapter for crimes against vulnerable groups.

• Chapter V of the BNS is titled 'Of Offences against Women and Children'. It consolidates and streamlines various provisions related to sexual offences, marriage-related crimes, and offences against minors.

• One significant addition in Chapter V is the introduction of Section 69, which criminalizes sexual intercourse by deceitful means or making false promises to marry.

• The act also introduces Community Service as a form of punishment for petty offences for the first time in Indian criminal law.

• Iceland also ranks highly in terms of gender equality, freedom of expression, and environmental sustainability, further solidifying its peaceful status.

• It ranks countries based on peacefulness, using data from various international organizations, including the United Nations.

• The 23 indicators used in the GPI include factors like homicide rates, incarceration rates, military expenditure, and relations with neighboring countries.

• Why Iceland is Peaceful: Iceland does not have a standing army, and its defense is managed through agreements with other NATO members.

• The country has low incarceration rates and an efficient, transparent judicial system.

• It has a homogeneous society with minimal socio-political conflicts and a strong emphasis on education and community welfare.

• These countries also exhibit strong governance, low crime rates, and high levels of socio-economic equity.

• It provides insights into trends in peace, conflict, and stability, aiding in the development of peace-building strategies.

• Global Trends in Peacefulness: The 2023 GPI report highlighted a global decline in peacefulness due to factors like geopolitical tensions, economic instability, and climate change.

• Regions like Europe continue to dominate the GPI rankings, while Middle Eastern and Sub-Saharan African countries face challenges due to ongoing conflicts.

• Notable Low-ranking Countries: Countries like Syria, Afghanistan, and South Sudan rank lowest in the GPI due to ongoing wars, political instability, and economic crises.
