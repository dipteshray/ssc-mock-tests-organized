5. In the question given below is given a statement followed by two assumptions numbered I and II. An assumption is something taken for granted. You have to consider the statement and the following assumptions and decide which of the assumptions is implicit in the statement. Statement: The tribals have realized that no good will come out of their sacrificing rituals for rain and have started focusing on forestation instead. Assumptions: 1. The tribals continue to attach immense value to their rituals. 2. Planting more trees helps in increasing rainfall.

A. Only assumption I is implicit

B. Only assumption II is implicit

C. Neither assumption I nor II is implicit

D. Both assumptions I and II are implicit

Answer B

Solution

The given statement provides us with the fact that the tribals have abandoned their superstitious practices of performing various rituals for rain and have instead started focusing on planting trees. The first assumption indicates that the tribals continue to attach immense value to their rituals. This assumption is clearly not true, since the tribals are ready to give up their superstitious practices. Hence, it can be eliminated. The second assumption indicates that planting trees increases rainfall. The given statement gives an opposition to the practice of performing rituals. It indicates that these rituals do not bring any benefit. Therefore, from the sentence composition, it can be easily inferred that planting trees would increase the rainfall. Hence, it is a safe assumption to make. Hence, option 2 is the correct answer.

10. If L denotes +, M denotes -, N denotes ×, P denotes ÷ , then 14 N 10 L 42 P 2 M 8 = ?

A. 153

B. 216

C. 248

D. 251

Answer A

Solution

BODMAS rule: Let us decode the given sign: Sign L M N P Meaning + - × ÷ Given: 14 N 10 L 42 P 2 M 8 = ? After interchanging we get: 14 × 10 + 42 ÷ 2 - 8 ⇒ 14 × 10 + 21 - 8 ⇒ 140 + 21 - 8 ⇒ 161 - 8 = 153 Hence, the correct answer is "153".
