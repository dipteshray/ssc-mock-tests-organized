A. Rs. 7, 815.50

B. Rs. 7, 187.50

Solution

Given: Total Amount is Rs. 5,500. Product ‘z’ bought at Rs. 5000. Therefore, the remaining amount is 500. Product ‘z’ sell at 15% profit. SP = 5000 × 115100 = 5750 Now total amount is = 500 + 5750 = 6250 Now he buys product ‘X’ at Rs. 6250 and then sell it at 25% profit then the total remaining amount = 6250 × 125100 = 7812.50 Hence, ’Rs. 7812.50’ is the correct answer.

Solution

16. The average age of A, B, C is 8 years more than B's age. If age of both A and C together is 56 years, then B's age is ?

A. 18 years

B. 24 years

C. 12 years

D. 16 years

Answer D

Solution

Given, The average age of A, B, C is 8 years more than B's age. ⇒ (A + B + C) / 3 = 8 + B ⇒ A + B + C = 24 + 3B ........... (i) If age of both A and C together is 56 years ⇒ A + C = 56 ......... (ii) Substituting the equation (ii) is equation (i) we get, ⇒ A + B + C = 24 + 3B ⇒ 56 + B = 24 + 3B ⇒ 3B - B = 56 - 24 ⇒ 2B = 32 ⇒ B = 16 ∴ Here, the age of B is 16 years. Hence, the correct answer is "16 years".
