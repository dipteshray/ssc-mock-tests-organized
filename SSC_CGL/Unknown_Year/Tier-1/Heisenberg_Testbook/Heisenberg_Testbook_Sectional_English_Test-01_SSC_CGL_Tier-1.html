2. Find a number such that when 15 is subtracted from 7 times the number, the result is 10 more than twice the number

A. 5

B. 6

C. 4

D. 8

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick For word problems involving equations, verifying the given options is the fastest approach. Let us check Option 1: The number is 5. Left Side (15 subtracted from 7 times) → 7 × 5 − 15 = 35 − 15 = 20. Right Side (10 more than twice) → 2 × 5 + 10 = 10 + 10 = 20. Since both sides match perfectly (20 = 20), this is the required number. ∴ The correct answer is 5. Alternate Method Given: When 15 is subtracted from 7 times a number, the result is equal to 10 more than twice the same number. Formula Used: Forming a basic linear equation in one variable: ax + b = cx + d Calculations: Let the required number be x. ⇒ 15 subtracted from 7 times the number = 7x − 15 ⇒ 10 more than twice the number = 2x + 10 According to the given question, we equate both expressions: ⇒ 7x − 15 = 2x + 10 ⇒ 7x − 2x = 10 + 15 ⇒ 5x = 25 ⇒ x = 25 ÷ 5 ⇒ x = 5 ∴ The correct answer is 5. Additional Information Linear Equation in One Variable An equation of the form ax + b = 0, where a and b are constant values and x is a variable, is called a linear equation. It always yields a single unique solution. Translating Word Problems to Algebra Key phrases like 'times' refer to multiplication, 'more than' refers to addition, and 'subtracted from' means taking away from a base value (e.g., '15 subtracted from y' translates directly to y − 15). Checking the Solution Always verify the final algebraic answer by substituting the value back into both sides of the original word problem to ensure the Left Hand Side (LHS) strictly equals the Right Hand Side (RHS).

4. In an office, 76% of the number of females is equal to the one-third of the number of males. What is the ratio of number of males to that of the females in the office?

A. 57 : 25

B. 25 : 57

C. 43 : 19

D. 19 : 43

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Given, 1/3 of Males = 76% of Females ⇒ M / 3 = (76 / 100) × F ⇒ M / 3 = (19 / 25) × F ⇒ M / F = (19 × 3) / 25 = 57 / 25 ∴ The correct answer is 57 : 25. Alternate Method Given: 76% of the number of females is equal to 1/3 of the number of males. Formula Used: Percentage value = (Rate ÷ 100) × Base value Calculations: Let the total number of males be M and the total number of females be F. ⇒ 76% of F = (1 / 3) × M ⇒ (76 ÷ 100) × F = M ÷ 3 ⇒ (19 ÷ 25) × F = M ÷ 3 Rearranging the terms to find the ratio of M to F: ⇒ M ÷ F = (19 ÷ 25) × 3 ⇒ M ÷ F = 57 ÷ 25 ⇒ M : F = 57 : 25 ∴ The correct answer is 57 : 25. Additional Information Ratio & Proportion Basics A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind. The ratio of a to b is written as a : b or a ÷ b. The first term is called the antecedent, and the second term is the consequent. Percentage to Fraction Conversion To convert a percentage into a fraction, simply divide the percentage value by 100 and simplify. For example, 76% = 76 ÷ 100 = 19 ÷ 25. Equivalent Ratios Multiplying or dividing both terms of a ratio by the same non-zero number does not change the ratio. This property is highly useful to simplify complex fractional equations into simplest form.

5. Ramesh sold his bike for ₹16,384 and incurred a loss of 20%. At what price should he have sold the bike to gain a profit of 30%?

A. ₹27,824

B. ₹26,724

C. ₹26,624

D. ₹26,800

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Selling Price at 20% loss = 80% of CP = ₹16,384 Required Selling Price at 30% profit = 130% of CP New SP = (16384 ÷ 80) × 130 New SP = 204.8 × 130 = ₹26,624 ∴ The correct answer is ₹26,624. Alternate Method Given: Initial Selling Price (SP1) = ₹16,384 Loss Percentage = 20% Required Profit Percentage = 30% Formula Used: Cost Price (CP) = SP1 × [100 ÷ (100 − Loss%)] New Selling Price (SP2) = CP × [(100 + Profit%) ÷ 100] Calculations: ⇒ CP = 16384 × [100 ÷ (100 − 20)] ⇒ CP = 16384 × (100 ÷ 80) ⇒ CP = 204.8 × 100 = ₹20,480 ⇒ SP2 = 20480 × [(100 + 30) ÷ 100] ⇒ SP2 = 20480 × (130 ÷ 100) ⇒ SP2 = 2048 × 13 = ₹26,624 ∴ The correct answer is ₹26,624. Additional Information Cost Price (CP) It is the exact price at which goods are originally bought. Profit and loss values are always calculated on the base cost price unless clearly specified otherwise. Successive Percentage Change When a value is updated successively by a% and b%, the total net percentage change is given by the formula: (a + b + ab ÷ 100)%. Marked Price (MP) & Discount Discount is fundamentally given on the marked price or printed price of the item. Consequently, Selling Price = MP − Discount.

6. Find the value of 2 cosec2 23° cot2 67° - sin2 23° - sin2 67° - cot2 67°

A. sec2 23°

B. 1

C. tan2 23°

D. 0

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Convert 67° angles to 23° using cot 67° = tan 23° and sin 67° = cos 23°. ⇒ 2 cosec2 23° tan2 23° − (sin2 23° + cos2 23°) − tan2 23° ⇒ 2(1 / sin2 23°) × (sin2 23° / cos2 23°) − 1 − tan2 23° ⇒ 2 sec2 23° − 1 − tan2 23° = sec2 23° + (sec2 23° − tan2 23°) − 1 ⇒ sec2 23° + 1 − 1 = sec2 23° ∴ The correct answer is sec2 23°. Alternate Method Given: 2 cosec2 23° cot2 67° − sin2 23° − sin2 67° − cot2 67° Formulas Used: sin(90° − θ) = cos θ cot(90° − θ) = tan θ sin2 θ + cos2 θ = 1 sec2 θ − tan2 θ = 1 Calculation: ⇒ 2 cosec2 23° cot2(90° − 23°) − sin2 23° − sin2(90° − 23°) − cot2(90° − 23°) ⇒ 2 cosec2 23° tan2 23° − sin2 23° − cos2 23° − tan2 23° ⇒ 2(1 / sin2 23°) × (sin2 23° / cos2 23°) − (sin2 23° + cos2 23°) − tan2 23° ⇒ 2(1 / cos2 23°) − 1 − tan2 23° ⇒ 2 sec2 23° − 1 − tan2 23° ⇒ sec2 23° + (sec2 23° − tan2 23°) − 1 ⇒ sec2 23° + 1 − 1 ⇒ sec2 23° ∴ The correct answer is sec2 23°. Additional Information Trigonometric Identities The fundamental Pythagorean identities are sin2 θ + cos2 θ = 1, sec2 θ − tan2 θ = 1, and cosec2 θ − cot2 θ = 1. Complementary Angles Two angles are complementary if their sum is 90°. Key relations: sin(90° − θ) = cos θ, tan(90° − θ) = cot θ, and sec(90° − θ) = cosec θ. Reciprocal Relations Trigonometric ratios are related by reciprocal identities: cosec θ = 1 / sin θ, sec θ = 1 / cos θ, and cot θ = 1 / tan θ.

7. A can do a work in 8 days and B in 12 days. If they work together on it for 3 days, then the fraction of the work that is left is:

A. (8/3)

B. (7/8)

C. (3/8)

D. (5/8)

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total work = LCM of 8 and 12 = 24 units. Combined efficiency of A and B = (24 ÷ 8) + (24 ÷ 12) = 3 + 2 = 5 units/day. Work completed in 3 days = 5 × 3 = 15 units. Work remaining = 24 − 15 = 9 units. Fraction of work left = 9 ÷ 24 = 3/8. ∴ The correct answer is 3/8. Alternate Method Given: Time taken by A to complete the work = 8 days Time taken by B to complete the work = 12 days Time they worked together = 3 days LCM Method: Person Days Total Work Efficiency A 8 24 3 B 12 2 Calculations: ⇒ Total efficiency of A and B together = 3 + 2 = 5 units/day ⇒ Total work done by A and B in 3 days = Efficiency × Time = 5 × 3 = 15 units ⇒ Remaining work = Total work − Completed work = 24 − 15 = 9 units ⇒ Fraction of work left = Remaining work ÷ Total work = 9 ÷ 24 = 3/8 ∴ The correct answer is 3/8. Additional Information Work and Wages Wages are distributed in proportion to the total work done by each person. If they work for the exact same duration, wages are directly proportional to their efficiencies. Alternate Day Work If two persons work on alternate days, find the total work done in a 2-day cycle and multiply it to get as close to the total work as possible, then add the remaining work for the next respective turn. Pipes and Cisterns This concept is identical to time and work, with the exception that an emptying pipe has a negative efficiency. Net efficiency = Sum of filling rates − Sum of emptying rates.

8. A radii of the bases of the two cylinders are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their height are in the ratio 3 : 5. Find the ratio of their curved surface areas.

A. 9 : 8

B. 2 : 7

C. 3 : 5

D. 3 : 7

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick The Curved Surface Area (CSA) of a cylinder is proportional to the product of its radius and height. ⇒ Ratio of CSA = (r1 × h1) : (r2 × h2) ⇒ Ratio of CSA = (5 × 3) : (7 × 5) ⇒ Ratio of CSA = 15 : 35 ⇒ Ratio of CSA = 3 : 7 ∴ The correct answer is 3 : 7. Alternate Method Given: ⇒ Let the radii of the two cylinders be r1 = 5x and r2 = 7x ⇒ Let the heights of the two cylinders be h1 = 3y and h2 = 5y Formula Used: ⇒ Curved Surface Area of a cylinder = 2πrh Calculations: ⇒ CSA of first cylinder (CSA1) = 2 × π × (5x) × (3y) = 30πxy ⇒ CSA of second cylinder (CSA2) = 2 × π × (7x) × (5y) = 70πxy ⇒ Ratio of their curved surface areas = CSA1 : CSA2 ⇒ Ratio = 30πxy / 70πxy ⇒ Ratio = 30 / 70 ⇒ Ratio = 3 / 7 ∴ The correct answer is 3 : 7. Additional Information Curved Surface Area (CSA) It is the area of the curved part of the cylinder, excluding the flat circular bases. Formula: CSA = 2πrh Total Surface Area (TSA) It includes the curved surface area plus the area of the two circular bases (top and bottom). Formula: TSA = 2πr(r + h) Volume of a Cylinder The total space occupied inside the cylinder. Formula: V = πr2h

9. The system of equations: 2x + 3y = 7 and (a - b)x + (a + b)y = 3a + b - 2 have infinitely many solutions:

A. a = 5, b = 3

B. a = 2, b = 3

C. a = 2, b = 1

D. a = 5, b = 1

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick For infinitely many solutions, the ratio of coefficients must be equal: a1/a2 = b1/b2. ⇒ 2 / (a − b) = 3 / (a + b) ⇒ 2a + 2b = 3a − 3b ⇒ a = 5b Check options: Only Option 4 has a = 5 and b = 1, which perfectly satisfies a = 5b. ∴ The correct answer is a = 5, b = 1. Alternate Method Given: 2x + 3y = 7 (a − b)x + (a + b)y = 3a + b − 2 Formula Used: For a system of equations to have infinitely many solutions, the ratio of their coefficients must be equal: a1 / a2 = b1 / b2 = c1 / c2 Calculations: ⇒ Here, a1 = 2, b1 = 3, c1 = 7 ⇒ a2 = (a − b), b2 = (a + b), c2 = (3a + b − 2) ⇒ 2 / (a − b) = 3 / (a + b) = 7 / (3a + b − 2) ⇒ Equating the first two parts: ⇒ 2 / (a − b) = 3 / (a + b) ⇒ 2(a + b) = 3(a − b) ⇒ 2a + 2b = 3a − 3b ⇒ a = 5b --- (Equation 1) ⇒ Equating the second and third parts: ⇒ 3 / (a + b) = 7 / (3a + b − 2) ⇒ 3(3a + b − 2) = 7(a + b) ⇒ 9a + 3b − 6 = 7a + 7b ⇒ 2a − 4b = 6 ⇒ a − 2b = 3 --- (Equation 2) ⇒ Substituting a = 5b into Equation 2: ⇒ 5b − 2b = 3 ⇒ 3b = 3 ⇒ b = 1 ⇒ Substitute b = 1 back into Equation 1: ⇒ a = 5 × 1 = 5 ∴ The correct answer is a = 5, b = 1. Additional Information Unique Solution A system of linear equations has a unique solution if the lines intersect at exactly one point. The condition is a1 / a2 ≠ b1 / b2. No Solution A system of equations has no solution if the lines are parallel and never intersect. The condition is a1 / a2 = b1 / b2 ≠ c1 / c2. Infinitely Many Solutions A system of equations has infinitely many solutions if the lines overlap entirely, meaning they represent the exact same line. The condition is a1 / a2 = b1 / b2 = c1 / c2.

10. In a government scheme, if an electricity bill is paid before due date, one gets a reduction of 13.5% on the amount of the bill. By paying the bill before due date, Ritu got a reduction of Rs. 6,075. The amount of her electricity bill was:

A. Rs. 48,000

B. Rs. 55,380

C. Rs. 60,750

D. Rs. 45,000

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick 13.5% of Bill Amount = Rs. 6075 1% of Bill Amount = 6075 ÷ 13.5 = 450 100% of Bill Amount = 450 × 100 = 45000 ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 45,000. Alternate Method Given: Reduction percentage = 13.5% Amount of reduction = Rs. 6075 Formula Used: Percentage Value = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Base Value Calculations: Let the total amount of the electricity bill be x. ⇒ 13.5% of x = 6075 ⇒ (13.5 ÷ 100) × x = 6075 ⇒ x = (6075 × 100) ÷ 13.5 ⇒ x = 607500 ÷ 13.5 ⇒ x = 6075000 ÷ 135 ⇒ x = 45000 ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 45,000. Additional Information Base and Percentage Relationship When a specific percentage of a base value is given, the complete base value can be found directly using the relation: Base = (Given Value × 100) ÷ Percentage Rate. Percentage Increase or Decrease New Value = Original Value × (1 ± r ÷ 100), where r is the percentage rate of change. Successive Percentage Change If an amount is subjected to two successive percentage changes of a% and b%, the net effective change is calculated as: (a + b + ab ÷ 100)%.

11. A man sells 10 pens for ₹18 gaining 24%. How many pens should he sell for ₹1,530 to earn a profit of 55%?

A. 540

B. 620

C. 680

D. 760

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Using the proportional ratio: (Amount ÷ Quantity) ÷ (100 + Profit%) = Constant ⇒ (18 ÷ 10) ÷ 124 = (1530 ÷ N) ÷ 155 ⇒ 1.8 ÷ 124 = 1530 ÷ (155 × N) ⇒ N = (1530 × 124) ÷ (1.8 × 155) ⇒ N = 680 ∴ The correct answer is 680 pens. Alternate Method Given: Selling Price (SP1) of 10 pens = ₹18 Initial Profit = 24% Target Profit = 55% Target Amount = ₹1530 Formula Used: CP = SP × [100 ÷ (100 + Profit%)] New SP = CP × [(100 + New Profit%) ÷ 100] Calculations: ⇒ SP1 of 1 pen = 18 ÷ 10 = ₹1.80 ⇒ CP of 1 pen = 1.80 × (100 ÷ 124) = ₹45/31 ⇒ Target SP2 of 1 pen for 55% profit = CP × (155 ÷ 100) ⇒ Target SP2 = (45 ÷ 31) × (155 ÷ 100) ⇒ Target SP2 = 45 × (5 ÷ 100) = ₹2.25 ⇒ Number of pens = Total Amount ÷ Target SP2 ⇒ Number of pens = 1530 ÷ 2.25 ⇒ Number of pens = 680 ∴ The correct answer is 680 pens. Additional Information Marked Price and Discount Discount is always calculated on the Marked Price (MP). Formula: SP = MP × [1 − (Discount% ÷ 100)]. Successive Discounts If two successive discounts of a% and b% are given, the effective single discount is (a + b − ab ÷ 100)%. Dishonest Dealer When a dealer sells goods at cost price but uses a false weight, the actual Profit% = [Error ÷ (True Value − Error)] × 100.

12. The average runs made by a batsman in 10 innings of cricket was 43. How many runs does he have to make in the next inning so that the average of his runs increases by 3?

A. 83

B. 76

C. 78

D. 91

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Using the average deviation method to find the required runs directly: Runs required = Old average + (Total new innings × Increase in average) Runs required = 43 + (11 × 3) Runs required = 43 + 33 = 76 ∴ The correct answer is 76 runs. Alternate Method Given: Average runs in the first 10 innings = 43 Required increase in average = 3 Formula Used: Average = Sum of all observations ÷ Total number of observations Total 11 Innings → New Average: 46 10 Innings (Avg = 43) 11th = ? Deviation: 10 innings need +3 runs each 11th Inning = 46 + (10 × 3) = 76 Calculations: ⇒ Total runs scored in 10 innings = 10 × 43 = 430 runs ⇒ Target new average for 11 innings = 43 + 3 = 46 runs ⇒ Total runs required in 11 innings = 11 × 46 = 506 runs ⇒ Runs required in the 11th inning = Total runs in 11 innings − Total runs in 10 innings ⇒ Runs required = 506 − 430 = 76 ∴ The correct answer is 76 runs. Additional Information Basic Average Concept The average of any set of values is formulated as: Average = Sum of all values ÷ Total count of values. Deviation Method for Included Items When a new item is added to a group, its value can be quickly found without large calculations using: Value of added item = Old Average + (Total new items × Change in Average). Combined Average When two groups of sizes n1 and n2 with averages A1 and A2 respectively are merged, the combined average is calculated as: (n1A1 + n2A2) ÷ (n1 + n2).

14. If 2 men or 4 women or 6 boys can finish a work in 50 days, then how many days will 1 man, 1 woman and 1 boy take to finish the same work?

A. 53 (6/11) days

B. 54 (6/13) days

C. 54 (5/11) days

D. 54 (6/11) days

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Time = Given Days ÷ [ (Mand / Mor) + (Wand / Wor) + (Band / Bor) ] Time = 50 ÷ [ (1 / 2) + (1 / 4) + (1 / 6) ] Time = 50 ÷ [ (6 + 3 + 2) / 12 ] Time = 50 × (12 / 11) = 600 / 11 ∴ The correct answer is 54 6/11 days. Alternate Method Given: ⇒ 2 men complete work in 50 days ⇒ 1 man takes 2 × 50 = 100 days ⇒ 4 women complete work in 50 days ⇒ 1 woman takes 4 × 50 = 200 days ⇒ 6 boys complete work in 50 days ⇒ 1 boy takes 6 × 50 = 300 days Person Days Total Work Efficiency 1 Man 100 600 6 1 Woman 200 3 1 Boy 300 2 Calculations: ⇒ Combined efficiency of 1 man, 1 woman, and 1 boy = 6 + 3 + 2 = 11 units/day ⇒ Time taken = Total Work ÷ Combined Efficiency ⇒ Time taken = 600 ÷ 11 = 54 6/11 days ∴ The correct answer is 54 6/11 days. Additional Information AND / OR Rule in Time & Work When a question states that A OR B can do a work in D days, the time taken by A AND B together is calculated by dividing the given days by the sum of ratios of required workers to given workers. MDH Concept For group work problems, use the formula M1 × D1 × H1 / W1 = M2 × D2 × H2 / W2, where M is men, D is days, H is hours, and W is work. Total Work & Efficiency Total Work is always equal to the Efficiency (amount of work done per unit time) multiplied by the Total Time taken to complete the job.

15. If the area of the base of a right circular cone is 157 cm2 and its slant height is √(491) cm, then the volume of the cone is _______. (Use π = 3.14.)

A. 997 cm3

B. 1172 cm3

C. 1099 cm3

D. 986 cm3

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Given Base Area = 157 cm2, so π × r2 = 157 ⇒ r2 = 157 ÷ 3.14 = 50. Height h = √(l2 − r2) = √(491 − 50) = √441 = 21 cm. Volume of cone = (1/3) × Base Area × Height. Volume = (1/3) × 157 × 21 = 157 × 7 = 1099 cm3. ∴ The correct answer is 1099 cm3. Alternate Method Given: Base area of the cone = 157 cm2 Slant height (l) = √491 cm (π = 3.14) Formula Used: Area of base = π × r2 Slant height l = √(r2 + h2) Volume of a cone V = 1/3 × π × r2 × h Calculations: ⇒ Area of base = π × r2 = 157 ⇒ 3.14 × r2 = 157 ⇒ r2 = 157 ÷ 3.14 = 50 ⇒ We know that, l2 = r2 + h2 ⇒ (√491)2 = 50 + h2 ⇒ 491 = 50 + h2 ⇒ h2 = 491 − 50 = 441 ⇒ h = √441 = 21 cm ⇒ Volume of the cone = 1/3 × π × r2 × h ⇒ Volume = 1/3 × (3.14 × 50) × 21 ⇒ Volume = 1/3 × 157 × 21 ⇒ Volume = 157 × 7 = 1099 cm3 ∴ The correct answer is 1099 cm3. Additional Information Volume of a Cone vs Cylinder A cone's volume is exactly one-third of the volume of a cylinder with the same base radius and height. The formula is V = 1/3 × πr2h. Surface Area of a Cone The curved surface area is given by πrl and the total surface area is given by πr(r + l), where r is the radius and l is the slant height. Frustum of a Cone When a cone is sliced parallel to its base, the remaining lower part is called a frustum. Its volume is 1/3 × πh(R2 + r2 + Rr).

16. P, Q, R and S are four points on a circle. PR and QS intersect at a point X such that ∠QXR = 120° and ∠XRS = 10°. The measure of ∠QPR is:

A. 120°

B. 90°

C. 100°

D. 110°

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick In ΔSXR, the exterior angle is equal to the sum of the two opposite interior angles. ⇒ ∠QXR = ∠XSR + ∠XRS ⇒ 120° = ∠XSR + 10° ⇒ ∠XSR = 110° Angles subtended by the same arc (QR) in the same segment of a circle are equal. ⇒ ∠QPR = ∠QSR = ∠XSR = 110° ∴ The correct answer is 110°. Alternate Method Given: ⇒ ∠QXR = 120° ⇒ ∠XRS = 10° Formula Used: ⇒ Linear pair sum = 180° ⇒ Sum of angles in a triangle = 180° ⇒ Angles subtended by the same arc at the circumference are equal. Q P S R X 120° 10° Calculations: ⇒ Since QS is a straight line segment, ∠SXR and ∠QXR form a linear pair. ⇒ ∠SXR + ∠QXR = 180° ⇒ ∠SXR = 180° − 120° = 60° ⇒ In ΔSXR, the sum of all interior angles is 180°. ⇒ ∠XSR + ∠SXR + ∠XRS = 180° ⇒ ∠XSR + 60° + 10° = 180° ⇒ ∠XSR = 180° − 70° = 110° ⇒ We know that angles subtended by the same arc at the circumference are equal. ⇒ Therefore, ∠QPR and ∠QSR (which is the same as ∠XSR) subtending the same arc QR must be equal. ⇒ ∠QPR = ∠QSR = 110° ∴ The correct answer is 110°. Additional Information Angles in the Same Segment Angles subtended by the same arc at any point on the remaining part of the circle are always equal. Exterior Angle Theorem The measure of an exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the measures of its two remote interior angles. Cyclic Quadrilateral A quadrilateral formed by four points on a circle is called a cyclic quadrilateral. The sum of its opposite interior angles is always 180°.

17. A vendor mixes two types of pulses costing ₹10/kg and ₹22/kg respectively. In what ratio ₹10/kg should be mixed with ₹22/kg to get a mixture of ₹12/kg.

A. 1 : 5

B. 5 : 1

C. 1 : 10

D. 10 : 1

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Using the Alligation Cross Method: Ratio of cheaper to dearer = (Price of dearer − Mean price) : (Mean price − Price of cheaper) Ratio = (22 − 12) : (12 − 10) Ratio = 10 : 2 = 5 : 1 ∴ The correct answer is 5 : 1. Alternate Method Given: Price of cheaper pulses (C) = ₹10/kg Price of dearer pulses (D) = ₹22/kg Mean price of mixture (M) = ₹12/kg Formula Used: According to the rule of alligation: Quantity of Cheaper ÷ Quantity of Dearer = (D − M) ÷ (M − C) Calculations: ⇒ Ratio = (22 − 12) ÷ (12 − 10) ⇒ Ratio = 10 ÷ 2 ⇒ Ratio = 5 : 1 ∴ The correct answer is 5 : 1. Additional Information Rule of Alligation It is a quick cross-difference method used to find the ratio in which two ingredients at given prices must be mixed to produce a mixture of a specific target price. The target mean price always lies strictly between the cheaper and dearer prices. Mean Price The mean price is the cost price of a single unit quantity of the combined final mixture. It is calculated as (Total cost of all mixed ingredients) ÷ (Total final quantity of the mixture). 18. What is the slope of the line whose equation is x + 2y = 4?

A. -1/2

B. 1

C. 1/2

D. -2

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick For a standard linear equation of the form Ax + By = C, the slope is directly given by −A / B. Given equation: x + 2y = 4 Here, the coefficient of x is A = 1 and the coefficient of y is B = 2. Slope = −1 / 2. ∴ The correct answer is −1/2. Alternate Method Given: The equation of the line: x + 2y = 4 Formula Used: The slope-intercept form of a line is y = mx + c, where 'm' represents the slope and 'c' represents the y-intercept. X Y (0, 2) (4, 0) x + 2y = 4 Run = 4 Rise = −2 Calculations: ⇒ We need to express the given equation in the slope-intercept form (y = mx + c). ⇒ Start with the given equation: x + 2y = 4 ⇒ Subtract x from both sides to isolate the y term: ⇒ 2y = −x + 4 ⇒ Divide the entire equation by 2 to solve for y: ⇒ y = (−1/2)x + (4/2) ⇒ y = (−1/2)x + 2 ⇒ By comparing this result with y = mx + c, we can identify that the slope m is −1/2 and the y-intercept c is 2. ∴ The correct answer is −1/2. Additional Information Slope of a Line (Gradient) The slope measures the steepness and direction of a line. It is calculated as the change in y divided by the change in x. Formula: m = (y2 − y1) ÷ (x2 − x1). X and Y Intercepts The x-intercept is the point where the line crosses the horizontal x-axis (where y = 0). The y-intercept is the point where it crosses the vertical y-axis (where x = 0). Parallel and Perpendicular Lines Two parallel lines always have equal slopes (m1 = m2). Two perpendicular lines have slopes that are negative reciprocals of each other (m1 × m2 = −1).

23. A diver rowing at the speed of 3 km/h in still water takes double the time going 50 km upstream compared to going 50 km downstream. The speed of the diver against the stream is

A. 4/3 km/h

B. 1 km/h

C. 7/3 km/h

D. 2 km/h

Answer D

Solution

Let the speed of the stream be v km/h, Speed of diver in still water = u. Time upstream = 50 / (3 - v) ⇒ Time downstream = 50 / (3 + v) ⇒ 50 / (3 - v) = 2 × (50 / (3 + v)) ⇒ 3 + v = 2 × (3 - v) ⇒ v = 1 km/h Speed upstream = u - v = 3 - 1 = 2 km/h
