2. The price of the X variety of rice is ₹6.95 per kg and the price of the Y variety of rice is ₹5.95 per kg. If the ratio of X variety of rice and Y variety of rice in which it is mixed is 3 : 7, then find the price of the mixed rice (per kg).

A. ₹6.75 per kg

B. ₹5.25 per kg

C. ₹5.55 per kg

D. ₹6.25 per kg

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Difference between Variety X and Variety Y prices = 6.95 − 5.95 = ₹1.00. Divide this price difference in the inverse ratio parts (Total = 3 + 7 = 10). Value of 3 parts = (3 ÷ 10) × 1.00 = ₹0.30. Mixed rice price = Lower price + Shift = 5.95 + 0.30 = ₹6.25. ∴ The correct answer is ₹6.25 per kg. Alternate Method Given: Price of Variety X (P1) = ₹6.95 per kg Price of Variety Y (P2) = ₹5.95 per kg Ratio of X to Y (n1 : n2) = 3 : 7 Formula Used: Mean Price (M) = (n1 × P1 + n2 × P2) ÷ (n1 + n2) Calculations: ⇒ M = (3 × 6.95 + 7 × 5.95) ÷ (3 + 7) ⇒ M = (20.85 + 41.65) ÷ 10 ⇒ M = 62.50 ÷ 10 ⇒ M = 6.25 ∴ The correct answer is ₹6.25 per kg. Additional Information Rule of Alligation It states that the ratio of the quantities is equal to the ratio of (Higher Price − Mean Price) to (Mean Price − Lower Price). Weighted Average When items are mixed in different quantities, the resulting average is the sum of (value × weight) divided by the total weight. Price vs Quantity Relationship The mean price always lies between the lowest and the highest individual component prices.

3. A shopkeeper marked his article 150% above the cost price and ratio of discount allowed to profit earned is 4 : 1. If profit earned is Rs.180 less than discount allowed, then find ratio of selling price to marked price of the article

A. 25 : 13

B. 15 : 17

C. 13 : 25

D. 13 : 27

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Ratio of Discount (D) to Profit (P) = 4 : 1, and D − P = 180. ⇒ 3 units = 180 ⇒ 1 unit = 60. Thus, P = 60 and D = 240. Total Markup = P + D = 60 + 240 = 300. Since Markup is 150% of CP, 1.5 × CP = 300 ⇒ CP = 200. MP = 200 + 300 = 500 and SP = 200 + 60 = 260. Ratio SP : MP = 260 : 500 = 13 : 25. ∴ The correct answer is 13 : 25. Alternate Method Given: Markup % = 150% above Cost Price (CP) Ratio of Discount (D) to Profit (P) = 4 : 1 Difference (D − P) = ₹180 Formula Used: 1. Marked Price (MP) = CP + Markup 2. Selling Price (SP) = CP + Profit = MP − Discount 3. Markup = Profit + Discount Calculations: ⇒ Let P = x and D = 4x ⇒ D − P = 180 ⇒ 4x − x = 180 ⇒ 3x = 180 ⇒ x = 60 ⇒ Profit = ₹60 and Discount = 4 × 60 = ₹240 ⇒ Markup = Profit + Discount = 60 + 240 = 300 ⇒ 150% of CP = 300 ⇒ (150/100) × CP = 300 ⇒ CP = (300 × 100) / 150 = ₹200 ⇒ MP = CP + Markup = 200 + 300 = ₹500 ⇒ SP = CP + Profit = 200 + 60 = ₹260 ⇒ Ratio SP : MP = 260 / 500 = 13 / 25 ∴ The correct answer is 13 : 25. Additional Information Markup Percentage It is the percentage by which the cost price is increased to reach the marked price: Markup % = [(MP − CP) / CP] × 100. Discount Percentage It is always calculated on the Marked Price: Discount % = [Discount / MP] × 100. Relation between CP and MP A very useful direct ratio is CP / MP = (100 − Discount%) / (100 + Profit%).

Solution

Solution

The segments from vertices to contact points are equal tangents, creating Isosceles Triangles at each corner: ▵AFE, ▵BDF, and ▵CDE. Property: The angle subtended by the chord joining two contact points at the third contact point is 90° − (Opposite Angle/2). For ∠EDF, the opposite vertex is A. ⇒ ∠EDF = 90° − (A/2). Formula Used: Alternate Segment Theorem states that the angle between a tangent and a chord is equal to the angle subtended by the chord in the alternate segment. 15. The population of a country has been growing at a rate of 4% every decade. If the projected population of the country in 2030 is given as 1,58,18,400, what was the population of the country in 2000?

A. 1,40,62,000

B. 1,40,65,200

C. 1,40,56,200

D. 1,40,62,500

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Multiplying factor for 4% growth = 1 + (4/100) = 104/100 = 26/25. Number of decades from 2000 to 2030 is 3, so the total compounding factor is (26/25)3 = 17576/15625. Initial Population = Final Population × (15625 / 17576) ⇒ Initial Population = 1,58,18,400 × (15625 / 17576) ⇒ Initial Population = 900 × 15625 = 1,40,62,500 ∴ The correct answer is 1,40,62,500. Alternate Method Given: Projected population in 2030 (A) = 1,58,18,400 Growth rate (R) = 4% per decade Time period (n) = (2030 − 2000) ÷ 10 = 3 decades Formula Used: A = P × (1 + R/100)n Where A is the final population, P is the initial population, R is the rate of growth, and n is the time period. Calculations: ⇒ 1,58,18,400 = P × (1 + 4/100)3 ⇒ 1,58,18,400 = P × (104/100)3 ⇒ 1,58,18,400 = P × (26/25)3 ⇒ P = 1,58,18,400 × (25/26) × (25/26) × (25/26) ⇒ P = 1,58,18,400 × (15625 / 17576) ⇒ P = 900 × 15625 ⇒ P = 1,40,62,500 ∴ The correct answer is 1,40,62,500. Additional Information Compound Population Growth When a population grows at a constant rate R over a certain period, it increases exponentially in a manner identical to compound interest. The standard formula applied is Final Population = Initial Population × (1 + R/100)n. Population Depreciation If the population decreases consistently by a certain percentage R over a period (such as due to mortality or migration), the decay formula becomes Final Population = Initial Population × (1 − R/100)n. Successive Percentage Change When the population grows or shrinks at varying rates (R1%, R2%, R3%) in successive time periods, the net final population is calculated as P × (1 ± R1/100) × (1 ± R2/100) × (1 ± R3/100).

D. They polarize incoming light to reduce the intensity of headlight glare

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is They provide a wider field of view by forming diminished and erect images. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• A convex mirror, also known as a diverging mirror, is a curved mirror in which the reflective surface bulges toward the light source.

• It is used as a rear-view mirror in vehicles because it always forms an erect (upright) and diminished (smaller) image of the objects behind the vehicle. This allows the driver to see a much larger area than a plane mirror would allow. Hence, statement A is correct.

• Convex mirrors cannot produce real and inverted images; they only produce virtual images because the reflected rays appear to diverge from a point behind the mirror. Hence, statement B is incorrect.

• Reflective Properties: The laws of reflection (angle of incidence equals angle of reflection) apply to spherical mirrors just as they do to plane mirrors, but the normal at the point of incidence always passes through the center of curvature (C).

145. Which of the following MS-Excel functions returns a number rounded up, away from zero, to the nearest multiple of significance?

A. ROUND( )

B. CEILING( )

C. CELL( )

D. FLOOR( )

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is CEILING( ) Key Points Additional Information Remember, both the FLOOR and CEILING functions will round towards or away from zero (negative values inclusive), respectively. For rounding strictly down or up (ignoring zero), Excel uses FLOOR.MATH and CEILING.MATH.

• CEILING(): This function rounds a number up, away from zero, to the nearest multiple of significance.

• The "significance" represents the multiple to which you want to round the number.

• For example, if you have a number like 4.1 and you wanted to round up to the nearest whole number, you would use CEILING(4.1, 1) and it would return 5.

• ROUND(): This Excel function round off numbers to a specified number of digits. It follows the standard rules of rounding numbers. If the next digit to the specified number of digits is 5 or above, it rounds up; if it's less than 5, it rounds down. For example, ROUND(2.15, 1) would return 2.2.

• CELL(): This function in Excel is used to get information about the formatting, location, or contents of a particular cell. It doesn't round numbers. It has two parts, first is the info-type e.g. address, row, col, etc. which represents what information you want to fetch and the second is optional, which refers to the reference cell.

• FLOOR(): Similar to the CEILING function, the FLOOR function in Excel rounds a number down (towards zero), to the nearest multiple of significance. For example, FLOOR(4.7, 1) would return 4.
