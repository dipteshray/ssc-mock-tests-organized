1. A sentence is provided in indirect speech. From the four given options, choose the one that most accurately conveys the sentence in its corresponding direct speech. He wondered whether he could ever master quantum theory.

A. He asked, "Could I master quantum theory?"

B. He said, "Will I ever master quantum theory?"

C. He wondered, "Can I ever master quantum theory?"

D. He wondered, "Could I ever master quantum theory?"

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Option 3. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 3. Correct Sentence: He wondered, "Can I ever master quantum theory?" Additional Information

• Indirect Speech: He wondered whether he could ever master quantum theory.

• Key Conversions: "whether he could" becomes "Can I" The conjunction "whether" indicates an indirect question, which is converted to a direct question format.

• The pronoun "he" (referring to the subject) changes to "I" in direct speech.

• The modal verb "could" is the past form of "can" used in indirect speech; it reverts to the present tense "can" in direct speech.

• "ever master quantum theory" remains the same The adverb "ever" and the base verb "master" are maintained to preserve the original meaning of the doubt/possibility.

• Reporting verb: The verb "wondered" is retained as it perfectly captures the self-reflective nature of the question.

• Punctuation and Format: The direct speech is enclosed in quotation marks.

• The sentence inside the quotes starts with a capital letter and ends with a question mark.

• A comma is used to separate the reporting verb from the quoted speech.

• Option 1: "He asked, 'Could I master quantum theory?'" Missing the word "ever," which was in the original sentence.

• "Asked" is less precise than "wondered" for a self-reflective thought.

• Option 2: "He said, 'Will I ever master quantum theory?'" The use of "will" in direct speech would require "would" in indirect speech (He wondered if he would ever...).

• Option 4: "He wondered, 'Could I ever master quantum theory?'" While "could" is used in the original indirect sentence, standard grammar rules dictate a back-shift from "can" to "could" when moving to indirect speech. Therefore, direct speech should use "can."

3. Convert the sentence provided below from its passive voice structure to an active voice structure: The CEO is known to have been influenced by external advisors.

A. External advisors are known to influence the CEO.

B. Someone knew the CEO was influenced by external advisors.

C. People know that the CEO was influenced by external advisors.

D. External advisors are said to have influenced the CEO.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Option 3. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 3. Correct Sentence: "People know that the CEO was influenced by external advisors." Additional Information

• The conversion is from Passive Voice to Active Voice.

• The given sentence follows the passive structure: Subject + is known + to have been + V3 + by + Agent.

• In the active voice, the doer of the action is placed as the subject, and the passive construction is changed into an active clause.

• Thus, "The CEO is known to have been influenced by external advisors" changes to "People know that the CEO was influenced by external advisors."

• Option 1: "External advisors are known to influence the CEO." – Incorrect because it changes the meaning and suggests a present influence rather than a completed action.

• Option 2: "Someone knew the CEO was influenced by external advisors." – Incorrect because it changes the tense and meaning of the original sentence.

• Option 4: "External advisors are said to have influenced the CEO." – Incorrect because it changes "is known" into "is said," which alters the meaning.

12. A sentence is provided in direct speech. From the four given options, choose the one that most accurately conveys the sentence in its corresponding indirect speech. “How strange that nobody noticed the missing file until today!” exclaimed the clerk.

A. The clerk exclaimed it was strange that nobody noticed the missing file until today.

B. The clerk exclaimed that it was strange that no one had noticed the missing file until that day.

C. The clerk said that it was strange no one had noticed file till now.

D. The clerk exclaimed how strange nobody had noticed the file.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: “The clerk exclaimed that it was strange that no one had noticed the missing file until that day.” Additional Information

• Indirect Speech: The clerk exclaimed that it was strange that no one had noticed the missing file until that day.

• Key Conversions: Tense Change: The verb "noticed" (Simple Past) in direct speech changes to "had noticed" (Past Perfect) in indirect speech because the reporting verb ("exclaimed") is in the past tense.

• Time Expression Change: The word "today" is a word of "nearness" and must be changed to "that day" to indicate distance in indirect speech.

• Sentence Type: The original sentence is exclamatory ("How strange...!").

• In indirect speech, the exclamation is transformed into an assertive structure: "exclaimed that it was strange that...".

• Pronoun/Subject: "Nobody" and "no one" are often interchangeable in reporting, but the tense and time markers are the primary indicators of the correct option.

• Option 1: Fails to change the tense from "noticed" to "had noticed" and fails to change "today" to "that day."

• Option 3: Incorrectly uses "till now" (which should be "until then" or "until that day") and uses the reporting verb "said," which loses the emotive force of the exclamation.

• Option 4: Incorrectly retains the exclamatory word "how" in the reported clause and omits the time reference entirely.

16. Change the following sentence into the Active form. The old castle was believed to have been built by a legendary king.

A. A legendary king built the old castle, it was believed.

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: "People believed that a legendary king had built the old castle." Additional Information

• Complex Passive Structure: The original sentence "The old castle was believed to have been built..." uses a double passive construction (a passive reporting verb "was believed" and a passive infinitive "to have been built").

• Identify the Reporting Agent: Since the sentence doesn't state who did the believing, we introduce a general subject like "People" or "They" to make the reporting verb active ("People believed").

• Identify the Action Agent: The agent of the building action is "a legendary king." In the active voice, he becomes the subject of the second clause.

• Tense Alignment: "To have been built" indicates an action that happened before the belief. Therefore, when converting to active, we use the Past Perfect ("had built") to show this sequence.

• Option 1: "A legendary king built... it was believed." – This keeps the "it was believed" part in the passive voice.

• Option 3: "The old castle had been built..." – This remains in the passive voice.

• Option 4: "A legendary king was believed..." – This is still a passive construction focusing on the king rather than the act of believing.
