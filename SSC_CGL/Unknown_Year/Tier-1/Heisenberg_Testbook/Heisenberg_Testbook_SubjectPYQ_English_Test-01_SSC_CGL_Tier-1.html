1. P starts a business with Rs. 8,000. Q joins after 5 months with Rs. 12,000. If the profit at the end of the year is Rs. 6,800, what is Q's share (approx.)?

A. Rs. 1,173.33

B. Rs. 2,173.33

C. Rs. 3,173.33

D. Rs. 4,173.33

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Profit ratio = (Capital1 × Time1) : (Capital2 × Time2) Ratio (P : Q) = (8000 × 12) : (12000 × 7) Ratio (P : Q) = 96000 : 84000 = 8 : 7 Total profit ratio parts = 8 + 7 = 15 parts Q's share = (7 / 15) × 6800 ≈ Rs. 3173.33 ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 3173.33. Alternate Method Given: P's initial investment = Rs. 8,000 Investment duration for P = 12 months Q's joining investment = Rs. 12,000 Investment duration for Q = 12 − 5 = 7 months Total annual profit = Rs. 6,800 Partner P Rs. 8,000 × 12 months = 96k units Ratio : 8 Partner Q Rs. 12,000 × 7 months = 84k units Ratio : 7 Total Profit = Rs. 6800 Formula Used: Profit Ratio = (Capital of A × Time of A) : (Capital of B × Time of B) Calculations: ⇒ Profit ratio of P to Q = (8000 × 12) : (12000 × 7) ⇒ Profit ratio = 96000 : 84000 ⇒ Profit ratio = 96 : 84 Dividing the ratio by 12: ⇒ Profit ratio (P : Q) = 8 : 7 ⇒ Total profit units = 8 + 7 = 15 units ⇒ Q's share in profit = (7 / 15) × Total Profit ⇒ Q's share = (7 / 15) × 6800 ⇒ Q's share = 47600 / 15 ⇒ Q's share = 3173.33 ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 3173.33. Additional Information Compound Partnership When partners invest different capital amounts for different time periods, the profit is divided in the ratio of the product of their capital and time: Profit Ratio = C1T1 : C2T2. Joining Mid-Way If a business profit is evaluated annually (12 months) and a partner joins after 't' months, their active period for generating profit is calculated as (12 − t) months. Sleeping Partner vs Active Partner An active partner manages the business and usually receives a fixed salary or a specific percentage of profit prior to general division. A sleeping partner only invests capital and receives profit strictly according to the investment ratio.

6. If sin A = 8/17 and cos B = 15/17,find sin A cos B+cosA sinB:

A. 120/289

B. 17/289

C. 240/289

D. 15/17

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick The expression sin A cos B + cos A sin B follows the compound angle identity sin(A + B). Given sin A = 8/17 and cos B = 15/17, notice they belong to the Pythagorean triplet (8, 15, 17). This implies cos A = 15/17 and sin B = 8/17 as they share the same hypotenuse 17. Substituting values: (8/17 × 15/17) + (15/17 × 8/17) = 120/289 + 120/289 = 240/289. ∴ The correct answer is 240/289. Alternate Method Given: sin A = 8/17 and cos B = 15/17 Formula Used: sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B | cos2θ = 1 − sin2θ ⇒ First, find cos A: cos A = √(1 − sin2 A) = √(1 − (8/17)2) ⇒ cos A = √(1 − 64/289) = √(225/289) = 15/17 ⇒ Next, find sin B: sin B = √(1 − cos2 B) = √(1 − (15/17)2) ⇒ sin B = √(1 − 225/289) = √(64/289) = 8/17 ⇒ Required value = (8/17 × 15/17) + (15/17 × 8/17) ⇒ Value = 120/289 + 120/289 = 240/289 ∴ The correct answer is 240/289. Additional Information Trigonometric Identities for Sum/Difference sin(A ± B) = sin A cos B ± cos A sin B and cos(A ± B) = cos A cos B ∓ sin A sin B. Pythagorean Triplets Sets of three positive integers a, b, and c such that a2 + b2 = c2. Common examples include (3, 4, 5), (5, 12, 13), and (8, 15, 17). Range of Sine and Cosine For any real angle θ, −1 ≤ sin θ ≤ 1 and −1 ≤ cos θ ≤ 1.

7. A circle has a radius of 13 cm. A chord is drawn at a distance of 5 cm from the center. What is the length of the chord?

A. 12 cm

B. 13 cm

C. 24 cm

D. 26 cm

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick In a circle, the radius, the perpendicular distance to the chord, and half the chord always form a right-angled triangle. We can directly use the standard Pythagorean triplet (5, 12, 13). Since the hypotenuse (radius) is 13 cm and one side is 5 cm, the other side (half the chord) must be 12 cm. Length of the full chord = 2 × 12 cm = 24 cm. ∴ The correct answer is 24 cm. Alternate Method Given: Radius of the circle (OA) = 13 cm Perpendicular distance from the center to the chord (OM) = 5 cm Formula Used: According to Pythagoras theorem: Hypotenuse2 = Base2 + Perpendicular2 The perpendicular drawn from the center of a circle to a chord bisects the chord. O A B M 13 cm 5 cm x Chord AB = 2 × AM = 24 cm Calculations: In right-angled triangle OMA: ⇒ OA2 = OM2 + AM2 ⇒ 132 = 52 + AM2 ⇒ 169 = 25 + AM2 ⇒ AM2 = 169 − 25 ⇒ AM2 = 144 ⇒ AM = √144 = 12 cm Since the perpendicular from the center bisects the chord, full chord AB = 2 × AM ⇒ Length of chord AB = 2 × 12 = 24 cm ∴ The correct answer is 24 cm. Additional Information Perpendicular from Center to Chord A perpendicular drawn from the center of a circle to any chord bisects the chord into two equal halves. Common Pythagorean Triplets Memorizing integer sets that satisfy a2 + b2 = c2 saves critical calculation time. Important triplets include (3, 4, 5), (5, 12, 13), (8, 15, 17), and (7, 24, 25) along with their multiples. Intersecting Chords Theorem If two chords AB and CD intersect internally at point P, then the products of their segments are always equal: PA × PB = PC × PD. 9. The average of 5 consecutive odd natural numbers is 27. Previous 5 consecutive odd natural numbers to these 5 odd natural numbers are also included. What will be the average of newly added numbers?

A. 9

B. 17

C. 15

D. 11

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is always the middle term. Middle term of original set = 27. The previous set also contains 5 consecutive odd numbers. Since both sets have 5 terms, the difference between their averages is 2 × 5 = 10. Average of new numbers = 27 − 10 = 17. ∴ The correct answer is 17. Alternate Method Given: Average of 5 consecutive odd natural numbers = 27. Calculations: ⇒ Let the 5 consecutive odd numbers be: (x − 4), (x − 2), x, (x + 2), (x + 4) ⇒ Average = Sum ÷ 5 = [ (x−4) + (x−2) + x + (x+2) + (x+4) ] ÷ 5 = 5x ÷ 5 = x ⇒ Given average = 27, so x = 27. ⇒ Original numbers are: 23, 25, 27, 29, 31. ⇒ The 5 consecutive odd numbers previous to these are: 13, 15, 17, 19, 21. ⇒ Average of these newly added numbers = (13 + 15 + 17 + 19 + 21) ÷ 5 = 85 ÷ 5 = 17. ∴ The correct answer is 17. Additional Information Average of Arithmetic Progression For any consecutive numbers (AP), the average is always (First term + Last term) ÷ 2 or the middle term if the count is odd. Consecutive Odd/Even Numbers In a sequence of consecutive odd or even numbers, the common difference between any two successive terms is always 2. Shift in Average If every number in a data set is increased or decreased by a constant value 'k', the average also increases or decreases by exactly 'k'.

11. A regular right triangular pyramid has base side = 10 cm, and slant height = 13 cm. Find the total surface area if all slant heights are equal and the base is equilateral.

A. 238.3 cm2

B. 282.7 cm2

C. 300.5 cm2

D. 320.3 cm2

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Lateral Surface Area (LSA) = (1/2) × Perimeter × Slant height = (1/2) × 30 × 13 = 195 cm2. Base Area = (√3 / 4) × 102 ≈ 0.433 × 100 = 43.3 cm2. Total Surface Area (TSA) = 195 + 43.3 = 238.3 cm2. ∴ The correct answer is 238.3 cm2. Alternate Method Given: Base side (a) of the equilateral triangle = 10 cm Slant height (l) of the pyramid = 13 cm Formula Used: Total Surface Area (TSA) = Lateral Surface Area (LSA) + Base Area LSA = (1/2) × Perimeter of base × Slant height Base Area = (√3 / 4) × a2 Calculations: ⇒ Perimeter of the base = 3 × 10 = 30 cm ⇒ Lateral Surface Area = (1/2) × 30 × 13 = 195 cm2 ⇒ Base Area = (√3 / 4) × 102 ⇒ Base Area = (1.732 / 4) × 100 = 0.433 × 100 = 43.3 cm2 ⇒ Total Surface Area = 195 + 43.3 = 238.3 cm2 ∴ The correct answer is 238.3 cm2. Additional Information Volume of a Pyramid The volume (V) of any pyramid is given by V = (1/3) × Base Area × Vertical Height (h). Slant Height vs. Slant Edge Slant height (l) is the altitude of the lateral triangular face, whereas slant edge (e) is the distance from the apex to a base vertex. Pythagorean Relation in Pyramids For a regular pyramid, l2 = h2 + r2, where r is the in-radius of the base, and e2 = h2 + R2, where R is the circum-radius.

A. -2

B. -3

C. 0

D. 1

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick The expression sec2A − tan2A is a fundamental trigonometric identity. By identity, for any angle A (where defined), sec2A − tan2A = 1. The value of sin A = 3/5 is given to define the angle, but the result of the identity remains constant regardless of the specific value of A. ∴ The correct answer is 1. Alternate Method Given: sin A = 3/5, where A is an acute angle. Formula: sin A = Perpendicular (P) / Hypotenuse (H), sec A = H / Base (B), tan A = P / B. ⇒ Let Perpendicular (P) = 3 and Hypotenuse (H) = 5. ⇒ Base (B) = √(H2 − P2) = √(52 − 32) = √(25 − 9) = √16 = 4. ⇒ sec A = H / B = 5/4. ⇒ tan A = P / B = 3/4. ⇒ sec2A − tan2A = (5/4)2 − (3/4)2 ⇒ sec2A − tan2A = 25/16 − 9/16 = 16/16 = 1. ∴ The correct answer is 1. 15. If a = √7 + √5 and b = √7 - √5, then a2 + b2 = ?

A. 21

B. 33

C. 24

D. 50

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Identify that a and b are conjugate pairs of the form (x + y) and (x − y). Use the direct algebraic identity: a2 + b2 = (x + y)2 + (x − y)2 = 2(x2 + y2) Here, x = √7 and y = √5. a2 + b2 = 2[(√7)2 + (√5)2] = 2(7 + 5) = 2 × 12 = 24 ∴ The correct answer is 24. Alternate Method Given: a = √7 + √5 and b = √7 − √5 Formula Used: (x + y)2 = x2 + y2 + 2xy and (x − y)2 = x2 + y2 − 2xy ⇒ a2 = (√7 + √5)2 = (√7)2 + (√5)2 + 2(√7 × √5) ⇒ a2 = 7 + 5 + 2√35 = 12 + 2√35 ⇒ b2 = (√7 − √5)2 = (√7)2 + (√5)2 − 2(√7 × √5) ⇒ b2 = 7 + 5 − 2√35 = 12 − 2√35 ⇒ a2 + b2 = (12 + 2√35) + (12 − 2√35) ⇒ a2 + b2 = 12 + 12 = 24 Therefore, the value of the expression is 24, which corresponds to option (3). ∴ The correct answer is 24. Additional Information Algebraic Identities for Conjugates For any two numbers x and y: (x + y)2 + (x − y)2 = 2(x2 + y2) and (x + y)2 − (x − y)2 = 4xy. Product of Conjugate Surds The product of conjugate surds (√m + √n) and (√m − √n) always results in a rational number: (√m + √n)(√m − √n) = m − n. Sum of Conjugate Surds Adding conjugates (√m + √n) + (√m − √n) eliminates the second irrational term, resulting in 2√m.

Solution

24. If x = (√(5) + √(3))/(√(5) - √(3)) + (√(5) - √(3))/(√(5) + √(3)), then the value of x is:
