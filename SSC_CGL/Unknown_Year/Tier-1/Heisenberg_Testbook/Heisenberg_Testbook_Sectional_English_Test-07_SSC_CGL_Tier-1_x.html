1. When 1 litre of water is cooled from 4°C to 0°C, its volume _____.

A. first decreases and then increases

B. remains the same

C. increases

D. decreases

Answer C

Solution

In normal cases, the volume of substances increases on heating and decreases while cooling. When 1 litre of water is cooled from 4°C to 0°C, its volume increases due to 'Anomalous Expansion of Water'. The density of water is maximum at 4°C. When water is cooled from 4°C to 0°C, its density decreases. As density decreases, the volume increases. Volume = mass/density.

9. The type of unemployment seen in India's agriculture sector is

A. Frictional

B. Cyclical

C. Open

D. Disguised

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Disguised unemploymentKey Points Additional Information Thus, The type of unemployment seen in India's agriculture sector is Disguised unemployment.

• Disguised unemployment is found in the agricultural sector of India. Disguised unemployment is also known as hidden unemployment. In this type of unemployment, some people seem employed but they are actually not.

• Disguised unemployment is when more people are involved in production then actually required.

• On account of small land holdings , joint family systems, lack of alternative job opportunities there is disguised unemployment.

• Frictional unemployment is a form of unemployment reflecting the gap between someone voluntarily leaving a job and finding another.

• Open Unemployment refers to that economic phenomenon in which persons are able and willing to work at the prevailing wage rate but fail to get work. So, Open Unemployment is different from Disguised Unemployment. In the case of open unemployment, workers are totally idle

• Cyclical unemployment is the component of overall unemployment that results directly from cycles of economic upturn and downturn. Unemployment typically rises during recessions and declines during economic expansions.

Solution

The Correct Answer is Article 61. Key Points Additional Information Important Points Articles Explanation Article 52 The President of India There shall be a President of India. • Procedure for impeachment of the President: When a President is impeached for violating the Constitution, either House of Parliament must support the charge. No such charge shall be preferred until it is accepted by not less than two-thirds of the total membership of the House.

• When either House of Parliament prefers an accusation, the other House investigates or causes the charge to be investigated, with the President having the right to appear and be served during the investigation.

• If a resolution is passed as a result of the investigation, with not less than two-thirds of the total membership of the House from which the charge was investigated or caused to be investigated, declaring that the charge preferred against the President has been sustained, such resolution would have the effect of excluding the President from office as of the date on which the resolution is passed.

25. Which force moves an aircraft through the air?

A. Lift Force

B. Drag Force

C. Thrust Force

D. Gravity Force

Answer C

Solution

Thrust force moves an aircraft through the air. It overcomes drag and gravity, enabling forward motion. Drag force slows the aircraft, gravity pulls it downward, and lift force counteracts gravity to keep it airborne. Thrust is essential for propulsion and maintaining flight.
