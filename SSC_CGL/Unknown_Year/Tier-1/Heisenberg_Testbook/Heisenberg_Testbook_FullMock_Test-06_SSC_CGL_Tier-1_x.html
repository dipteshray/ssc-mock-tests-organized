96. Direction: Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. The scientist will discover a new formula.

A. A new formula is discovered by the scientist.

B. A new formula will be discovered by the scientist.

C. A new formula has been discovered by the scientist.

D. A new formula will have been discovered by the scientist.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is: "A new formula will be discovered by the scientist." Key Points The correct answer is option 2. Correct sentence: A new formula will be discovered by the scientist.

• The given sentence is in the Future Simple Tense and in Active Voice.

• To change a Future Simple Tense from Active voice to Passive voice: Interchange the object and the subject with each other, i.e. object of the active sentence become the subject of the passive sentence.

• Structure: Subject + will + V1 + object (Active Voice)

• Object + will be + V3 + by Subject (Passive Voice)

• Example: They will conduct the experiment. (Active Voice)

• The experiment will be conducted by them. (Passive Voice)

• The subject of the given sentence is 'The scientist.'

• The object of the given sentence is 'a new formula.'

• Apply the rule and changes - 'will discover' will be changed into 'will be discovered'.
