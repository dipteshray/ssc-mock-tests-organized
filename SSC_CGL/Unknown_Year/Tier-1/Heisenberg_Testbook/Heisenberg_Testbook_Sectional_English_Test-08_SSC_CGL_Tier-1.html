7. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. The bridge was being renovated when it was struck by lightning.

A. Someone renovated the bridge when lightning struck it.

B. They were renovating the bridge when lightning struck it.

C. They were renovating the bridge when lightning was striking it.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is '2' i.e. They were renovating the bridge when lightning struck it. Key Points Correct sentence: They were renovating the bridge when lightning struck it. Additional Information

• The given sentence is in the passive voice, which indicates that the subject is receiving the action.

• To convert the sentence into the active voice, the subject performing the action must be clearly stated.

• The correct active voice sentence is: "They were renovating the bridge when lightning struck it."

• This transformation retains the original meaning while shifting the focus to the subject performing the action.

• Active Voice: The subject performs the action in the sentence. Example: She reads the book.

• Passive Voice: The subject receives the action in the sentence. Example: The book is read by her.

• Converting passive voice to active voice involves identifying the performer of the action and restructuring the sentence accordingly.

C. The captain ordered, "Man the stations and you must be ready for the storm that was approaching."

Solution

The correct answer is '2' i.e. The captain said to the crew, "Man the stations! You must be ready for the storm that is approaching." . Key Points Correct sentence: The captain said to the crew, "Man the stations! You must be ready for the storm that is approaching." Additional Information

• The given sentence is in indirect speech and needs to be converted into direct speech.

• The phrase "commanded the crew to man the stations" in the indirect speech corresponds to the imperative statement, "Man the stations!" in direct speech.

• The phrase "they must be ready for the storm that was approaching" in indirect speech is converted to "You must be ready for the storm that is approaching" in direct speech, addressing the crew directly.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is '4' i.e. if she does not complete the delegated target . Key Points Correct sentence: Suman will be fired if she does not complete the delegated target set by her boss. Additional Information

• The given sentence is an example of the first conditional, which is used to talk about real or possible situations in the future.

• The structure of the first conditional is "If + present simple, will + base verb".

• In a first conditional sentence using "If" with a real future situation, the correct form of the verb to use is "will" for the result clause and present simple for the condition clause. For example: I will fail if I don't study.

• Similarly, in this question, the verb form is 'will' and hence, present simple tense (does not complete) is grammatically correct.

• 2nd Conditional Sentence: Structure: If + Past Simple, would + base verb.

• Example: If I were an astronaut, I would look at the stars all the time.

• Usage: Describes hypothetical situations in the present or future.

• 3rd Conditional Sentence: Structure: If + Past Perfect, would have + past participle.

• Example: If she had studied harder, she would have passed the exam.

• Usage: Refers to unreal past situations and their imagined outcomes.
