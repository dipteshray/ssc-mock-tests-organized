16. If : 49 ¥ 7 × 7 = 14, and 144 ¥ 6 × 6 = 30, then the value of 56 ¥ 8 × 3 =?

A. 11

B. 10

C. 9

D. 8

Answer B

Solution

The pattern followed here is: 49 ¥ 7 → 49 ÷ 7 = 7; 7 × 7 → 7 + 7 = 14. And 144 ¥ 6 → 144 ÷ 6 = 24; 24 × 6 → 24 + 6 = 30. Similarly, 56 ¥ 8 → 56 ÷ 8 = 7; 7 × 3 → 7 + 3 = 10 Hence, 10 is the correct answer
