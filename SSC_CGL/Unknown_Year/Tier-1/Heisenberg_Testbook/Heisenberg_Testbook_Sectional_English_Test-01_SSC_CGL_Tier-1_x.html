4. Direction: In the question below is given a statement followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. You have to assume everything in the statement to be true, then consider the two conclusions together and decide which of them logically follows beyond a reasonable doubt from the information given in the statement. Statement: Domestic demand for steel is outpacing the supply of steel- both indigenous and Imported. Conclusions: I. Tariffs will be increased for steel imports. II. Indigenous production might be increased or quotas for steel imports might be increased.

A. Only conclusion I follows

B. Neither conclusion I nor conclusion II follows

C. Both the conclusions follow

D. Only conclusion II follows

Answer D

Solution

I. Tariffs will be increased for steel imports - Raising tariffs on imports discourages imports. So, it is unlikely that such a situation will arise. Thus, such a definite conclusion can be ruled out. Hence, conclusion I does not follow. II. Indigenous production might be increased or quotas for steel imports might be increased - The possibility of an increase in domestic production or imports to deal with the problem highlighted in the statement. Raising quotas on imports encourages imports by allowing more imports. Similarly, indigenous production can be increased to match the demand. Either of these or both of these actions can help deal with the issue. So, these actions might be considered to meet the domestic demand. Hence, conclusion II follows.
