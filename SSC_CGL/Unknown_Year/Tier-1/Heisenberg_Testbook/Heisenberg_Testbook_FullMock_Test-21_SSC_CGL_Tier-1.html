Solution

The correct answer is infinity and convex mirrorKey Points Additional Information

• A virtual image cannot be obtained on a screen and appears to be formed on the same side of the mirror as the object.

• A highly diminished image means the image is much smaller in size compared to the object.

• A convex mirror always forms a virtual, erect, and diminished image regardless of the position of the object.

• A concave mirror can form a virtual image, but this happens only when the object is between the focus (F) and the pole (P).

• However, the image in this case would be magnified, not diminished.

• For a convex mirror, if the object is at infinity, the image is formed at the focus and is virtual and highly diminished.

• Thus, a convex mirror with the object at infinity forms a virtual and highly diminished image.

• Convex mirror: A spherical mirror whose reflecting surface is curved outwards, is called a convex mirror.

• The nature of the image formed by a convex mirror does not depend on the distance of the object from the mirror.

• Convex mirror always forms a virtual, erect and diminished image of the object placed at any position in front of the mirror.

• Convex mirror is also known as a diverging mirror.

• Convex mirrors are commonly used as rear-view (wing) mirrors in vehicles.

• These mirrors are fitted on the sides of the vehicle, enabling the driver to see traffic behind him/her to facilitate safe driving.

• Concave mirror: A spherical mirror, whose reflecting surface is curved inwards, that is, faces towards the center of the sphere, is called a concave mirror.

• A concave mirror is also known as a converging mirror. Depending on the point of the object:

• The image may be real or virtual.

• image may be erect or inverted.

• image may be magnified or diminished.

• Concave mirrors are commonly used in shaving mirrors, torches, search lights and vehicle headlights to get powerful parallel beams of light.
