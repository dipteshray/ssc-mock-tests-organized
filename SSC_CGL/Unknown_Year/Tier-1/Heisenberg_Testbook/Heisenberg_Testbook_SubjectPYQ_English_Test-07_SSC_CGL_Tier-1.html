1. In March 2025, Prime Minister of India announced that cheetah introduction will be expanded to Gandhisagar Sanctuary, which is in ________

A. Madhya Pradesh

B. Gujarat

C. Maharashtra

D. Kerala

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Madhya Pradesh. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• Gandhisagar Sanctuary is located in the state of Madhya Pradesh, India.

• It is situated on the northern boundary of the Mandsaur and Neemuch districts of Madhya Pradesh.

• In March 2025, the Prime Minister of India announced the expansion of the cheetah reintroduction program to Gandhisagar Sanctuary as part of efforts to conserve and reintroduce cheetahs in India.

• The cheetah reintroduction initiative was first launched in Kuno National Park, Madhya Pradesh, which is part of India’s commitment to global wildlife conservation and rewilding efforts.

• The Gandhisagar Sanctuary is known for its diverse flora and fauna and is considered a suitable habitat for cheetahs due to its expansive grasslands and prey availability.

• About the Cheetah Reintroduction Program: Cheetahs were declared extinct in India in 1952 due to hunting and habitat loss.

• The reintroduction program was initiated to bring cheetahs back to India, with the first batch of cheetahs being imported from Namibia and South Africa.

• The project is a collaboration between the Indian government and international conservation organizations.

• The aim is to restore ecosystems and promote biodiversity in India.

• Key Features of Gandhisagar Sanctuary: The sanctuary spans an area of approximately 368 square kilometers.

• It is named after the Gandhisagar Dam, which is located on the Chambal River.

• The sanctuary features a mix of dry deciduous forests and grasslands, making it suitable for various wildlife species.

• It is home to species like leopards, wild boars, nilgais, and several bird species.

• Why Madhya Pradesh? Madhya Pradesh is often referred to as the "Tiger State of India" due to its rich wildlife and numerous national parks.

• The state has been a pioneer in wildlife conservation, with renowned reserves like Kanha, Bandhavgarh, and Satpura.

• The availability of suitable habitats and prey species in Madhya Pradesh makes it an ideal location for the reintroduction of cheetahs.

• Other States in the Options: Gujarat: Known for the Gir National Park, which is home to the Asiatic lions, but not for cheetah reintroduction.

• Maharashtra: Famous for Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve but not linked to the cheetah project.

• Kerala: Known for its tropical biodiversity and backwaters but unsuitable for cheetahs due to dense forests.

• India's Wildlife Conservation Efforts: India has launched various programs like Project Tiger (1973) and Project Elephant (1992) to conserve its wildlife.

• The reintroduction of cheetahs is a significant step in India’s conservation history and is expected to boost tourism and local economies.

• Global Significance: The cheetah reintroduction program in India is the first intercontinental translocation of wild cheetahs.

• This initiative highlights India’s commitment to global wildlife conservation efforts under international agreements like CITES (Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species).

3. Which of the following is the India's first interplanetary mission?

A. Mangalyaan

B. Shukrayaan

C. Chndrayaan-1

D. Aditya L-1

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Mangalyaan. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• Mangalyaan, also known as the Mars Orbiter Mission (MOM), is India’s first interplanetary mission, launched by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) on 5 November 2013.

• It was launched using the Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV-C25) from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota.

• The mission's primary objective was to demonstrate India’s ability to design, launch, and operate an interplanetary spacecraft. It also aimed to study the Martian atmosphere, surface, and morphology.

• Mangalyaan successfully entered Mars' orbit on 24 September 2014, making India the first Asian nation to reach Martian orbit and the first country in the world to achieve this feat in its maiden attempt.

• The mission was completed at a cost of approximately ₹450 crore ($74 million), making it one of the most cost-effective interplanetary missions ever.

• Chandrayaan-1: India’s first lunar probe, launched by ISRO on 22 October 2008.

• The mission discovered the presence of water molecules on the Moon.

• The spacecraft operated until 29 August 2009 and helped map the Moon's surface.

• Shukrayaan: This is ISRO’s proposed mission to study Venus, focusing on its atmosphere and surface.

• The mission is currently under development and is planned for launch in the 2020s.

• Aditya L-1: It is India’s first mission to study the Sun, scheduled for launch in 2024.

• The spacecraft will be placed in the Lagrangian Point 1 (L1), about 1.5 million km from Earth, to observe solar activities and their effect on space weather.

• Significance of Mangalyaan: Mangalyaan helped India join the elite club of space agencies capable of interplanetary exploration, including NASA (USA), Roscosmos (Russia), and ESA (Europe).

• The mission provided valuable data on Mars' surface, atmosphere, and weather patterns, including methane levels.

• The success of Mangalyaan inspired subsequent Indian missions like Chandrayaan-2 and upcoming deep-space explorations.

• ISRO’s Achievements: ISRO is globally recognized for its cost-effective space missions and innovative technologies.

• Its missions like Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan, and GSAT satellites have bolstered India's position in global space exploration.

• Comparison with Other Missions: Mangalyaan's cost was significantly lower than NASA's Maven mission to Mars, which launched the same year.

• Despite a limited budget, Mangalyaan achieved remarkable scientific and technological milestones.

• Future Missions: ISRO plans to launch Gaganyaan (India’s first manned space mission) and Chandrayaan-3 in the near future.

• Collaborations with other space agencies, such as NASA and ESA, are also being explored for advanced missions.

9. The "Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U)" aims to address the housing shortage in urban areas. Which of the following approaches is a key component of this scheme to achieve its objectives?

A. Primarily focusing on providing rental housing for migrant workers.

B. Providing financial assistance to beneficiaries for the construction, purchase, or enhancement of affordable housing.

C. Mandating the use of only indigenous building materials in all PMAY-U projects.

D. Concentrating housing development efforts in Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Providing financial assistance to beneficiaries for the construction, purchase, or enhancement of affordable housing. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) was launched in 2015 by the Government of India to address the urban housing shortage, especially among the economically weaker sections (EWS), low-income groups (LIG), and middle-income groups (MIG).

• The scheme includes a component of providing financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries for constructing, purchasing, or enhancing affordable housing units. This is a key approach to achieving the scheme's objectives.

• The financial assistance is provided through a Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS), under which beneficiaries can avail of interest subsidies on housing loans based on their income categories.

• Under PMAY-U, the focus is on creating “Housing for All” by 2022, with the target of constructing over 1 crore houses in urban areas.

• The scheme also encourages public-private partnerships (PPP) and state-level implementation to ensure effective execution and broader reach.

• Key Components of PMAY-U: In-situ Slum Redevelopment (ISSR): Focuses on using land as a resource to provide housing to slum dwellers with the participation of private developers.

• Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP): Encourages partnerships with private and public sectors to provide affordable housing for the EWS category.

• Beneficiary-led Construction (BLC): Provides financial assistance to eligible individuals to construct or enhance their houses on their own land.

• Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS): Offers interest subsidies on housing loans to EWS, LIG, and MIG beneficiaries.

• Eligibility Criteria: The applicant must belong to the EWS, LIG, or MIG categories, with annual income ranging from up to ₹3 lakh (EWS) to ₹18 lakh (MIG).

• The beneficiary should not own a pucca house in their name or any family member’s name.

• Women, Scheduled Castes (SCs), Scheduled Tribes (STs), and other vulnerable groups are given priority.

• Achievements: As of 2023, over 1.2 crore houses have been sanctioned under PMAY-U, with significant progress in construction and delivery.

• The scheme has successfully mobilized private investment and created employment opportunities in the construction sector.

• Challenges: Land acquisition and high real estate costs in urban areas pose challenges to affordable housing projects.

• Ensuring the timely delivery of houses and maintaining quality standards remain critical issues.

• Option 1: While rental housing for migrant workers is essential, it is not the primary focus of PMAY-U. Instead, it is covered under schemes like the Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHCs).

• Option 3: The scheme does not mandate the exclusive use of indigenous building materials. It allows flexibility to ensure cost-effectiveness and sustainability.

• Option 4: PMAY-U focuses on all urban areas, including Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 cities, rather than concentrating solely on Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities.

12. 'Raja' festival is celebrated in which of the following state?

A. Nagaland

B. Gujarat

C. Karnataka

D. Odisha

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Odisha. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• Raja Festival, also called Raja Parba or Raja Sankranti, is a traditional festival celebrated in the state of Odisha in India.

• It is observed to mark the advent of the agricultural season and the first monsoon rains, symbolizing fertility and the worship of Mother Earth (Bhuma Devi).

• The festival spans three days, with each day having its own significance: First Day - Pahili Raja, Second Day - Raja Sankranti, and Third Day - Basi Raja.

• During this festival, women are given importance as Raja celebrates femininity and fertility. They refrain from household chores, enjoy swings, wear new clothes, and apply alta (a red-colored dye) on their feet.

• Special dishes like Poda Pitha (a type of baked cake) are prepared, and people engage in festivities, including playing games, singing folk songs, and enjoying traditional rituals.

• Significance of Raja Festival: The festival is deeply connected with the agricultural and rural traditions of Odisha. It symbolizes the transition of Mother Earth to her menstruation period, a time when she rejuvenates herself.

• It is believed that during Raja, the land should not be tilled to give Mother Earth rest, emphasizing the importance of respecting nature and its cycles.

• The celebration of Raja also highlights the role of women in society, focusing on their health and well-being.

• Regional Practices: People in Odisha decorate swings (locally called Ram Doli or Jhula) with flowers and enjoy swinging as a form of recreation during the festival.

• Villages organize community games and cultural programs, including traditional folk songs dedicated to Raja.

• Markets are flooded with seasonal fruits, sweets, and items like new clothes and ornaments, making it a joyous occasion for shopping and socializing.

• Cultural Importance: Raja Festival is one of the most prominent festivals in Odisha, celebrated with enthusiasm by all communities.

• It showcases Odisha's rich cultural heritage, including its unique cuisine, attire, and rituals.

• The festival fosters community bonding and serves as a platform for preserving traditional practices.

• Comparison with Other Festivals: Unlike harvest festivals like Pongal in Tamil Nadu or Baisakhi in Punjab, Raja focuses on the preparation for the agricultural season rather than the culmination of harvest.

• The emphasis on swings and women's celebration is unique to Raja and sets it apart from similar festivals in other parts of India.

• Chandrayaan-3 As of October 2023, Chandrayaan-3 is ISRO’s latest lunar mission and focuses on demonstrating safe landing and roving capabilities.

• It is a follow-up mission to Chandrayaan-2, consisting of a lander and rover but no orbiter.

• The primary goal is to ensure a successful soft landing on the Moon and study its surface composition and seismic activity.

• Chandrayaan-4 Chandrayaan-4 is not an officially announced mission by ISRO as of now. It is speculated to be a potential future lunar mission.

• ISRO is currently focusing on Chandrayaan-3 and other interplanetary missions like Gaganyaan and Aditya-L1.

• Lunar Hydration Discovery The discovery of water on the Moon is crucial for future human exploration, as it can support life, act as a resource for producing oxygen, and be used as rocket fuel.
