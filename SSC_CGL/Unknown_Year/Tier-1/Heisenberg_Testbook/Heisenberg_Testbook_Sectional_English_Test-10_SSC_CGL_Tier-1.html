3. Which of the following statements about neutralisation is NOT true?

A. It occurs only between strong acids and strong bases.

B. It involves transfer of H⁺ ions from acid to base.

C. It is always exothermic.

D. It always results in the formation of salt and water.

Answer A

Solution

CONCEPT: Neutralisation EXPLANATION: Neutralisation does not occur only between strong acids and strong bases. It can happen between any acid and base combination.

• Neutralisation is a chemical reaction that occurs between an acid and a base to produce salt and water.

• It involves the transfer of H⁺ ions from the acid to the base.

• Neutralisation reactions are typically exothermic, meaning they release heat.

• They can occur between strong acids and bases, weak acids and bases, or even combinations of strong and weak acids or bases.

• "It occurs only between strong acids and strong bases." This statement is NOT true. Neutralisation can occur between strong acids and strong bases, weak acids and weak bases, or combinations of strong and weak acids or bases.

• "It involves transfer of H⁺ ions from acid to base." This statement is true as neutralisation involves H⁺ ions from the acid reacting with OH⁻ ions from the base.

• "It is always exothermic." This statement is true. Neutralisation reactions generally release heat.

• "It always results in the formation of salt and water." This statement is true. A neutralisation reaction produces salt and water as products.

8. The image formed by a convex mirror is always _________.

A. inverted and diminished

B. inverted and of same size as the object

C. erect and diminished

D. erect and of same size as the object

Answer C

Solution

The image formed by a convex mirror is always erect and diminished. Convex mirrors diverge light rays, forming virtual, upright, and smaller images regardless of the object's position. These properties make convex mirrors useful for applications like rearview mirrors in vehicles, ensuring a wider field of view.

17. A person observing a passing train hears a shift in whistle frequency. Which principle explains this effect?

A. Sound refraction

B. Sound diffraction

C. Doppler effect

D. Resonance

Answer C

Solution

The Doppler effect explains this phenomenon, where the frequency increases as the source approaches and decreases as it moves away. It's widely applied in astronomy, medical imaging, and radar. Other phenomena like refraction or diffraction are unrelated to this effect.

25. Which sector includes transport, banking and communication services?

A. Primary sector

B. Secondary sector

C. Tertiary sector

D. Quaternary sector

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Tertiary sector. Key Points Additional Information

• The tertiary sector, also called the service sector, encompasses industries that provide services rather than goods.

• This sector includes transportation, banking, communication, retail, education, and healthcare, among others.

• Services in the tertiary sector are essential for the functioning of the primary and secondary sectors by facilitating trade, communication, and economic growth.

• The tertiary sector plays a pivotal role in modern economies by contributing significantly to the GDP and creating employment opportunities.

• With globalization and technological advancements, the scope and importance of the tertiary sector have expanded significantly in recent years.

• Primary sector: The primary sector involves activities related to the extraction and harvesting of natural resources directly from the Earth.

• It includes agriculture, fishing, forestry, mining, and animal husbandry.

• This sector forms the foundation of the economy, especially in developing countries, as it provides raw materials for the secondary and tertiary sectors.

• Secondary sector: The secondary sector focuses on manufacturing and industrial production, transforming raw materials into finished goods.

• It includes industries like construction, factory production, and utilities.

• This sector is crucial for economic development as it adds value to raw materials and promotes industrialization.

• Quaternary sector: The quaternary sector involves knowledge-based activities and services, such as research and development, information technology, financial planning, and consulting services.

• It is considered an extension of the tertiary sector and is associated with intellectual and information-driven services.

• This sector plays a crucial role in innovation and the advancement of technology in modern economies.
