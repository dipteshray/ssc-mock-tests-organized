2. Convert the sentence provided below from its passive voice structure to an active voice structure: The final draft of the report has been reviewed by the supervisor thoroughly.

A. the supevisor has reviewed the final draft of the report thoroughly.

B. The final draft was reviewed by the supervisor thoroughly.

C. The supervisor was reviewing the final draft thoroughly.

D. The supervisor had reviewed the final draft thoroughly.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Option 1. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 1. Correct Sentence: "The supervisor has reviewed the final draft of the report thoroughly." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• Active voice structure: The subject performs the action on the object.

• Original sentence in passive voice: "The final draft of the report has been reviewed by the supervisor thoroughly."

• Converted to active voice: The subject "the supervisor" is performing the action "has reviewed" on the object "the final draft of the report."

• The correct active voice sentence is: "The supervisor has reviewed the final draft of the report thoroughly."

• Option 2: "The final draft was reviewed by the supervisor thoroughly." – This is in the past tense passive voice, not active.

• Option 3: "The supervisor was reviewing the final draft thoroughly." – This is in the past continuous tense, which does not match the original sentence.

• Option 4: "The supervisor had reviewed the final draft thoroughly." – This is in the past perfect tense, which does not match the original sentence.

11. Choose the correct passive voice transformation of the sentence: My grandmother tells us stories.

A. We are told stories by my grandmother.

B. We were told stories by my grandmother.

C. We have been told stories by my grandmother.

D. Stories were told to us by my grandmother.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is '1' i.e. We are told stories by my grandmother . Key Points Correct sentence: We are told stories by my grandmother. Additional Information

• The given sentence is in the present simple tense, as indicated by the verb "tells".

• When transforming an active sentence in the present simple tense into the passive voice, the structure is: Passive Voice Structure: Object + is/are + past participle + by + subject.

• In the given sentence: Subject: My grandmother

• Verb: tells

• Object: us stories

• When converted into the passive voice: Object (us stories): becomes the subject of the passive sentence.

• Verb (tells): changes to "are told" (present simple passive form).

• The subject (my grandmother) is moved after the verb with the preposition "by."

• Passive Voice Rules: In the passive voice, the focus shifts from the subject performing the action to the object receiving the action.

• The tense of the verb remains the same as in the active voice, but the verb is changed to its passive form.

• Examples: Active: She writes a letter. → Passive: A letter is written by her.

• Active: They make cakes. → Passive: Cakes are made by them.

16. Identify Spot the error in the given sentence. "Neither of the two boys have completed his homework."

A. Neither of the two

B. boys have

C. completed his

D. homework

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: "Neither of the two boys has completed his homework." Additional Information

• The error is in the phrase "boys have."

• When a sentence begins with "Neither of," "Either of," or "Each of," the subject is considered singular because it refers to the individuals one at a time.

• According to the rules of Subject-Verb Agreement, a singular subject must be followed by a singular verb.

• Therefore, the plural verb "have" should be replaced with the singular verb "has."

• Singular Pronouns: Since "Neither" is singular, the possessive pronoun that follows must also be singular. In this sentence, "his" is already correctly used, reinforcing that the verb should also be singular.

• Option 1 & 4: These parts are grammatically correct. "Neither of the two" correctly sets up the choice between two individuals.

• Distinction: Don't be confused by the plural noun "boys." While the noun following "of" is plural, the real subject is "Neither," which is singular.

22. Identify the correct type of sentence used in the line: "at the heart of the debate lies a fundamental tension between technological progress and moral responsibility."

B. Complex

C. Simple

D. Compound-Complex

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Option 3. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 3. Additional Information

• A simple sentence contains a single independent clause (one subject + one predicate) and may include phrases but no subordinate clauses. (सरल वाक्य)

• The sentence "At the heart of the debate lies a fundamental tension between technological progress and moral responsibility." has one main clause: subject phrase ("a fundamental tension") and predicate ("lies").

• Because there is only one independent clause and no subordinate or coordinate clauses, the sentence is best classified as simple.
