10. What is the faulty weight used by a dishonest shopkeeper instead of the original weight of 1 kg to get a profit of 25%?

A. 250 grams

B. 800 grams

C. 640 grams

D. 980 grams

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Profit of 25% is equivalent to the fraction 1/4. In dishonest weight cases, Profit Ratio = Error / Weight Sold. Here, Error = 1 unit and Weight Sold = 4 units. Total (True Weight) = Error + Weight Sold = 1 + 4 = 5 units. Since 5 units = 1000 g, then 1 unit = 200 g. Faulty weight used = 4 units = 4 × 200 = 800 g. ∴ The correct answer is 800 g. Alternate Method Given: Original weight (True Weight) = 1 kg = 1000 g Profit percentage (P%) = 25% Formula Used: Profit % = [(True Weight − Faulty Weight) / Faulty Weight] × 100 Calculations: Let the Faulty Weight be x grams. ⇒ 25 = [(1000 − x) / x] × 100 ⇒ 25 / 100 = (1000 − x) / x ⇒ 1 / 4 = (1000 − x) / x ⇒ x = 4 × (1000 − x) ⇒ x = 4000 − 4x ⇒ 5x = 4000 ⇒ x = 4000 / 5 ⇒ x = 800 g ∴ The correct answer is 800 g. Additional Information Dishonest Dealer (Price & Weight Manipulation) If a dealer sells goods at cost price but uses a faulty weight, Profit % = [Error / (True Value − Error)] × 100. 15. Two equal arcs of different circles C1 and C2 subtend angles of 60° and 75° respectively, at the centres. What is the ratio of the radius of C1 to the radius of C2?

A. 4 ∶ 5

B. 5 ∶ 4

C. 1 ∶ 1

D. 3 ∶ 2

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick For two equal arcs of different circles, the radius is inversely proportional to the angle subtended at the center. Formula: r1θ1 = r2θ2 ⇒ r1 × 60° = r2 × 75° ⇒ r1 / r2 = 75 / 60 ⇒ r1 : r2 = 5 : 4 ∴ The correct answer is 5 : 4. Alternate Method Given: Angle subtended by arc of circle C1 (θ1) = 60° Angle subtended by arc of circle C2 (θ2) = 75° Arc length of C1 (l1) = Arc length of C2 (l2) Formula Used: Arc length (l) = (2 × π × r × θ) / 360° Calculations: ⇒ Let r1 and r2 be the radii of circles C1 and C2 respectively. ⇒ l1 = (2 × π × r1 × 60) / 360 ⇒ l2 = (2 × π × r2 × 75) / 360 ⇒ Since l1 = l2, we have: ⇒ (2 × π × r1 × 60) / 360 = (2 × π × r2 × 75) / 360 ⇒ r1 × 60 = r2 × 75 ⇒ r1 / r2 = 75 / 60 ⇒ r1 / r2 = 5 / 4 ∴ The correct answer is 5 : 4. 22. The average of 13 numbers is 42. The average of the first 7 numbers is 38 and that of the last 7 numbers is 48. If the 7th number is excluded, then what is the average of the remaining 12 numbers?

A. 40.8

B. 38.6

C. 42.6

D. 36.4

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Net deviation for the 7th number = 7 × (38 − 42) + 7 × (48 − 42) = 7 × (−4) + 7 × (6) = −28 + 42 = +14. Value of 7th number = Average + Net Deviation = 42 + 14 = 56. Sum of remaining 12 numbers = (13 × 42) − 56 = 546 − 56 = 490. New Average = 490 ÷ 12 = 40.833... ∴ The correct answer is 40.8. Alternate Method Given: Average of 13 numbers = 42 Average of first 7 numbers = 38 Average of last 7 numbers = 48 Calculations: ⇒ Total sum of 13 numbers = 13 × 42 = 546 ⇒ Sum of first 7 numbers = 7 × 38 = 266 ⇒ Sum of last 7 numbers = 7 × 48 = 336 ⇒ Sum of (first 7 + last 7) = 266 + 336 = 602 ⇒ 7th number = (Sum of first 7 + Sum of last 7) − Total sum ⇒ 7th number = 602 − 546 = 56 ⇒ Sum of remaining 12 numbers = Total sum − 7th number ⇒ Sum of remaining 12 numbers = 546 − 56 = 490 ⇒ Average of remaining 12 numbers = 490 ÷ 12 ⇒ Average = 40.833... ∴ The correct answer is 40.8. Additional Information Overlap Case in Averages When the sum of two subsets (first n and last n) exceeds the total count, the middle element is calculated as: Overlap Value = (Sum of subsets) − (Total Sum).
