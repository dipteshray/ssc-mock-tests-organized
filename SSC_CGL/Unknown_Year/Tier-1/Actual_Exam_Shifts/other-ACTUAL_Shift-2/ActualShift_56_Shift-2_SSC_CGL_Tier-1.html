1. A person borrowed some money on simple interest. After 4 years, he returned (9/5) of the money to lender. What was the rate of interest?

A. 20% p.a.

B. 18% p.a.

C. 17% p.a.

D. 19% p.a.

Answer A

Solution

Let principal amount be P (9/5)P - P = SI = 4P/5 SI = PRT/100 4P/5 = (P × R × 4)/100 4/5 = 4R/100 R = (4 × 100)/(5 × 4) = 20%.

2. Every month, a man consumes 20 kg of rice and 8 kg of wheat. The price of rice is 20% of the price of wheat, resulting in a total expenditure of ₹300 per month for rice and wheat combined. If the price of wheat is increased by 20%, what percentage reduction in rice consumption is needed to maintain the same expenditure of ₹300? (Given that price of rice and consumption of wheat is constant.)

A. 40%

B. 38%

C. 24%

D. 22%

Answer A

Solution

Let the price of wheat be W Price of rice = 0.2W 20 × 0.2W + 8W = 300 12W = 300 W = 25 New price of wheat = 25 × 1.20 = 30 New total expenditure 20 × 5 + 8 × 30 = 340 Excess expenditure = 340 - 300 = 40 5x + 240 = 300 x = 12 Reduction in rice consumption = 8 kg Percentage reduction = (8/20) × 100 = 40%

3. A thief pursued by a policeman was 150 m ahead at the start. If the ratio of the speed of the policeman to that of the thief was 5 : 4, then how far (distance in metres) could the thief go before he is caught by the policeman?

A. 350

B. 200

C. 600

D. 400

Answer C

Solution

Let speed of thief be 4x m/s and speed of policeman be 5x m/s Relative speed = x m/s Time = 150 / x sec Distance = 4x × 150 / x = 600 meters.

11. In triangle ABC, the length of side BC is 2 cm less than twice the length of side AB. The length of side AC exceeds the length of side AB by 10 cm. The perimeter of the triangle is 40 cm. Determine the length (in cm) of the smallest side of the triangle.

A. 12

B. 14

C. 10

D. 8

Answer D

Solution

Let the length of side AB = x cm. length of side BC = 2x - 2 cm and length of side AC = x + 10 cm. perimeter = 40 cm. x + (2x - 2) + (x + 10) = 40. 4x + 8 = 40 4x = 32 x = 8. Therefore, the length of the smallest side (AB) = 8 cm.

18. In triangle DEF, the bisector of angle D intersects side EF at point N. If DE = 36 cm, DF = 40 cm and EF = 38 cm, then the length (in cm) of NF is _____.

A. 16

B. 20

C. 18

D. 12

Answer B

Solution

By the Angle Bisector Theorem: EN / NF = DE / DF EN / NF = 36 / 40 EN = 9x NF = 10x EN + NF = 38 ⇒ 9x + 10x = 38 ⇒ x = 2 NF = 10x = 10 × 2 = 20
