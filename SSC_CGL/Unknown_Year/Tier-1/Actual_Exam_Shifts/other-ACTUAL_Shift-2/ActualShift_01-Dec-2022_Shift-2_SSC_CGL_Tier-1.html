29. Which of the following statements about the cell cycle is FALSE?

B. In M phase, the cell grows physically larger and copies organelles.

C. In S phase, replication of DNA takes place.

D. In G1 phase, the cell grows physically larger and copies organelles.

Answer B

Solution

The cell cycle consists of two phases, Interphase and M Phase. Interphase is divided into G1, S, and G2 phases. In G1, the cell grows but does not replicate DNA. S phase is when DNA replication occurs. G2 phase prepares for mitosis and protein synthesis. The FALSE statement is that in M phase, the cell grows physically larger and copies organelles.

37. Thorny bushes are found in _____ region.

B. polar

C. dry desert

D. high rainfall

Answer C

Solution
