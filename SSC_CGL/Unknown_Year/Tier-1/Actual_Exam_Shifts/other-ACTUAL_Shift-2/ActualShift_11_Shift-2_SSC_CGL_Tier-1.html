25. In a shelter, there are only hens and dogs. The total number of heads is 30, and the total number of legs is 94. How many hens are there?

A. 11

B. 12

C. 13

D. 14

Answer C

Solution

Given: Total heads = 30 and Total legs = 94 Calculation: Assume all 30 animals have at least 2 legs: 30 heads x 2 legs = 60 legs. Find the "extra" legs: The problem says there are 94 legs. So, 94 - 60 = 34 extra legs. Assign the extra legs: These 34 legs must belong to the dogs (since they have 2 more legs than hens). Each dog takes 2 extra legs. Number of dogs: 34 ÷ 2 = 17 dogs. Number of hens: 30 heads - 17 dogs = 13 hens. Thus, there are 13 hens. Hence, the correct answer is 'Option 3'.
