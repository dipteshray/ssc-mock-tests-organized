2. The least number which must be subtracted from 7278745 so as to obtain a sum that is divisible by 11 is:

A. 3

B. 1

C. 5

D. 2

Solution

3. A motorboat, whose speed in 15 km/h in still water goes 50 km downstream and comes back in a total of 7 hours 30 minutes. The speed of the stream (in km/h) is:

A. 9

B. 5

C. 11

D. 7

Answer B

Solution

Let speed of stream = x km/h. Downstream speed = (15 + x), Upstream speed = (15 - x) Total time: (50/15+x) + (50/15-x) = 7.5 LCM: 50(15 - x) + 50(15 + x) = 7.5(225 - x2) 1500 = 7.5(225 - x²) ⇒ x² = 25 ⇒ x = 5 Speed of the stream = 5 km/h.

A. 15% profit

B. 12.25% profit

C. 12.5% profit

D. 12.5% loss

Answer C

Solution

55 MP = 99 CP ⇒ MP = 9/5 CP 56 SP = 35 MP ⇒ SP = 5/8 MP SP = 5/8 × 9/5 CP = 9/8 CP, Profit% = (9/8 CP - CP)/CP × 100 = 1/8 × 100 = 12.5%

18. If a + b + c = 0, then the value of (a2 + b2 + 2ab) is equal to:

A. c2

B. -c2

C. c

D. -c

Answer A

Solution

21. A mango juice is made by mixing water and mango concentrate in the ratio 9 : 7. If x litres of water and 3x litres of mango concentrate is mixed in 160 litres of mango juice, then the new ratio becomes 13 : 14. What is the quantity of the new mango juice (in litres)?

A. 197

B. 212

C. 206

D. 216

Answer D

Solution

Initial quantities: Water = (9/16) × 160 = 90 litres, Mango concentrate = (7/16) × 160 = 70 litres New quantities: Water = 90 + x, Mango concentrate = 70 + 3x New ratio: (90 + x)/(70 + 3x) = 13/14, 14(90 + x) = 13(70 + 3x) Solving for x: 14x - 13(3x) = 910 - 1260, x = 14 Total quantity: 160 + x + 3x = 216 litres

22. Three pipes, P, Q and R, together take four hours to fill a tank. All the three pipes were opened at the same time. After three hours, P was closed, and Q and R filled the remaining tank in two hours. How many hours will P alone take to fill the tank?

A. 8

B. 10

C. 12

D. 9

Answer A

Solution

Part of the tank filled per hour by P + Q + R = 1/4 Part of the tank filled per hour by Q + R = 1/8 P + Q + R = 1/4 ⇒ 3(P + Q + R) + 2(Q + R) = 1 ⇒ 3/4 + 2(Q + R) = 1 2(Q + R) = 1 - 3/4 = 1/4 ⇒ Q + R = 1/8 P = 1/4 - 1/8 = 1/8 ⇒ P alone : 1/(1/8) = 8 hours.
