23. Husk of a coconut is made of ______ tissue.

A. Collenchyma

B. Xylem

C. Sclerenchyma

D. Parenchyma

Answer C

Solution

The coconut husk is made up of Sclerenchyma tissue. This tissue is characterized by long, tapering cells with thick, lignin-based cell walls. These cells lack protoplasm and are typically dead. Sclerenchyma tissues are permanent and provide mechanical support to the plant. In the case of the coconut husk, this tissue provides protection and support to the developing coconut.
