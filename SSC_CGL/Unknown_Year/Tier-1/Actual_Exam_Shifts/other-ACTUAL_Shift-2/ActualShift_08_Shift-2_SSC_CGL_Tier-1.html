1. If A : B = 2 : 3, B : C = 4 : 5, and C : D = 6 : 7, then find the ratio B : D.

A. 12 : 35

B. 18 : 35

C. 12 : 21

D. 24 : 35

Answer D

Solution

A : B = 2 : 3, B : C = 4 : 5, C : D = 6 : 7 ⇒ B : C = (4 × 6) : (5 × 6) = 24 : 30 ⇒ C : D = (6 × 5) : (7 × 5) = 30 : 35 ⇒ B : D = (24 : 30) × (30 : 35) = 24 : 35

2. What is the result of 3 ÷ 0.25 + 1/5 ?

A. 12.2

B. 12.5

C. 11.2

D. 11.8

Answer A

Solution

⇒ 3 ÷ 0.25 = 3 × (1 ÷ 0.25) ⇒ 3 × 4 = 12 ⇒ 12 + (1 ÷ 5) = 12 + 0.2 = 12.2

3. If 55% of a number is 1.2 more than 40% of it, what is the number?

A. 10

B. 6

C. 8

D. 12

Answer C

Solution

Let the number be x. 55% × x = 40% × x + 1.2 (55x ÷ 100) − (40x ÷ 100) = 1.2 (15x ÷ 100) = 1.2 x = (1.2 × 100) ÷ 15 x = 8

4. What should be subtracted from 12, 16, 18, 24 so that the remaining numbers may be proportional?

A. 6

B. 8

C. 0

D. 2

Answer C

Solution

Let (12 − x) ÷ (16 − x) = (18 − x) ÷ (24 − x). ⇒ (12 − x) × (24 − x) = (16 − x) × (18 − x). ⇒ 288 − 36x + x2 = 288 − 34x + x2. ⇒ −36x = −34x ⇒ x = 0. ∴ The correct answer is 0.

7. Two individuals, X and Y, rent a pasture together. X has 10 goats that they graze for 3 months. Y grazes 15 sheep for 4 months and 5 horses for 2 months. The following relationships are given: 2 goats are equivalent to 3 sheep, 1 sheep is equivalent to 2 horses. What amount of rent should X pay if the total rent is split by grazing units?

A. 9/22

B. 2/5

C. 1/4

D. 3/4

Answer A

Solution

⇒ X goats in sheep-units = 10 × 1.5 = 15 ⇒ X sheep-months = 15 × 3 = 45 ⇒ Y sheep-months from sheep = 15 × 4 = 60 ⇒ Y horses in sheep-units = 5 × 0.5 = 2.5 ⇒ Y sheep-months from horses = 2.5 × 2 = 5 ⇒ Y total = 60 + 5 = 65 ⇒ Ratio X : Y = 45 : 65 ⇒ Simplified ratio = 9 : 13 ⇒ X share = 9 ÷ (9 + 13) = 9 ÷ 22 ∴ X pays 9/22 of the total rent.

8. P, Q, and R started a business with capitals in the ratio 1:2:3. After 4 months, R withdrew 50% of his capital. After another 4 months, Q withdrew 25% of his capital. The business continued for a total of 12 months. Find the ratio of profit shares of P : Q : R.

A. 4 : 6 : 7

B. 3 : 5 : 6

C. 4 : 5 : 8

Solution

12. The Cost Price of 7 articles is equal to the Selling Price of 10 articles. The loss percent is:

A. 30%

B. 20%

C. 40%

D. 300/7 %

Answer A

Solution

Let the Selling Price of 1 article = ₹1. ⇒ Cost Price of 1 article = ₹10 ÷ 7. ⇒ Loss = ₹(10 ÷ 7) − ₹1 = ₹(10 − 7) ÷ 7 = ₹3 ÷ 7. ⇒ Loss Percent = (Loss ÷ Cost Price) × 100 = (3 ÷ 7) ÷ (10 ÷ 7) × 100 = (3 ÷ 10) × 100 = 30%.

13. Suresh ordered 6 kg of premium flour and some additional kilograms of ordinary flour. The price of the premium flour per kg was twice that of the ordinary flour. When the order was delivered, the quantities of premium and ordinary flour had been swapped. This change increased his bill by 20%. Find the ratio of the original quantity of premium flour to the original quantity of ordinary flour.

A. 2 : 3

B. 3 : 5

C. 5 : 4

D. 4 : 7

Answer D

Solution

Let the price per kg of ordinary flour = x and the original quantity of ordinary flour = y kg. Original bill = (6 × 2x) + (y × x) = 12x + yx. New bill = (y × 2x) + (6 × x) = 2xy + 6x. Given 2xy + 6x = 1.2 × (12x + yx) ⇒ 2xy + 6x = 14.4x + 1.2xy. ⇒ 0.8xy = 8.4x ⇒ y = 8.4 ÷ 0.8 ⇒ y = 10.5 kg. Ratio of premium flour to ordinary flour = 6 : 10.5 = 12 : 21 = 4 : 7.

16. A merchant marked an article such that its price was 40% above its cost price. He offered two consecutive discounts of 10% and 30% to a buyer. If he incurred a loss of 500, what was the marked price of the article?

A. 8,000

B. 9,000

C. 5932.2

D. 6,500

Answer C

Solution

Let C.P = x and M.P = 1.4 × x S.P = M.P × (1 − 0.10) × (1 − 0.30) ⇒ S.P = 1.4 × x × 0.90 × 0.70 ⇒ S.P = 0.882 × x Loss = C.P − S.P ⇒ 500 = x − 0.882 × x ⇒ 500 = 0.118 × x ⇒ x = 500 ÷ 0.118 ⇒ x = 4237.29 M.P = 1.4 × x ⇒ M.P = 1.4 × 4237.29 ⇒ M.P = 5932.2

17. A fruit vendor buys three varieties of apples. The first variety is bought at 3 for 8, the second at 4 for 9, and the third at 1 for 4. He mixes them in the ratio 1 : 2 : 1 respectively. If he sells all the apples at 5 for 13, what is his approximate gain or loss percentage?

A. Profit of 2.13%

B. Loss of 6.8%

C. Profit of 3.5%

D. Loss of 3.5%

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Take LCM of individual quantities (3, 4, 1) and selling quantity (5) = 60. Assume mix ratio 1 : 2 : 1 as 60, 120, and 60 apples respectively. Total Cost Price (CP) = (60 ÷ 3)×8 + (120 ÷ 4)×9 + (60 ÷ 1)×4 = 160 + 270 + 240 = ₹670. Total Selling Price (SP) = (240 ÷ 5) × 13 = 48 × 13 = ₹624. Loss % = [(670 − 624) ÷ 670] × 100 = (46 ÷ 670) × 100 ≈ 6.86%. ∴ The correct answer is Loss of 6.8%. Alternate Method Given: Variety 1: 3 apples for ₹8 | Variety 2: 4 apples for ₹9 | Variety 3: 1 apple for ₹4. Mixing Ratio: 1 : 2 : 1 | Selling Price: 5 apples for ₹13. Let the number of apples of each variety be 60, 120, and 60. ⇒ CP of Variety 1 = (8 ÷ 3) × 60 = ₹160. ⇒ CP of Variety 2 = (9 ÷ 4) × 120 = ₹270. ⇒ CP of Variety 3 = (4 ÷ 1) × 60 = ₹240. ⇒ Total Cost Price (CP) = 160 + 270 + 240 = ₹670. ⇒ Total Apples = 60 + 120 + 60 = 240 apples. ⇒ Total Selling Price (SP) = (13 ÷ 5) × 240 = ₹624. ⇒ Loss = CP − SP = 670 − 624 = ₹46. ⇒ Loss % = (Loss ÷ CP) × 100 = (46 ÷ 670) × 100 = 6.865...% ∴ The correct answer is Loss of 6.8%. Additional Information Weighted Average CP When multiple items with different rates are mixed, the net CP is (n1c1 + n2c2 + ...) ÷ (n1 + n2 + ...). Profit and Loss Percentage Gain % = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100 and Loss % = (Loss ÷ CP) × 100. Both are always calculated on the CP. Unitary Method for Quantities To simplify calculations involving different quantities, assume a total quantity that is the LCM of all given denominators.

18. A container holds a mixture of three liquids: A, B, and C, in the ratio of 4:3:1. 40 litres of this mixture are removed, and then 10 litres of liquid A and 15 litres of liquid B are poured into the container. If the resultant quantity of liquid B is 5 litres more than the resultant quantity of liquid A, what was the initial total quantity of the mixture in the container (in litres)?

A. 40

B. 80

C. 14

D. 20

Answer A

Solution

Let total quantity = 8k. Initial A = 4k, Initial B = 3k. A removed = (4k ÷ 8k) × 40 = 20, B removed = (3k ÷ 8k) × 40 = 15. A final = (4k − 20) + 10 = 4k − 10, B final = (3k − 15) + 15 = 3k. Given: B final = A final + 5 ⇒ 3k = (4k − 10) + 5 ⇒ 3k = 4k − 5 ⇒ k = 5. Initial mixture = 8k = 8 × 5 = 40.

22. If the width of a cuboid is increased by 20% and the length and height remain the same, what is the percentage increase in volume?

A. 10%

B. 0.4

C. 20%

D. 0.5

Answer C

Solution

Let initial width = w, length = l, height = h. New width = 1.2w. Initial Volume = l × w × h; New Volume = l × 1.2w × h = 1.2 × l × w × h. Percentage increase = [(1.2 × l × w × h − l × w × h) ÷ l × w × h] × 100 = (0.2 × l × w × h ÷ l × w × h) × 100 = 0.2 × 100 = 20%.

24. A circular table with a radius of 30 cm has a decorative cut where a chord subtends a 90° angle at the center. What percentage of the table's total area does the major segment represent? (Use π = 3.14)

A. 75.50%

B. 85.50

C. 71.50

D. 90.92

Answer D

Solution

⇒ Area of the circle = π × r2 = 3.14 × 302 = 2826 cm2 ⇒ Area of the sector = (θ ÷ 360) × π × r2 = (90 ÷ 360) × 3.14 × 302 = 706.5 cm2 ⇒ Area of the triangle = (1 ÷ 2) × r2 × sin(90°) = (1 ÷ 2) × 900 × 1 = 450 cm2 ⇒ Area of the minor segment = Area of the sector − Area of the triangle = 706.5 − 450 = 256.5 cm2 ⇒ Area of the major segment = Area of the circle − Area of the minor segment = 2826 − 256.5 = 2569.5 cm2 ⇒ Percentage of the major segment = (2569.5 ÷ 2826) × 100 = 90.9%
