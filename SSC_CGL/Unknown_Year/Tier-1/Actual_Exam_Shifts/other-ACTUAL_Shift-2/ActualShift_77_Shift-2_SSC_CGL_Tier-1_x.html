5. The ratio of expenditure to savings of a woman is 4 : 1. If her income and expenditure are increased by 20% and 10%, respectively, then find the percentage change in her savings.

A. 40%

B. 50%

C. 60%

D. 30%

Answer C

Solution

Original income = 5x, Original expenditure = 4x, Savings = x New income = 6x, New expenditure = 4.4x, New savings = 6x - 4.4x = 1.6x Percentage change in savings = ((1.6x - x)/x) × 100 = 0.6 × 100 = 60%

10. If cot2 θ - 2 cos2 θ = 0, (0° < θ < 90°) , then the value of θ is:

A. 45º

B. 60º

C. 90º

D. 30º

Answer A

Solution

cot2θ - 2 cos2θ = 0 ⇒ cot2θ = cos2θ / sin2θ ⇒ (cos2θ / sin2θ) - 2 cos2θ = 0 ⇒ cos2θ (1 / sin2θ - 2) = 0 ⇒1 / sin2θ - 2 = 0 ⇒ 1 / sin2θ = 2 ⇒ sin2θ = 1 / 2 ⇒ sinθ = 1 / √2 ⇒ θ = 45º

20. If m is even, then 8m - 1 is divisible by:

A. 63

B. 42

C. 65

D. 8

Answer A

Solution

m = 2n ⇒ 8m - 1 = 8(2n) - 1 = (8n)² - 1² = (8n - 1)(8n + 1) ⇒ 8 ≡ 1 (mod 7) ⟹ 8n - 1 ≡ 0 (mod 7) ⇒ 8 ≡ -1 (mod 9) ⟹ (-1)² = 1 ⟹ 8n - 1 ≡ 0 (mod 9) LCM(7, 9) = 63 ⟹ 8m - 1 is divisible by 63.
