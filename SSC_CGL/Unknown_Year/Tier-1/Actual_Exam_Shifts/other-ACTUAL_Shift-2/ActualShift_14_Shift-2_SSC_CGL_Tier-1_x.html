1. If p : q = 5 : 2, find (4p + 5q) : (4p − 5q).

A. 25 : 15

B. 30 : 10

C. 35 : 5

D. 45 : 5

Answer B

Solution

Given: p : q = 5 : 2 Let p = 5k and q = 2k. ⇒ (4p + 5q) : (4p − 5q) = [4(5k) + 5(2k)] : [4(5k) − 5(2k)] ⇒ (20k + 10k) : (20k − 10k) = 30k : 10k ⇒ 30 : 10

6. There are 7 consecutive integers and 5 consecutive integers. The average of the 7 integers is 3 less than the average of the 5 integers. The sum of the 5 integers is 8 more than the sum of the 7 integers. Find the average of the 7 integers.

A. 3

B. 3.2

C. 3.8

D. 3.5

Answer D

Solution

Let the average of 7 integers = x and the average of 5 integers = y. Given: x = y − 3 and 5y = 7x + 8. Substitute x = y − 3 into 5y = 7x + 8: 5y = 7(y − 3) + 8 ⇒ 5y = 7y − 21 + 8 ⇒ 5y = 7y − 13. 2y = 13 ⇒ y = 6.5. x = y − 3 = 6.5 − 3 = 3.5. ∴ The average of the 7 integers is 3.5.

10. An article sold at a 20% discount gives a profit of 25% on cost price. If the profit is ₹500, what would be the selling price if sold at 30% discount?

A. ₹2,500

B. ₹2,750

C. ₹2187.5

D. ₹3,254.5

Answer C

Solution

Let CP = ₹2000 (since 25% of CP = ₹500 ⇒ CP = 500 ÷ 0.25). SP at 25% profit = CP + Profit ⇒ SP = 2000 + 500 = ₹2500. Marked Price (MP) = SP ÷ 0.8 ⇒ MP = 2500 ÷ 0.8 = ₹3125. New SP at 30% discount = 0.7 × MP ⇒ New SP = 0.7 × 3125 = ₹2187.50.

11. A can do a work in 24 days, B in 36 days and C in 72 days. A works daily, while B and C assist every fourth day. The work will be completed on which day from the start?

A. 12th

B. 20th

C. 16th

D. 18th

Answer B

Solution

LCM of 24, 36, and 72 = 72 ⇒ Total work = 72 units. A's 1 day work = 72 ÷ 24 = 3 units, B's 1 day work = 72 ÷ 36 = 2 units, C's 1 day work = 72 ÷ 72 = 1 unit. In 4 days: A's work = 4 × 3 = 12 units, B and C's work = 2 + 1 = 3 units, Total work = 12 + 3 = 15 units. Work in 16 days (4 cycles) = 15 × 4 = 60 units, Remaining work = 72 − 60 = 12 units. A alone completes remaining work in 12 ÷ 3 = 4 days, Total days = 16 + 4 = 20 days.
