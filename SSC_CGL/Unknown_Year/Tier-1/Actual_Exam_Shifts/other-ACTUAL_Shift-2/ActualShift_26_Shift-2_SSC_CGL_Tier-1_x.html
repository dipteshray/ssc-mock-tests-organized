2. Initial capital from partners A, B, and C follows a 6 : 7 : 9 ratio. One year later, A adds 60000, B adds 50000, and C adds a certain sum. The ratio shifts to 8 : 9 : 11. What amount did partner C contribute?

A. 20000

B. 25000

C. 30000

D. 36000

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Let the initial capitals of A and B be 6x and 7x. According to the ratio shift: (6x + 60000) / (7x + 50000) = 8 / 9. Cross-multiplying: 54x + 540000 = 56x + 400000 ⇒ 2x = 140000 ⇒ x = 70000. Initial capital of C = 9 × 70000 = 630000. New capital of C = (New capital of A ÷ 8) × 11 = (480000 ÷ 8) × 11 = 660000. Contribution of C = 660000 − 630000 = 30000. ∴ The correct answer is 30000. Alternate Method Given: Initial Ratio (A : B : C) = 6 : 7 : 9 Additions: A = 60000, B = 50000, C = y New Ratio (A : B : C) = 8 : 9 : 11 Formula: New Capital = Initial Capital + Added Amount Calculations: ⇒ Let initial capitals be 6x, 7x, and 9x. ⇒ New Capital A = 6x + 60000 ⇒ New Capital B = 7x + 50000 ⇒ (6x + 60000) / (7x + 50000) = 8 / 9 ⇒ 9(6x + 60000) = 8(7x + 50000) ⇒ 54x + 540000 = 56x + 400000 ⇒ 2x = 140000 ⇒ x = 70000 ⇒ Initial Capital of C = 9 × 70000 = 630000 ⇒ New Capital of A = 6(70000) + 60000 = 480000 ⇒ New Capital of C = (New A / 8) × 11 = (480000 / 8) × 11 = 660000 ⇒ Partner C contribution (y) = 660000 − 630000 = 30000 ∴ The correct answer is 30000. Additional Information Partnership Investment Rule The ratio of profits is equal to the ratio of the product of investment and time: P1:P2 = C1×T1 : C2×T2. Ratio Multiplier Method When multiple variables are added to a ratio, use a common multiplier (x) to convert ratio parts into actual absolute values for calculation. Capital Adjustment In partnership problems, introducing or withdrawing capital changes the effective investment for the remaining period of the business.

3. A staff member donates 20% of their earnings to a fund. Of the leftover cash, 40% goes to savings. The remainder is split between utilities and leisure in a 7:5 ratio. If leisure spending is 12000, what is the staff member's full earnings?

A. 56000

B. 60000

C. 64000

D. 70000

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Let the Total Earnings be 100%. Remaining after 20% donation = 80%. Remainder after 40% savings = 60% of 80% = 48%. Leisure portion = 48% × (5 ÷ 12) = 20%. Given 20% = 12000 ⇒ 100% = 12000 × 5 = 60000. ∴ The correct answer is 60000. Alternate Method Given: Donation = 20% | Savings = 40% of leftover | Ratio (Utilities : Leisure) = 7 : 5 | Leisure = 12000. Calculations: ⇒ Let Total Earnings be E. ⇒ Leftover cash = E − 20% of E = 0.80E. ⇒ Remainder after savings = 60% of 0.80E = 0.48E. ⇒ This remainder is split in 7 : 5 ratio. Leisure share = [5 ÷ (7 + 5)] × 0.48E. ⇒ Leisure = (5 ÷ 12) × 0.48E = 0.20E. ⇒ Given 0.20E = 12000. ⇒ E = 12000 ÷ 0.20 = 60000. ∴ The correct answer is 60000. Additional Information Successive Percentage Decrease When values are reduced sequentially, the final percentage is the product of the remaining multipliers. Final % = 100 × (1 − r1) × (1 − r2). Ratio Division To find a specific share from a total T with ratio a:b, the share of b is calculated as T × [b ÷ (a + b)]. Percentage to Fraction Converting percentages to fractions (e.g., 20% = 1/5, 40% = 2/5) often simplifies multi-step arithmetic problems.

4. A value quintuples every 10 years through compound growth. How many years does it take for the value to reach twenty-five times the starting point?

A. 22

B. 20

C. 25

D. 18

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick If a value becomes n times in t years, it becomes nk times in (k × t) years under compound growth. Given: The value quintuples (5 times) in 10 years. Target: To reach 25 times, which can be written as 52. Here, n = 5, t = 10, and k = 2. ⇒ Total time required = 2 × 10 = 20 years. ∴ The correct answer is 20 years. Alternate Method Given: Growth rate: 5 times in 10 years. Target multiplier: 25 times. Let the starting value be P. As per the question, the value grows by compound growth. Value after first 10 years = 5 × P = 5P Value after next 10 years = 5 × (5P) = 25P ⇒ Total time elapsed = 10 years + 10 years ⇒ Total time = 20 years ∴ The correct answer is 20 years. Additional Information Compound Growth Property In compound growth, if a quantity increases by a fixed multiple in a fixed time interval, it follows a geometric progression (G.P.). Effective Growth Factor If the growth factor is x for time t, then for time kt, the growth factor becomes xk. Rule of 72 To find the approximate time needed to double an investment, divide 72 by the annual growth rate percentage: Time ≈ 72 ÷ Rate.

5. A tech firm produced 1600 keys for a program at a cost of 8,000,000. They gave 100 keys for free to testers. For the rest, they gave a 15% markdown on the 12000 price per key. They also gave 1 bonus key for every 9 purchased. If all 1600 keys were handed out, what is the firm's profit or loss %?

A. 15.64% profit

B. 72.125% profit

C. 73.94% loss

D. 15.64% loss

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total keys for sale = 1600 − 100 = 1500 keys. Effective paid keys = 1500 × (9 ÷ 10) = 1350 (since 1 key is free for every 9 purchased). Discounted price per key = 12000 × 0.85 = ₹10,200. Total revenue = 1350 × 10,200 = ₹1,37,70,000. Profit % = [(1.377 − 0.8) ÷ 0.8] × 100 = 72.125%. ∴ The correct answer is 72.125% profit. Alternate Method Given: Total Production = 1600 keys Total Cost Price (CP) = ₹80,00,000 Keys given to testers = 100 Marked Price (MP) = ₹12,000 per key Markdown (Discount) = 15% Bonus Scheme = Buy 9, Get 1 Free Step-by-step Calculation: ⇒ Net Selling Price (SP) per key after 15% discount = 12000 − (15% of 12000) ⇒ SP per key = 12000 − 1800 = ₹10,200 ⇒ Keys remaining for customers = 1600 − 100 = 1500 keys ⇒ Under the Buy 9 Get 1 Free scheme, customers pay for 9 out of every 10 keys. ⇒ Number of keys sold (paid for) = 1500 × (9 ÷ 10) = 1350 keys ⇒ Total Revenue = 1350 × 10200 = ₹1,37,70,000 ⇒ Profit = Total Revenue − Total CP ⇒ Profit = 1,37,70,000 − 80,00,000 = ₹57,70,000 ⇒ Profit % = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100 ⇒ Profit % = (57,70,000 ÷ 80,00,000) × 100 = 72.125% ∴ The correct answer is 72.125% profit. Additional Information Profit and Loss Percentage Profit % is always calculated on the Cost Price (CP) unless stated otherwise. Formula: Profit % = (Profit ÷ CP) × 100. Buy X Get Y Free Discount The effective discount in such schemes is calculated as [Y ÷ (X + Y)] × 100. For 'Buy 9 Get 1 Free', the free item discount is 10%. Markdown and Net Selling Price Markdown is the reduction from the Marked Price (MP). Net SP = MP × (100 − Discount%) ÷ 100.

6. The velocity of two racers is 5 : 3. If the slower racer has a 48 km lead and travels at 24 km/h, how many km must the faster racer cover to catch them?

A. 80 km

B. 96 km

C. 120 km

D. 144 km

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Since the time taken to catch is the same for both, the ratio of speed is directly proportional to the ratio of distance. Ratio of Distance (Faster : Slower) = 5 : 3 Difference in distance units = 5 − 3 = 2 units Given initial lead (difference) = 48 km 2 units = 48 km ⇒ 1 unit = 24 km Distance covered by faster racer = 5 units = 5 × 24 = 120 km ∴ The correct answer is 120 km. Alternate Method Given: Ratio of velocities (VF : VS) = 5 : 3 Velocity of slower racer (VS) = 24 km/h Initial lead = 48 km Formula used: Speed = Distance ÷ Time Relative Speed (Same direction) = V1 − V2 Calculations: ⇒ Velocity of faster racer (VF) = (5 ÷ 3) × 24 = 40 km/h ⇒ Relative speed = VF − VS = 40 − 24 = 16 km/h ⇒ Time taken to catch the slower racer = Lead ÷ Relative speed ⇒ Time = 48 ÷ 16 = 3 hours ⇒ Total distance covered by the faster racer = VF × Time ⇒ Distance = 40 × 3 = 120 km ∴ The correct answer is 120 km. Additional Information Relative Speed in Same Direction When two objects move in the same direction, their relative speed is the difference of their individual speeds: Vrel = |V1 − V2|. Catch-up Time Formula The time required for a faster object to catch a slower one is: Time = Initial Gap ÷ Relative Speed. Proportionality in Motion When time is constant, the distance covered is directly proportional to the speed (D1/D2 = S1/S2).

7. A trader borrowed 15000 from a lender on April 1st at 9% simple interest. They settled the debt on September 1st. What was the total repayment?

A. 15550.00

B. 15562.50

C. 15569.58

D. 15580.00

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Time duration from April 1st to September 1st is exactly 5 months. Monthly interest rate = 9% ÷ 12 = 0.75%. Total interest percentage for 5 months = 0.75% × 5 = 3.75%. Interest value = 3.75% of 15000 = ₹562.50. Total Repayment = 15000 + 562.50 = ₹15562.50. ∴ The correct answer is ₹15562.50. Alternate Method Given: Principal (P) = ₹15000 Rate (R) = 9% per annum Time (T) = 5 months (from April 1st to September 1st) Formula Used: Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 Total Amount (A) = P + SI Calculations: ⇒ T = 5 ÷ 12 years ⇒ Simple Interest (SI) = (15000 × 9 × 5) ÷ (100 × 12) ⇒ SI = (150 × 45) ÷ 12 ⇒ SI = 6750 ÷ 12 = ₹562.50 ⇒ Total Repayment (Amount) = 15000 + 562.50 ⇒ Amount = ₹15562.50 ∴ The correct answer is ₹15562.50. Additional Information Simple Interest vs Compound Interest Simple interest is calculated only on the principal amount, whereas compound interest is calculated on the principal plus any accumulated interest from previous periods. Time Conversion Rules Always convert time into years for the standard formula. For days, use T ÷ 365; for weeks, use T ÷ 52; and for months, use T ÷ 12. Effective Annual Rate The effective rate of interest depends on the frequency of compounding. For simple interest, the nominal rate and effective rate remain the same regardless of the payment schedule.

8. Which of these belongs to both the Integer and Whole number categories?

A. -8

B. 0

C. 7.2

D. 3/4

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick The set of Whole Numbers (W) starts from 0 and includes all positive counting numbers. The set of Integers (Z) includes all Whole Numbers along with their negative counterparts. Since the Whole Number set is a subset of the Integer set (W ⊆ Z), any number that is a Whole Number is also an Integer. Among the options, only 0 is a Whole Number. ∴ The correct answer is 0. Alternate Method Given: Options −8, 0, 7.2, and 3/4. Definitions: Integers (Z): {..., −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...} Whole Numbers (W): {0, 1, 2, 3, ...} ⇒ Option (1) −8: It is an integer, but not a whole number because whole numbers cannot be negative. ⇒ Option (2) 0: It is the starting whole number and it is also part of the integer set. ⇒ Option (3) 7.2: It is a decimal, so it is neither an integer nor a whole number. ⇒ Option (4) 3/4: It is a fraction, so it is neither an integer nor a whole number. ⇒ Therefore, 0 is the only value belonging to both categories. ∴ The correct answer is 0. Additional Information Natural Numbers Counting numbers starting from 1 (1, 2, 3...). Zero is not a Natural Number. Rational Numbers Numbers that can be expressed as p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0. All integers are rational numbers. Number Hierarchy Natural Numbers ⊆ Whole Numbers ⊆ Integers ⊆ Rational Numbers ⊆ Real Numbers.

Solution

11. An L-shaped room consists of two quadrants (12 m x 8 m and 16 m x 12 m). Carpeting is 450 per m2, but 10% off is given for areas over 70 m2. What is the final bill?

A. 122400

B. 116640

C. 116960

D. 118160

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total Area of the L-shaped room = (12 × 8) + (16 × 12) = 96 + 192 = 288 m2. Since the area 288 m2 is greater than 70 m2, a 10% discount is applicable. Effective rate = 450 − 10% of 450 = ₹405 per m2. Final Bill = Total Area × Effective Rate = 288 × 405 = ₹1,16,640. ∴ The correct answer is ₹116640. Alternate Method Given: Dimensions of Quadrant 1 = 12 m × 8 m Dimensions of Quadrant 2 = 16 m × 12 m Standard cost of carpeting = ₹450 per m2 Discount = 10% (for area > 70 m2) Formula Used: Total Area = Area1 + Area2 Final Bill = Total Area × Rate × (100 − Discount)% Calculations: ⇒ Area of the first section = 12 × 8 = 96 m2 ⇒ Area of the second section = 16 × 12 = 192 m2 ⇒ Total Area of the L-shaped room = 96 + 192 = 288 m2 ⇒ Since 288 > 70, the discount is applicable. ⇒ Discount amount per m2 = 10% of 450 = (10/100) × 450 = ₹45 ⇒ Discounted Rate = 450 − 45 = ₹405 per m2 ⇒ Final Bill = 288 × 405 ⇒ Final Bill = 288 × (400 + 5) = 115200 + 1440 = ₹1,16,640 ∴ The correct answer is ₹116640. Additional Information Composite Area Calculation For L-shaped or irregular polygons, divide the shape into smaller rectangles and sum their individual areas: Total Area = Σ(lengthi × widthi). Net Price After Discount The net price after a discount is calculated as Net Price = Original Price × (100 − d%)/100, where d is the discount percentage. Unit Conversion for Area Always ensure units are consistent. For example, 1 m2 = 10,000 cm2 and 1 hectare = 10,000 m2.

12. A geometric pattern is formed by placing 6 hexagons around a central point. What is the ratio of the total pattern area to a single hexagon?

A. 5:1

B. 6:1

C. 7:1

D. 8:1

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick A regular hexagon has an internal angle of 120°. To perfectly surround a central point or a central hexagon, exactly 6 hexagons are required (6 × 60° symmetry or 3 × 120° vertex meeting). In a standard hexagonal tiling (honeycomb), 1 central hexagon is enclosed by 6 neighboring hexagons. Total hexagons in the pattern = 1 (Center) + 6 (Surround) = 7 hexagons. Ratio = (Area of 7 hexagons) : (Area of 1 hexagon) = 7 : 1. ∴ The correct answer is 7:1. Alternate Method Given: Number of hexagons placed around a central point = 6 Total hexagons in the complete pattern = 1 (central) + 6 (surrounding) = 7 Formula Used: Area of a regular hexagon = (3√3 / 2) × s2 Calculations: ⇒ Let the area of a single hexagon be A. ⇒ The total pattern area comprises the central hexagon (H0) and 6 surrounding hexagons (H1 to H6). ⇒ Total Pattern Area = Area of H0 + Area of (H1 + H2 + H3 + H4 + H5 + H6) ⇒ Total Pattern Area = A + (6 × A) = 7A ⇒ Required Ratio = Total Pattern Area : Single Hexagon Area ⇒ Required Ratio = 7A : A ⇒ Required Ratio = 7 : 1 ∴ The correct answer is 7:1. Additional Information Hexagonal Tiling A regular hexagon is one of only three regular polygons (alongside the equilateral triangle and the square) that can perfectly tile a 2D plane without gaps. Internal Angle Property The internal angle of a regular hexagon is 120°. At any vertex in a tiling, 3 hexagons meet to sum up to 360° (3 × 120° = 360°). Geometric Efficiency The hexagonal pattern (often seen in honeycombs) provides the maximum area for the minimum perimeter among all possible tilings.

13. A triangular warning plate has sides of 26 m, 28 m, and 30 m. If coloring the plate costs 5 per m2, what is the bill for the whole surface?

A. 1680

B. 1520

C. 1440

D. 1920

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Calculate the semi-perimeter (s): (26 + 28 + 30) ÷ 2 = 42 m. Apply Heron's Formula for Area: √[42 × (42−26) × (42−28) × (42−30)] = √[42 × 16 × 14 × 12]. Simplify: √[14 × 3 × 16 × 14 × 3 × 4] = 14 × 3 × 4 × 2 = 336 m2. Total Bill = 336 × 5 = ₹1680. ∴ The correct answer is ₹1680. Alternate Method Given: Sides of the triangular plate are a = 26 m, b = 28 m, and c = 30 m. Cost of coloring is ₹5 per m2. Formula Used: Heron's Formula for Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)], where s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2. ⇒ Semi-perimeter (s) = (26 + 28 + 30) ÷ 2 = 84 ÷ 2 = 42 m. ⇒ Area = √[42 × (42 − 26) × (42 − 28) × (42 − 30)] ⇒ Area = √[42 × 16 × 14 × 12] ⇒ Area = √[14 × 3 × 16 × 14 × 3 × 4] ⇒ Area = √[142 × 32 × 42 × 22] ⇒ Area = 14 × 3 × 4 × 2 = 336 m2. ⇒ Total bill = Area × Cost per m2 ⇒ Total bill = 336 × 5 = ₹1680. ∴ The correct answer is ₹1680. Additional Information Heron's Formula Used to find the area of any scalene triangle when all three sides are known. Area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]. Equilateral Triangle Area The area of an equilateral triangle with side 'a' is given by (√3 ÷ 4) × a2. Right-Angled Triangle Area The area is calculated as (1 ÷ 2) × Base × Height. Pythagorean Triplets If sides follow a2 + b2 = c2, the triangle is right-angled, simplifying area calculation significantly.

Solution

15. The footprint of a tower is expanded by 25%, with the elevation unchanged. By what percentage does the tower's volume grow?

A. 10%

B. 20%

C. 25%

D. 30%

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick The Volume (V) of a tower or prism-like structure is directly proportional to its Footprint (Area, A) and Elevation (Height, h). Relation: V ∝ A × h Since the Elevation remains unchanged (constant), the percentage growth in Volume is exactly equal to the percentage growth in the Footprint. % Change in Volume = % Change in Footprint = 25% ∴ The correct answer is 25%. Alternate Method Given: Increase in Footprint (Base Area) = 25%; Elevation (Height) = Constant (0% change). Formula Used: Volume (V) = Base Area (A) × Height (h) ⇒ Let the initial Footprint be A1 and Elevation be h. ⇒ Initial Volume (V1) = A1 × h ⇒ New Footprint (A2) = A1 + 25% of A1 = 1.25A1 ⇒ New Volume (V2) = A2 × h = 1.25A1 × h ⇒ V2 = 1.25 × (A1 × h) = 1.25V1 ⇒ Growth Percentage = [(V2 − V1) / V1] × 100 ⇒ Growth Percentage = [(1.25V1 − V1) / V1] × 100 = 0.25 × 100 = 25% ∴ The correct answer is 25%. Additional Information Direct Proportion of Volume For any solid where Volume = Area × Height, if the height is constant, the volume is directly proportional to the base area. Successive Percentage Change If both Area increases by x% and Height increases by y%, the net increase in Volume is [x + y + (x × y)/100]%. Dimensional Scaling Rule If every linear dimension (length, width, height) of an object is increased by k times, then the Area increases by k2 times and Volume increases by k3 times.

16. A boy and a girl are filling a pool. The boy adds 8 liters every 3 minutes; the girl adds 6 liters every 4 minutes. How long to reach 200 liters?

A. 38 min

B. 40 min

C. 44 min

D. 48 min

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Find the LCM of time intervals (3 min and 4 min) = 12 minutes. In 12 minutes, the boy fills 8 × (12 ÷ 3) = 32 liters. In 12 minutes, the girl fills 6 × (12 ÷ 4) = 18 liters. Total filled in 12 minutes = 32 + 18 = 50 liters. Time for 200 liters = (200 ÷ 50) × 12 = 48 minutes. ∴ The correct answer is 48 min. Alternate Method Given: Boy's filling rate = 8 liters / 3 min Girl's filling rate = 6 liters / 4 min Target volume = 200 liters Formula: Total Time = Total Volume ÷ Combined Filling Rate ⇒ Rate of Boy = 8/3 liters/min ⇒ Rate of Girl = 6/4 = 3/2 liters/min ⇒ Combined Rate = (8/3 + 3/2) liters/min ⇒ Combined Rate = (16 + 9) ÷ 6 = 25/6 liters/min ⇒ Time required = 200 ÷ (25/6) ⇒ Time required = 200 × (6/25) ⇒ Time required = 8 × 6 = 48 minutes ∴ The correct answer is 48 min. Additional Information Work and Time Relationship Total Work = Efficiency × Time. If multiple people work together, their individual efficiencies (rates) are added. Combined Efficiency If person A does work in T1 and B in T2, their combined rate is 1/T1 + 1/T2. For rates given as Volume/Time, simply sum the fractions. LCM Method for Work Commonly used to avoid fractions by assuming Total Work as the Least Common Multiple of the time periods taken by individuals.

17. Three integers have a 2:3:5 ratio. If their mean is 200, what is the gap between the largest and smallest?

A. 200

B. 180

C. 160

D. 140

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total ratio parts = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10. Mean of ratio parts = 10 ÷ 3 units. Given Mean 10/3 units = 200 ⇒ 1 unit = (200 × 3) ÷ 10 = 60. Difference between Largest (5) and Smallest (2) = 3 units. Required Gap = 3 × 60 = 180. ∴ The correct answer is 180. Alternate Method Given: Ratio of three integers = 2 : 3 : 5 Mean of the integers = 200 Formula Used: Total Sum = Mean × Number of terms Calculations: ⇒ Let the numbers be 2x, 3x, and 5x. ⇒ Number of integers = 3. ⇒ Total Sum = 200 × 3 = 600. ⇒ 2x + 3x + 5x = 600. ⇒ 10x = 600 ⇒ x = 60. ⇒ Smallest integer = 2 × 60 = 120. ⇒ Largest integer = 5 × 60 = 300. ⇒ Gap = 300 − 120 = 180. ∴ The correct answer is 180. Additional Information Arithmetic Mean and Sum The sum of n numbers is always equal to the Mean × n. For ratio-based problems, sum the parts and equate them to the total value. Ratio Scaling Multiplying or dividing each term of a ratio by the same non-zero constant does not change the ratio (a:b:c = kx:ky:kz). Range/Gap in Ratios The difference between the largest and smallest terms is found by (Max Ratio − Min Ratio) × (Single Unit Value).

B. (6, 8)

C. (8, 8)

D. (4, 4)

Solution

25. A 10-foot pole casts an 8-foot shadow. Simultaneously, a tower casts a 40-foot shadow. How tall is the tower?

A. 40 feet

B. 50 feet

C. 60 feet

D. 80 feet

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick The ratio of height to shadow remains constant for all objects at the same time. Shadow of tower is 5 times the shadow of the pole (40 ÷ 8 = 5). Height of tower = 5 × Height of pole = 5 × 10 = 50 feet. ∴ The correct answer is 50 feet. Alternate Method Given: Height of the pole (Hp) = 10 feet Shadow of the pole (Sp) = 8 feet Shadow of the tower (St) = 40 feet Formula: For similar triangles formed by the sun's rays: Height1 / Shadow1 = Height2 / Shadow2 Calculations: ⇒ Hp / Sp = Ht / St ⇒ 10 / 8 = Ht / 40 ⇒ Ht = (10 ÷ 8) × 40 ⇒ Ht = 1.25 × 40 ⇒ Ht = 50 feet ∴ The correct answer is 50 feet. Additional Information Concept of Similarity Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal, meaning the ratio of their corresponding sides is the same. Angle of Elevation At any given moment, the angle of elevation of the sun is the same for all objects in the same vicinity, creating proportional shadows. Trigonometric Relation The relationship can also be expressed as tan θ = Height / Shadow, where θ is the sun's angle.
