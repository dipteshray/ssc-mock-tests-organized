1. Select the most appropriate ANTONYM of the underlined word. Their sporting achievements at the highest level are being utterly ignored.

A. Extremely

B. Correctly

C. Minimally

D. Totally

Solution

The correct answer is: Minimally. "Utterly" means completely or totally, whereas "Minimally" refers to a very small degree. The meanings of the other words are: Extremely: To a very great degree; Correctly: In a way that is accurate; Totally: Completely.

C. OPQRS

D. SRPQO

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: RPQOS. The sentence follows the standard Subject-Verb-Object structure. 12. Select the option that correctly expresses the given sentence in passive voice. The shareholders will elect him director.

A. He will be elected director by the shareholders.

B. The shareholders have elected him a director.

C. The shareholders elected him a director.

D. He will be elected a director.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: He will be elected director by the shareholders. The Active voice in the question is in Future Indefinite tense (Subject + will + V1 + Object + Complement). Thus, the Passive voice will also be in Future Indefinite (Object of the active voice (Subjective case) + will be + V3 + Complement + by + Subject of the active voice (Objective case)).

14. 4 In the given question, a statement divided into different segments is given. Rearrange all the segments to form a coherent statement. O: its first constitution P: and soon got himself elected Q: the administration of the country R: general Ayub Khan took over S: after Pakistan framed

A. SORQP

B. SPORQ

C. POSRQ

D. ROPQS

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: A. SORQP. The sentence begins with the temporal context (S) "after Pakistan framed" followed by the object (O) "its first constitution". This sets the stage for the main action (R) "general Ayub Khan took over" the subject of the action (Q) "the administration of the country", and concludes with the subsequent event (P) "and soon got himself elected". The complete coherent sentence is: "After Pakistan framed its first constitution, general Ayub Khan took over the administration of the country and soon got himself elected."

18. Select the option that expresses the following sentence in active voice. The letter will be delivered by the postman tomorrow.

A. The postman delivered the letter tomorrow

B. The postman will have delivered the letter tomorrow

C. The postman will deliver the letter tomorrow

D. The postman will be delivering the letter tomorrow

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: The postman will deliver the letter tomorrow. The sentence is in the Passive voice of the Future Indefinite tense (Object + will be + V3 + by + Subject). To convert it into Active voice, the structure used is (Subject + will + V1 + Object). Thus, "The postman" becomes the subject, followed by "will deliver" and the object "the letter".
