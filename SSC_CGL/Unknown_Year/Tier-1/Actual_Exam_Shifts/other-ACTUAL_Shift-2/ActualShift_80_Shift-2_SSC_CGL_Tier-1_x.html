15. Select the option that expresses the following sentence in passive voice. Gill scored 3 centuries in Tata IPL 2023

A. 3 centuries has been scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023.

B. 3 centuries were scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023.

C. 3 centuries had been scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023.

D. 3 centuries were being scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: "3 centuries were scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "Gill scored 3 centuries in Tata IPL 2023."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "3 centuries" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "scored" to "were scored" (past simple passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "Gill."

• Ensure the time phrase "in Tata IPL 2023" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "3 centuries has been scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023." – Incorrect verb form (singular instead of plural).

• Option 3: "3 centuries had been scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023." – Incorrect tense (past perfect passive).

• Option 4: "3 centuries were being scored by Gill in Tata IPL 2023." – Incorrect aspect (past continuous passive).
