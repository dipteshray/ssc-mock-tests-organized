6. Three years ago, the age of a person was six times the age of his younger brother. Eight years later, the age of the person will be three years less than twice the age of his younger brother. Find the present ages of the person and his younger brother.

A. 15 years and 5 years

B. 5 years and 15 years

C. 12 years and 4 years

D. 4 years and 12 years

Answer A

Solution

Let the present age of the person be P years and the present age of his younger brother be B years. P - 3 = 6(B - 3) ⇒ P = 6B - 15 P + 8 = 2(B + 8) - 3 ⇒ P = 2B + 5 6B - 15 = 2B + 5 ⇒ 4B = 20 ⇒ B = 5 P = 6(5) - 15 ⇒ P = 15 The present ages of the person and his younger brother are 15 years and 5 years, respectively.

13. The value of the e×pression (1 - sin(2t)/1 + sin(2t)) × (cos(t) + sin(t)/cos(t) - sin(t)) is

A. (1 - 2 (t)/1 + 2 (t))

B. (1 - (t)/1 + (t))

C. (1 + 2 (t)/1 - 2 (t))

D. (1 + (t)/1 - (t))

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Put t = 30° to quickly evaluate the expression and eliminate options. Expression = [(1 − sin(60°)) / (1 + sin(60°))] × [(cos(30°) + sin(30°)) / (cos(30°) − sin(30°))] Expression = [(1 − √3/2) / (1 + √3/2)] × [(√3/2 + 1/2) / (√3/2 − 1/2)] Expression = (7 − 4√3) × (2 + √3) = 2 − √3. Checking given options for t = 30°, Option 2 gives: (1 − tan(30°)) / (1 + tan(30°)) = (1 − 1/√3) / (1 + 1/√3) = (√3 − 1) / (√3 + 1) = 2 − √3. ∴ The correct answer is (1 − tan(t)) / (1 + tan(t)). Alternate Method Given: Expression = [(1 − sin(2t)) / (1 + sin(2t))] × [(cos(t) + sin(t)) / (cos(t) − sin(t))] Formula Used: 1 = sin2(t) + cos2(t) sin(2t) = 2sin(t)cos(t) (a − b)2 = a2 + b2 − 2ab Calculations: ⇒ 1 − sin(2t) = cos2(t) + sin2(t) − 2sin(t)cos(t) = (cos(t) − sin(t))2 ⇒ 1 + sin(2t) = cos2(t) + sin2(t) + 2sin(t)cos(t) = (cos(t) + sin(t))2 Substitute these values into the given expression: ⇒ Expression = [(cos(t) − sin(t))2 / (cos(t) + sin(t))2] × [(cos(t) + sin(t)) / (cos(t) − sin(t))] Cancel out the common terms from numerator and denominator: ⇒ Expression = (cos(t) − sin(t)) / (cos(t) + sin(t)) Divide numerator and denominator by cos(t): ⇒ Expression = [1 − (sin(t) / cos(t))] / [1 + (sin(t) / cos(t))] ⇒ Expression = (1 − tan(t)) / (1 + tan(t)) ∴ The correct answer is (1 − tan(t)) / (1 + tan(t)). Additional Information Double Angle Formulas sin(2θ) = 2sin(θ)cos(θ) and cos(2θ) = cos2(θ) − sin2(θ). Tangent Addition Formula tan(A − B) = (tan(A) − tan(B)) / (1 + tan(A)tan(B)). Applying this for A = 45° gives tan(45° − θ) = (1 − tan(θ)) / (1 + tan(θ)). Pythagorean Identities sin2(θ) + cos2(θ) = 1, which is commonly used to convert 1 ± sin(2θ) into perfect square expressions like (cos(θ) ± sin(θ))2.

18. A thief is spotted by a policeman from a distance of 200 metres. When the policeman starts the chase, the thief also starts running. If the speed of the thief is 9 km/h and that of the policeman 10 km/h, how far will the thief have to run before he is overtaken?

A. 1.2 km

B. 1.4 km

C. 1.8 km

D. 1.6 km

Answer C

Solution

Relative speed = 10 km/h - 9 km/h = 1 km/h Distance between thief and policeman = 200 meters = 0.2 km Time to catch thief = 0.2 km / 1 km/h = 0.2 hours Distance run by thief = 9 km/h × 0.2 hours = 1.8 km

Solution
