19. Kala-azar disease is caused by ______.

A. fungi

B. viruses

C. bacteria

D. protozoa

Answer D

Solution

Kala-azar, also known as Dumdum fever, is caused by the parasitic protozoan Leishmania donovani, which belongs to the class Mastigophora or flagellata. The disease is transmitted through the bite of an infected sand fly (Phlebotomus). Leishmania donovani exhibits dimorphic and digenetic characteristics. Kala-azar can manifest in three forms: Cutaneous Leishmaniasis (skin ulcers), Mucocutaneous Leishmaniasis (ulcers in the mouth, throat, and nose, nosebleeds, breathing difficulties), and Visceral Leishmaniasis (weakness, weight loss, appetite loss, enlarged spleen, liver, lymph nodes, and decreased blood cell production).
