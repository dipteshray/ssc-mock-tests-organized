1. A seller sold three paintings, D, E, and F, with their selling prices in the ratio 2:3:5. He made a profit of 20% on item D, suffered a loss of 10% on item E, and made a profit of 25% on item F. What was his approximate percentage gain or loss in the overall deal?

A. 10% gain

B. 11.11% gain

C. 9.5% loss

D. 12.0% gain

Answer B

Solution

Let the ratio be 200 : 300 : 500. ⇒ CP of D = 200 ÷ 1.20 = 166.67, CP of E = 300 ÷ 0.90 = 333.33, CP of F = 500 ÷ 1.25 = 400. ⇒ Total SP = 200 + 300 + 500 = 1000, Total CP = 166.67 + 333.33 + 400 = 900. ⇒ Gain = 1000 − 900 = 100, Gain% = (100 ÷ 900) × 100 = 11.11%.

2. A 60-liter solution contains alcohol and water in the ratio 7:5. If 12 liters of this solution are removed and replaced with 6 liters of pure water, what is the new ratio of alcohol to water in the final solution?

A. 14 : 13

B. 7 : 5

C. 8 : 5

D. 3 : 8

Answer A

Solution

⇒ Total ratio = 7 + 5 = 12 ⇒ Alcohol = (7 ÷ 12) × 60 = 35 L, Water = (5 ÷ 12) × 60 = 25 L ⇒ Removed alcohol = (12 ÷ 60) × 35 = 7 L, Removed water = (12 ÷ 60) × 25 = 5 L ⇒ Remaining alcohol = 35 − 7 = 28 L, Remaining water = 25 − 5 + 6 = 26 L ⇒ Final ratio = 28 : 26 = 14 : 13

3. Worker K takes 18 hours to assemble a device, and Worker L takes 9 hours. When Worker M assists them, they complete the assembly in just 3 hours. How long would it take for M to complete the job alone?

A. 7 hours

B. 8 hours

C. 6 hours

D. 5 hours

Answer C

Solution

Let total work = 18 units (LCM of 18, 9, and 3). K's rate = 18 ÷ 18 = 1 unit/hour, L's rate = 18 ÷ 9 = 2 units/hour. (K + L + M)'s rate = 18 ÷ 3 = 6 units/hour ⇒ M's rate = 6 − (1 + 2) = 3 units/hour. Time taken by M alone = 18 ÷ 3 = 6 hours.

4. Worker X paints a fence in 10 days while Worker Y can strip the paint in 5 days. X has worked for 5 days and then Y joined to work with X for another 1 day only. In how many days will X alone finish painting the remaining fence?

A. 5 days

B. 6 days

C. 7 days

D. 8 days

Answer B

Solution

⇒ LCM of 10 and 5 = 10 units (total work = 10 units) ⇒ X's rate = 10 ÷ 10 = 1 unit/day, Y's rate = −(10 ÷ 5) = −2 units/day ⇒ Work done: X (5 days) = 5 × 1 = 5 units, X + Y (1 day) = 1 − 2 = −1 unit ⇒ Total work done = 5 − 1 = 4 units, Remaining work = 10 − 4 = 6 units ⇒ Time for X alone = 6 ÷ 1 = 6 days

5. A submarine travels a specific distance at a speed of 180 knots over the course of 8 hours. To cover the same distance in just 3 hours, it needs to travel at a speed of:

A. 540 knots

B. 480 knots

C. 400 knots

D. 450 knots

Answer B

Solution

Distance = 180 × 8 ⇒ Distance = 1440 knots-hour Speed = Distance ÷ Time ⇒ Speed = 1440 ÷ 3 Speed = 480 knots

6. A cyclist travels from his house to the park. If he travels at a speed of 20 km/h, he arrives 15 minutes late. If he travels at a speed of 30 km/h, he arrives 15 minutes early. What is the distance (in km) between the house and the park?

A. 25 km

B. 30 km

C. 35 km

D. 40 km

Answer B

Solution

Let distance = D km Time difference = 15 min + 15 min = 30 min = 0.5 hours (D ÷ 20) − (D ÷ 30) = 0.5 D × ((3 − 2) ÷ 60) = 0.5 D = 0.5 × 60 D = 30

13. Ken and Lisa started a business with 80,000 and 120,000 respectively. After 3 months, Ken added 40,000 more. After 9 months (from the start), Lisa withdrew 60,000. What is the profit-sharing ratio at the end of 1 year?

A. 22:21

B. 21:22

C. 11:10

D. 10:11

Answer A

Solution

Ken's investment: (80,000 × 3) + (120,000 × 9) = 240,000 + 1,080,000 = 1,320,000 Lisa's investment: (120,000 × 9) + (60,000 × 3) = 1,080,000 + 180,000 = 1,260,000 Profit-sharing ratio: 1,320,000 : 1,260,000 ⇒ 132 : 126 ⇒ 22 : 21

15. C invests 50,000, while D invests 70,000. D receives an additional 10% of the total profit for administration. The total profit is 80,000. What is D's share?

A. 45,000

B. 48,000

C. 50,000

D. 52,000

Answer C

Solution

⇒ Admin = 10% × 80,000 = 0.1 × 80,000 = 8,000 ⇒ Remaining = 80,000 − 8,000 = 72,000 ⇒ Share(D) = (70,000 ÷ 120,000) × 72,000 = (7 ÷ 12) × 72,000 = 7 × 6,000 = 42,000 ⇒ Total share(D) = 42,000 + 8,000 = 50,000

16. A marathon runner's average speed for a certain number of races is 15 km/h. If in the next race they average 25 km/h, the overall average speed becomes 16 km/h. How many races did they run earlier?

A. 9

D. 12

Answer A

Solution

17. In an election, 90% of eligible voters cast their votes. Of these votes, 10% were declared invalid. If a losing candidate received 12,960 votes, which accounted for 40% of the total valid votes, what was the total number of eligible voters enrolled?

A. 38,000

B. 40,000

C. 42,000

D. 45,000

Answer B

Solution

Let E = total eligible voters Cast votes = 90% × E = 0.9 × E Valid votes = 0.9 × E × (1 − 10%) = 0.9 × E × 0.9 = 0.81 × E 0.4 × Valid votes = 12,960 ⇒ Valid votes = 12,960 ÷ 0.4 = 32,400 0.81 × E = 32,400 ⇒ E = 32,400 ÷ 0.81 = 40,000

18. The average of 11 numbers is 80. The average of the first four numbers is 70. The average of the next four numbers is 20 % lower than the average of the first four numbers. The 9th number is 1 less than the 11th number, and the 10th number is 2 greater than the 11th number. What is the average of the 9th and 10th numbers?

A. 125.5

B. 126

C. 125

D. 124.5

Answer A

Solution

⇒ Total sum = 11 × 80 = 880 ⇒ Sum of first 4 = 4 × 70 = 280 ⇒ Average of next 4 = 70 − (20 ÷ 100) × 70 = 56 ⇒ Sum of next 4 = 4 × 56 = 224 ⇒ Sum of first 8 = 280 + 224 = 504 ⇒ Let x = 11th number ⇒ 9th + 10th + 11th = (x − 1) + (x + 2) + x = 3x + 1 ⇒ 504 + (3x + 1) = 880 ⇒ 3x + 505 = 880 ⇒ 3x = 375 ⇒ x = 375 ÷ 3 = 125 ⇒ 9th = 125 − 1 = 124, 10th = 125 + 2 = 127 ⇒ Average of 9th and 10th = (124 + 127) ÷ 2 = 125.5

19. A grocer bought 20 kg of wheat type P at 50 per kg and 30 kg of wheat type Q at 80 per kg. He mixed them and sold the entire mixture at 70 per kg. Calculate his total profit or loss.

A. Profit of 100

B. Loss of 100

C. Profit of 50

D. No profit/loss

Answer A

Solution

Total cost price = (20 × 50) + (30 × 80) = ₹1000 + ₹2400 = ₹3400 Total quantity = 20 + 30 = 50 kg Total selling price = 50 × 70 = ₹3500 Profit = ₹3500 − ₹3400 = ₹100

20. The compound interest on a sum at 20 % p.a. for 112 years, compounded yearly, is 6,600. Find the principal.

A. 20,625

B. 20,000

C. 21,000

D. 19,800

Answer A

Solution

Let P = principal Amount after 1 year = P × (1 + 20 ÷ 100) = P × 1.2 Interest for next 0.5 year = P × 1.2 × (20 ÷ 100) × 0.5 = 0.12P Total amount = 1.2P + 0.12P = 1.32P Compound interest = 1.32P − P = 0.32P 0.32P = 6600 ⇒ P = 6600 ÷ 0.32 = 20625 ∴ The correct answer is 20,625.

21. If the compound interest on a certain sum at 10% per annum for 3 years is 3,310, find the simple interest on the same sum at the same rate and for the same period.

A. 3,000

B. 3,100

C. 3,250

D. 3,300

Answer A

Solution

Let P = principal ⇒ Amount = P × (1 + 10 ÷ 100)3 = P × 1.331 ⇒ Compound interest = Amount − P = 1.331P − P = 0.331P ⇒ 0.331P = 3310 ⇒ P = 3310 ÷ 0.331 = 10000 ⇒ Simple interest = P × (10 ÷ 100) × 3 = 10000 × 0.1 × 3 = 3000

22. A manufacturer's base cost is P. The distributor applies a 25% markup. They offer a 10% trade discount to the store. The store applies a 60% markup on its cost and offers a flat 25% discount to customers on the printed price. If a customer finally pays 10,800, what is the original base cost (P)?

A. 8,000

B. 7,500

C. 9,000

D. 10,000

Answer A

Solution

Let P = 100. ⇒ Distributor price = 100 × 1.25 = 125. ⇒ Store cost = 125 × 0.90 = 112.5. ⇒ Marked price = 112.5 × 1.60 = 180. ⇒ Final price = 180 × 0.75 = 135. ⇒ Ratio = 10,800 ÷ 135 = 80. ⇒ Actual base cost = 100 × 80 = 8,000.

25. A circular path surrounds a fountain. The outer radius is 5 meters and the inner radius is 3 meters. What percentage of the full outer circle's area is the path?

A. 56%

B. 60%

C. 64%

D. 72%

Answer C

Solution

Outer circle area ⇒ π × 52 = π × 25 Inner circle area ⇒ π × 32 = π × 9 Path area ⇒ π × (25 − 9) = π × 16 Percentage of path area ⇒ (π × 16 ÷ π × 25) × 100 = (16 ÷ 25) × 100 = 64%
