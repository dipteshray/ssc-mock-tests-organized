17. A sentence is provided in direct speech. From the four given options, choose the one that most accurately conveys the sentence in its corresponding indirect speech. He said, "I saw him last week."

A. He said that he had seen him the previous week.

B. He said that he has seen him last week.

C. He said that he was seeing him last week.

D. He said that he had saw him the previous week.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Option 1. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 1. Correct Sentence: He said that he had seen him the previous week. Additional Information

• Direct Speech: He said, "I saw him last week."

• Rule for Conversion: When changing from direct to indirect speech: The past simple tense (saw) changes to past perfect tense (had seen).

• Time expressions also change: "last week" → "the previous week"

• Quotation marks are removed and the conjunction that is added after the reporting verb.

• Converted Sentence: He said that he had seen him the previous week.

• Option 2: He said that he has seen him last week. Incorrect because the reporting verb said is in the past, so the tense inside the reported speech should be in past perfect, not present perfect.

• Also, "last week" should be changed to "the previous week."

• Option 3: He said that he was seeing him last week. Incorrect because the tense changes the meaning — it implies continuous action instead of a completed past action.

• Option 4: He said that he had saw him the previous week. Grammatically incorrect because "had saw" is wrong; the correct past perfect form is "had seen."
