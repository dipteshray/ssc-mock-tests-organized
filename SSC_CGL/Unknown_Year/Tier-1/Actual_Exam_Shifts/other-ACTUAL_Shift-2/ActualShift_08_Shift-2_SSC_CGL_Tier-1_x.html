• 7 % 3 → 7 × (7 − 3) = 7 × 4 = 28

• 5 % 1 → 5 × (5 − 1) = 5 × 4 = 20

20. A 40 kg alloy contains Gold:Copper = 7:3. How much Copper should be added to make Gold:Copper = 2:3?

A. 25 kg

B. 35 kg

C. 40 kg

D. 30 kg

Answer D

Solution

Gold = 40 × 7 ÷ 10 = 28 kg Copper = 40 × 3 ÷ 10 = 12 kg Let added Copper = x kg Final ratio: ⇒ 28 : (12 + x) = 2 : 3 ⇒ 28 × 3 = 2 × (12 + x) ⇒ 84 = 24 + 2x ⇒ 84 − 24 = 2x ⇒ 60 = 2x ⇒ x = 30 kg
