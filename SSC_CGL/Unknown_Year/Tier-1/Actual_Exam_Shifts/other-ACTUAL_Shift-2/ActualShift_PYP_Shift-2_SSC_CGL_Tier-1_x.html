Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 4. Correct Sentence: He has taught trigonometry for donkey's years. Additional Information

• Active voice structure: The subject of the passive voice sentence becomes the object of the active voice sentence.

• In the passive voice, the sentence is: "Trigonometry has been taught by him for donkey's years.."

• To convert to active voice: Place the subject "He" at the beginning.

• Change the passive verb form "has been taught" to "has taught" (present perfect).

• Remove the preposition "by" to make it grammatically correct.

• Ensure the time phrase "for donkey's years" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "He had been teaching trigonometry for donkey's years." – Incorrect tense (past perfect continuous).

• Option 2: "He has been trigonometry teaching for donkey's years." – Incorrect word order.

• Option 3: "He is teaching trigonometry for donkey's years." – Incorrect tense (present continuous).
