1. After the fall of the Guptas, different kingdoms emerged in various parts of India. Among them, the Maukharis emerged in which of the following areas?

A. Thanesar

B. Kunnur

C. Valabhi

D. Kannauj

Answer D

Solution

After the fall of the Gupta Empire, the Maukharis emerged as a significant dynasty in northern India, establishing their rule independently. Initially vassals of the Guptas, they later made Kannauj their capital, turning it into a major political, economic, and cultural center. The Maukharis ruled over regions that include parts of present-day Uttar Pradesh and Bihar. Notable rulers include Harivarman and Adityavarman.

3. Presently, which of the following countries is NOT a beneficiary of India's Duty-Free Tariff Preference (DFTP) Scheme?

A. Rwanda

B. Burundi

C. Myanmar

D. Angola

Answer D

Solution

The Duty-Free Tariff Preference (DFTP) Scheme, launched by the Government of India in 2008, aims to provide zero-duty market access to products originating from Least Developed Countries (LDCs). The scheme enhances South-South cooperation and supports the development of LDCs by covering around 98% of tariff lines. Beneficiaries include countries like Rwanda, Burundi, and Myanmar, while Angola is not currently a beneficiary. LDCs, recognized by the UN for their low income, weak human assets, and economic vulnerability, benefit from international support measures to overcome challenges, eradicate poverty, and achieve sustainable development. As of October 2023, there are 46 LDCs.

6. What kind of phyllotaxy was examined in the Guava plant?

A. Superimposed

B. Whorled

C. Alternate

D. Opposite

Answer D

Solution

In opposite phyllotaxy, two leaves emerge from opposite sides of the stem at the same level, typically in a crisscross pattern with each pair perpendicular to the next. This arrangement maximizes light exposure for each leaf, aiding in photosynthesis. Opposite phyllotaxy is common in plants such as guava (Psidium guajava), calotropis, and jamun. Phyllotaxy refers to the arrangement of leaves on a stem, with primary types including alternate, opposite, and whorled.

16. A small object is placed on the focus on the left side of a convex lens. Where will the image be formed?

A. At the centre on the right side of the lens.

B. At infinity on the left side of the lens.

C. At infinity on the right side of the lens.

D. At the focus on the right side of the lens.

Answer C

Solution

When an object is placed at the focus of a convex lens, the refracted rays become parallel, forming the image at infinity on the opposite side of the lens. This image is highly enlarged and real. A convex lens, thicker at the center, converges parallel light rays to a point known as the focus. Convex lenses are used in magnifying glasses, cameras, and corrective lenses.

23. Exfoliation is a form of ______.

A. physical weathering

B. chemical weathering

C. mass wasting

D. biochemical weathering

Answer A

Solution

Exfoliation is a type of physical weathering involving the peeling away of rock layers due to temperature fluctuations that cause expansion and contraction. This process leads to the separation of outer layers and is commonly observed in large rock formations like mountainous regions and granite domes.

24. Which Bill was passed by the Parliament, in July 2021, to help in the availability of working capital for the micro, small and medium enterprises sector?

A. The Essential Commodities (Amendment) Bill, 2020

B. The Minerals Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2020

C. The Factoring Regulation (Amendment) Bill, 2021

D. The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Amendment) Bill, 2021

Answer C

Solution

The Factoring Regulation (Amendment) Bill, 2021 was passed to address the challenges faced by micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) in accessing working capital. Factoring involves selling accounts receivable to a third party at a discount for immediate funds. The amendment expands the entities that can undertake factoring business, increasing fund availability to MSMEs. The bill aims to simplify operational constraints in the factoring business, making it more accessible and affordable. This initiative is crucial for the financial stability and growth of MSMEs, ensuring they have the necessary working capital to sustain and expand their operations.

25. Identify the structural formula for magnesium hydroxide.

A. MgOH

B. MgO2

C. Mg2H2

D. Mg(OH)2

Answer D

Solution

Magnesium hydroxide (Mg(OH)2) is composed of one magnesium (Mg) atom and two hydroxide (OH) groups. It is naturally occurring as the mineral brucite and is used in medicine as an antacid and laxative. Magnesium (Mg), with atomic number 12, is abundant in the Earth's crust and crucial for biological processes.
