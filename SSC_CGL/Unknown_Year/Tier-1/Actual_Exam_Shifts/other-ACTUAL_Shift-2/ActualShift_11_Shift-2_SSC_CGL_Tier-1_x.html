3. If a : b = 7 : 5, find (8a + 4b) : (8a − 4b).

A. 13 : 1

B. 17 : 4

C. 11 : 1

D. 19 : 9

Answer D

Solution

Given a : b = 7 : 5, let a = 7x and b = 5x. 8a + 4b = 8×7x + 4×5x = 56x + 20x = 76x. 8a − 4b = 8×7x − 4×5x = 56x − 20x = 36x. ⇒ (8a + 4b) : (8a − 4b) = 76x : 36x = 19 : 9.

6. There are 6 consecutive even numbers and 5 consecutive odd numbers. The average of the even numbers is 8 more than the average of the odd numbers. If the sum of the even numbers exceeds the sum of the odd numbers by 76, find the average of the odd numbers.

A. 19

B. 28

C. 23

D. 25

Answer B

Solution

Let the average of odd numbers = x. Average of even numbers = x + 8. Sum of odd numbers = 5 × x = 5x. Sum of even numbers = 6 × (x + 8) = 6x + 48. (6x + 48) − 5x = 76 ⇒ x + 48 = 76 ⇒ x = 28. ∴ The average of the odd numbers is 28.

8. Ravi borrowed 24,000 at simple interest from two lenders at 8% and 5% per annum for 5 years. Total interest paid was 8,400. If interchanging the principals reduced interest by 1,200, how much was borrowed at 8%?

A. 16000

B. 10000

C. 12000

D. 14000

Answer A

Solution

Let amount borrowed at 8% = x. Interest at 8% = (x × 8 × 5) ÷ 100 = 0.4x. Interest at 5% = ((24,000 − x) × 5 × 5) ÷ 100 = 6,000 − 0.25x. Total interest: 0.4x + 6,000 − 0.25x = 8,400 ⇒ 0.15x + 6,000 = 8,400 ⇒ 0.15x = 2,400. ⇒ x = 16,000. ∴ Ravi borrowed ₹16,000 at 8%.

9. A trader sells an article at price P incurring 10% loss. He later marks it at 1.25P and offers 20% discount. Find his gain or loss percent.

A. 10% Profit

B. 6.25% Profit

C. 10% Loss

D. 6.25% Loss

Answer C

Solution

Let Cost Price = x ⇒ P = 0.9x ⇒ x = P ÷ 0.9 New Selling Price: ⇒ SP = 1.25P × 0.8 = P Gain/Loss: ⇒ Gain/Loss = P − (P ÷ 0.9) = −(P ÷ 9) Loss %: ⇒ Loss % = [(P ÷ 9) ÷ (P ÷ 0.9)] × 100 = 10% ∴ The trader incurs a loss of 10%.

15. A tower subtends angles of elevation 45° and 30° from two points in the same line, 20 m apart. Find the height of the tower.

A. 10

B. 10(√3 + 1)

C. 20

D. 20(√3 + 1)

Answer B

Solution

Let height of tower = h and distance of nearer point from tower = x. From nearer point: tan 45º = h ÷ x ⇒ h = x. From farther point: tan 30º = h ÷ (x + 20) ⇒ 1 ÷ √3 = x ÷ (x + 20). x + 20 = √3x ⇒ x(√3 − 1) = 20 ⇒ x = 20 ÷ (√3 − 1) ⇒ x = 10(√3 + 1). ∴ Height h = 10(√3 + 1) m.
