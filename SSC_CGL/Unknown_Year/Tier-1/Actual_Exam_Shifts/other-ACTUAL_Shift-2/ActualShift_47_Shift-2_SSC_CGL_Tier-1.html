3. Parts of a sentence are given below in jumbled order. Arrange the parts in the correct order to form a meaningful sentence. (O) as it was the last one of the evening (P) the city bus was overcrowded (Q) and this gave an opportunity (R) to the pickpockets (S) to try their skill

A. SRPOQ

B. PSORQ

C. POQRS

D. SROPQ

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: POQRS. The sentence begins with the subject and main clause (P) "the city bus was overcrowded," followed by the reason (O) "as it was the last one of the evening." This situation leads to the consequence (Q) "and this gave an opportunity," which is then specified by (R) "to the pickpockets" and (S) "to try their skill." The logical flow is: The bus was crowded (P) because it was the last one (O), which provided a chance (Q) for pickpockets (R) to steal (S).

6. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. The meal was not prepared by us.

A. We had not prepare the meal.

B. We did not prepare the meal.

C. We did not preparing the meal.

D. We did prepare the meal.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is: We did not prepare the meal. The conversion is from Passive to Active. In the passive voice, the subject receives the action (The meal was not prepared by us), whereas in the active voice, the subject performs the action (We did not prepare the meal).

8. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. The homework must be done by you.

A. You should have done the homework.

B. You do the homework.

C. You will do the homework.

D. You must do the homework.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is: You must do the homework. The conversion is Passive to Active. Explanation: In the given passive voice sentence, "The homework must be done by you," the object "homework" is being acted upon. When converting to active voice, "you" becomes the subject performing the action, and "must do" indicates the necessity of the action.
