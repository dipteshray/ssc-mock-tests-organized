17. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. The children have made the box.

A. The box is being made by the children.

B. Children had made the box.

C. The box had been made by the children.

D. The box has been made by the children.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 4. Correct Sentence: "The box has been made by the children." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "The children have made the box."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "the box" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "have made" to "has been made" (present perfect passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "the children."

• Ensure that the new sentence makes sense and retains the original meaning.

• Option 1: "The box is being made by the children." – This is present continuous passive, not present perfect passive.

• Option 2: "Children had made the box." – This is not in passive voice; it's past perfect active.

• Option 3: "The box had been made by the children." – This is past perfect passive, not present perfect passive.

19. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. They are organizing a concert for charity.

A. A concert is being organized by them for charity.

B. They are organizing a charity concert.

C. They are being organized a concert for charity.

D. A concert for charity is organizing by them.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: Option 1. The conversion is from Active to Passive voice. Explanation: The Active voice in this sentence is in Present Continuous tense (Subject + is/are + V4 + Object). Thus, the Passive voice will also be in Present Continuous tense (Object of the active voice (Subjective case) + is/are + being + V3 + by + Subject of the active voice (Objective case)). Here, "They" (Subject in Active) becomes "by them," and "a concert for charity" (Object in Active) becomes the Subject in Passive.
