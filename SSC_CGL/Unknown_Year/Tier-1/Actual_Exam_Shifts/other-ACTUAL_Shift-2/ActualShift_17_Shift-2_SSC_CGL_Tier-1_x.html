23. A sentence is given in direct speech. Choose the correct indirect form:She said, “I have completed the assignment.”

A. She said that she completed the assignment.

B. She said that she had completed the assignment.

C. She said that she has completed the assignment.

D. She said that she was completing the assignment.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: She said that she had completed the assignment. Additional Information

• Indirect Speech: She said that she had completed the assignment.

• Key Conversions: Present perfect tense ("have completed") becomes past perfect tense ("had completed"). When converting from direct to indirect speech, the tense is shifted back in time if the reporting verb is in the past tense.

• Pronoun "I" becomes "she". Pronouns in reported speech are adjusted based on the subject of the reporting verb.

• Reporting verb: Direct: She said, "I have completed the assignment."

• Indirect: She said that she had completed the assignment.

• Punctuation and Format: The quotation marks are removed in indirect speech.

• The conjunction "that" is added to connect the reporting clause and the reported clause.

• Option 1: She said that she completed the assignment. Incorrect tense. The past simple tense "completed" does not match the present perfect tense in the original sentence.

• Option 3: She said that she has completed the assignment. Incorrect tense. "Has completed" is present perfect tense and does not match the shift to past perfect tense required in reported speech.

• Option 4: She said that she was completing the assignment. Incorrect tense and meaning. "Was completing" (past continuous) does not match "have completed" (present perfect).
