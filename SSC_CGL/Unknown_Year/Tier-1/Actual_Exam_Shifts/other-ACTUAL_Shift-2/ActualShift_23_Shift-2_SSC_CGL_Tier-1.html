1. Consider the following statement and choose the option that follows. Statement: A technology firm has launched a wellness mobile application to help employees manage work-related stress. Conclusions: I. The firm is taking steps to improve employee mental health. II. Mobile applications are more effective than face-to-face counseling for stress. ?

A. Only I follows

B. Only II follows

C. Both follow

D. Neither follows

Answer A

Solution

Statement: A technology firm has launched a wellness mobile application to help employees manage work-related stress. Conclusion I: The firm is taking steps to improve employee mental health → Follows (The statement explicitly mentions the firm launched a wellness app specifically to "help employees manage work-related stress." Stress management is a direct component of mental health, so this is a logical inference). Conclusion II: Mobile applications are more effective than face-to-face counselling for stress → Does not follow (The statement only mentions that the firm has launched an app. It provides no data or comparison regarding the effectiveness of the app versus other methods like face-to-face counselling). Only I follows. Hence, the correct answer is “Option 1”.

3. Choose the two mathematical signs that should be interchanged to make the following equation correct. 60 ÷ 6 + 12 - 4 × 3 = 10

A. ÷ and -

B. + and -

C. ÷ and ×

D. - and ×

Answer B

Solution

Given equation: 60 ÷ 6 + 12 - 4 × 3 = 10 1) Interchanging ÷ and - New equation: 60 - 6 + 12 ÷ 4 × 3 = 60 - 6 + 3 × 3 = 60 + 9 - 6 = 69 - 6 = 63 ≠ 10 (Incorrect) 2) Interchanging + and - New equation: 60 ÷ 6 - 12 + 4 × 3 = 10 - 12 + 12 = 10 + 12 - 12 = 22 - 12 = 10 10 = 10 (Correct) 3) Interchanging ÷ and × New equation: 60 × 6 + 12 - 4 ÷ 3 = 360 + 12 - 1.3333 = 372 - 1.3333 = 370.6667 ≠ 10 (Incorrect) 4) Interchanging - and × New equation: 60 ÷ 6 + 12 × 4 - 3 = 10 + 48 - 3 = 58 - 3 = 55 ≠ 10 (Incorrect) Hence, the correct answer is "Option 2".

8. Read the following statement carefully and identify the action that follows. Statement: Commuters are reporting a high frequency of lost luggage at the main metropolitan transit hub. A. Only 1 follows

B. Only 2 follows

C. Both follow

D. Neither follows

Answer A

Solution

Statement: Commuters are reporting a high frequency of lost luggage at the main metropolitan transit hub. Course of Action: 1. Increase security surveillance and staff presence at luggage points → True (This is a direct and proportional response to the problem. By increasing monitoring (CCTV) and physical staff, the hub can deter theft, assist commuters who have lost items, and identify flaws in the luggage handling process). 2. Suspend all transit services at the hub indefinitely → False (This is an extreme and disproportionate measure. Suspending an entire metropolitan transit hub due to luggage issues would cause massive public inconvenience and economic disruption that far outweighs the original problem. A solution should fix the issue, not destroy the service) Only 1 follows. Hence, the correct answer is “Option 1”.

22. A group of 30 people are sitting in a circle. If each person shakes hands with exactly 2 neighbors, how many handshakes occur in total?

A. 30

B. 45

C. 60

D. 90

Answer A

Solution

Each of the 30 people performs 2 handshakes. If we sum these up, we get 30 × 2 = 60. However, each handshake involves two people, meaning every handshake has been counted twice in the sum above. Therefore, The total number of unique handshakes is: (30 × 2/2) = 30 There are 30 handshakes in total. Hence, the correct answer is “Option 1”.
