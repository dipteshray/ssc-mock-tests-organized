12. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. They are building a new hospital in the city.

A. A new hospital was built in the city by them.

B. A new hospital has been built in the city by them.

C. A new hospital will be built in the city by them.

D. A new hospital is being built in the city by them.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 4. Correct Sentence: "A new hospital is being built in the city by them." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "They are building a new hospital in the city."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "a new hospital" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "are building" to "is being built" (present continuous passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "them."

• Ensure the location phrase "in the city" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "A new hospital was built in the city by them." – Incorrect tense (past simple passive).

• Option 2: "A new hospital has been built in the city by them." – Incorrect tense (present perfect passive).

• Option 3: "A new hospital will be built in the city by them." – Incorrect tense (future simple passive).
