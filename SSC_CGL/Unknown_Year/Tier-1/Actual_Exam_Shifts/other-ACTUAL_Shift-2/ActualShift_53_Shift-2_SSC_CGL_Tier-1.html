1. What will come in the place of ‘?’ in the following equation, if ‘÷’ and ‘–‘ are interchanged and ‘×’ and ‘+’ are interchanged? 8 + 6 ÷ 51 – 17 × 13 = ?

A. 32

B. 58

C. 63

D. 71

Answer B

Solution

8 + 6 ÷ 51 – 17 × 13 = ? ⇒ 8 × 6 - 51 ÷ 17 + 13 = ? ⇒ 8 × 6 - 51 ÷ 17 + 13 = ? ⇒ 8 × 6 - 3 + 13 = ? ⇒ 48 + 13 - 3 = ? ⇒ 61 - 3 = ? ⇒ 58 = ?

11. Three statements are followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. You have to consider these statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts. Decide which of the given conclusions logically follow/s from the given statements. Statements: All pens are pages. Some pages are pins. Some pins are boards. Conclusions: (I) Some pins are pens. (II) Some boards are pages. (III) Some pens are boards.

A. Only conclusion I follows.

B. None of the conclusions follow.

C. Only conclusion II follows.

D. Either conclusion I or conclusion III follows.

Answer B

Solution

Conclusions: (I) Some pins are pens→ Does not follow (As all pens are pages and some pages are pins. So it is possible but not definite.) (II) Some boards are pages → Does not follow (As some pages are pins and some pins are boards. So it is possible but not definite.) (III) Some pens are boards → Does not follow (As all pens are pages, some pages are pins and some pins are boards. So it is possible but not definite.) ∴ Here, None of the conclusions follow.

16. Two different positions of the same dice are given below. Which is the number on the face opposite the face containing 1?

A. 6

B. 5

C. 4

D. 3

Answer A

Solution

For find the opposite surface of '1', we need to compare the common surface in the given dices, Now, From the given Dice, → 6 is the common surface in both Dice, and 2, 3, 4,and 5 are the adjacent surfaces, so the remaining surface '1' is the opposite surface of '6'. Here, '6' is opposite to the face containing '1'. Hence, the correct answer is "Option 1".
