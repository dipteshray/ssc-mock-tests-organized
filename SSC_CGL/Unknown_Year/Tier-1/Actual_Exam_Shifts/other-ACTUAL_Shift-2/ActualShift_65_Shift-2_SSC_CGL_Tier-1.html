10. After offering two successive discounts a toy with a marked price of ₹150 is sold at ₹105. If the value of the first discount is 12.5%, what is the value of the second discount?

A. 21%

B. 18%

C. 20%

D. 22%

Answer C

Solution

Sale price after first discount = Marked price × (1 - First discount) Sale price after second discount = Sale price after first discount × (1 - Second discount) First discount = 12.5% = 12.5/100 = 0.125 Sale price after first discount = ₹150 × (1 - 0.125) ⇒ ₹131.25 Now, let the second discount be x. Then: Sale price after second discount = ₹131.25 × (1 - x) So, ₹131.25 × (1 - x) = ₹105 ⇒ 1 - x = 105 / 131.25 ⇒ x = 0.2 The value of the second discount is 0.2 (or 20%).

D. 21

Answer A

Solution
