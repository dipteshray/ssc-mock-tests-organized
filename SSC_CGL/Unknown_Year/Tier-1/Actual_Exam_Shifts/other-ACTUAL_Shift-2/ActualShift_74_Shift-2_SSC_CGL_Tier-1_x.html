• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "a reward" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "gave" to "was given" (past simple passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "the governor."

• Ensure the phrase "for his contribution" remains for clarity.

• Option 1: "For his contribution, he was rewarded by the governor." – This is a passive voice sentence.

• Option 2: "The governor rewarded him." – This is a correct active voice sentence but less detailed.
