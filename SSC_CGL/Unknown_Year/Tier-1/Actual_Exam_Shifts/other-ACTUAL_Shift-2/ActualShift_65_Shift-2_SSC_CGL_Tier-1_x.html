3. Which of the following statements are true based on the 4NH3 (g)+5O2 (g)→4NO(g)+6H2O(g)? (a) N gets oxidized. (b) O gets oxidized. (c) N gets reduced. (d) O gets reduced.

A. (a) and (d)

B. (a) and (b)

C. (c) and (d)

D. (b) and (c)

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is (a) and (d). Key Points Additional Information

• In the given reaction, nitrogen (N) in NH3 goes from -3 oxidation state to +2 oxidation state in NO, which indicates oxidation (loss of electrons).

• Oxygen (O) in O2 goes from 0 oxidation state to -2 oxidation state in H2O, which indicates reduction (gain of electrons).

• Since nitrogen is being oxidized and oxygen is being reduced, statements (a) and (d) are true.

• The other statements (b) and (c) are incorrect as they contradict the changes in oxidation states observed in the reaction.

• Oxidation: Oxidation is the process where an atom, ion, or molecule loses electrons, leading to an increase in its oxidation state.

• In the given reaction, nitrogen in NH3 (oxidation state -3) is oxidized to NO (oxidation state +2).

• Reduction: Reduction is the process where an atom, ion, or molecule gains electrons, leading to a decrease in its oxidation state.

• In the given reaction, oxygen in O2 (oxidation state 0) is reduced to H2O (oxidation state -2).

• Oxidation State: The oxidation state, or oxidation number, is a measure of the degree of oxidation of an atom in a substance.

• It is a hypothetical charge that an atom would have if all bonds to atoms of different elements were 100% ionic.

• Redox Reaction: A redox (reduction-oxidation) reaction is a type of chemical reaction that involves a transfer of electrons between two species.

• It consists of two half-reactions: oxidation (loss of electrons) and reduction (gain of electrons).

9. How was the growth in all food grains after the Green Revolution?

A. Unitary

B. Proportionate

C. Unpredictable

D. Disproportionate

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Disproportionate. Key Points Additional Information

• The Green Revolution led to a significant increase in the production of food grains, but the growth was not uniform across all types of grains.

• While crops like wheat and rice saw substantial yield improvements due to the introduction of high-yielding varieties (HYVs), other grains like pulses and coarse cereals did not benefit as much.

• The growth in food grain production was therefore termed as disproportionate because it heavily favored certain crops over others.

• This uneven growth also led to regional disparities, with states like Punjab and Haryana benefiting more compared to other regions.

• Green Revolution: The Green Revolution refers to a period during the 1960s and 1970s when agriculture in many developing countries was transformed by the introduction of high-yielding varieties of grains, particularly wheat and rice.

• This revolution was spearheaded by the use of chemical fertilizers, pesticides, and improved irrigation techniques.

• Norman Borlaug, an American agronomist, is often called the "Father of the Green Revolution" for his work in developing high-yielding crop varieties.

• The Green Revolution is credited with preventing famines in countries like India and significantly increasing food security.

• High-Yielding Varieties (HYVs): These are strains of crops that have been scientifically bred to produce more grain per acre compared to traditional varieties.

• HYVs often require specific conditions such as high levels of chemical fertilizers and controlled irrigation.

• In India, the introduction of HYVs of wheat and rice during the Green Revolution led to a substantial increase in production.

• Impact on Food Security: The Green Revolution played a key role in making countries like India self-sufficient in food grains.

• It helped in reducing the reliance on food imports and improved the overall food security of the nation.

• However, the focus on a few high-yielding crops also led to a reduction in the cultivation of traditional crops, affecting the agricultural biodiversity.

• Regional Disparities: The benefits of the Green Revolution were not evenly distributed across all regions.

• States with better irrigation facilities and infrastructure, like Punjab and Haryana, saw greater gains compared to states with less developed agricultural systems.

• This led to economic and developmental disparities among different regions within the country.

B. The Epidemic Diseases Act, 1895

C. The Epidemic Diseases Act, 1890

D. The Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is The Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897. Key Points Additional Information

• The Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897 was enacted to provide for better prevention of the spread of dangerous epidemic diseases.

• The Act gives special powers to state and central governments to take measures necessary to prevent the outbreak or spread of an epidemic.

• The Epidemic Diseases (Amendment) Ordinance, 2020 was introduced to include protections for healthcare personnel combatting epidemic diseases.

• The Ordinance also provides for stricter penalties for acts of violence against healthcare service personnel and damage to property during epidemics.

• Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897 The Act was passed by the British colonial government in India in 1897 in response to the bubonic plague outbreak in Mumbai (then Bombay).

• The Act allows the central and state governments to take special measures and prescribe regulations to prevent the spread of epidemic diseases.

• The Act also provides the legal framework for the implementation of containment measures such as quarantine, isolation, and restrictions on the movement of people.

• The Act has been invoked several times in India, including during the outbreaks of H1N1 influenza (swine flu), dengue, and COVID-19.

• The Epidemic Diseases (Amendment) Ordinance, 2020 The Ordinance was promulgated by the President of India on April 22, 2020, in response to the COVID-19 pandemic.

• It amends the Epidemic Diseases Act, 1897 to include protections for healthcare service personnel combating epidemic diseases.

• The Ordinance defines "healthcare service personnel" to include healthcare professionals, nurses, paramedical workers, and any other person empowered under the Act to take measures to prevent the outbreak or spread of an epidemic disease.

• It provides for imprisonment and fines for acts of violence against healthcare service personnel and damage to property during epidemics.

• Other Important Terminologies Quarantine: A restriction on the movement of people and goods intended to prevent the spread of disease or pests.

• Isolation: The separation of people who are infected with a contagious disease from those who are healthy to prevent the spread of the disease.

• Containment Measures: Strategies and actions taken to prevent the spread of a disease within a community, including quarantine, isolation, and restrictions on movement.

13. Filtration of waste products in humans happens in the kidneys. There is a large number of filtration units present inside the kidneys to help them in doing this job. These filtration units are called:

A. Bowman's capsules

B. nephrons

C. capillaries

D. alveoli

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is nephrons. Key Points Additional Information

• Nephrons are the basic structural and functional units of the kidney.

• Each kidney contains around 1 to 1.5 million nephrons.

• Nephrons are responsible for the removal of waste products, excess substances, and toxins from the blood.

• They play a crucial role in the regulation of water, electrolytes, and maintaining the body's acid-base balance.

• Bowman's Capsule The Bowman's capsule is a cup-like sac at the beginning of the nephron in the kidney.

• It encases the glomerulus and is involved in the first step of the filtration of blood to form urine.

• It captures the filtrate produced from the blood and passes it into the renal tubule.

• Capillaries Capillaries are the smallest blood vessels in the body.

• They facilitate the exchange of oxygen, carbon dioxide, nutrients, and waste products between blood and tissues.

• In the kidneys, capillaries are part of the glomerulus and play a critical role in the filtration process.

• Alveoli Alveoli are small air sacs in the lungs where gas exchange takes place.

• Oxygen from the inhaled air passes through the walls of the alveoli and into the blood in the capillaries, while carbon dioxide from the blood passes into the alveoli to be exhaled.

• They are crucial for efficient respiratory function but are not related to kidney function.

14. Which number is called Avogadro's constant, named after the 19th century scientist Amedeo Avogadro?

B. 6.020 × 1020

C. 6.032 × 1019

D. 6.012 × 1021

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is 6.022 × 1023. Key Points Additional Information

• Avogadro's constant, also known as Avogadro's number, is named after the Italian scientist Amedeo Avogadro.

• It is defined as the number of constituent particles (usually atoms or molecules) that are contained in one mole of a substance.

• The value of Avogadro's constant is 6.022 × 1023 mol-1.

• Avogadro's constant is a fundamental constant in chemistry and physics that allows chemists to count the number of atoms, ions, or molecules in a given sample.

• Avogadro's Law: Avogadro's law states that equal volumes of all gases, at the same temperature and pressure, have the same number of molecules.

• This law provides the relationship between the volume of a gas and the amount of substance of the gas present.

• The Mole Concept: The mole is a unit of measurement for the amount of substance in the International System of Units (SI).

• One mole contains exactly 6.02214076 × 1023 elementary entities (Avogadro's constant).

• This concept is used to convert between the number of particles and the amount of substance.

• Importance in Chemistry: Avogadro's constant is crucial for understanding and working with chemical reactions, as it allows chemists to determine the number of particles involved.

• It is essential for calculations involving the ideal gas law, stoichiometry, and other chemical principles.

• Historical Context: Amedeo Avogadro (1776-1856) was an Italian scientist who made significant contributions to molecular theory.

• Avogadro's hypothesis, proposed in 1811, led to the eventual development of the concept of the mole and Avogadro's constant.
