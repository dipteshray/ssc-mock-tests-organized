2. Two candidates contested in an election. 80% of the voters cast their votes, out of which 5% of the polled votes were invalid. The winning candidate got 70% of valid votes and won by a majority of 36,480 votes. Find the total number of voters.

A. 1,15,000

B. 1,20,000

C. 1,25,000

D. 1,18,000

Answer B

Solution

Let the total number of voters be N. Votes cast = 0.8N Invalid votes = 5% of 0.8N = 0.05 × 0.8N = 0.04N Valid votes = 0.8N - 0.04N = 0.76N Votes for the winning candidate = 70% of valid votes = 0.7 × 0.76N = 0.532N Votes for the other candidate = 30% of valid votes = 0.3 × 0.76N = 0.228N The majority = Votes for the winning candidate - Votes for the other candidate ⇒ 0.532N - 0.228N = 36,480 ⇒ N = (36,480/0.304) ⇒ N = 1,20,000

7. A cistern can be filled by two pipes in 8 minutes and 10 minutes, separately. Both the pipes are opened together for a certain time, but due to an obstruction, only 5/8 of the full quantity of water flowed through the former pipe and 3/5 through the latter pipe. However, the obstruction was suddenly removed, and the cistern was filled in 3 minutes from that moment. How long did it take before the full flow began?

A. 3 (1/16) minutes

B. 2 (6/17) minutes

C. 9 (6/7) minutes

D. 2 (30/37) minutes

Answer B

Solution

LCM of 8 and 10 = 40 (Total work is taken as 40 units of work). Efficiency of Pipe A = 40/8 = 5 units/min Efficiency of Pipe B = 40/10 = 4 units/min Pipe A works at 5/8 efficiency: (5 × 5/8) = 25/8 units/min Pipe B works at 3/5 efficiency: (4 × 3/5) = 12/5 units/min Combined efficiency = (25/8 + 12/5) units/min = 221/40 units/min Let time before full flow begins = t minutes. Work done during obstruction = t × (221/40) units Efficiency after obstruction removed: 5 + 4 = 9 units/min Work done in 3 minutes = 9 × 3 = 27 units t × (221/40) + 27 = 40 t × (221/40) = 40 - 27 = 13 t = 13 × (40/221) = 520/221 = 2 (6/17) minutes

Solution

(p + q)2 = p2 + q2 + 2pq Step 1: Square the expressions for a and b: a2 = (xcosθ + ysinθ)2, b2 = (xsinθ - ycosθ)2 Step 2: Expand a2: a2 = (xcosθ)2 + (ysinθ)2 + 2(xcosθ)(ysinθ) a2 = x2cos2θ + y2sin2θ + 2xycosθsinθ Step 3: Expand b2: b2 = (xsinθ)2 + (ycosθ)2 - 2(xsinθ)(ycosθ) b2 = x2sin2θ + y2cos2θ - 2xycosθsinθ Step 4: Add a2 and b2: a2 + b2 = [x2cos2θ + y2sin2θ + 2xycosθsinθ] + [x2sin2θ + y2cos2θ - 2xycosθsinθ] Combine like terms: a2 + b2 = x2(cos2θ + sin2θ) + y2(sin2θ + cos2θ) Note that cos2θ + sin2θ = 1: a2 + b2 = x2(1) + y2(1) a2 + b2 = x2 + y2

12. Find the least value of x for which 57x716 is divisible by 9.

A. 1

B. 9

C. 0

D. 8

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is a multiple of 9. Sum of digits = 5 + 7 + x + 7 + 1 + 6 = 26 + x. The nearest multiple of 9 greater than 26 is 27. 26 + x = 27 ⇒ x = 1. ∴ The correct answer is 1. Alternate Method Given: The number is 57x716, which is completely divisible by 9. Concept Used: For a number to be perfectly divisible by 9, the sum of all its digits must be a multiple of 9. Calculations: ⇒ Sum of the digits of 57x716 = 5 + 7 + x + 7 + 1 + 6 ⇒ Sum = 26 + x ⇒ Possible values for (26 + x) that are multiples of 9 are 27, 36, 45, etc. ⇒ Since x is a single-digit positive integer (from 0 to 9), the maximum possible sum can be 26 + 9 = 35. ⇒ Therefore, the only possible multiple of 9 in this range is 27. ⇒ 26 + x = 27 ⇒ x = 27 − 26 = 1 ∴ The correct answer is 1. Additional Information Divisibility Rule of 3 A number is completely divisible by 3 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3. Divisibility Rule of 4 A number is divisible by 4 if the number formed by its last two digits is perfectly divisible by 4. Divisibility Rule of 11 A number is divisible by 11 if the difference between the sum of digits at odd positions and the sum of digits at even positions is 0 or a multiple of 11. Divisibility Rule of 8 A number is divisible by 8 if the number formed by its last three digits is perfectly divisible by 8.

13. In a rhombus PQRS, O is any interior point such that OP = OR. What is the degree measure of ∠SOQ?

A. 120°

B. 180°

C. 240°

D. 200°

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick The locus of a point equidistant from two vertices (P and R) is their perpendicular bisector. In a rhombus, the diagonal SQ is the perpendicular bisector of the diagonal PR. Since OP = OR, the point O must lie exactly on the diagonal SQ. Because O is an interior point, S, O, and Q lie on the same straight line. A straight line always forms an angle of 180°. ∴ The correct answer is 180°. Alternate Method Given: PQRS is a rhombus. O is an interior point such that OP = OR. Formula / Concept Used: The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at 90°. The set of all points equidistant from two given points forms the perpendicular bisector of the segment connecting them. M O P S R Q OP OR Calculations: ⇒ Let the diagonals PR and SQ intersect at point M. ⇒ In rhombus PQRS, SQ ⊥ PR and PM = MR. ⇒ This implies that the diagonal SQ acts as the perpendicular bisector of the diagonal PR. ⇒ We are given that OP = OR, which means O is perfectly equidistant from the endpoints P and R. ⇒ By the locus theorem, any point equidistant from P and R must lie exactly on the perpendicular bisector of PR. ⇒ Therefore, the point O must lie completely on the line segment SQ. ⇒ Since O is placed strictly inside the rhombus on the diagonal SQ, the points S, O, and Q are collinear. ⇒ A collinear arrangement forms a straight angle. ⇒ ∠SOQ = 180° ∴ The correct answer is 180°. Additional Information Properties of a Rhombus All four sides are equal in length. The diagonals bisect each other at right angles (90°) and also perfectly bisect the opposite interior angles. Locus of Equidistant Point The locus of a point that is completely equidistant from two given points A and B is the perpendicular bisector of the line segment AB. Collinear Points Three or more points that lie directly on the same straight line are termed collinear points. The angle formed by three collinear points with the middle point acting as the vertex is exactly 180°.

16. If 8a3 + b3 + 27c3 = 18 abc, then the relation among a, b and c is:

A. b + 3c = 2a

B. 2a + b = 3c

C. 2a + 3c = b

D. b + 3c = −2a

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Standard Identity: If x3 + y3 + z3 = 3xyz, then x + y + z = 0. Given equation: 8a3 + b3 + 27c3 = 18abc Rewrite as: (2a)3 + (b)3 + (3c)3 = 3 × (2a) × (b) × (3c) Here x = 2a, y = b, and z = 3c. ⇒ 2a + b + 3c = 0 ⇒ b + 3c = −2a ∴ The correct answer is b + 3c = −2a. Alternate Method Given: 8a3 + b3 + 27c3 = 18abc Formula Used: x3 + y3 + z3 − 3xyz = (x + y + z)(x2 + y2 + z2 − xy − yz − zx) If x3 + y3 + z3 = 3xyz, then either x + y + z = 0 or x = y = z. Calculations: ⇒ The given expression can be arranged as follows by bringing all terms to one side: ⇒ 8a3 + b3 + 27c3 − 18abc = 0 ⇒ Now, represent each term as a perfect cube: ⇒ (2a)3 + (b)3 + (3c)3 − 3 × (2a) × (b) × (3c) = 0 ⇒ Comparing this directly with the algebraic identity, we can substitute x = 2a, y = b, and z = 3c. ⇒ According to the identity rule, for the expression to be zero, the sum of the base variables must be zero. ⇒ x + y + z = 0 ⇒ 2a + b + 3c = 0 ⇒ Moving 2a to the right side of the equation: ⇒ b + 3c = −2a This strictly matches the relation given in the options. ∴ The correct answer is b + 3c = −2a. Additional Information Core Algebraic Identity a3 + b3 + c3 − 3abc = (a + b + c)(a2 + b2 + c2 − ab − bc − ca). Conditional Cubes Identity If a + b + c = 0, then substituting this into the main identity simplifies to a3 + b3 + c3 = 3abc. Sum of Squares Form a2 + b2 + c2 − ab − bc − ca = ½[(a − b)2 + (b − c)2 + (c − a)2]. Equality Condition If a3 + b3 + c3 = 3abc and a + b + c ≠ 0, it strictly means a = b = c.

17. An article passing through two hands is sold at a total profit of 40% of the original cost price. If the first dealer makes a profit of 30%, then the profit percentage made by the second dealer is:

A. 8 (6/13) \%

B. 6 (3/13) \%

C. 7 (1/13) \%

D. 7 (9/13) \%

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Let the original Cost Price = 100 units. First dealer makes 30% profit ⇒ SP1 = Cost Price for second dealer = 130 units. Total profit is 40% on original Cost Price ⇒ Final SP = 140 units. Profit for the second dealer = Final SP − CP2 = 140 − 130 = 10 units. Profit % for second dealer = (10 ÷ 130) × 100 = 100 ÷ 13 = 7 9/13 %. ∴ The correct answer is 7 9/13 %. Alternate Method Given: Total profit on the article = 40% Profit made by the first dealer = 30% Formula Used: Successive Percentage Formula: Net Profit % = a + b + (a × b) ÷ 100 Calculations: ⇒ Let the profit percentage made by the second dealer be b. ⇒ By applying the successive percentage formula we equate it to the total profit of 40%. ⇒ 40 = 30 + b + (30 × b) ÷ 100 ⇒ 40 − 30 = b + 0.3b ⇒ 10 = 1.3b ⇒ b = 10 ÷ 1.3 = 100 ÷ 13 ⇒ b = 7 9/13 % ∴ The correct answer is 7 9/13 %. Additional Information Successive Profits When an item is sold multiple times sequentially, the net profit percentage is calculated using the successive percentage formula: Net % = a + b + (a × b) ÷ 100. Marked Price & Discount Discount is always given on the Marked Price (MP). The resulting selling price is: SP = MP × (100 − Discount%) ÷ 100. Dishonest Dealer If a dealer sells goods at Cost Price but uses false weight, the actual profit percentage is calculated as: Profit % = (True Weight − False Weight) ÷ False Weight × 100.

24. If the height and diameter of a cylinder are 12 cm and 28 cm, respectively, then find the ratio of the total surface area to the curved surface area.

A. 13 : 5

B. 10 : 3

C. 13 : 6

D. 11 : 6

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick For a given cylinder, the ratio of Total Surface Area (TSA) to Curved Surface Area (CSA) is derived directly as (h + r) / h. Given, height (h) = 12 cm and diameter = 28 cm ⇒ radius (r) = 14 cm. Required Ratio = (12 + 14) / 12 Required Ratio = 26 / 12 = 13 / 6 ∴ The correct answer is 13 : 6. Alternate Method Given: ⇒ Height of the cylinder (h) = 12 cm ⇒ Diameter of the cylinder (d) = 28 cm ⇒ Radius (r) = d ÷ 2 = 28 ÷ 2 = 14 cm Formulas Used: ⇒ Total Surface Area (TSA) = 2πr(h + r) ⇒ Curved Surface Area (CSA) = 2πrh Calculation: ⇒ Ratio = TSA ÷ CSA ⇒ Ratio = [2 × π × r × (h + r)] ÷ [2 × π × r × h] ⇒ Ratio = (h + r) ÷ h ⇒ Ratio = (12 + 14) ÷ 12 ⇒ Ratio = 26 ÷ 12 ⇒ Ratio = 13 ÷ 6 ⇒ Ratio = 13 : 6 ∴ The correct answer is 13 : 6.
