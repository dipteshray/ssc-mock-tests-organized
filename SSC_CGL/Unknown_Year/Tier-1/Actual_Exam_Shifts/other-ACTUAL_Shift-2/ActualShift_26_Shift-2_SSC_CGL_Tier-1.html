A. 34

B. 10

C. 18

D. 24

Answer B

Solution

Given Equation: 14 × 8 / 4 - 2 + 6 = ? BODMAS Rule: Decoding the given information: Symbol × / + - Meaning + - × / Now replacing the signs from left to right using BODMAS Rule, we get: ⇒ 14 + 8 - 4 / 2 × 6 ⇒ 14 + 8 - 2 × 6 ⇒ 14 + 8 - 12 ⇒ 22 - 12 ⇒ 10 Hence, the correct answer is “Option 2”.

21. As a teacher, you notice a student bullying another. What should you do?

A. Ignore it

B. Intervene and counsel

C. Expel immediately

D. Tell the victim to stay quiet

Answer B

Solution

As an educator, your primary responsibility is to maintain a safe learning environment while fostering the social and emotional development of all students. Bullying is often a complex behavior that requires a multi-layered response rather than just avoidance or extreme punishment. Hence, the correct answer is “Option 2”.

• Immediate Safety: Intervening stops the harmful behavior instantly and protects the victim from further distress.

• Root Cause Analysis: Counseling allows you to understand why the student is bullying. Often, bullies are dealing with their own emotional struggles, or they lack empathy and social skills.

• Restorative Justice: Counseling provides the victim with support and gives the bully a chance to take responsibility and learn positive ways to interact.

24. In a group of 10, a committee of 3 is to be formed. How many ways?

A. 120

B. 110

C. 130

D. 140

Answer A

Solution

The number of ways to choose r items from a set of n items is given by the formula: C(n, r) = = (n!/r!(n - r)!) Here, n = 10 r = 3 Plugging these values into the formula: 103 = (10!/3!(10 - 3)!) 103 = (10 × 9 × 8 × 7!/ (3 × 2 × 1) × 7!) 103 = (720/6) 103 = 120 Hence, the correct answer is “Option 1”.
