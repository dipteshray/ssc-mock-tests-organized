4. Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements. Statements: Some bags are purses. All purses are wallets. All wallets are sacks. Conclusions: (I) All purses are sacks. (II) Some wallets are bags.

A. Both conclusions (I) and (II) are follow

B. Only conclusion (II) is follow

C. Neither conclusion (I) nor (II) is follow

D. Only conclusion (I) is follow

Answer A

Solution

Conclusions: I. All purses are sacks → Follow (As some bags are purses, all purses are wallets and all wallets are sacks. As whole purses comes in wallets which whole comes in sacks, therefore it is true.) II. Some wallets are bags → Follow (As some bags are purses and all purses are wallets. As whole purses comes in wallets and has some part common with bags, therefore it is true.) ∴ Here, Both conclusions (I) and (II) are follow.

5. Three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements. Statements: No bench is chair. All sofas are benches. All tables are sofas. Conclusions: I. No table is a chair. II. Some sofas are chairs. III. All tables are benches.

A. Only conclusion I follows

B. Only conclusions I and II follow

C. Only conclusion II follows

D. Only conclusions I and III follow

Answer D

Solution

Conclusions: I. No table is a chair → Follow (As no bench is chair, all sofas are benches and all tables are sofas. As whole tables comes in sofas which whole comes in benches which cannot be chair, therefore it is true.) II. Some sofas are chairs → Does not follow (As no bench is chair and all sofas are benches. As whole sofas comes in benches which cannot be chair, therefore it is false.) III. All tables are benches → Follow (As all sofas are benches and all tables are sofas. As whole tables comes in sofas which whole comes in benches, therefore it is true.) ∴ Here, Only conclusions I and III follow.

16. Which two numbers (not digits) should be interchanged to make the given equation correct? 116 - 92 ÷ 4 + 25 × 2 - 20 = 150

A. 116 and 92

B. 25 and 20

C. 2 and 4

D. 20 and 92

Answer C

Solution

Option 3) 2 and 4 116 - 92 ÷ 4 + 25 × 2 - 20 = 150 ⇒ 116 - 92 ÷ 2 + 25 × 4 - 20 = 150 ⇒ 116 - 92 ÷ 2 + 25 × 4 - 20 = 150 ⇒ 116 - 46 + 25 × 4 - 20 = 150 ⇒ 116 + 100 - 20 - 46 = 150 ⇒ 216 - 20 - 46 = 150 ⇒ 150 = 150 (LHS = RHS)
