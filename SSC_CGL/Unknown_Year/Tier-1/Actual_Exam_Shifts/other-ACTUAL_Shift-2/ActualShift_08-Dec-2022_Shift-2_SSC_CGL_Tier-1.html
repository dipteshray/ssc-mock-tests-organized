8. If A × B means that A is the father of B, A + B means that A is the mother of B, A ÷ B means that A is the brother of B then which of the following expression shows that A is the paternal uncle of C?

A. A ÷ C + B

B. A ÷ B + C

C. A + B ÷ C

D. A ÷ B × C

Answer D

Solution

Given: A × B A is the father of B A + B A is the mother of B A ÷ B A is the brother of B Now we substitute the values in the given options: Option 1) A ÷ C + B means, A is the brother of C, C is the mother of B. Hence, the correct answer is "Option 4".
