22. The pH of a neutral solution is ______.

A. 7

B. 0

C. 8

D. 14

Answer A

Solution

The pH of a neutral solution is 7. It represents an equal concentration of hydrogen ions (H+) and hydroxyl ions (OH-) in pure water. At pH 7, the solution is neither acidic nor basic. The pH is the negative logarithm of H+ ion concentration, indicating neutrality at this value.
