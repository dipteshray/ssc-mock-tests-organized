1. Madhav purchased an item for ₹42,000 and sold it at a loss of 20%. With remaining amount, he purchased another item and sold it at a gain of 30%. What is the overall gain (in ₹)?

A. 6720

B. 4200

C. 1680

D. 2520

Answer C

Solution

Loss on 1st item = 20/100 × 42,000 = 8,400 Selling Price = 42,000 - 8,400 = 33,600 Gain on 2nd item = 30/100 × 33,600 = 10,080 Selling Price = 33,600 + 10,080 = 43,680 Overall Gain = 43,680 - 42,000 = 1,680

3. Seven years ago, Prachi was four times as old as her daughter was at that time. Four years from now, Prachi will be two-and-a-half times as old as her daughter would then be. Find the sum of the present ages (in years) of Prachi and her daughter.

A. 69

B. 77

C. 49

D. 72

Answer A

Solution

Let Prachi's present age be P years and her daughter's present age be D years. P - 7 = 4(D - 7) ⇒ P - 7 = 4D - 28 ⇒ P = 4D - 2 P + 4 = 2.5(D + 4) ⇒ P + 4 = 2.5D + 10 ⇒ P = 2.5D + 6 4D - 21 = 2.5D + 6 ⇒ 1.5D = 27 ⇒ D = 18 P = 4(18) - 21 ⇒ P = 72 - 21 = 51 Sum of present ages = P + D ⇒ 51 + 18 = 69

Solution

20. Pipes M, N and S can fill a tank in 25, 50 and 100 minutes, respectively. Initially, pipes N and S are kept open for 10 minutes, and then pipe N is shut while pipe M is opened. Pipe S is closed 15 minutes before the tank overflows. How much time (in minutes) will it take to fill the tank if the three pipes work in this pattern?

A. 30

B. 33

C. 42

D. 27

Answer D

Solution

Work by N and S in 10 min: (10/50) + (10/100) = 3/10 Remaining work = 1 - 3/10 = 7/10 Work by M and S in (T-25) min: (T-25)/25 + (T-25)/100 = (T-25)/20 Work by M in 15 min: 15/25 = 3/5 Total: 3/10 + (T-25)/20 + 3/5 = 1 6/20 + (T-25)/20 + 12/20 = 1 (T-7)/20 = 1 T - 7 = 20 T = 27 minutes

22. The average temperature of a city for the first sixteen days of January is 22ºC, and the average temperature for the last sixteen days of the same month is 26ºC. If the average temperature for the entire month is 24ºC, what is the temperature on the sixteenth day?

A. 24ºC

B. 25ºC

C. 23ºC

D. 22ºC

Answer A

Solution

Total (1st 16 days) = 22 × 16 = 352ºC Total (last 16 days) = 26 × 16 = 416ºC Total (31 days) = 24 × 31 = 744ºC For 16th day temp (T): 352 + 416 - T = 744, T = 768 - 744, T = 24ºC
