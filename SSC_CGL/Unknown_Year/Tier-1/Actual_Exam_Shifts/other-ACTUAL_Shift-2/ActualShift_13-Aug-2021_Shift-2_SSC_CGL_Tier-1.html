35. An elastic wave generated by an impulse such as an earthquake or an explosion is called ______.

A. tectonic shift

B. epicentre

C. seismic wave

D. sound wave

Answer C

Solution

• An elastic wave generated by an impulse such as an earthquake or an explosion is called a seismic wave.

• Seismic waves are waves of energy that travel through Earth's layers and are a result of earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, magma movement, large landslides, and large man-made explosions that give out low-frequency acoustic energy.

• Earthquakes are usually caused when the underground rock suddenly breaks and there is rapid motion along a fault. This sudden release of energy causes seismic waves that make the ground shake.

• The earthquake is over when the fault stops moving. Seismic waves are generated throughout the earthquake.

• Three fundamental kinds of seismic waves are Primary (P = pressure) waves, Secondary (S = shear), and Surface waves.

• Seismic waves may travel either along or near the earth's surface (Rayleigh and Love waves) or through the earth's interior (P and S waves).
