B. 67

Solution

Given equation: 16 ÷ 4 + 8 × 8 − 4 = ? Now, if ‘+’ and ‘−’ are interchanged and ‘×’ and ‘÷’ are interchanged: ⇒ 16 × 4 − 8 ÷ 8 + 4 = ? ⇒ 16 × 4 − 1 + 4 = ? ⇒ 64 − 1 + 4 = ? ⇒ 68 - 1 = 67.

15. Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements. Statements: All cards are postcards. Some postcards are books. All books are novels. Conclusions: I. Some postcards are novels. II. No card is a book. III. All cards are novels.

A. Only II and III conclusion follow

B. Only conclusion III follows

C. Only I and II conclusion follow

D. Only conclusion I follows

Answer D

Solution

Conclusions: I. Some postcards are novels → Follow (As some postcards are books and all books are novels. As some part of the postcards is common with books which whole comes in novels, therefore it is false.) II. No card is a book → Does not follow (As all cards are postcards and some postcards are books. As no direct relation is given between cards and books, therefore it is false.) III. All cards are novels → Does not follow (As all cards are postcards, some postcards are books and all books are novels. So it is possible but not definite.)
