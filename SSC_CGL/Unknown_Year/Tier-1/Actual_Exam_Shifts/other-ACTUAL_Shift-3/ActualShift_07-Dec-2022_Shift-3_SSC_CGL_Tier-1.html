22. The motion of the body moving along a circular path is an example of ______.

A. decelerated motion

B. accelerated motion

C. non-uniform motion

D. uniform motion

Answer B

Solution

Circular motion is an example of accelerated motion as the velocity changes due to change in direction. A body travelling in a circle at a constant speed is said to be moving at an accelerated rate since its motion is uniform in speed but not in velocity, which can be expressed in magnitude and direction as a vector quantity.

26. Which of the following are food preservatives?

A. Salt, sodium nitrite, spices and vinegar

B. Gypsum, soda and methyl alcohol

C. Gypsum, salt and formic acid

D. Formic acid, vinegar and methyl alcohol

Answer A

Solution

Food preservatives are substances that are added to food to prevent spoilage and increase their shelf life. Salt, sodium nitrite, spices, and vinegar are all examples of food preservatives. These substances work by inhibiting the growth of bacteria, fungi, and other microorganisms that can cause food spoilage. By slowing down decomposition and rancidification, food preservatives help to reduce food waste and promote environmental sustainability. It is important to note that some food preservation techniques may produce carcinogens and that processed meat is considered carcinogenic to humans.
