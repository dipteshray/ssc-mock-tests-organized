1. Consider the following statements: 1. Perini Shivatandavam is a martial dance form that has been revived in recent times. 2. The dance tradition originated in the royal courts of the Chola rulers of Tamil Nadu. Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. Only statement 1 is correct

B. Only statement 2 is correct

C. Both statements 1 and 2 are correct

D. Neither statement 1 nor 2 is correct

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Only statement 1 is correct. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• Statement 1: Perini Shivatandavam is a martial dance form that has been revived in recent times. The Perini Shivatandavam, also known as the ‘Dance of Warriors’, is a traditional dance form that originated in the Kakatiya dynasty in the present-day state of Telangana.

• It was performed during wartime to energize and motivate soldiers before they went to battle.

• The dance form was lost over time but has been revived in recent times by dance enthusiasts and cultural organizations.

• It is now performed as a symbol of cultural heritage and martial spirit. Hence, this statement is correct.

• Statement 2: The dance tradition originated in the royal courts of the Chola rulers of Tamil Nadu. The statement is incorrect as the Perini Shivatandavam is associated with the Kakatiya dynasty, which ruled parts of present-day Telangana, and not with the Chola dynasty of Tamil Nadu.

• The Chola dynasty is known for promoting other art forms such as Bharatanatyam and temple architecture, but Perini Shivatandavam is not among them.

• Hence, this statement is not correct.

• Perini Shivatandavam: The dance form derives its name from ‘Perini,’ meaning a warrior, and ‘Shivatandavam,’ referring to the cosmic dance of Lord Shiva.

• It involves vigorous movements, rhythmic footwork, and expressions that represent the warrior spirit, devotion, and energy.

• Typically, it is performed by male dancers, who depict various warrior-like postures and emotions during the performance.

• Musical instruments such as the mridangam and cymbals accompany the dance, enhancing its intensity and rhythm.

• Revival of Perini Shivatandavam: The dance form declined with the fall of the Kakatiya dynasty and remained dormant for centuries.

• Efforts to revive this art form were initiated in the 20th century by cultural activists, particularly Nataraja Ramakrishna, a renowned dance guru and historian.

• Today, the dance is performed at cultural festivals and events, showcasing the rich heritage of Telangana.

• The Chola Dynasty and Art: The Chola dynasty, which ruled Tamil Nadu, is famous for its contributions to temple art and architecture.

• They were patrons of classical dance forms like Bharatanatyam, which was performed as an offering to deities in temples.

• The Cholas established a distinct tradition of temple art, sculptures, and inscriptions, which remain iconic in Indian history.

• The Kakatiya Dynasty: The Kakatiyas ruled parts of present-day Telangana and Andhra Pradesh between the 12th and 14th centuries.

• They were known for their patronage of the arts, especially dance forms like Perini Shivatandavam.

• The Kakatiya rulers also contributed significantly to temple architecture, as seen in the Ramappa Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

• Significance of Perini Shivatandavam: The dance is deeply rooted in Hindu mythology and represents the cosmic energy of Lord Shiva.

• It emphasizes the themes of valor, devotion, and spirituality, making it a unique blend of martial and devotional art.

• Its revival has helped preserve the cultural identity of Telangana and promote its rich heritage globally.

2. Which architectural element is characteristically associated with the Capuchin Church?

A. Highly decorated domes and tall spires

B. Elaborate rose windows with tracery

C. Flat arched doorway crowned with a pediment

D. Towering multi-tiered shikhara

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Flat arched doorway crowned with a pediment. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• The Capuchin Church, associated with the Capuchin Order of monks, is known for its simple and austere architecture. It avoids excessive ornamentation and follows a minimalistic design approach.

• One of the characteristic features of the Capuchin Church is the use of a flat arched doorway crowned with a pediment. This design reflects the order’s emphasis on humility and functionality. Hence, this is the correct option.

• Unlike other Baroque churches, which are highly decorative, Capuchin Churches maintain a plain and unembellished appearance, focusing on simplicity over grandeur.

• The architectural style of the Capuchin Church aligns with the principles of the Counter-Reformation, emphasizing piety, restraint, and practicality in design.

• Capuchin Church Architecture: The Capuchin Order, a branch of the Franciscans, emerged in the 16th century and promoted a return to simplicity and austerity in both monastic life and architecture.

• Capuchin Churches often feature plain façades, with minimal decorative elements. The flat arched doorway crowned with a pediment is a hallmark of this style.

• These churches also emphasize functionality, with interiors designed to focus attention on the altar.

• Comparison with Other Architectural Styles: Highly decorated domes and tall spires: This feature is typical of Baroque or Gothic architecture, emphasizing grandeur and ornamentation. It is not associated with the Capuchin Church.

• Elaborate rose windows with tracery: This is a key feature of Gothic architecture, focusing on intricate stained glass windows. It does not align with the simplistic design of Capuchin Churches.

• Towering multi-tiered shikhara: This is a distinct feature of Indian temple architecture, particularly Hindu temples, and is unrelated to the Capuchin Church.

• Examples of Capuchin Churches: Many Capuchin Churches exist across Europe, particularly in Italy, such as the Santa Maria della Concezione dei Cappuccini in Rome, which exemplifies the order’s architectural principles.

• The interiors of these churches are often stark and may feature religious art that aligns with the Counter-Reformation’s goals of simplicity and accessibility.

• Key Characteristics of Capuchin Church Architecture: The design reflects the values of the Capuchin Order, emphasizing simplicity, austerity, and humility.

• Architectural elements such as flat arched doorways and pediments are functional and avoid unnecessary ornamentation.

• Historical Context: The Capuchin Order was established during the Counter-Reformation, a period of Catholic revival that sought to address the challenges posed by the Protestant Reformation.

• Their architectural style was influenced by this movement, focusing on accessibility and simplicity to connect with the faithful.

6. In pole vault events, how many unsuccessful attempts are permitted before an athlete is eliminated?

A. 2

B. 3

C. 4

D. 1

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is 3 unsuccessful attempts. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• Unsuccessful attempts in pole vault: In pole vault events, athletes are allowed a maximum of 3 unsuccessful attempts at any height or combination of heights before they are eliminated from the competition. This is in accordance with standard rules of track and field events.

• An unsuccessful attempt is recorded when an athlete fails to clear the bar or knocks it down during their vault attempt. If an athlete fails three consecutive attempts, irrespective of the height, they are eliminated from the competition.

• The rule ensures fairness in the competition and maintains a consistent standard for evaluating performance across all athletes. Hence, the correct answer is 3 unsuccessful attempts.

• Pole Vault Overview: Pole vault is a track and field event where athletes use a pole, usually made of fiberglass or carbon fiber, to clear a high bar.

• The event tests a combination of speed, strength, and agility. Athletes must use their running momentum and pole-bending techniques to propel themselves over the bar.

• Rules for Attempts: Each athlete is given three attempts to clear a height. If the athlete fails to clear the bar three times consecutively, they are disqualified.

• If an athlete clears the bar in fewer than three attempts, they move to the next height. The competition continues until only one athlete remains or no one clears the next height.

• Example: An athlete attempting 4.50 meters might fail the first attempt but clear it on the second. They will then proceed to the next height without penalty for the first failure.

• Factors Affecting Performance: Technique: Proper pole grip, run-up speed, and body positioning are crucial for successful vaults.

• Equipment: The type and flexibility of the pole can significantly impact performance.

• Environmental conditions, such as wind or rain, can also affect the athlete’s ability to clear the bar.

• Height Progression: The bar is raised in predetermined increments, and athletes must decide whether to attempt the initial heights or wait for higher ones. Skipping lower heights can be a strategy to conserve energy.

• However, skipping heights increases the risk of quick elimination if the athlete fails three consecutive attempts at the chosen height.

• Record and Achievements: World Record: The current men's pole vault world record is held by Armand Duplantis with a height of 6.23 meters, achieved in 2023.

• Olympic Record: The Olympic record in men’s pole vault stands at 6.03 meters, set by Thiago Braz in 2016.

• The women's pole vault world record is 5.06 meters, held by Yelena Isinbayeva.

18. If the speed of sound is 340 m/s and the frequency is 680 Hz, what is the wavelength of the sound wave?

A. 0.5 m

B. 2 m

C. 20 m

D. 50 m

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is 0.5 m. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

• The wavelength of a wave is calculated using the formula: Wavelength (λ) = Speed of Sound (v) / Frequency (f).

• Here, the given values are: Speed of sound (v) = 340 m/s Frequency (f) = 680 Hz.

• Substituting these values into the formula: λ = 340 / 680 = 0.5 m.

• Thus, the wavelength of the sound wave is 0.5 m. Hence, the correct answer is 0.5 m.

• This calculation demonstrates the relationship between the speed, frequency, and wavelength of a wave, which are fundamental concepts in wave mechanics.

• Relationship Between Speed, Frequency, and Wavelength: The formula λ = v / f shows that the wavelength is inversely proportional to the frequency. This means that as frequency increases, wavelength decreases, and vice versa.

• The speed of sound depends on the medium through which it travels. For example: 340 m/s in air, approximately 1500 m/s in water, and around 5000 m/s in steel.

• Frequency (f): Frequency refers to the number of wave cycles that pass a given point per second. It is measured in Hertz (Hz).

• For example, a sound wave with a frequency of 680 Hz means 680 oscillations per second.

• Wavelength (λ): Wavelength is the distance between two consecutive points of a wave that are in phase, such as two crests or troughs.

• It is measured in meters (m) and determines the spatial extent of one wave cycle.

• Speed of Sound: The speed of sound varies based on the medium's properties such as density and elasticity. For example: - In air at 20°C, the speed is approximately 340 m/s. - In water, it is around 1500 m/s. - In steel, it is about 5000 m/s.

• The speed of sound increases with an increase in temperature or the elasticity of the medium.

• Applications of Sound Waves: Communication: Sound waves are used in devices like telephones and radios to transmit information.

• Medical Imaging: Ultrasound waves (a type of sound wave) are widely used in diagnostic imaging.

• Navigation: SONAR (Sound Navigation and Ranging) uses sound waves to detect objects underwater.

• Real-life Example of Wavelength Calculation: Suppose a tuning fork generates a sound wave with a frequency of 512 Hz in the air. If the speed of sound in air is 340 m/s, the wavelength is calculated as: λ = v / f = 340 / 512 ≈ 0.664 m.

Solution

The correct answer is Statement 2 only. Key Points Additional Information Important Points

X-rays are emitted by the hot matter (such as gas and dust) in the accretion disk surrounding the black hole, as the material heats up while spiraling inwards. The event horizon itself does not produce any radiation. Hence, statement 1 is incorrect.

• Statement 2: Pulsars are indeed rapidly spinning neutron stars. These stars emit beams of radiation (often radio waves) from their magnetic poles. • Event Horizon: The boundary around a black hole beyond which nothing can escape. The event horizon is invisible and does not emit radiation.

• Statement 2: Article 131A was removed by the 43rd Constitutional Amendment: The 43rd Constitutional Amendment Act of 1977 repealed Article 131A, restoring the original position where both the High Courts and the Supreme Court could determine the validity of central laws.
