4. What number will occupy the place of the question mark in the following series: 3, 10, 101, 10202, ?

A. 104080805

B. 104040201

C. 103020401

D. 104040401

Answer A

Solution

The logic followed here is: The pattern is (n+1) = (n)2 + 1 Given series: 3, 10, 101, 10202, ? Logic: Each next number = (previous number × previous number) + 1 From 3 → 3 × 3 + 1 = 10 From 10 → 10 × 10 + 1 = 101 From 101 → 101 × 101 + 1 = 10202 From 10202 → 10202 × 10202 + 1 = 104080805 Thus, "104080805" will replace the question mark in the given series. Hence, the correct option is "Option 1".

8. Instruction: Identify the assumptions that must hold true for the statement to be considered valid, then choose the correct option. Statement: All employees must attend the mandatory cybersecurity training session next week to mitigate potential corporate data breaches. Assumptions: I. Data breaches are a serious threat to the company. II. Employee participation in training will reduce the risk of future breaches. III. Every employee is currently unaware of basic cybersecurity protocols.

A. Only I and II are implicit

B. Only I and III are implicit

C. All are implicit

D. Only I is implicit

Answer A

Solution

I. Data breaches are a serious threat to the company. ⇒ Implicit Because the training is aimed at preventing data breaches, it assumes such breaches are a serious concern. II. Employee participation in training will reduce the risk of future breaches. ⇒ Implicit Because making training mandatory implies it is expected to reduce breach risks. III. Every employee is currently unaware of basic cybersecurity protocols. ⇒ Not Implicit Because the statement doesn't suggest total unawareness, only the need for training. Thus, Only I and II are implicit.

21. If m @ n = 50, where m @ n = 3m + 5n, which pair of values for m and n is not possible?

A. m=5, n=7

B. m=10, n=4

C. m=15, n=1

D. m=8, n=6

Answer D

Solution

Then, Putting the value of (1) in (2): 50 = 3m + 5n Option 1) m=5, n=7 50 = 3 × 5 + 5 × 7, 50 = 15 + 35, 50 = 50 (LHS = RHS) Option 2) m=10, n=4 50 = 3 × 10 + 5 × 4, 50 = 30 + 20, 50 = 50 (LHS = RHS) Option 3) m=15, n=1 50 = 3 × 15 + 5 × 1, 50 = 45 + 5, 50 = 50 (LHS = RHS) Option 4) m=8, n=6 50 = 3 × 8 + 5 × 6, 50 = 24 + 30, 50 ≠ 54 (LHS ≠ RHS) Thus, out of all "m=15, n=1" pair is not possible.
