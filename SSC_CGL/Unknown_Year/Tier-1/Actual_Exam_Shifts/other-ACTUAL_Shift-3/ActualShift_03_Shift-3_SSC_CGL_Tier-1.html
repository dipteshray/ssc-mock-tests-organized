1. Choose the word that is opposite in meaning to LEARNED.

C. Expert

Solution

8. Select the appropriate option to complete the sentence: So delicate ___ the negotiations that a single leak could have ruined them.

A. were

B. are

C. was

D. is

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is 'were'. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 1. Complete Sentence: "So delicate were the negotiations that a single leak could have ruined them." Additional Information

• The verb form must agree with the plural subject "negotiations", so the plural auxiliary "were" is required.

• The pattern used here is an inversion: "So + adjective + auxiliary + subject" (So delicate were the negotiations...).

• The clause "could have ruined" indicates a past possibility, so a past form ("were") fits the time reference of the sentence.

• Option 2: "are" is incorrect because it is present tense, while the sentence context ("could have ruined") refers to a past situation.

• Option 3: "was" is singular, but the subject "negotiations" is plural, so subject–verb agreement is violated.

• Option 4: "is" is also present tense and does not match the implied past conditional idea in the rest of the sentence.

11. Convert the sentence from Active to Passive Voice: Why did the editor overlook the stylistic issues?

A. Why did the stylistic issues be overlooked by the editor?

B. Why have the stylistic issues been overlooked by the editor?

C. Why are the stylistic issues being overlooked by the editor?

D. Why were the stylistic issues overlooked by the editor?

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 4. Correct Sentence: "Why were the stylistic issues overlooked by the editor?" Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "Why did the editor overlook the stylistic issues?"

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "the stylistic issues" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "did overlook" to "were overlooked" (past simple passive).

• Ensure the question form remains intact by starting with "Why."

• Option 1: "Why did the stylistic issues be overlooked by the editor?" – Incorrect structure (verb "be" is improperly used).

• Option 2: "Why have the stylistic issues been overlooked by the editor?" – Incorrect tense (present perfect passive).

• Option 3: "Why are the stylistic issues being overlooked by the editor?" – Incorrect tense (present continuous passive).

15. What does the concept of groupthink emphasize?

A. Promoting independent thought

B. Valuing consensus over critical thinking

C. Encouraging open debate

D. Strengthening team diversity

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2 (Valuing consensus over critical thinking). Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Additional Information

• Groupthink refers to a situation where the desire for harmony or conformity within a group results in poor decision-making.

• Members of the group suppress dissenting opinions and avoid critical evaluation of ideas to maintain unity.

• As a result, consensus is valued more than logical reasoning or evidence-based thinking.

• The term was introduced by psychologist Irving Janis to explain policy and corporate failures caused by uncritical group agreement.

• Option 1: Promoting independent thought — Incorrect, as groupthink discourages independent thinking.

• Option 3: Encouraging open debate — Incorrect, groupthink suppresses open discussion.

• Option 4: Strengthening team diversity — Incorrect, diversity in thought is reduced under groupthink.
