6. Instruction: Identify the assumptions that must hold for the statement to be valid, then choose the correct option. Statement: The city council mandated that all residents wear masks when using public transport systems. Assumptions: I. Wearing masks reduces the transmission rate of airborne illnesses. II. A significant number of residents use public transport. III. The city council has the authority to enforce such regulations.

A. Only I and III are implicit

B. All I, II, and III are implicit

C. Only I is implicit

D. Only II and III are implicit

Answer A

Solution

I. Wearing masks reduces the transmission rate of airborne illnesses.

• The council would mandate masks only if it believes masks are effective. Required assumption

• III. The city council has the authority to enforce such regulations.

• A mandate presupposes that the issuing body has the legal authority. Required assumption

7. In a row of students, Sita is 12th from the right end. If she shifts 5 positions to her left, she becomes 18th from the left end. What is the total number of students in the row?

A. 34

D. 32

Answer A

Solution

N – 16 = 18. N = 18 + 16 = 34. Total number of students = 34.
