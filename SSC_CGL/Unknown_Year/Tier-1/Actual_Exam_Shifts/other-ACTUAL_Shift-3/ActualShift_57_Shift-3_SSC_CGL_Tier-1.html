2. Three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements. Statements: No lotus is a daisy. All lotuses are tulips. All tulips are sunflowers. Conclusions: I. All daisies are tulips. II. Some sunflowers are daisies. III. No sunflower is a daisy.

A. Only conclusion III follows

B. Either conclusion II or III follows

C. Only conclusion I follows

D. Only conclusion II follows

Answer B

Solution

Conclusions: I. All daisies are tulips → Does not follow(As no lotus is a daisy and all lotuses are tulips. So it is possible but not definite.) II. Some sunflowers are daisies → Does not follow (As no lotus is a daisy, all lotuses are tulips and all tulips are sunflowers. So it is possible but not definite.) III. No sunflower is a daisy → Does not follow (As no lotus is a daisy, all lotuses are tulips and all tulips are sunflowers. As no direct relation is given between sunflower and daisy, so it is possible but not definite.)

C. 57 and 64

D. 33 and 44

Answer D

Solution

Option 4): 33 and 44 257 - 33 ÷ 11 × 13 - 2 + 364 - 56 ÷ 8 × 4 - 44 = 35 ⇒ 257 - 44 ÷ 11 × 13 - 2 + 364 - 56 ÷ 8 × 4 - 33 = 35 ⇒ 257 - 44 ÷ 11 × 13 - 2 + 364 - 56 ÷ 8 × 4 - 33 = 35 ⇒ 257 - 4 × 13 - 2 + 364 - 7 × 4 - 33 = 35 ⇒ 257 - 52 - 2 + 364 - 28 - 33 = 35 ⇒ 23 + 33 = 35 ⇒ 8 + 27 = 35 ⇒ 35 = 35 (LHS = RHS)
