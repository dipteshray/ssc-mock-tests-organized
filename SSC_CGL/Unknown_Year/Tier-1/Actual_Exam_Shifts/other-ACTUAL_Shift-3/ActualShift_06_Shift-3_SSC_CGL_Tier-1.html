13. Convert into active voice: The parcel will have been handed over by the courier before evening.

A. The courier hands over the parcel before evening.

B. The courier had handed over the parcel before evening.

C. The courier has handed over the parcel before evening.

D. The courier will have handed over the parcel before evening.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 4. Correct Sentence: "The courier will have handed over the parcel before evening." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the passive voice, the sentence is: "The parcel will have been handed over by the courier before evening."

• To convert to active voice: Place the subject "the courier" at the beginning.

• Change the passive verb form "will have been handed over" to "will have handed over" (future perfect active).

• Ensure the object "the parcel" and time phrase "before evening" remain in their appropriate positions for clarity.

• Option 1: "The courier hands over the parcel before evening." – Incorrect tense (present simple active).

• Option 2: "The courier had handed over the parcel before evening." – Incorrect tense (past perfect active).

• Option 3: "The courier has handed over the parcel before evening." – Incorrect tense (present perfect active).
