Solution

A. 22

B. 18

C. 16

D. 20

Answer D

Solution

Given :27 − 6 + 4 × 8 ÷ 6 = ? After interchanging the signs then: ⇒ 27 ÷ 6 × 4 + 8 - 6 = ? ⇒ ? = 4.5 × 4 + 8 - 6 ⇒ ? = 18 + 8 - 6 ⇒ ? = 26 - 6 = 20
