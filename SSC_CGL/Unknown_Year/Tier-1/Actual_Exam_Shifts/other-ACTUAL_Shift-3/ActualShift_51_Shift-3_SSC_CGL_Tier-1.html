12. Pipes P and Q can completely fill a water tank in 10 hours and 15 hours, respectively. A pipe R can empty a tank filled completely with water in 12 hours. Initially, the tank is empty and only pipes P and Q are opened at 6 a.m. and pipe R is also opened at 9 a.m. By what time will the tank be completely filled?

A. 1 p.m.

B. 2 p.m.

C. 11 a.m.

D. 3 p.m.

Answer D

Solution

Work rate of P = 1/10, Work rate of Q = 1/15, Work rate of R = 1/12 From 6 a.m. to 9 a.m.: Work done = (1/10 + 1/15) × 3 = 1/2 After 9 a.m.: Net work rate = 1/10 + 1/15 - 1/12 = 1/12 Time required = (1/2) / (1/12) = 6 hours 9 a.m. + 6 hours = 3 p.m.
