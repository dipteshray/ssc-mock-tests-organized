1. Select the most appropriate synonym of the word: APOGEE

11. Change the sentence from active to passive:“The panel has drafted a final proposal.”

A. A final proposal has been drafted by the panel.

B. A final proposal was being drafted by the panel.

C. A final proposal had been drafted by the panel.

D. A final proposal is drafted by the panel.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Option 1. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 1. Correct Sentence: "A final proposal has been drafted by the panel." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "The panel has drafted a final proposal."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "a final proposal" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "has drafted" to "has been drafted" (present perfect passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "the panel."

• Option 2: "A final proposal was being drafted by the panel." – Incorrect tense (past continuous passive).

• Option 3: "A final proposal had been drafted by the panel." – Incorrect tense (past perfect passive).

• Option 4: "A final proposal is drafted by the panel." – Incorrect tense (simple present passive).
