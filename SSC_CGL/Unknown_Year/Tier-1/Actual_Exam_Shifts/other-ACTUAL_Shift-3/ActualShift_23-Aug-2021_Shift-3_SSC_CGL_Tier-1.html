1. If 19 July 2000 was a Wednesday, then what would be the day of the week on 15 June 2012?

A. Saturday

B. Friday

C. Thursday

D. Wednesday

Answer B

Solution

Odd days: A non-leap year has 365 days and a leap year has 366 days. Non-leap year = 365 days or 52 weeks and 1 day. So, here the number of odd days is 1. Leap year = 366 days or 52 weeks and 2 days So, here the number of odd days are 2. Leap year between 2000 to 2012 = 2004, 2008 and 2012 And, The non-leap year between 2000 and 2012 = 2001, 2002, 2003, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2009, 2010 and 2011 So, total number of odd day between 2000 and 2012 = 9 (Total Non- leap year) + 2 + 2 + 2 = 15 days = 1 odd day Code of the day Day 0 Sunday 1 Monday 2 Tuesday 3 Wednesday 4 Thursday 5 Friday 6 Saturday 19 July 2000 was a Wednesday => Day on 19 July 2012 = Wednesday + 15 = Wednesday + remainder of 15/7 = Wednesday + 1 (odd day) = Thursday Therefore, 19 July 2012 = Thursday Now, 15 June 2012 = 19 July 2012 – 34 days = Thursday – 34 = Thursday – the remainder of 34/7 = Thursday – 6 (In reverse direction) = Friday Hence, 15 June 2012 was “Friday”.
