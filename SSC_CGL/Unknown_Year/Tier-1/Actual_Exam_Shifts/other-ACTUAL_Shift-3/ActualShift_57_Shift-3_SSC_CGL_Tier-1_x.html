1. A pair of straight lines from an external point F intersects a circle at A and B (FA < FB), and touches the circle at C. O is the centre of the circle. Given that ∠ACF = 50º and ∠AFC = 30º, find ∠AOB.

A. 80º

B. 90º

C. 100º

D. 60º

Answer C

Solution

∠ACF = 50º ∠AOB = 2 × ∠ACF ∠AOB = 2 × 50º = 100º

2. The difference between a discount of 25% and two successive discounts of 15% and 20% on a certain bill was ₹28. Find the amount of the bill.

A. ₹500

B. ₹400

C. ₹450

D. ₹550

Answer B

Solution

P × (1 - 25/100) = P × 0.75 P × (1 - 15/100) × (1 - 20/100) = P × 0.85 × 0.80 = P × 0.68 (P × 0.75) - (P × 0.68) = 28 P × 0.07 = 28 P = 28 / 0.07 = 400

9. The following pie chart shows the percentage distribution of a total of 1600 employees in different departments of a company. A. 64.3%

B. 50.7%

C. 44.9%

D. 59.1%

Answer D

Solution

Given: Total employees in the company = 1600 Percentage distribution of employees in different departments: Production = 30% Marketing = 22% Accounts = 25% IT = 15% HR = 8% Ratio of male to female employees in each department: Production = 5:3 Marketing = 7:4 Accounts = 3:7 IT = 2:3 HR = 3:5 Calculations: Calculate the number of employees in the Production department: Number of employees in Production = (30/100) × 1600 = 480 Calculate the number of female employees in each department: Production: Total employees = 480, Ratio = 5:3 (male:female) Female employees in Production = (3/8) × 480 = 180 Marketing: Total employees = (22/100) × 1600 = 352, Ratio = 7:4 (male:female) Female employees in Marketing = (4/11) × 352 = 128 Accounts: Total employees = (25/100) × 1600 = 400, Ratio = 3:7 (male:female) Female employees in Accounts = (7/10) × 400 = 280 IT: Total employees = (15/100) × 1600 = 240, Ratio = 2:3 (male:female) Female employees in IT = (3/5) × 240 = 144 HR: Total employees = (8/100) × 1600 = 128, Ratio = 3:5 (male:female) Female employees in HR = (5/8) × 128 = 80 Calculate the total number of female employees: Total female employees = 180 + 128 + 280 + 144 + 80 = 812 Calculate the percentage of Production department employees to total female employees: Percentage = (Number of employees in Production / Total female employees) × 100 Percentage = (480 / 812) × 100 ≈ 59.1% Therefore, the percentage of the number of employees in the Production department to the number of female employees of all departments taken together is approximately 59.1%.

10. Two pipes, A and B, can fill a tank in 1(1/3) hours and 2 hours, respectively. If both the pipes are opened simultaneously, then in how many hours will the tank be full?

A. 1 (1/4) hours

B. 55 minutes

C. 48 minutes

D. 1 (2/3) hours

Answer C

Solution

Rate of A = 1 / (4/3) = 3/4 tank/hr Rate of B = 1 / 2 = 1/2 tank/hr Combined rate = (3/4) + (1/2) = (3/4) + (2/4) = 5/4 tank/hr Time to fill = 1 / (5/4) = 4/5 hrs = 48 mins

12. A boatman goes 2 km against the current of the stream in 1 hour and goes 1 km along with the current in 10 minutes. How long will it take to go 6 km in stationary water?

A. 1 hr 30 min

B. 1 hr 15 min

C. 2 hr 30 min

D. 1 hr 45 min

Answer A

Solution

Speed against current = 2 km/hr Speed with current = 6 km/hr Speed in stationary water = (2 + 6) / 2 = 4 km/hr Time for 6 km = 6 / 4 = 1.5 hours = 1 hr 30 min

C. 75.7%

D. 63.8%

Answer B

Solution

A. 32.38

D. 26.24

Solution

D. 4.21

Answer C

Solution

18. The proportion of Suresh's spending to saving is 3 : 1. His salary rises by 25%. What percentage of his expenditure will increase if his saving grows by 10%?

A. 40%

B. 20%

C. 10%

D. 30%

Answer D

Solution

Old saving = S Old spending = 3S Old salary = 4S New salary = 4S × 1.25 = 5S New saving = S × 1.10 = 1.1S New spending = 5S - 1.1S = 3.9S Percentage Increase in Spending = (3.9S - 3S)/(3S) × 100% = (0.9S/3S) × 100% = 0.3 × 100% = 30%

19. The table below shows the production of three types of cars (A, B and C) manufactured (in thousands) by an automobile company over the years. Year A B C 2012 1750 1880 1900 2013 1800 2950 2070 2014 2950 3020 3150 2015 3100 3240 3290 The average number of A-type cars produced by the company during 2012 to 2014 is what percent (rounded off to the 1 decimal place) less than the combined average number of B-type and C-type cars produced by the company from 2013 to 2015?

A. 36.6%

B. 26.6%

C. 28.4%

D. 25.5%

Answer B

Solution

Given: Production of A-type cars in 2012: 1750 (thousand) Production of A-type cars in 2013: 1800 (thousand) Production of A-type cars in 2014: 2950 (thousand) Production of B-type cars in 2013: 2950 (thousand) Production of B-type cars in 2014: 3020 (thousand) Production of B-type cars in 2015: 3240 (thousand) Production of C-type cars in 2013: 2070 (thousand) Production of C-type cars in 2014: 3150 (thousand) Production of C-type cars in 2015: 3290 (thousand) Calculations: Calculate the average production of A-type cars (2012-2014): Average production of A-type cars = (1750 + 1800 + 2950) / 3 = 6500/3 Calculate the combined average production of B-type and C-type cars (2013-2015): Combined average production of B-type cars and C-type cars = (2950 + 3020 + 3240 + 2070 + 3150 + 3290) / 6 = 17,720/6 = 8860/3 Calculate the difference between the averages: Difference = 8860/3 - 6500/3 = 2360/3 Calculate the percentage difference: Percentage difference = (2360/3 / 8860/3) × 100 = 26.6% (rounded to 1 decimal place) Therefore, the average number of A-type cars produced by the company during 2012 to 2014 is 26.6% less than the combined average number of B-type and C-type cars produced by the company from 2013 to 2015.
