17. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. By whom were you injured?

A. Who has injured you?

B. Who injured you?

C. Who had injured you?

D. Who had been injuring you?

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is: Option 2 (Who injured you?). The conversion is Passive to Active. Explanation: The original sentence is in Passive voice (Past Indefinite: "By whom were you injured?" - Object + was/were + V3 + by + Subject). The correct answer converts this to Active voice in Past Indefinite (Subject + V2 + Object). Here, "Who" becomes the subject performing the action (injured), and "you" remains the object.

20. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. Were the plants being grafted by the gardener?

A. Was the gardener grafting the plants?

B. Was the gardener grafted the plants?

C. Were the plants grafting the gardener?

D. Were the gardener grafting the plants?

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Option 1. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 1. Correct Sentence: "Was the gardener grafting the plants?" Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the passive voice, the sentence is: "Were the plants being grafted by the gardener?"

• To convert to active voice: Place the subject "the gardener" at the beginning.

• Change the passive verb form "were being grafted" to "was grafting" (past continuous active).

• Ensure the object "the plants" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 2: "Was the gardener grafted the plants?" – Incorrect as "grafted" is a past participle and does not form a valid sentence here.

• Option 3: "Were the plants grafting the gardener?" – Incorrect as it reverses the subject and object.

• Option 4: "Were the gardener grafting the plants?" – Incorrect due to subject-verb agreement ("were" does not match "the gardener").
