• It is named after a German professor of meteorology, Wilhelm von Bezold.

• He discovered that a colour may appear different depending on its relation to adjacent colours.

• It happens when small areas of colour are interspersed.

• Domino effect It is a cumulative effect produced when one event initiates a succession of similar events.

• It occurs when a significant change in the abundance (or presence) of one species results in a further change to the species composition or relative abundance of other species in a community.
