A. Neither conclusion I nor II follows.

B. Both conclusions I and II follow.

C. Only conclusion II follows.

D. Only conclusion I follows.

Answer D

Solution

Conclusions: I. Some trousers are shirts → Follow (As some shirts are pants, all pants are jackets and all jackets are trousers. As whole pants comes in jackets and whole jacket comes in trousers and some part of pants is common with shirts, therefore it is true.) II. All jackets are shirts → Does not follow (As some shirts are pants and all pants are jackets. So it is possible but not definite.) ∴ Here, Only conclusion I follows.
