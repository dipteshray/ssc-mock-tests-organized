A. 89

B. 84

C. 101

D. 97

Answer D

Solution

Given equation: 61 − 42 × 6 ÷ 9 + 27 = ? After interchanging the signs then: ⇒ 61 + 42 ÷ 6 × 9 - 27 = ? ⇒ ? = 61 + 7 × 9 - 27 ⇒ ? = 61 + 63 - 27 = 124 - 27 = 97

16. In a certain code language, A + B means ‘A is the husband of B’, A − B means ‘B is the mother of A’, A × B means ‘B is the sister of A’ and A ÷ B means ‘A is the daughter of B’. Based on the above, how is Q related to T if ‘P + Q × R ÷ S ÷ T’?

A. Daughter’s husband

B. Daughter’s daughter

C. Sister

D. Mother’s mother

Answer B

Solution

P is the husband of Q, R is the sister of Q, S is the mother of R and S is the daughter of T. So, Q is the "Daughter’s daughter" of T.
