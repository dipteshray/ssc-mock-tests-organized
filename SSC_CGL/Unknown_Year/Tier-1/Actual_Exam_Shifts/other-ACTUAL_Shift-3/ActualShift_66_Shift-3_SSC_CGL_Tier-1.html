4. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. People will respect his bravado.

A. His bravado will being respected by people.

B. His bravado will have to be respected by people.

C. His bravado will be respected by people.

D. His bravado will be respect by people.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Option 3. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 3. Correct Sentence: "His bravado will be respected by people." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "People will respect his bravado."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "his bravado" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "will respect" to "will be respected" (future simple passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "people."

• Option 1: "His bravado will being respected by people." – Incorrect verb form.

• Option 2: "His bravado will have to be respected by people." – Incorrect modal usage.

• Option 4: "His bravado will be respect by people." – Incorrect verb form.

C. Stellenbosch had been known to people for its greenery and delicious street foods.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: "Stellenbosch is known to people for its greenery and delicious street foods." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "People know Stellenbosch for its greenery and delicious street foods."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "Stellenbosch" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "know" to "is known" (present simple passive).

• Add the preposition "to" followed by the original subject "people."

• Ensure the rest of the sentence remains unchanged for clarity.

• Option 1: "Stellenbosch was known to people for its greenery and delicious street foods." – Incorrect tense (past simple passive).

• Option 3: "Stellenbosch had been known to people for its greenery and delicious street foods." – Incorrect tense (past perfect passive).

• Option 4: "Stellenbosch have been known to people for its greenery and delicious street foods." – Incorrect verb form (should be "has been" instead of "have been").
