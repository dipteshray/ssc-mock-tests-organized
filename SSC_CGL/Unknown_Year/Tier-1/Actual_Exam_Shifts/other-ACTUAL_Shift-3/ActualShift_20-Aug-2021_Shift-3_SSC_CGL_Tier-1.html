26. ______ is the water transporting tissue in plants.

D. Xylem

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Xylem. Key Points Additional Information

• Xylem is the water transporting tissue in plants.

• Xylem is one of two types of transport tissue in vascular plants. The basic function of the xylem is to transport water from the root to the stem and leaves, but it also transports nutrients.

• The term xylem was introduced by Carl Nagelei in 1858, derived from an ancient Greek word meaning wood.

• Phloem is the living tissue in vascular plants that transports soluble organic compounds formed during photosynthesis and known as photosynthesis, especially the sugar sucrose, to parts of the plant where needed. This transport process is called transfer.

• In a vascular plant, the stele is the central part of the root or stem containing the tissues derived from the procambium.

• The cortex is the outer layer of the stem or root in a plant, which lies beneath the epidermis but outside the vascular bundles. It is responsible for transporting material to the central cylinder of the root via diffusion and can also be used for food storage in the form of starch.
