3. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. Martha made pancakes yesterday.

A. Pancakes had been made by Martha yesterday.

B. Pancakes were being made by Martha yesterday.

C. Pancakes were made by Martha yesterday.

D. Pancakes was made by Martha yesterday.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Option 3. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 3. Correct Sentence: "Pancakes were made by Martha yesterday." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "Martha made pancakes yesterday."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "pancakes" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "made" to "were made" (past simple passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "Martha."

• Ensure the time phrase "yesterday" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "Pancakes had been made by Martha yesterday." – Incorrect tense (past perfect passive).

• Option 2: "Pancakes were being made by Martha yesterday." – Incorrect tense (past continuous passive).

• Option 4: "Pancakes was made by Martha yesterday." – Subject-verb agreement error.
