1. Parts of a sentence are given below in jumbled order. Arrange the parts in the correct order to form a meaningful sentence. P. having the opportunity of observing people's Q. we respect and appreciate their point of views R. the advantage of travelling different places and S. culture, custom and lifestyle, is that

A. RQPS

B. PSQR

C. RPSQ

D. SQPR

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: RPSQ. The sentence begins with the subject "the advantage of travelling different places and" (R), followed by the gerund phrase "having the opportunity of observing people's" (P). This leads into the objects of observation, "culture, custom and lifestyle, is that" (S), and concludes with the result of that advantage, "we respect and appreciate their point of views" (Q). The complete sentence is: "The advantage of travelling different places and having the opportunity of observing people's culture, custom and lifestyle, is that we respect and appreciate their point of views."

4. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. The painting was admired by art enthusiasts from around the world.

A. The painting was being admired by art enthusiasts from around the world.

B. Art enthusiasts from around the world admired the painting.

C. Art enthusiasts from around the world have admired the painting.

D. The painting has been admired by art enthusiasts from around the world.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is: Art enthusiasts from around the world admired the painting. The conversion from the question to the answer is Passive to Active. In the passive voice sentence, "The painting" is the subject receiving the action, and "was admired by art enthusiasts from around the world" indicates the action being performed on it. To convert it to active voice, "art enthusiasts from around the world" becomes the subject performing the action, and "admired the painting" becomes the action they performed on the object, "the painting." The structure changes from Passive (Object + was/were + V3 + by + Subject) to Active (Subject + V2 + Object).

8. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. He will be made President by the people.

A. People will be making him the President.

B. The people will make him President.

C. By the people, he shall be made the President.

D. He will be made President by the people.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is: The people will make him President. The sentence is in the Passive voice of the Future Indefinite tense (Subject + will be + V3 + by + Object). To convert it into Active voice, the structure follows: Subject (The people) + will + V1 (make) + Object (him) + Complement (President).
