• Right to freedom (Articles 19–22)

• Right against exploitation (Articles 23–24)

• Right to freedom of religion (Articles 25–28)

• Cultural and educational rights (Articles 29–30)

• Right to constitutional remedies (Article 32)

• Part IV Part IV (Article 36-51) of the Indian Constitution is related to the Directive Principles of our State Policy.

• It is not enforceable by any court but these are fundamental in the governance of the country.

• The government must apply these principles in law-making.

• These principles have been taken from the Constitution of Ireland.

• Part IX Part IX of the Indian Constitution contains provisions for the Panchayats.

• Part IX tells about a three-tier Panchayati Raj System which would be constituted in every state at the village level, intermediate level, and district level.

33. The Council of Ministers shall not exceed ______% of the total number of members of the Assembly of States.

A. 15

B. 20

C. 12

D. 10

Answer A

Solution

The maximum number of ministers, including the Chief Minister, in the Council of Ministers in a state cannot exceed 15% of the total number of members of the Legislative Assembly of that state. This means that if a state has 100 members in the Legislative Assembly, the Council of Ministers can have a maximum of 15 ministers. This provision ensures that the executive does not become too large and remains accountable to the legislature. It also ensures that there is a balance of power between the executive and the legislature.
