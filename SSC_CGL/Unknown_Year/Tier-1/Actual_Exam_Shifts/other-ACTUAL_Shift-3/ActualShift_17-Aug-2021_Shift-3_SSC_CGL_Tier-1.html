20. Who among the following economists formulated the Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility (DMU)?

A. Adam Smith

B. David Ricardo

C. Milton Friedman

D. HH Gossen

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is HH Gossen. Key Points Important Points

• HH Gossen formulated the Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility (DMU). He formulated this law in 1854.

• Marginal Utility is the change in total utility due to a one-unit change in the level of consumption.

• Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility It states that as a person consumes an item or a product, the satisfaction or utility that they derive from the product declines as they consume more and more of that product.

• In other words, the additional utility gained from an increase in consumption decreases with each subsequent increase in the level of consumption.

• Demand curves are downward-sloping in microeconomic models since each additional unit of a good or service is put toward a less valuable use.

• Marketers use the law of diminishing marginal utility because they want to keep marginal utility high for products that they sell.

89. Select the most appropriate option to fill in blank number 5.

A. accomplice

B. colleague

C. admirer

D. adventurer

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is 'accomplice.' Key Points Hence, the correct answer is option 1. The complete passage is- Ben committed (1) a crime when he (2) robbed a bank. Someone (3) witnessed the crime and told the police. The police charged him (4) with the crime and put him behind bars. They also accused his twin brother, Bill, of being his (5) accomplice. Additional Information Hinglish अत: सही उत्तर विकल्प 1 है।

• The given sentence "They also accused his twin brother, Bill, of being his ___(5)___.'' is talking about accusing Bill of being something.

• The use of the word ''accused'' in the sentence indicates to charge someone with an offense or crime.

• Therefore, the most appropriate word to be filled in the blank is 'accomplice'.

• The word 'accomplice' means a person who helps somebody to do something bad, especially a crime. Example: She was her husband's accomplice in murdering a rich old man.

• Let us explore the other options: 'Colleague' means a person who works at the same place as you. (सहकर्मी)

• 'Progress' means to become better; to develop well. (प्रगति करना; विकास करना)

• 'Decline' means to become weaker, smaller, or less good. (किसी का ह्रास होना, क्षीण होना)
