15. By Interchanging the given two numbers which of the following equation will be correct? 5 and 9

B. 9 × 3 + 5 ÷ 1 – 6 = 20

C. 7 × 9 + 5 – 8 ÷ 4 = 40

D. 9 × 4 + 5 – 8 ÷ 2 = 25

Answer D

Solution

1) 9 – 5 × 4 + 6 ÷ 3 = 30 After interchanging the numbers 5 and 9, we get 5 – 9 × 4 + 6 ÷ 3 = 30 Applying the BODMAS Rule, we get 5 – 9 × 4 + 2 = 30 ⇒ 5 – 36 + 2 = 30 ⇒ 7 - 36 = 30 ⇒ - 29 ≠ 30 The given equation is incorrect. 2) 9 × 3 + 5 ÷ 1 – 6 = 20 After interchanging the numbers 5 and 9, we get 5 × 3 + 9 ÷ 1 – 6 = 20 Applying the BODMAS Rule, we get 5 × 3 + 9 – 6 = 20 ⇒ 15 + 9 - 6 = 20 ⇒ 24 - 6 = 20 ⇒ 18 ≠ 20 The given equation is incorrect. 3) 7 × 9 + 5 – 8 ÷ 4 = 40 After interchanging the numbers 5 and 9, we get 7 × 5 + 9 – 8 ÷ 4 = 40 Applying the BODMAS Rule, we get 7 × 5 + 9 – 2= 40 ⇒ 35 + 9 - 2 = 40 ⇒ 44 - 2 = 40 ⇒ 42 ≠ 40 The given equation is incorrect. 4) 9 × 4 + 5 – 8 ÷ 2 = 25 After interchanging the numbers 5 and 9, we get 5 × 4 + 9 – 8 ÷ 2 = 25 Applying the BODMAS Rule, we get 5 × 4 + 9 – 4 = 25 ⇒ 20 + 9 – 4 = 25 ⇒ 29 - 4 = 25 ⇒ 25 = 25 The given equation is correct. Solution
