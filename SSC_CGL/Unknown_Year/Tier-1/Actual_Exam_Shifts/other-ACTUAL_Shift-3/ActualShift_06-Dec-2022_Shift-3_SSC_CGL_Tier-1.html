42. The Balance of Payment Account of an economy is related to the ________.

A. agriculture sector

B. external sector

C. government sector

D. private sector

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is the external sector. The balance of payments account summarizes an economy's international economic dealings, including the export and import of products, services, financial assets, and transfer payments. The BOP has three subcategories: current account, capital account, and financial account. The current account represents the influx and outflow of goods and services, while the capital account notes all overseas capital transfers. The financial account records international monetary flows associated with investments. The BOP should be 0 when the current account is in balance with the total of the capital and finance accounts.
