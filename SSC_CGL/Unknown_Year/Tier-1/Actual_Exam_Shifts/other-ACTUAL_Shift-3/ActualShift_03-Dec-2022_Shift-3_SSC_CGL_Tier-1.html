24. Which Article deals with the 'Power of Parliament to amend the Constitution and procedure therefor'?

A. Article 352

B. Article 249

C. Article 368

D. Article 356

Answer C

Solution

Article 368 of the Indian Constitution deals with the power of Parliament to amend the Constitution and the procedure for doing so. It states that Parliament may amend any provision of the Constitution by way of addition, variation, or repeal, but only through the introduction of a Bill in either House of Parliament. The Bill must be passed by a two-thirds majority of members present and voting in each House before being presented to the President for assent.
