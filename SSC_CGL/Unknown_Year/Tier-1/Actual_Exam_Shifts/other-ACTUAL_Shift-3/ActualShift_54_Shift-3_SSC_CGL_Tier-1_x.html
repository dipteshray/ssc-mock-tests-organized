7. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. The letter was being typed by the secretary when the phone rang.

A. The secretary will type the letter when the phone rings.

B. The secretary types the letter when the phone rings.

C. The secretary was typing the letter when the phone rang.

D. The secretary is typing the letter when the phone rings.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: The secretary was typing the letter when the phone rang. The given sentence is in the Passive Voice of the Past Continuous tense (Subject + was/were + being + V3 + by + Object). To convert it into the Active Voice, the structure should be: Subject (the secretary) + was/were + V1-ing (typing) + Object (the letter). Thus, Option C is the correct transformation.

15. Sentences of a paragraph are given below in jumbled order. Arrange the sentences in the correct order to form a meaningful and coherent paragraph. A. A big fancy dress ball was arranged to celebrate their homecoming. B. It must have been a terrible shock for a young girl. C. They had been married a month, just home from their honeymoon. D. Just as the guests were starting to arrive, Charnley locked himself into his bedroom and shot himself.

A. BCDA

B. ABCD

C. BCAD

D. DACB

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: 3) BCAD. Explanation of the logical order: Sentence B serves as the opening reflection, introducing the central emotional tone and topic: "It must have been a terrible shock for a young girl." Sentence C follows directly to provide background context about who the girl is and her circumstances: "They had been married a month, just home from their honeymoon." Sentence A sets up the specific occasion being celebrated by the couple: "A big fancy dress ball was arranged to celebrate their homecoming." Sentence D completes the sequence by revealing the tragic event occurring at the party that justifies the initial statement: "Just as the guests were starting to arrive, Charnley locked himself into his bedroom and shot himself." Therefore, the sequence BCAD establishes the thematic frame, gives the background, describes the setting, and delivers the climax.

16. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. He must deliver pizza.

A. Pizza has to be delivered by him.

B. Pizza have to be delivered by him.

C. Pizza must be delivered by him.

D. Pizza can be delivered by him.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: Pizza must be delivered by him. The conversion is from Active to Passive. The Active voice in the question is in Present Modal (Subject + must + V1 + Object). Thus, the Passive voice will be in Present Modal (Object of the active voice + must be + V3 + by + Subject of the active voice).
