2. If 12 @ 2 = 216 and 16 @ 4 = 64, then what is the value of 10 @ 2

A. 100

B. 125

C. 150

D. 216

Answer B

Solution

Logic: The operation @ represents division, and the final result is the cube of that quotient. Formula: (first number ÷ second number)3 For, 12 @ 2 ⇒ (12 ÷ 2)3 = 63 = 216 16 @ 4 ⇒ (16 ÷ 4)3 = 43 = 64 Similarly, 10 @ 2 ⇒ (10 ÷ 2)3 = 53 = 125 So, the value of 10 @ 2 is 125. Hence, the correct answer is "Option 2".

3. Choose the correct mathematical signs to swap so that the following equation becomes correct. 60 ÷ 12 + 6 – 3 × 2 = 5

A. ÷ and ×

B. + and –

C. ÷ and +

D. – and ×

Answer B

Solution

Given equation: 60 ÷ 12 + 6 – 3 × 2 = 5 Let's check each option one by one. Option 1) ÷ and × Given: 60 ÷ 12 + 6 – 3 × 2 = 5 After interchanging the signs we get; ⇒ 60 × 12 + 6 – 3 ÷ 2 = 5 ⇒ 60 × 12 + 6 – 1.5 = 5 ⇒ 720 + 6 – 1.5 = 5 ⇒ 726 – 1.5 = 5 ⇒ 724.5 ≠ 5 ⇒ LHS ≠ RHS Option 2) + and – Given: 60 ÷ 12 + 6 – 3 × 2 = 5 After interchanging the signs we get; ⇒ 60 ÷ 12 – 6 + 3 × 2 = 5 ⇒ 5 – 6 + 3 × 2 = 5 ⇒ 5 + 6 – 6 = 5 ⇒ 11 – 6 = 5 ⇒ 5 = 5 ⇒ LHS = RHS Option 3) ÷ and + Given: 60 ÷ 12 + 6 – 3 × 2 = 5 After interchanging the signs we get; ⇒ 60 + 12 ÷ 6 – 3 × 2 = 5 ⇒ 60 + 2 – 3 × 2 = 5 ⇒ 60 + 2 – 6 = 5 ⇒ 62 – 6 = 5 ⇒ 56 ≠ 5 ⇒ LHS ≠ RHS Option 4) – and × Given: 60 ÷ 12 + 6 – 3 × 2 = 5 After interchanging the signs we get; ⇒ 60 ÷ 12 + 6 × 3 – 2 = 5 ⇒ 5 + 6 × 3 – 2 = 5 ⇒ 5 + 18 – 2 = 5 ⇒ 23 – 2 = 5 ⇒ 21 ≠ 5 ⇒ LHS ≠ RHS Hence, the correct answer is "Option 2".
