17. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. The chef prepares the meal with great care.

A. The meal was prepared with great care by the chef.

B. The meal will be prepared with great care by the chef.

C. The meal is prepared with great care by the chef.

D. The meal has been prepared with great care by the chef.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is Option 3. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 3. Correct Sentence: "The meal is prepared with great care by the chef." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "The chef prepares the meal with great care."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "the meal" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "prepares" to "is prepared" (present simple passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "the chef."

• Ensure the phrase "with great care" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "The meal was prepared with great care by the chef." – Incorrect tense (past simple passive).

• Option 2: "The meal will be prepared with great care by the chef." – Incorrect tense (future simple passive).

• Option 4: "The meal has been prepared with great care by the chef." – Incorrect tense (present perfect passive).
