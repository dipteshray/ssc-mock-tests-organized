1. In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements. Statements: All phones are TVs. Some TVs are cars. Some big are cars. Conclusions: I. Some TVs are phones. II. Some big are TVs. III. All big are cars.

A. Only conclusions I follows.

B. None of the conclusions follows.

C. Only conclusion III follows.

D. Only conclusions II and III follow.

Answer A

Solution

The least possible Venn diagram is as follows: Conclusions: I. Some TVs are phones. - True (because All phones are TVs given. So, Some TVs are phones too) II. Some big are TVs. - False (because there is no direct relation between big and TVs. So, it can be possible but not definite) III. All big are cars. - False (because Some big are cars given. So, it can be possible but not definite).

A. 13

B. 12

C. 14

D. 11

Answer A

Solution

Given equation: 95 − 19 × 4 + 6 ÷ 16 = ? After interchanging the signs then: ⇒ 95 ÷ 19 + 4 × 6 - 16 = ? ⇒ ? = 5 + 4 × 6 - 16 ⇒ ? = 5 + 24 - 16 ⇒ ? = 29 - 16 = 13

Solution
