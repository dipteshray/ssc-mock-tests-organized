20. Which of the following elements is a metalloid?

C. Sodium

D. Silicon

Answer D

Solution

Silicon is the metalloid among the given elements, possessing properties of both metals and nonmetals. It has a metallic luster but is a poor conductor of electricity. Silicon is significant in industry, used to make alloys with iron and aluminum, particularly in the steel and automotive sectors. 21. Which country has the Republic feature of the Indian Constitution been borrowed from?

A. French Constitution

B. Irish Constitution

C. British Constitution

D. Australian Constitution

Answer A

Solution

The Republic feature of the Indian Constitution was borrowed from the French Constitution, which emphasizes the ideals of liberty, equality, and fraternity, originating from the French Revolution. These principles are enshrined in the Preamble and underline the importance of democratic values, republicanism, and the sovereignty of the people. Additionally, the Indian Constitution incorporates elements from various other constitutions: amendment procedures and Rajya Sabha elections from South Africa, legal procedures from Japan, the concurrent list and parliamentary procedures from Australia, federal structure from Canada, and Directive Principles from Ireland, along with emergency provisions from Germany.

25. As of 2021, which is the world's largest drainage basin with an area of about 70,00,000 km2?

A. Congo basin

B. Amur basin

C. Amazon basin

D. Nile basin

Answer C

Solution

The Amazon River Basin is the world's largest drainage basin, covering approximately 7,000,000 square kilometers and located in South America, including parts of Brazil, Peru, Colombia, Ecuador, Bolivia, Suriname, French Guiana, and Venezuela. It is more than twice the size of the Congo River basin and covers about 35.5% of the South American continent. The basin hosts the world's largest rainforest, Amazonia, which contains a vast diversity of plant and animal species. Additionally, it accounts for about one-fifth of the global freshwater runoff. The Congo, Amur, and Nile basins are also significant but smaller river basins with unique ecosystems and resources.

34. Which cell organelle is defined as the small round organelle that undergoes oxidation reaction to produce hydrogen peroxide?

A. Centrosome

B. Vacuole

C. Nucleus

D. Peroxisomes

Answer D

Solution

Peroxisomes are small, membrane-bound organelles containing enzymes crucial for various metabolic reactions, such as the oxidation of fatty acids and amino acids. They play a significant role in detoxifying harmful substances and in lipid metabolism. During these processes, peroxisomes produce hydrogen peroxide, which is subsequently broken down by the enzyme catalase. In contrast, centrosomes assist in cell division and the formation of cilia and flagella, vacuoles store water and molecules in plant cells, and the nucleus regulates cellular activities and differentiates cells into prokaryotic or eukaryotic based on its presence.

35. The cause of a redox reaction is the:

A. transfer of electrons between two reactants

B. transfer of electrons between two products

C. transfer of neutrons between two reactants

D. exchange of halogens between two reactants

Answer A

Solution

Redox reactions are chemical reactions where the oxidation states of atoms change, involving the transfer of electrons between substances. In these reactions, one substance undergoes oxidation (loses electrons) while another undergoes reduction (gains electrons). Examples include Zn + CuSO4 → ZnSO4 + Cu, where Zn is oxidized and Cu is reduced, and 2KClO3 + I2 → 2KIO3 + Cl2, where Cl is reduced and I is oxidized. Another example is H2 + Cl2 → 2HCl, where H is oxidized and Cl is reduced. These examples illustrate the fundamental principle of redox reactions: the transfer of electrons between reactants.

40. If assets of a bank are greater than liabilities, it will be recorded as:

A. liabilities

B. net worth

C. reserves

D. assets

Answer B

Solution

Net worth is a financial metric indicating the overall value of an individual, company, or entity, calculated by subtracting total liabilities from total assets. A positive net worth reflects financial health, whereas a negative net worth shows that debts exceed assets. Assets are valuable items owned, such as real estate, vehicles, investments, and bank accounts. Liabilities are financial obligations or debts, including mortgages, loans, and credit card debt. When a bank’s assets exceed its liabilities, this surplus is recorded as net worth, reflecting the bank's financial stability.
