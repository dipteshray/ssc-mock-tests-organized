1. The ratio of the ages of A and B, 3 years ago, was 5 : 7. The ratio of their ages, 13 years from now, will be 9 : 11. If the present age of C is 11 years less than that of B, then the present age of C, in years, is:

A. 23

B. 36

C. 31

D. 20

Answer D

Solution

Let the present ages of A and B be A and B respectively. ⇒ (A - 3) / (B - 3) = 5 / 7 ⇒ 7A - 5B = 6 .....(1) ⇒ (A + 13) / (B + 13) = 9 / 11 ⇒ 11A - 9B = -26 ....(2) ⇒ A = 23 and B = 31 ⇒ C = B - 11 = 20 years.

21. Two pipes A and B can fill a tank in 20 and 30 hours, respectively. Both pipes are opened to fill the tank, but when the tank is one-third full, a leak develops through which one-fourth of the water supplied by both pipes goes out. Find the total time (in hours) taken to fill the tank.

A. 14 (2/3)

B. 14

C. 11 (2/5)

D. 12 (1/3)

Answer A

Solution

Rate of A = 1/20, Rate of B = 1/30, Combined rate = 1/12 tank/hour Time to fill 1/3 tank = (1/3) / (1/12) = 4 hours Effective rate with leak = (3/4) × (1/12) = 1/16 tank/hour Time to fill remaining 2/3 tank = (2/3) / (1/16) = 32/3 hours Total time = 4 + (32/3) = 4 + 102/3 = 142/3 hours
