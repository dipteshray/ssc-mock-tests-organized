46. The speed of a boat down the stream is 125% of the speed in still water. If the boat takes 30 minutes to cover 20 km in still water, then how much time (in hours) will it take to cover 15 km upstream?

B. (1/2)

C. (1/4)

Solution

Alternate Method Given: ⇒ Speed downstream = 125% of speed in still water. ⇒ Time taken to cover 20 km in still water = 30 minutes = 0.5 hours. Additional Information Speed in Still Water It is the average of downstream and upstream speeds: B = (D + U) ÷ 2 Speed of Stream It is half the difference between downstream and upstream speeds: S = (D − U) ÷ 2 Average Speed of Journey If a boat goes a certain distance downstream and comes back upstream, the average speed is (2 × D × U) ÷ (D + U).
