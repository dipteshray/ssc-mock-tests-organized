22. Fundamental duties were not placed in Part III of the Indian Constitution because Part III is ______.

A. non-justiciable

B. justiciable

C. not enforceable

D. absolute

Answer B

Solution

Fundamental duties were not placed in Part III of the Indian Constitution because Part III is justiciable. Meaning, the rights included in Part III of the Indian Constitution (which contain the fundamental rights of Indian citizens) are legally enforceable by the courts. This contrasts with the fundamental duties, which are moral obligations for all citizens but are not legally enforced by the court of law.
