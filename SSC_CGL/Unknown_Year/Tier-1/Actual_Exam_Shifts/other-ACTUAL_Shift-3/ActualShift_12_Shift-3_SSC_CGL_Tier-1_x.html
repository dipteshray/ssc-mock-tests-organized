Solution

6. The average salary of all employees in an office is 18,000. Managers earn an average of 30,000 and workers earn 12,000. If there are 10 managers, how many workers are there?

A. 20

B. 25

C. 30

D. 40

Answer A

Solution

Let the number of workers = x. ⇒ 18000(10 + x) = 300000 + 12000x ⇒ 180000 + 18000x = 300000 + 12000x ⇒ 6000x = 120000 ⇒ x = 20 ∴ The correct answer is 20 workers.

7. Three numbers are such that the sum of any two plus one-third of the third gives 180, 174 and 168 respectively. What is the average of the three numbers?

A. 72(4/7)

B. 74(4/7)

C. 60(4/7)

D. 54(4/7)

Answer B

Solution

Let the three numbers be a, b, c. a + b + c ÷ 3 = 180, b + c + a ÷ 3 = 174, c + a + b ÷ 3 = 168. ⇒ 2(a + b + c) + (a + b + c) ÷ 3 = 522. ⇒ (7 ÷ 3)(a + b + c) = 522. ⇒ a + b + c = (522 × 3 ÷ 7) = 1566 ÷ 7. ⇒ Average = (1566 ÷ 7) ÷ 3 = 522 ÷ 7 = 74(4 ÷ 7). ∴ The average of the three numbers is 74(4 ÷ 7).

11. A shopkeeper buys pens at 6 for ₹30, 5 for ₹28, and 4 for ₹24. He mixes them in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5 and sells the mixture at 5 for ₹34. Find his approximate gain or loss percentage.

A. Loss 8%

B. Profit 20%

C. Profit 10%

D. Loss 10%

Answer B

Solution

⇒ Total pens = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 ⇒ Total cost = (2 × 5) + (3 × 5.6) + (5 × 6) = ₹56.8 ⇒ Cost price per pen = 56.8 ÷ 10 = ₹5.68 ⇒ Selling price per pen = ₹6.8 ⇒ Gain per pen = 6.8 − 5.68 = ₹1.12 ⇒ Gain % = (1.12 ÷ 5.68) × 100 ≈ 19.7% ∴ Approximate gain = 20%

12. A trader sells three types of bags such that the ratio of their selling prices is 2 : 3 : 5. He makes a gain of 20% on the first, 10% on the second, and a loss of 10% on the third. Find his approximate overall gain or loss percentage.

A. Loss 2%

B. Profit 2%

C. Loss 4%

D. Profit 0.5%

Answer D

Solution

Let selling prices be 2x, 3x, 5x. CP of first = 2x ÷ 1.2 = 1.667x, CP of second = 3x ÷ 1.1 = 2.727x, CP of third = 5x ÷ 0.9 = 5.556x. Total SP = 2x + 3x + 5x = 10x, Total CP = 1.667x + 2.727x + 5.556x = 9.95x. Gain = 10x − 9.95x = 0.05x, Gain % = (0.05x ÷ 9.95x) × 100 ≈ 0.5%.

The final selling price is ₹4200, and the shopkeeper still makes a profit of 5% on the cost price after all discounts. Find the cost price of the jacket and the value of x.

A. CP 3,800 and x = 10

B. CP 4,000 and x = 12.5

C. CP 3,600 and x = 8

D. CP 4,200 and x = 6

Answer B

Solution

14. A and B can do a job in 10 and 15 days respectively. They work together for 3 days and then B leaves. How many more days will A take to finish the work?

A. 4

B. 5

C. 6

D. 7

Answer B

Solution

⇒ LCM of 10 and 15 = 30 units (Total work) ⇒ A's 1 day work = 30 ÷ 10 = 3 units/day, B's 1 day work = 30 ÷ 15 = 2 units/day ⇒ Work done in 3 days = (3 + 2) × 3 = 15 units ⇒ Remaining work = 30 − 15 = 15 units ⇒ Required days = 15 ÷ 3 = 5 days

16. A and B together can complete a work in 24 days. After working together for 8 days, B alone finishes the remaining work in 32 days. In how many days can B alone do the entire work?

A. 36

B. 40

C. 48

D. 60

Answer C

Solution

Total work = 24 units (LCM of time values). Work completed in 8 days by (A + B) = 8 × 1 = 8 units; Remaining work = 24 − 8 = 16 units. B's 1 day work = 16 ÷ 32 = 0.5 units/day; Time taken by B alone = 24 ÷ 0.5 = 48 days.

17. A vessel contains milk, water and juice in the ratio 4 : 5 : 3. 24 litres of mixture is removed and replaced by 10 litres of milk and 6 litres of juice. If water becomes 12 litres more than milk, find the initial quantity.

A. 180

B. 192

C. 288

D. 240

Answer C

Solution

Let initial total quantity = 12x litres ⇒ Milk = 4x, Water = 5x, Juice = 3x. Fraction removed = 24 ÷ 12x = 2 ÷ x. Milk removed = 4x × 2 ÷ x = 8, Water removed = 5x × 2 ÷ x = 10, Juice removed = 3x × 2 ÷ x = 6. Milk left = 4x − 8 + 10 = 4x + 2, Water left = 5x − 10, Juice left = 3x. Given: Water = Milk + 12 ⇒ 5x − 10 = (4x + 2) + 12 ⇒ 5x − 10 = 4x + 14 ⇒ x = 24. Initial quantity = 12x = 12 × 24 = 288 litres.

18. A car covers a distance at 72 km per hour in 2 hours. If it takes 30 minutes more due to traffic, by what fraction should the speed be reduced?

A. 1/8

B. 1/6

C. 1/5

D. 1/4

Answer C

Solution

⇒ Distance = 72 × 2 = 144 km ⇒ New time = 2 + 0.5 = 2.5 hours ⇒ New speed = 144 ÷ 2.5 = 57.6 km/h ⇒ Fraction reduced = (72 − 57.6) ÷ 72 = 14.4 ÷ 72 = 1 ÷ 5 ∴ The correct answer is 1/5.
