21. Which group does not match the pattern??

A. 12, 19, 31

B. 16, 24, 40

C. 21, 35, 56

D. 10, 20, 40

Answer D

Solution

The logic followed here is: For each group, we check if the sum of the first number and the second number equals the third number. Option 1) 12, 19, 31 → 12 + 19 = 31 (Matches the pattern) Option 1) 16, 24, 40 → 16 + 24 = 40 (Matches the pattern) Option 1) 21, 35, 56 → 21 + 35 = 56 (Matches the pattern) Option 1) 10, 20, 40 → 10 + 20 = 30 (Does not match, as the third number is 40) Whereas, In Option 4, the sum of 10 and 20 is 30, but the given third number is 40. So, 10, 20, 40 is odd from the given options. Hence, the correct answer is "Option 4".
