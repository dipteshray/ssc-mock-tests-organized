17. Change the following from active to passive:The editors consider such actions unethical.

A. Such actions are considered unethical by the editors.

B. Editors are considered unethical.

C. Such actions considered unethical.

D. Ethics are considered by editors.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is '1' i.e. Such actions are considered unethical by the editors. . Key Points Correct sentence: Such actions are considered unethical by the editors. Additional Information

• The given sentence is in the active voice: The editors consider such actions unethical.

• To change a sentence from active to passive voice: The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence.

• The verb form is changed to its past participle form, preceded by an appropriate form of the verb "to be."

• The subject of the active sentence is introduced by the preposition "by."

• Following this process: Active voice: The editors (subject) consider (verb) such actions (object) unethical.

• Passive voice: Such actions (subject) are considered (verb) unethical by the editors (agent).

• Active Voice: The subject performs the action described by the verb. Structure: Subject + Verb + Object.

• Example: The teacher explains the lesson.

• Passive Voice: The subject receives the action described by the verb. Structure: Object (of the active sentence) + appropriate form of "to be" + past participle + "by" + Subject (of the active sentence).

• Example: The lesson is explained by the teacher.

22. Choose the correct direct speech:The commander instructed the soldiers to withdraw immediately.

A. “Withdraw immediately,” ordered the commander.

B. “Soldiers must withdraw,” he said.

C. “They should withdraw now,” said the commander.

D. “Immediate withdrawal,” said the commander.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is '1' i.e. “Withdraw immediately,” ordered the commander. . Key Points Correct sentence: “Withdraw immediately,” ordered the commander. Additional Information

• The given sentence is in indirect speech, where the commander is instructing the soldiers to withdraw immediately.

• In direct speech, the actual words of the speaker are enclosed in quotation marks to preserve their original meaning and tone.

• Option 1, “Withdraw immediately,” ordered the commander, is the correct direct speech form because it retains the original tone and urgency of the command.

• Other options either change the meaning, tone, or grammatical structure of the sentence:

• Key Point: Quotation marks are used to enclose the speaker’s words.

• Indirect Speech: It involves reporting what someone said without quoting their exact words.

• Example: She said that she was happy that day.

• Key Point: No quotation marks are used, and the sentence structure is often modified.

23. Choose the correct indirect speech:She said, “I had completed the assignment.”

A. She said that she had completed the assignment.

B. She said she has completed the assignment.

C. She said she completed the assignment.

D. She said she would complete the assignment.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is '1' i.e. She said that she had completed the assignment . Key Points Correct sentence: She said that she had completed the assignment. Additional Information

• The sentence is in direct speech, and we are required to convert it into indirect speech.

• The verb in the direct speech, "had completed," is in the past perfect tense.

• When converting direct speech to indirect speech, the tense of the verb remains the same if the reporting verb (in this case, "said") is in the past tense.

• Thus, the correct conversion is: "She said that she had completed the assignment."

• When converting direct to indirect speech: If the reporting verb is in the past tense, the verb in the reported speech is shifted one step back in tense.

• For example: Direct Speech: She said, "I am happy."

• Indirect Speech: She said that she was happy.

• In this case, since the verb in the direct speech is already in past perfect tense, it remains unchanged in the indirect speech.
