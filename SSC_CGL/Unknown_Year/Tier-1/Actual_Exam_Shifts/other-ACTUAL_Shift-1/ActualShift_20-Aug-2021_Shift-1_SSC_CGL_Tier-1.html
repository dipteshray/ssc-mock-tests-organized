13. The monthly income of three cricketers Ankit, Sanjay and Roshan, from different sources, are in the ratio of 12 : 9 : 7, and their expenditures are in the ratio 15 : 9 : 8. If Ankit saves 25% of his income for future investments, what is the ratio of the savings of Ankit, Sanjay and Roshan?

A. 25 : 16 : 13

B. 23 : 18 : 11

C. 5 : 8 : 7

D. 15 : 18 : 11

Answer D

Solution

GIVEN: Income ratio of Ankit, Sanjay and Roshan is respectively: 12x : 9x : 7x Expenditures ratio of Ankit, Sanjay and Roshan is respectively: 15y : 9y : 8y FORMULA: Savings = Income - Expenditures CALCULATION: Saving ratio of Ankit, Sanjay and Roshan is respectively: (12x - 15y) : (9x - 9y) : (7x - 8y) [Saving ratio = Income ratio - Ependiture ratio] Ankit saves 25% of his income, So saving of Ankit is 12x - 15y = 25% of 12x ⇒ 12x - 15y = (1/4) × 12x ⇒ 12x - 15y = 3x ⇒ 9x = 15y ⇒ (x/y) = (15/9) ⇒ (x/y) = (5/3) (divided by 3) So, x = 5; y = 3 Put the value of x and y saving of Ankit, Sanjay and Roshan, (12x -15y) : (9x - 9y) : (7x - 8y) ⇒ (12 × 5 - 15 × 3) : (9 × 5 – 9 × 3) : (7 × 5 - 8 × 3) ⇒ (60 - 45) : (45 - 27) : (35 - 24) ⇒ 15 : 18 : 11 Hence, ‘15 : 18 : 11’ is the ratio of the savings of Ankit, Sanjay and Roshan.

A. geological

B. botanical

C. physical

D. geographical

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is 'physical.' Key Points Hence, the correct answer is option 3. The complete passage is:- The five senses (1) are sight, hearing, taste, touch, and (2) smell. What is sometimes (3) referred to as a ‘sixth sense’ is a power to be aware (4) of things independently of the five (5) physical senses, a kind of supernatural sense. Additional Information Hinglish अत: सही उत्तर विकल्प 3 है। पूर्ण वाक्य: What is sometimes referred to as a ‘sixth sense' is a power to be aware of things independently of the five physical senses, a kind of supernatural sense.

• The given sentence "What is sometimes referred to as a ‘sixth sense' is a power to be aware of things independently of the five ___(5)___ senses, a kind of supernatural sense.'' is talking about the sixth sense referred independent of the senses connected with the body.

• Therefore, the most appropriate word to be filled in the blank is 'physical' because the five known senses are connected with our body.

• The word 'physical' means connected with your body rather than your mind. Example: He tends to avoid all physical contact.

• Let us explore the other options: 'Geological' means relating to the study of the earth's physical structure and substance. (भूविज्ञान-विषयक)

• 'Botanical' means relating to botany. (वनस् पति-शास् त्र संबंधित)

• 'Geographical' means based on or derived from the physical features of an area. (भूगोल-विषयक)
