24. Which of the following have mountain range young fold mountains?

B. Alps mountain range

C. Aravali mountain range

D. Ural mountain range

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2, the Alps mountain range. The Alps are classified as a young fold mountain range formed by the collision of the African and Eurasian tectonic plates. This collision caused the Earth's crust to buckle and fold, forming the Alps. The range began forming about 65 million years ago and is still rising by about 1-2 millimeters per year due to ongoing tectonic activity. The other options, such as the Appalachian Mountains, Aravali Mountain Range, and Ural Mountains, are older mountain ranges formed by different geological processes.
