7. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. Dipesh will send Rakesh away to college for certain reasons.

A. Rakesh will have to be sent away to college for certain reasons by Dipesh.

B. Rakesh will be sent away to college for certain reasons by Dipesh.

C. Rakesh would have been sent away to college for certain reasons by Dipesh.

D. Rakesh would be sent away to college for certain reasons by Dipesh.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is: Option 2. The conversion is from Active to Passive voice. Explanation: The Active voice in the question is in Future Indefinite tense (Subject + will/shall + V1 + Object). In Passive voice, Future Indefinite is formed as: Object of the active voice (Subjective case) + will/shall + be + V3 + by + Subject of the active voice (Objective case). Hence, "Dipesh will send Rakesh away to college for certain reasons" changes to "Rakesh will be sent away to college for certain reasons by Dipesh."
