6. Parts of a sentence are given below in jumbled order. Arrange the parts in the correct order to form a meaningful sentence. (A) while the employees (B) meeting on time (C) the boss arrived at the (D) despite the heavy traffic (E) were late

A. DCBAE

B. DACEB

C. BACED

D. ACEDB

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: DCBAE. The sentence begins with the prepositional phrase "despite the heavy traffic" (D), which sets the context. It is followed by the main subject and action, "the boss arrived at the" (C) "meeting on time" (B). The sentence then introduces a contrast using "while the employees" (A) and concludes with their status, "were late" (E). The complete sentence is: "Despite the heavy traffic, the boss arrived at the meeting on time while the employees were late."

9. Select the option that correctly expresses the given sentence in passive voice. The teachers of our school have already taken this training.

A. This training has already been taken by the teachers of our school.

B. This training has been taken by the teachers of our school.

C. This training has already been taken by the our school teachers.

D. This training was already taken by the teachers of our school.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: This training has already been taken by the teachers of our school. The conversion from the question to answer is Active to Passive. Explanation: The Active voice in the question is in Present Perfect (Subject + has/have + V3 + Object). Thus, the Passive voice will also be in Present Perfect (Object of the active voice (Subjective case) + has/have + been + V3 + by + Subject of the active voice (Objective case)).

12. Select the option that expresses the following sentence in passive voice. Ravi will play the match.

A. The match is played by Ravi.

B. The match will have been played by Ravi.

C. The match will be playing by Ravi.

D. The match will be played by Ravi.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is: The match will be played by Ravi. The conversion is from Active to Passive. The Active voice in this question is in Future Simple (Subject + will + V1 + Object). Thus, the Passive voice will also be in Future Simple (Object of the active voice (Subjective case) + will be + V3 + by + Subject of the active voice (Objective case)).
