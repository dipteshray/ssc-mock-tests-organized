8. Not until the report had been independently reviewed ____ approved by the committee.

A. was it

B. was the report

C. did it

D. had it

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is 'Option 1' i.e. was it. Key Points Therefore, the correct sentence is: "Not until the report had been independently reviewed was it approved by the committee." Additional Information

• Negative Inversion: When a sentence begins with a restrictive or negative expression like "Not until," the main clause must be inverted. This means the auxiliary verb (was) comes before the subject (it).

• Pronoun Reference: Since "the report" is the noun introduced in the first clause, we use the pronoun "it" in the second clause to maintain a smooth flow and avoid redundancy.

• Passive Voice: The report is being approved by the committee (the actor), which requires a passive construction. "Was it approved" correctly follows the passive structure (Verb to be + Past Participle).

• Option 2 (was the report): While grammatically correct in isolation, repeating "the report" immediately after using it in the first clause is stylistically repetitive.

• Option 3 (did it): This would be used for the active voice (e.g., "did it approve the budget"). Since the committee is doing the approving, the passive "was it" is necessary for the report.

• Option 4 (had it): This suggests a Past Perfect sequence that isn't necessary here. The first action (reviewing) is already in Past Perfect ("had been reviewed"), so the second action (approval) should be in Simple Past.
