1. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. Ishika saw the tiger in the forest.

A. The tiger saw by Ishika in the forest.

B. The tiger was seen by the forest in Ishika.

C. The tiger was seen by Ishika in the forest.

D. The tiger sees Ishika in the forest.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: The tiger was seen by Ishika in the forest. The active voice sentence is in the Simple Past tense (Subject + V2 + Object). The passive voice structure for Simple Past is: Object + was/were + V3 + by + Subject. Here, 'the tiger' (Object) becomes the subject, 'saw' (V2) becomes 'was seen' (was + V3), and 'Ishika' (Subject) becomes the object of the preposition 'by'.

16. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in active voice. Lovely tunes are composed by Domnica.

A. Domnica composed lovely tunes.

B. Domnica composes tunes lovely.

C. Domnica will compose lovely tunes.

D. Domnica composes lovely tunes.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is: Domnica composes lovely tunes. The given sentence is in the Passive voice of the Present Indefinite tense (Object + am/is/are + V3 + by + Subject). To convert it into the Active voice, we follow the structure: Subject + V1(s/es) + Object. Here, 'Domnica' becomes the subject, 'composes' is the V1(s) form, and 'lovely tunes' is the object.
