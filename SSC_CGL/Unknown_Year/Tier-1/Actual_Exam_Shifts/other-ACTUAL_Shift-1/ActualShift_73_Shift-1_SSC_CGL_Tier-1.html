11. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. He wrote an inspiring speech for the event.

A. An inspiring speech was being written by him for the event.

B. An inspiring speech was written by him for the event.

C. An inspiring speech is written by him for the event.

D. An inspiring speech has been written by him for the event.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: "An inspiring speech was written by him for the event." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "He wrote an inspiring speech for the event."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "an inspiring speech" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "wrote" to "was written" (past simple passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "him."

• Ensure the phrase "for the event" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "An inspiring speech was being written by him for the event." – Incorrect tense (past continuous passive).

• Option 3: "An inspiring speech is written by him for the event." – Incorrect tense (present simple passive).

• Option 4: "An inspiring speech has been written by him for the event." – Incorrect tense (present perfect passive).
