23. Identify the function of lipase in the process of digestion.

A. Breaking down roughage

B. Breaking down emulsified fats

C. Secreting intestinal fluids

D. Digesting proteins

Answer B

Solution

Lipase breaks down emulsified fats. The process of digestion starts in the mouth where the food is chewed and mixed with saliva. Saliva contains an enzyme called salivary lipase that starts breaking down fats. However, the majority of fat digestion happens in the small intestine where the emulsified fats are acted upon by pancreatic lipase. This enzyme breaks down the emulsified fats into fatty acids and glycerol, which can be absorbed by the body.

Solution

35. Select the option that is true regarding the following two statements labelled Assertion (A) and Reason (R). (A): An object slips less on a rough surface than a smooth surface. (R): When a surface is rough, frictional force increases.

C. Assertion (A) is false and Reason (R) is true.

D. Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are the true and Reason (R) is a correct explanation of Assertion (A).

Answer D

Solution

Assertion (A) is true because a rough surface provides more contact points between the object and the surface, increasing the frictional force and reducing slipping. Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are related and true, and Reason (R) provides a correct explanation for Assertion (A).
