1. If A : B = 3 : 4, B : C = 5 : 2, and C : D = 3 : 7, find the ratio A : D.

A. 27 : 56

B. 35 : 48

C. 45 : 56

D. 50 : 63

Answer C

Solution

⇒ A : B = 3 : 4, B : C = 5 : 2, C : D = 3 : 7 ⇒ A : C = (A : B × B : C) = (3 × 5) : (4 × 2) = 15 : 8 ⇒ A : D = (A : C × C : D) = (15 × 3) : (8 × 7) = 45 : 56

5. A container holds a mixture of three liquids X, Y, and Z in ratio 3 : 4 : 5. If 9 liters of liquid X, 16 liters of liquid Y, and a certain amount of liquid Z are added to the container, the new ratio of liquids A, B, and C becomes 5 : 8 : 10. Find the quantity (in liters) of liquid Z added.

A. 15 liters

B. 16 liters

C. 18 liters

D. 20 liters

Answer D

Solution

Let initial quantities be: X = 3k, Y = 4k, Z = 5k New quantities: X = 3k + 9, Y = 4k + 16, Z = 5k + x (3k + 9)/(4k + 16) = 5/8 ⇒ 8(3k + 9) = 5(4k + 16) ⇒ 24k + 72 = 20k + 80 ⇒ 4k = 8 ⇒ k = 2 (3k + 9)/(5k + x) = 5/10 ⇒ (6 + 9)/(10 + x) = 1/2 ⇒ 15/(10 + x) = 1/2 ⇒ 30 = 10 + x ⇒ x = 20 ∴ The quantity of liquid Z added is 20 liters.

10. The ratio of the profit to the cost price of a product is 3: 5. What is the ratio of its selling price to its profit?

A. 8 : 3

B. 7 : 2

C. 11 : 3

D. 9 : 2

Answer A

Solution

Let CP = 5x and Profit = 3x. ⇒ SP = CP + Profit = 5x + 3x = 8x. ⇒ SP : Profit = 8x ÷ 3x = 8 : 3.

12. A gadget is marked up by 50% and sold at a 10% discount. If the profit earned is 700, what was the cost price (CP)?

A. 1,500

B. 1800

C. 2000

D. 2500

Answer C

Solution

Let CP = x. Marked Price (MP) = x × 1.5 Selling Price (SP) = MP × 0.9 = (x × 1.5) × 0.9 = x × 1.35 Profit = SP − CP ⇒ 700 = x × 1.35 − x ⇒ 700 = 0.35x ⇒ x = 700 ÷ 0.35 = 2000 ∴ The correct answer is 2000.

23. The distance between the centers of two circles is d. The lengths of their direct and transverse common tangents are L and M, respectively. If L² + M² = 180 and the sum of the squares of their radii is 80, what is the value of d?

A. √170

B. 15

C. 12

D. 10√2

Answer A

Solution

Let radii be r1 and r2, r12 + r22 = 80. L2 + M2 = [d2 − (r1 − r2)2] + [d2 − (r1 + r2)2]. ⇒ L2 + M2 = 2d2 − [(r1 − r2)2 + (r1 + r2)2]. ⇒ (r1 − r2)2 + (r1 + r2)2 = 2 × 80 = 160. ⇒ 180 = 2d2 − 160. ⇒ 2d2 = 340. ⇒ d2 = 170. ⇒ d = √170.
