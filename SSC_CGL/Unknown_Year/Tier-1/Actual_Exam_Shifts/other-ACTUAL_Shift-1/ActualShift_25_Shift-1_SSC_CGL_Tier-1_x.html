3. If '/' means '+', '×' means '-', '+' means '/' and '-' means '×', which of the following options is correct?

A. 52 × 49 + 7 / 2 + 3 = 30

B. 7 × 17 + 3 / 5 - 4 = 15

C. 10 × 17 + 9 / 7 - 4 = 33

D. 11 × 21 + 7 / 7 - 6 = 50

Answer D

Solution

BODMAS Rule: Shutterstock According to the problem, the symbols are to be replaced as follows: Original Sign New Sign / + × − + ÷ − × 1) 52 × 49 + 7 / 2 + 3 = 30 New Equation: 52 − 49 ÷ 7 + 2 ÷ 3 = ? → 52 − (49 ÷ 7) + (2 ÷ 3) → 52 − 7 + 0.66 → 45.66 ≠ 30 (Incorrect) 2) 7 × 17 + 3 / 5 − 4 = 15 New Equation: 7 − 17 ÷ 3 + 5 × 4 = ? → 7 − (17 ÷ 3) + 5 × 4 → 7 − 5.66 + (5 × 4) → 7 − 5.66 + 20 = 21.33 ≠ 15 (Incorrect) 3) 10 × 17 + 9 / 7 − 4 = 33 New Equation: 10 − 17 ÷ 9 + 7 × 4 = ? → 10 − (17 ÷ 9) + 7 × 4 → 10 − 1.88 + (7 × 4) → 8.111... + 28 = 36.11 ≠ 33 (Incorrect) 4) 11 × 21 + 7 / 7 − 6 = 50 New Equation: 11 − 21 ÷ 7 + 7 × 6 = ? → 11 − (21 ÷ 7) + 7 × 6 → 11 − 3 + (7 × 6) → 8 + 42 = 50 Hence, the correct answer is "Option 4".

16. A driver travels 10 km to a site in 1 hour. On the return trip, they drive the same 10 km in 30 minutes. What is the average speed of the driver for the entire trip?

A. 13.33 km/h

B. 15 km/h

C. 16 km/h

D. 20 km/h

Answer A

Solution

Given: Distance (one way) = 10 km Speed while going = 10 km in 1 hour Speed while returning = 10 km in 30 minutes = 0.5 hour Formula used: Average speed = Total distance ÷ Total time Calculation: Total distance = 10 + 10 = 20 km Total time = 1 + 0.5 = 1.5 hours ⇒ Average speed = 20 ÷ 1.5 = 13.33 km/h Hence, the correct answer is "Option 1".
