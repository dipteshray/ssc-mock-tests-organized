1. WXYZ is a quadrilateral in which XQ and ZP are perpendicular to WY such that XQ = ZP. Diagonals ZX and WY intersect each other at point O. If OX = 12 cm, then find the value of ZX (in cm).

A. 12

B. 24

C. 36

D. 18

Answer B

Solution

OX = 12 cm and XQ = ZP, Triangles XQY and ZPY are congruent Thus, OX = OZ = 12 cm ZX = OX + OZ ⇒ ZX = 12 cm + 12 cm ⇒ ZX = 24 cm.

7. In a family, the combined age of husband, wife and a son is 90 years. The husband is the oldest. The difference in the ages of the husband and wife is (1/3)rd the age of their son. After 10 years, the age of the son would be half the age of his father. What is the present age of his mother (in years)?

A. 40

B. 45

C. 35

D. 30

Answer C

Solution

Let the present ages of husband, wife, and son be H, W, and S respectively. H + W + S = 90, H - W = S/3, S + 10 = (H + 10)/2 ⇒ 2(S + 10) = H + 10 ⇒ H = 2S + 10 ⇒ (2S + 10) + W + S = 90 ⇒ W = 80 - 3S ⇒ (2S + 10) - (80 - 3S) = S/3 ⇒ 15S - 210 = S ⇒ 14S = 210 ⇒ S = 15 ⇒ H = 40 ⇒ W = 35 The present age of his mother is 35 years.

22. Two trains of lengths 150 m and 250 m run on parallel lines. When they run in the same direction, they take 20 seconds to cross each other and, when they run in the opposite direction, they take 5 seconds to cross each other. What is the speed of the two trains?

A. 150 km/h and 105 km/h

B. 150 km/h and 250 km/h

C. 180 km/h and 108 km/h

D. 160 km/h and 106 km/h

Answer C

Solution

Relative Speed (Same Direction) = 400 / 20 = 20 m/s Relative Speed (Opposite Direction) = 400 / 5 = 80 m/s Convert to km/h: Same Direction = 20 × 3.6 = 72 km/h Opposite Direction = 80 × 3.6 = 288 km/h Speed of first train = (288 + 72) / 2 = 180 km/h Speed of second train = (288 - 72) / 2 = 108 km/h

23. Each of Ravi and Kavita had some marbles. Kavita had 12 more marbles than Ravi had. If each of them had one more marble, then three times the number of marbles Kavita had would be equal to four times the number of marbles Ravi had. How many marbles did Kavita actually have?

A. 43

B. 47

C. 51

D. 48

Answer B

Solution

Let Ravi's marbles be x. Kavita's marbles = x + 12. If each had one more marble: Kavita = x + 13, Ravi = x + 1. 3(x + 13) = 4(x + 1) 3x + 39 = 4x + 4 35 = x Kavita's marbles = 35 + 12 = 47.

Solution

25. If 3sin2θ + 5cos2θ = 4 and θ is an acute angle, then the value of tanθ is:

A. 1√(2)

B. 1√(3)

C. (1/2)

D. 1

Answer D

Solution

3sin2θ + 5cos2θ = 4 We can rewrite it in the form: a sin2θ + b cos2θ = c Here, a = 3, b = 5, c = 4. cos2θ (a tan2θ + b) = c ⇒ a tan2θ + b = c sec2θ a tan2θ + b = c + c tan2θ ⇒ (a - c) tan2θ = c - b ∴ tan2θ = (c - b) / (a - c) ⇒ tan2θ = (4 - 5) / (3 - 4) tan2θ = -1 / -1 ⇒ tan2θ = 1 Since θ is an acute angle: tanθ = 1
