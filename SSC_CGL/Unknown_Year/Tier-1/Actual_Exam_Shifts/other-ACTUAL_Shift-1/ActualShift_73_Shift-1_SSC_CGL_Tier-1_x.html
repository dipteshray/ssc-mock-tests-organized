D. 348

Solution

17. 61 is related to 18 following a certain logic. Following the same logic, 213 is related to 38. To which of the following is 509 related, following the same logic? (NOTE : Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. E.g. 13 – Operations on 13 such as adding/deleting/multiplying etc., to 13 can be performed. Breaking down 13 into 1 and 3 and then performing mathematical operations on 1 and 3 is not allowed.)

A. 66

B. 78

C. 64

D. 72

Answer A

Solution

The logic follows here is: Logic: n3 - 3 & n2 + 2 Now, 61 is related to 18 → 43 - 3 = 64 - 3 = 61, → 42 + 2 = 16 + 2 = 18. 213 is related to 38 → 63 - 3 = 216 - 3 = 213, → 62 + 2 = 36 + 2 = 38. Similarly, for 509 → 83 - 3 = 512 - 3 = 509, → 82 + 2 = 64 + 2 = 66. Hence, the correct answer is "Option 1".
