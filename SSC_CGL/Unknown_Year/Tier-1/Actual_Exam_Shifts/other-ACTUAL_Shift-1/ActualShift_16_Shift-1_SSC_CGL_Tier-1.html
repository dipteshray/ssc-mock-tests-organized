4. The following equation is incorrect. Which two signs should be interchanged to correct the equation? 36 + 12 × 8 - 24 ÷ 6 = 34

A. + and ×

B. × and ÷

C. + and -

D. - and ÷

Answer D

Solution

Given equation: 36 + 12 × 8 - 24 ÷ 6 = 34 1) Interchanging + and × New equation: 36 × 12 + 8 - 24 ÷ 6 = 36 × 12 + 8 - 24 ÷ 6 = 432 + 8 - 4 = 440 - 4 = 436 ≠ 34 (Incorrect) 2) Interchanging × and ÷ New equation: 36 + 12 ÷ 8 × 24 - 6 = 36 + 1.5 × 24 - 6 = 36 + 36 - 6 = 72 - 6 = 66 ≠ 34 (Incorrect) 3) Interchanging + and - New equation: 36 - 12 × 8 + 24 ÷ 6 = 36 - 12 × 8 + 24 ÷ 6 = 36 - 96 + 4 = 40 - 96 = -56 ≠ 34 (Incorrect) 4) Interchanging - and ÷ New equation: 36 + 12 × 8 ÷ 24 - 6 = 36 + 12 × (8 ÷ 24) - 6 = 36 + 12 × 0.3333 - 6 = 36 + 4 - 6 = 40 - 6 = 34 34 = 34 (Correct) Hence, the correct answer is "Option 4".

10. Instruction: Identify the assumptions that must hold for the statement to be valid, then choose the correct option. Statement: While modern electric vehicles are becoming more affordable, their reliance on specific charging infrastructure means that widespread use will require major investments in high-speed charging networks to support long-distance travel. Assumptions: I. Electric vehicles currently cannot travel long distances without recharging. II. The lack of charging networks is the only factor preventing people from buying electric cars. III. High-speed charging networks are essential for making electric vehicles practical for long-distance trips.

A. Only I and III are implicit

B. Only II and III are implicit

C. Only I is implicit

D. All assumptions are implicit

Answer A

Solution

Statement: While modern electric vehicles are becoming more affordable, their reliance on specific charging infrastructure means that widespread use will require major investments in high-speed charging networks to support long-distance travel. Assumptions: I. Electric vehicles currently cannot travel long distances without recharging → Implicit The statement claims that high-speed charging networks are needed specifically to "support long-distance travel." This logic only holds if we assume that current electric vehicles (EVs) are unable to complete long-distance journeys on a single charge. If they could travel indefinitely without recharging, the infrastructure wouldn't be a requirement. II. The lack of charging networks is the only factor preventing people from buying electric cars → NOT Implicit The statement identifies infrastructure as a necessary condition for widespread use, but it does not claim it is the only condition. There could be other barriers (such as battery lifespan, cold-weather performance, or manufacturing supply chains) that the statement simply doesn't address. In logical reasoning, "only" usually makes an assumption too broad to be implicit unless specified. III. High-speed charging networks are essential for making electric vehicles practical for long-distance trips → Implicit The statement explicitly links the success of long-distance travel to "major investments in high-speed charging networks." Therefore, it inherently assumes that these specific networks are an essential piece of the puzzle to make EVs practical for those trips. Only I and III are implicit. Hence, the correct answer is “Option 1”.

11. Read the following statement carefully and identify the conclusion that follows. Statement: Employee productivity has risen sharply since the company implemented a four-day work week. Conclusions:I. Better work-life balance encourages higher efficiency. II. Employees were lazy when they worked five days a week.

A. Only Conclusion I follows

B. Only Conclusion II follows

C. Both I and II follow

D. Neither I nor II follows

Answer A

Solution

Statement: Employee productivity has risen sharply since the company implemented a four-day work week. Conclusions: I. Better work-life balance encourages higher efficiency → Follow The statement identifies a direct positive correlation: changing to a four-day work week (a policy typically aimed at improving work-life balance) resulted in a "sharp rise" in productivity. In logical reasoning, when a specific action leads to a specific positive result, we can infer that the underlying principle—in this case, that better balance promotes efficiency—is supported by the evidence provided. II. Employees were lazy when they worked five days a week → Does not follow This is an unwarranted assumption. Lower productivity during a five-day week does not mean employees were "lazy." There are many structural and physiological reasons why a longer work week might be less productive. Only Conclusion I follows. Hence, the correct answer is “Option 1”.

22. A 60 L mixture contains juice and water in the ratio 3:2. How much juice must be added to make the ratio 4:1?

A. 102 L

B. 120 L

C. 60 L

D. 80 L

Answer C

Solution

Given: The ratio of juice and water in a 60 litre mixture is 3:2. Let the quantity of juice = 3x and water = 2x. Total mixture = 60 litres. To make the ratio 4:1, the quantity of juice to be added is required. Formula used: New ratio = (Juice):(Water) = 4:1 Calculations: Given: Juice = 3x, Water = 2x, Total = 60 ⇒ 3x + 2x = 60 ⇒ 5x = 60 ⇒ x = 12 Juice = 3x = 3 × 12 = 36 litres Water = 2x = 2 × 12 = 24 litres Let y be the quantity of Juice to be added to make the ratio 4:1. ⇒ According to the new ratio, ⇒ (Juice / Water) = 4/1 ⇒ J / 24 = 4 / 1 Cross-multiplying gives us, ⇒ J = 24 × 4 ⇒ J = 96 L Juice added = New volume of juice - Initial volume of juice ⇒ Juice added = 96 - 36 ⇒ Juice added = 60 L Hence, the correct answer is “Option 3”.

Solution
