Solution

• The 'Law of Octaves' was propounded by the English chemist J.A.R. Newlands in 1865.

• The 'Law of Octaves'states that if the chemical elements are arranged according to increasing atomic weight, those with similar physical and chemical properties occur after each interval of seven elements.

• J.A.R. Newlands was the first to disclose a periodic pattern in the properties of the elements and anticipated later developments of the periodic law.

• It was found that the Law of Octaves was applicable only up to calcium, as after calcium every eighth element did not possess properties similar to that of the first.

• After the discovery of the Noble gases, the Law of Octaves became irrelevant. Thus, Newlands’ Law of Octaves worked well with lighter elements only.
