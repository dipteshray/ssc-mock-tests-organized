12. Change the sentence from active to passive: The committee had been debating complex amendments when the bell rang.

A. Complex amendments had been debated by the committee as the bell rang.

B. Complex amendments had been being debated by the committee when the bell rang.

C. Complex amendments were being debated by the committee as the bell rang.

D. Complex amendments had debated by the committee when the bell rang.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: "Complex amendments had been being debated by the committee when the bell rang." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "The committee had been debating complex amendments when the bell rang."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "complex amendments" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "had been debating" to "had been being debated" (past perfect continuous passive).

• Ensure the rest of the sentence "when the bell rang" remains intact for clarity.

• Option 1: "Complex amendments had been debated by the committee as the bell rang." – Incorrect because it changes the tense and omits the continuous aspect.

• Option 3: "Complex amendments were being debated by the committee as the bell rang." – Incorrect because it uses past continuous instead of past perfect continuous.

• Option 4: "Complex amendments had debated by the committee when the bell rang." – Incorrect because it uses an incorrect verb form.

20. Replace the highlighted part with the correct option: It is high time you must change your habits.

A. changed your habits

B. must have changed your habits

C. change your habit

D. will change the habit

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is Option 1. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 1. Correct Sentence: “It is high time you changed your habits.” Additional Information

• The phrase “It is high time” is always followed by a verb in the simple past tense to express urgency or that something should have already been done.

• Therefore, using “must change” is grammatically incorrect because “must” cannot be used after “It is high time”.

• The correct structure is: It is high time + subject + V2 (past simple).

• Thus, the correct replacement is “changed your habits”.

• Option 2: must have changed your habits – Incorrect, expresses a past assumption, not urgency.

• Option 3: change your habit – Incorrect because the verb is not in past tense as required after “It is high time”.

• Option 4: will change the habit – Incorrect; this expresses future action and is grammatically wrong in this structure.
