1. Suppose a = √(10) + 1, b = √(6) + √(5), and c = 2√(3) + 1. ; then which of the following is true?

A. a < b < c

B. b < c < a

C. a < c < b

D. c < a < b

Answer C

Solution

Let a = √10 + 1, b = √6 + √5, and c = 2√3 + 1. ⇒ √10 ≈ 3.162 ⇒ a ≈ 3.162 + 1 = 4.162 ⇒ √6 ≈ 2.449, √5 ≈ 2.236 ⇒ b ≈ 2.449 + 2.236 = 4.685 ⇒ √3 ≈ 1.732 ⇒ c ≈ 2 × 1.732 + 1 = 4.464 ⇒ Order: a (4.162)

D. 6.0% decrease

Answer C

Solution

Let total capital = 4 + 3 + 1 = 8 units Technology new = 4 + 10% of 4 = 4 + 0.4 = 4.4 Marketing new = 3 − 5% of 3 = 3 − 0.15 = 2.85 Operations new = 1 + 20% of 1 = 1 + 0.2 = 1.2 New total = 4.4 + 2.85 + 1.2 = 8.45 % change = ((8.45 − 8) ÷ 8) × 100 = 5.625%

7. A buyer ordered 12 kg of premium sugar and some standard sugar. The price of premium sugar per kg was three times that of the standard sugar. Due to an ordering error, the quantities of the two types of sugar were interchanged. This decreased the total cost by 25%. Find the ratio of the quantity of premium sugar to standard sugar in the original order.

A. 3:2

B. 9:5

C. 5:4

D. 7:3

Answer B

Solution

Let price of premium = 3p, price of standard = p. Original cost = 12 × 3p + x × p = 36p + xp. New cost = 12 × p + x × 3p = 12p + 3xp. Decrease = 25% of original ⇒ (36p + xp) − (12p + 3xp) = (1/4)(36p + xp). 24p − 2xp = (1/4)(36p + xp) ⇒ 96p − 8xp = 36p + xp. 60p = 9xp ⇒ x = 60 ÷ 9 = 20/3. Premium : Standard = 12 : 20/3 = 36 : 20 = 9 : 5. ∴ Required ratio = 9 : 5.

9. P and Q together can complete a work in 10 days. Q and R together can complete the same work in 15 days. R and P together can do it in 30 days. In how many days can Q alone complete the job?

A. 20 days

B. 15 days

C. 30 days

D. 45 days

Answer B

Solution

Let the total work be the LCM of 10, 15, and 30. ⇒ LCM(10, 15, 30) = 30 units. Work done by P + Q in 1 day = 30 ÷ 10 = 3 units. Work done by Q + R in 1 day = 30 ÷ 15 = 2 units. Work done by R + P in 1 day = 30 ÷ 30 = 1 unit. Adding all: (P + Q) + (Q + R) + (R + P) = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 units. ⇒ 2(P + Q + R) = 6 ⇒ P + Q + R = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 units. For Q: (P + Q + R) − (R + P) = 3 − 1 = 2 units. Total days = 30 ÷ 2 = 15 days. ∴ The correct answer is 15 days.

10. A trader sells one brand of coffee (X) at 120 per kg, incurring a 20% loss. He also sells another brand (Y) at 300 per kg, incurring a 25% profit. If he mixes the two brands in equal proportions and sells the mixture at 200 per kg, what is his overall profit or loss percentage?

A. Profit of 2.56%

B. Loss of 2.56%

C. Profit of 3.0%

D. Loss of 3.0%

Answer A

Solution

⇒ CP of X = 120 ÷ 0.80 = 150 ⇒ CP of Y = 300 ÷ 1.25 = 240 ⇒ CP per kg = (150 + 240) ÷ 2 = 195 ⇒ Profit per kg = 200 − 195 = 5 ⇒ % profit = (5 ÷ 195) × 100 ≈ 2.56%

D. 2:9

Answer A

Solution

Solution
