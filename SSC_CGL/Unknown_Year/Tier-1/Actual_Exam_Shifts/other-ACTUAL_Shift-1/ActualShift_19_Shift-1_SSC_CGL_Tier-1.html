12. Change the following from active to passive:They had been reviewing the proposal for weeks.

A. The proposal had been reviewed for weeks.

B. The proposal had been being reviewed for weeks.

C. The proposal was reviewed for weeks.

D. The proposal is being reviewed.

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is Option 2. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 2. Correct Sentence: "The proposal had been being reviewed for weeks." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "They had been reviewing the proposal for weeks."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "the proposal" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "had been reviewing" to "had been being reviewed" (past perfect continuous passive).

• Ensure the time phrase "for weeks" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "The proposal had been reviewed for weeks." – Incorrect, as it does not retain the continuous aspect of the verb.

• Option 3: "The proposal was reviewed for weeks." – Incorrect tense (simple past passive).

• Option 4: "The proposal is being reviewed." – Incorrect tense (present continuous passive).
