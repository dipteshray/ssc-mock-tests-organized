2. The value of θ, when: √3 cos θ + sin θ = 1, (1 ≤ θ ≤ 90°), is:

A. 20°

B. 60°

C. 90°

D. 30°

Answer C

Solution

√3cosθ + sinθ = 1 ⇒ √3cosθ = 1 - sinθ ⇒ 3cos2θ = 1 + sin2θ - 2sinθ ⇒ 3 - 3sin2θ = 1 + sin2θ - 2sinθ ⇒ 2sin2θ - sinθ - 1 = 0 ⇒ (2sinθ + 1)(sinθ - 1) = 0 ⇒ sinθ = 1 or -1/2 ⇒ θ = 90º

7. 10 years ago, the age of Sunita was thrice the age of her daughter Tanya. 10 years hence, the age of Sunita will be twice the age of Tanya. What is the ratio of their present ages?

A. 3 : 7

B. 7 : 3

C. 2 : 7

D. 7 : 2

Answer B

Solution

Let the present age of Sunita be S and the present age of Tanya be T. S - 10 = 3(T - 10) ⇒ S + 10 = 2(T + 10) S = 3T - 20 ⇒ S = 2T + 10 ⇒ 3T - 20 = 2T + 10 ⇒ T = 30 ⇒ S = 70 The ratio of their present ages: 70 : 30 ⇒ 7 : 3

10. Study the given table and answer the question that follows. The following table shows the production of printers by four plants of a company over the five years. What is the difference between the average production of plant 1 and plant 2 during five years?

A. 1600

B. 3000

C. 3840

D. 1400

Answer A

Solution

Average Production = Total Production / 5 (since there are 5 years). Calculations: For Plant 1: Total Production = 35,000 + 43,000 + 48,000 + 32,000 + 42,000 = 200,000 Average Production = 200,000 / 5 = 40,000 For Plant 2: Total Production = 25,000 + 44,000 + 39,000 + 40,000 + 44,000 = 192,000 Average Production = 192,000 / 5 = 38,400 Difference between averages: 40,000 - 38,400 = 1,600 Conclusion: The difference between the average production of Plant 1 and Plant 2 during the five years is 1,600 (in thousands).

11. A tank can be filled by pipe A in 4 hours and pipe B in 6 hours. At 8:00 a.m., pipe A was opened. At what time will the tank be filled if pipe B is opened at 9:00 a.m.?

A. 10:16 a.m.

B. 10:22 a.m.

C. 10:48 a.m.

D. 10:18 a.m.

Answer C

Solution

Work done by A in 1 hour = 1/4 Combined work done by A and B in 1 hour = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12 Remaining work after 9:00 a.m. = 1 - 1/4 = 3/4 Time to complete remaining work = (3/4) / (5/12) = 9/5 hours = 1.8 hours 1.8 hours = 1 hour and 48 minutes Tank will be filled at 9:00 a.m. + 1 hour 48 minutes = 10:48 a.m.
