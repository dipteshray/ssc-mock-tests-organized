• The thickness of the Lithosphere varies, being thinnest at mid-ocean ridges and thickest beneath continental crust.

• It extends from about 10 km to 50 km above Earth's surface.

• This layer contains the ozone layer, which absorbs and scatters ultraviolet solar radiation.

• Commercial jet aircraft typically fly in the lower stratosphere.

• Troposphere The Troposphere is the lowest layer of Earth's atmosphere, extending from Earth's surface up to about 8-15 km.

• It contains approximately 75% of the atmosphere's mass and 99% of its water vapor and aerosols.

• Weather events occur in the Troposphere.

• Mesosphere The Mesosphere is the layer of the atmosphere above the Stratosphere and below the Thermosphere.

• It extends from about 50 km to 85 km above the Earth's surface.

• This layer is where most meteorites burn up upon entering the Earth's atmosphere.

• The temperature decreases with altitude in the Mesosphere.
