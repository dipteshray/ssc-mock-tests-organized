1. Which word is closest in meaning to the term “perfidious”?

A. Deceitful

B. Upright

C. Trustworthy

D. Devoted

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is: Deceitful. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is: Deceitful. Additional Information Here are the other options explained along with their Hindi meanings and example sentences:

• The word "perfidious" refers to someone or something that is deceitful or untrustworthy. It implies betrayal or dishonesty. (धोखेबाज़)

• Example: Her perfidious actions shattered the trust we had in her.

• "Deceitful" means someone who is dishonest, capable of misleading others, or being duplicitous. (धोखेबाज़)

• Example: The deceitful salesman tricked the customers into buying faulty products.

• Hence, the word closest in meaning to "perfidious" is "deceitful".

• Upright (ईमानदार): Refers to someone who is honest, ethical, and morally correct.

• Example: The upright judge was admired for his fairness and integrity.

• Trustworthy (विश्वसनीय): Refers to someone who can be relied upon or trusted.

• Example: She is a trustworthy friend who always keeps her promises.

• Devoted (समर्पित): Refers to someone who is loyal or dedicated, often with love and care.

• Example: He is devoted to his family and works hard to provide for them.

2. What does the idiom “Hobson’s choice” essentially imply?

A. A decision made with pressure

B. A completely open selection

C. Having no real alternative

D. Selecting between equal options

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: Option 3 i.e. Having no real alternative. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is: Option 3. Additional Information Here are the other options explained along with their Hindi meanings and example sentences:

• The idiom "Hobson's choice" refers to a situation where there is no real choice available; it is essentially "take it or leave it." (कोई वास्तविक विकल्प नहीं होना) Example: When the company announced its new policy, it was a Hobson's choice for employees—either comply or lose the job.

• "Having no real alternative" means being forced to accept one option because there are no other alternatives available. (कोई अन्य विकल्प न होना) Example: The teacher gave the students Hobson's choice: finish the assignment today or fail the class.

• Hence, the idiom "Hobson's choice" essentially implies "Having no real alternative."

• Option 1: A decision made with pressure (दबाव में किया गया निर्णय): This option refers to making a choice under stress or external influence, but it does not align with the meaning of "Hobson's choice." Example: She made a decision with pressure because her supervisor was watching her closely.

• Option 2: A completely open selection (पूरी तरह से खुला चयन): This option implies freedom to choose from all available options, which is contrary to the idea of "Hobson's choice." Example: The buffet provided a completely open selection of dishes for the guests.

• Option 4: Selecting between equal options (समान विकल्पों के बीच चयन): This refers to choosing between alternatives that are equally good or bad, which is different from "Hobson's choice." Example: Choosing between two candidates with similar qualifications is selecting between equal options.

13. Change to passive voice: They have been ignoring the upkeep of the archives for years.

A. The upkeep had been ignored by them for years.

B. The upkeep is being ignored by them for years.

C. The upkeep was being ignored by them for years.

D. The upkeep has been being ignored by them for years.

Answer D

Solution

The correct answer is Option 4. Key Points Therefore, the correct answer is Option 4. Correct Sentence: "The upkeep of the archives has been being ignored by them for years." Additional Information

• Passive voice structure: The object of the active voice sentence becomes the subject of the passive voice sentence.

• In the active voice, the sentence is: "They have been ignoring the upkeep of the archives for years."

• To convert to passive voice: Place the object "the upkeep of the archives" at the beginning.

• Change the active verb form "have been ignoring" to "has been being ignored" (present perfect continuous passive).

• Add the preposition "by" followed by the original subject "them."

• Ensure the time phrase "for years" remains at the end of the sentence for clarity.

• Option 1: "The upkeep had been ignored by them for years." – Incorrect tense (past perfect passive).

• Option 2: "The upkeep is being ignored by them for years." – Incorrect tense (present continuous passive).

• Option 3: "The upkeep was being ignored by them for years." – Incorrect tense (past continuous passive).
