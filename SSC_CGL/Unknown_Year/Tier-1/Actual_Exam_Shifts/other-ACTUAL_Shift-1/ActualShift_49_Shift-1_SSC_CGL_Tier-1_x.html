1. In which of the following cities of Manipur is the Jawaharlal Nehru Manipur Dance Academy located?

Solution

6. In which musical note did Newland put the metals Co and Ni with halogens?

A. Fa

B. Do

C. Re

D. Mi

Answer B

Solution

John Newlands, a British chemist, proposed the Law of Octaves in 1864, which stated that when elements are arranged by increasing atomic mass, every eighth element shares properties with the first, similar to musical octaves. However, this law had limitations, particularly for elements with higher atomic masses, leading to inaccuracies. For instance, cobalt (Co) and nickel (Ni) were placed in the same slot as halogens, highlighting the system's flaws. Although significant, Newlands' classification was eventually replaced by more accurate periodic tables, such as Mendeleev's Periodic Table.

8. The climate of a place is NOT affected by which of the following?

A. Relief

B. Type of soil

C. Location

D. Distance from the sea

Answer B

Solution

The climate of a place is not affected by the type of soil. Vasily Dokuchaev was the first to classify soil scientifically. In India, the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) classifies soil into eight categories: Alluvial, Black Cotton, Red & Yellow, Laterite, Mountainous or Forest, Arid or Desert, Saline and Alkaline, and Peaty and Marshy. Each soil type has specific characteristics and distribution, but soil type does not influence climate. Factors such as relief, location, and distance from the sea are more significant in affecting the climate of a place.

11. Which of the following statements is INCORRECT about the Directive Principles of State Policy?

A. They promote welfare of individuals. Hence, they are personal and individualistic.

B. These have moral and political sanctions.

C. They aim at establishing social and economic democracy in the country.

D. They are positive, as they require the State to do certain things.

Answer A

Solution

The Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSPs) in India are guidelines for governance provided in Part IV of the Constitution. They aim to establish social and economic democracy by creating conditions for a good life. These principles embody the ideals of justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity, promoting a welfare state. Though non-justiciable, meaning they cannot be legally enforced, they are fundamental to governance and aid courts in assessing the constitutionality of laws. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar likened them to the "Instrument of Instructions" from the Government of India Act of 1935.

18. The Parliament passed the Marine Aids to Navigation Bill, 2021 to repeal and replace which of the following Acts?

A. The Dangerous Machines (Regulation) Act, 1983

B. The Personal Injuries (Emergency Provisions) Act, 1962

C. The Lighthouse Act, 1927

D. The Dourine Act, 1910

Answer C

Solution

The Marine Aids to Navigation Bill, 2021 repealed and replaced the Lighthouse Act of 1927, which regulated lighthouses and navigational techniques in India for nearly nine decades. Introduced in Lok Sabha on March 15, 2021, the bill aims to develop, maintain, and manage aids to navigation in India, improve the legal framework for navigation and vessel traffic services, enhance shipping safety and efficiency, and develop lighthouses for education, culture, and tourism. This initiative by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways seeks to modernize maritime legislation by replacing outdated colonial laws.

19. Which of the following is a non-perishable food?

A. Pulses

B. Meat

C. Curds

D. Milk

Answer A

Solution

Perishable foods are items that spoil quickly within one or two days, such as milk, curds, fish, and meat. Semi-perishable foods can last for 1–2 weeks, including fruits and vegetables, while root crops like onions and potatoes can last for 2–4 weeks. Non-perishable foods, which generally have a longer shelf life of about one year, include grains like rice, wheat, pulses, dals, and oilseeds. Non-perishable foods are ideal for long-term storage and do not require immediate consumption.

24. The Indian Constitution has established ________.

A. partial judicial system

B. dual judicial system

C. independent judicial system

D. plural judicial system

Answer C

Solution

The Indian Constitution established an independent judicial system. 25. Which organisms are classified as Aves?

A. Fishes

B. Frogs

C. Snakes

D. Birds

Answer D

Solution

Aves, or birds, are a class of vertebrates characterized by features such as feathers, egg-laying, two legs, and endothermic (warm-blooded) nature. Birds have a coat of feathers and rest on their hindlimbs. They respire through the lungs, which are connected to large air sacs, making respiration efficient. Their bones are pneumatic (contain air cavities) to reduce weight, contributing to their ability to fly.
