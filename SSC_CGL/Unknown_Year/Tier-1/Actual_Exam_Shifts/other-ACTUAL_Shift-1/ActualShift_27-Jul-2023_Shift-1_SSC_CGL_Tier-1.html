40. The characteristic garlicky odour of garlic is due to ________.

A. iodine

B. copper

C. chlorine

D. sulphur

Answer D

Solution

When garlic is chopped or crushed, it releases an enzyme called alliinase, which converts alliin (an amino acid) into allicin (an organosulfur compound). Allicin is responsible for the pungent smell and taste of garlic. When allicin breaks down, it produces a variety of sulfur-containing compounds, including diallyl disulfide, allyl mercaptan, and allyl methyl sulfide, which give garlic its characteristic garlicky odour.
