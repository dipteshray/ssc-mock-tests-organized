1. Which of the following interchanges of numbers (not digits) would make the given equation correct? (480 ÷ 0.3 × 5 + 500/190 + 9 × 27 ÷ 1.6) = 2

A. 500 and 190

B. 0.3 and 1.6

C. 27 and 480

D. 5 and 9

Answer B

Solution

Option 2): 0.3 and 1.6 (480 ÷ 0.3 × 5 + 500/190 + 9 × 27 ÷ 1.6) = 2 ⇒ (480 ÷ 1.6 × 5 + 500/190 + 9 × 27 ÷ 0.3) = 2 ⇒ (300 × 5 + 500/190 + 9 × 90) = 2 ⇒ (1500 + 500/190 + 810) = 2 ⇒ (2000/1000) = 2 ⇒ 2 = 2 (LHS = RHS)
