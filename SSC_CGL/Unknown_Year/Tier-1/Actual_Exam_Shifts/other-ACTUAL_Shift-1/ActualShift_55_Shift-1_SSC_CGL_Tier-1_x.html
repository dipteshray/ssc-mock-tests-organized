10. Select the option that expresses the following sentence in passive voice. Sonam does not like bananas.

A. Banana is not liked by Sonam.

B. Bananas have not been liked by Sonam.

C. Bananas are not liked by Sonam.

D. Bananas had not been liked by Sonam.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: Bananas are not liked by Sonam. The active voice sentence is in the Present Indefinite tense (Subject + do/does + not + V1 + Object). To convert it to passive voice, the structure is: Object of active voice (Subjective case) + is/am/are + not + V3 + by + Subject of active voice (Objective case). Since "Bananas" is plural, "are" is used.

13. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice Someone has taken my secret diary.

A. My secret diary had been taken by someone.

B. My secret diary has to be taken by someone.

C. My secret diary has been taken by someone.

D. My secret diary will have been taken by someone.

Answer C

Solution

The correct answer is: My secret diary has been taken by someone. The Active voice in the question is in Present Perfect tense (Subject + has/have + V3 + Object). Thus, the Passive voice will also be in Present Perfect (Object of the active voice (Subjective case) + has/have + been + V3 + by + Subject of the active voice (Objective case)).
