5. The incomes of P, Q and R are in the ratio 10 : 12 : 9 and their expenditures are in the ratio 12 : 15 : 8. If Q saves 25% of his income, then what is the ratio of the savings of P, Q and R?

A. 15 : 14 : 21

B. 14 : 15 : 21

C. 21 : 15 : 14

D. 21 : 14 : 15

Answer B

Solution

Let incomes = 10x, 12x, 9x; expenditures = 12y, 15y, 8y. Q saves 25% of income: 15y = 12x - 3x = 9x ⇒ y = 3x/5. Expenditure: P = 36x/5, R = 24x/5. Savings: P = 10x - 36x/5 = 14x/5, R = 9x - 24x/5 = 21x/5. Ratio of savings: (14x/5) : 3x : (21x/5) ⇒ 14 : 15 : 21. Final ratio: 14 : 15 : 21

8. Two pipes, A and B, can fill a tank in 10 minutes and 20 minutes, respectively. The pipe C can empty the tank in 30 minutes. All the three pipes are opened at a time in the beginning. However, pipe C is closed 2 minutes before the tank is filled. In what time, will the tank be full (in minutes)?

A. 12

B. 10

C. 8

D. 6

Answer C

Solution

Rate of A = 1/10, Rate of B = 1/20, Rate of C = 1/30 Net rate when all pipes are open = (1/10 + 1/20 - 1/30) = 7/60 Rate of A + Rate of B = (1/10 + 1/20) = 3/20 Work done in last 2 minutes = 2 × (3/20) = 3/10 Work done before last 2 minutes = 1 - 3/10 = 7/10 Time taken for 7/10 work at rate 7/60 = (7/10) × (60/7) = 6 minutes Total time T = 6 + 2 = 8 minutes

23. If 2cosec2θ + 3cot2θ = 17, then the value of 'θ' when 0° ≤ θ ≤ 90° is:

A. 45°

B. 90°

C. 30°

D. 75°

Answer C

Solution

Substitute cosec2θ = 1 + cot2θ into the given equation: 2(1 + cot2θ) + 3cot2θ = 17 ⇒ 2 + 2cot2θ + 3cot2θ = 17 ⇒ 5cot2θ = 17 - 2 ⇒ cot2θ = 3 ⇒ cotθ = √3 Since 0° ≤ θ ≤ 90°, cotθ = √3 corresponds to θ = 30°.

24. A certain sum of money becomes seven times itself when invested at a certain rate of simple interest, in 14 years. How much time (in years, rounded off to 2 decimal places) will it take to become 18 times itself at the same rate?

A. 27.33

B. 39.67

C. 42.78

D. 35.5

Answer B

Solution

7P = P + (P × R × 14) / 100 ⇒ 6P = 14PR / 100 ⇒ R = 42.86% Find time for sum to become 18 times: 18P = P + (P × 42.86 × T) / 100 ⇒ 17P = 0.4286PT ⇒ T = 39.67 years
