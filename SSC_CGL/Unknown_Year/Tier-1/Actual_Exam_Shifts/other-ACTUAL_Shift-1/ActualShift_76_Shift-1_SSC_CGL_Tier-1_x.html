8. Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements. Statements: Some vegetables are fruits. Some fruits are flowers. All flowers are garlands. Conclusions: I. Some garlands are fruits. II. Some vegetables are flowers. III. Some vegetables are garlands.

A. Only conclusions I and II follow

B. Only conclusion I follows

C. Only conclusions II and III follow

D. Only conclusions I and III follow

Answer B

Solution

Conclusions: I. Some garlands are fruits → Follow (As some fruits are flowers and all flowers are garlands. As some part of fruits is common with flowers which whole comes in garlands, therefore it is true.) II. Some vegetables are flowers → Does not follow (As some vegetables are fruits and some fruits are flowers. So it is possible but not definite.) III. Some vegetables are garlands → Does not follow (As some vegetables are fruits, some fruits are flowers and all flowers are garlands. So it is possible but not definite.) ∴ Here, Only conclusion I follows.
