1. What kind of radiation is absorbed by the ozone layer?

A. Green radiation

B. Infrared radiation

C. Visible radiation

D. Ultraviolet radiation

Answer D

Solution

The ozone layer, located in Earth's stratosphere, absorbs most of the Sun's ultraviolet (UV) radiation, protecting living organisms from potential damage such as skin cancer and cataracts. It is rich in ozone (O3) molecules, which are formed when UV light splits oxygen molecules (O2) that then recombine with other oxygen molecules. The UV radiation is divided into UVA, UVB, and UVC, with UVC being the most harmful but completely absorbed by the ozone layer. The stratosphere, ranging from 10 to 50 kilometers above Earth, experiences a temperature increase with altitude due to UV absorption by the ozone.

2. Which of the following organisations, divided into two branches, was established by Lokmanya Bal Gangadhar Tilak and Annie Besant?

A. Indian Association

B. Theosophical society

C. East Indian Association

D. Home Rule League

Answer D

Solution

The Home Rule League, established in 1916 by Lokmanya Bal Gangadhar Tilak and Annie Besant, was a significant political organization aimed at achieving self-government within the British Empire. The organization was divided into two branches: Tilak's league in Maharashtra and Besant's league in Madras. The leagues played a crucial role in mobilizing Indian public opinion and educating the masses about self-rule and nationalistic ideas, drawing inspiration from the Irish Home Rule Movement. This initiative marked an important milestone in India's struggle for independence.

5. Which of the following options is mostly caused by overpopulation?

A. Land and other renewable resources are under pressure

B. Adequate natural resource storage

C. Reduction in the scarcity of water

D. Increase in the area covered by forests

Answer A

Solution

Overpopulation leads to excessive demand for land, causing urban sprawl and deforestation as more land is needed for housing, agriculture, and infrastructure. Renewable resources such as water, forests, and soil face immense pressure due to overpopulation, leading to their degradation and depletion. High population density increases consumption and waste production, putting additional stress on land and renewable resources. Overpopulation exacerbates environmental pollution and reduces the availability of clean air and water, impacting overall ecosystem health. It results in severe economic, social, and environmental challenges, including unemployment, poverty, and loss of biodiversity.

7. Palladium on barium sulphate (Pd/BaSO4) is also known as:

A. a Hillman reaction catalyst

B. a Cannizzaro reaction catalyst

C. the Rosenmund catalyst

D. an aldol reaction catalyst

Answer C

Solution

The Rosenmund catalyst, consisting of palladium (Pd) on barium sulfate (BaSO4), is specifically used for the hydrogenation of acyl chlorides to aldehydes. The barium sulfate helps to poison the palladium, preventing over-reduction to alcohols. Named after Karl Wilhelm Rosenmund, this catalyst is distinct from those used in the Hillman reaction (which requires a base catalyst), the Cannizzaro reaction (which uses strong bases like NaOH or KOH), and the aldol reaction (which typically involves a base or acid catalyst). Palladium on barium sulfate is not used in these other reactions.

8. Ebony and Mahogany trees are found in which type of forests?

A. Mangrove forests

B. Tropical deciduous forests

C. Tropical evergreen forests

D. Montane forests

Answer C

Solution

Tropical evergreen forests are dense, biodiverse forests located near the equator with a warm, wet climate year-round. Ebony and Mahogany trees thrive in these conditions, supported by high rainfall and humidity. These forests have a multi-layered structure with diverse flora, including trees, shrubs, climbers, and epiphytes, making them some of the most productive ecosystems. In contrast, mangrove forests are coastal and salt-tolerant, tropical deciduous forests have seasonal rainfall and shed leaves in the dry season, and montane forests are found in cooler mountainous areas with distinct vegetation, including coniferous trees at higher elevations.

10. When was the Duty-Free Tariff Preference (DFTP) Scheme for Least Developed Countries (LDCs) announced by India?

A. 2010

B. 2009

C. 2006

D. 2008

Answer D

Solution

The Duty-Free Tariff Preference (DFTP) Scheme for Least Developed Countries (LDCs) was announced by India in 2008 to provide preferential market access for exports from LDCs to the Indian market. It offers duty-free access to 98% of India's tariff lines for products originating from LDCs, including agricultural goods, textiles, and industrial products. This initiative aims to support the economic development of LDCs, integrate them into the global trading system, and promote their export capabilities. India's trade policy for LDCs is guided by South-South cooperation principles and aligns with its broader foreign policy objectives of promoting inclusive growth.

13. Which of the following categories does Gonyaulax belong to?

A. Euglenoids

B. Chrysophytes

C. Protozoans

D. Dinoflagellates

Answer D

Solution

Gonyaulax is a genus of dinoflagellates, a type of plankton found in marine and freshwater environments. Dinoflagellates are characterized by their two flagella that aid in movement. Many, including Gonyaulax, are bioluminescent and can produce light through chemical reactions within their cells. Gonyaulax species are often linked to harmful algal blooms, known as "red tides," which can produce toxins harmful to marine life and humans. Dinoflagellates play a crucial role in marine ecosystems and can be photosynthetic or heterotrophic.

14. Valabhi, a city of ancient India was the capital of the _______ dynasty from the 5th to 8th Century CE.

A. Maitraka

B. Chalukya

C. Pallava

D. Vakataka

Answer A

Solution

The Maitraka dynasty ruled the western part of India, particularly present-day Gujarat, from the 5th to 8th century CE, with Valabhi as its capital. Valabhi was a prominent cultural and educational hub, known for its Buddhist monasteries and Jain temples. The dynasty, founded by Senapati Bhatarka, a former Gupta Empire military governor, significantly contributed to the cultural and religious development of the region. The Maitrakas, patrons of Buddhism and Jainism, saw their dynasty's decline in the mid-8th century due to Arab invasions and internal conflicts.

15. Bharatanatyam dance style is associated with which of the following states?

A. Karnataka

B. Tamil Nadu

C. Bihar

D. Manipur

Answer B

Solution

Bharatanatyam is a classical dance form that originated in Tamil Nadu, India, and is one of the oldest classical dance traditions in India, with roots over 2000 years old. It is characterized by a fixed upper torso, bent legs, intricate footwork, expressive hand gestures, and elaborate costumes. Typically accompanied by classical music, Bharatanatyam performances often revolve around Hindu mythology and spiritual themes. The dance integrates pure dance (Nritta), expressive dance (Nritya), and dramatic dance (Natya). It embodies the South Indian cultural and spiritual ethos and is traditionally performed by women.

18. On which of the following dates does India celebrate its Constitution Day?

A. 26th January

B. 26th November

C. 15th August

D. 2nd October

Answer B

Solution

Constitution Day, also known as Samvidhan Divas, is observed on 26th November every year in India to commemorate the adoption of the Constitution of India on 26th November 1949. The Constitution came into effect on 26th January 1950. Declared on 19th November 2015 by the Government of India, Constitution Day honors the importance of the Constitution and its framers. It emphasizes values like justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity. Key features include a federal structure, parliamentary system, fundamental rights, directive principles, and secularism. Activities include reading the Preamble and organizing discussions on constitutional values.

19. The organisation that Comptroller and Auditor General heads is known as ________.

A. Indian Civil Accounts Organisation

B. Controller General of Accounts

C. Indian Audit and Accounts Department

D. Institute of Chartered Accountants of India

Answer C

Solution

The Indian Audit and Accounts Department (IAAD) audits the accounts of Union and State governments and public sector organizations, ensuring efficient use of public funds. The Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India heads the IAAD, operating independently under Article 148 of the Constitution of India. The CAG is appointed by the President of India for a tenure of six years or until the age of 65. 22. Shab-e-Barat is celebrated by which religious group?

A. Muslim

B. Buddhist

C. Jain

D. Parsi

Answer A

Solution

Shab-e-Barat is an important observance in Islam, celebrated by Muslims globally on the night between the 14th and 15th day of Sha'ban, the eighth month of the Islamic calendar. It is believed that God determines destinies for the coming year and forgives sins of those who seek forgiveness on this night. The observance includes prayer, reflection, and staying awake in devotion. Sha'ban is significant for increased worship in preparation for Ramadan. The Islamic lunar calendar has 12 months and is used to determine Islamic rituals. Prayer and seeking forgiveness are crucial aspects of Muslim faith and practice.
