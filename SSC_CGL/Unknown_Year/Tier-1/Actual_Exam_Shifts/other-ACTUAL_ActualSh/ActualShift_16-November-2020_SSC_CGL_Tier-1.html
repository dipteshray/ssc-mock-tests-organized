1. The average of twenty-five numbers is 54. The average of the first 13 numbers and that of the last 13 numbers is 52.8 and 62.2, respectively. If the 13th number is excluded, then what is the average of the remaining numbers (correct to one decimal place)?

A. 51.2

B. 49.8

C. 50.2

D. 50.6

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Net deviation for the middle (13th) number = 13 × [(52.8 − 54) + (62.2 − 54)] Net deviation = 13 × [−1.2 + 8.2] = 13 × 7 = 91 Value of 13th number = 54 + 91 = 145 Sum of remaining 24 numbers = (25 × 54) − 145 = 1350 − 145 = 1205 New Average = 1205 ÷ 24 = 50.208... ≈ 50.2 ∴ The correct answer is 50.2. Alternate Method Given: Total numbers (n) = 25 Total Average (A) = 54 Average of first 13 = 52.8 Average of last 13 = 62.2 Formula Used: Total Sum = Average × Number of terms Value of repeated term = (Sum of subgroups) − (Total Sum) Calculations: ⇒ Total Sum of 25 numbers = 25 × 54 = 1350 ⇒ Sum of first 13 numbers = 13 × 52.8 = 686.4 ⇒ Sum of last 13 numbers = 13 × 62.2 = 808.6 ⇒ Sum of (13 + 13) numbers = 686.4 + 808.6 = 1495 ⇒ Value of 13th number = 1495 − 1350 = 145 ⇒ Sum of remaining 24 numbers = 1350 − 145 = 1205 ⇒ Average of 24 numbers = 1205 ÷ 24 ⇒ Average ≈ 50.208 ∴ The correct answer is 50.2. Additional Information Net Deviation Concept The sum of deviations of all observations from their arithmetic mean is always zero. If a term is repeated, the net deviation is the value by which the sum increases. Exclusion Principle When a value is excluded, the new average = (Original Total Sum − Excluded Value) ÷ (Original Count − 1). Weighted Average If groups with different averages are combined, the overall average = (n1A1 + n2A2) ÷ (n1 + n2).

2. (sin^2 θ/cosθ(1 + cosθ)) +(1 + cos θ/cosθ) = ?

A. cosecθ

B. 2secθ

C. 2cosθ

D. secθ

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Substitute θ = 60° to simplify calculations: sin 60° = √3/2 and cos 60° = 1/2. First term: sin2 60° / [cos 60° (1 + cos 60°)] = (3/4) / [1/2 × 3/2] = (3/4) / (3/4) = 1. Second term: (1 + cos 60°) / cos 60° = (3/2) / (1/2) = 3. Total value: 1 + 3 = 4. Check options: Option (2) 2sec 60° = 2 × 2 = 4 (Match). ∴ The correct answer is 2secθ. Alternate Method Given expression: sin2θ / [cosθ(1 + cosθ)] + (1 + cosθ) / cosθ Formula used: sin2θ + cos2θ = 1 and secθ = 1/cosθ ⇒ Take LCM: [sin2θ + (1 + cosθ)2] / [cosθ(1 + cosθ)] ⇒ Expand the numerator: [sin2θ + 1 + 2cosθ + cos2θ] / [cosθ(1 + cosθ)] ⇒ Rearrange terms: [(sin2θ + cos2θ) + 1 + 2cosθ] / [cosθ(1 + cosθ)] ⇒ Using identity sin2θ + cos2θ = 1: [1 + 1 + 2cosθ] / [cosθ(1 + cosθ)] ⇒ Simplify numerator: [2 + 2cosθ] / [cosθ(1 + cosθ)] ⇒ Factor out 2: 2(1 + cosθ) / [cosθ(1 + cosθ)] ⇒ Cancel (1 + cosθ): 2 / cosθ = 2secθ ∴ The correct answer is 2secθ. 5. Let x be the greatest number which divides 955, 1027, 1075, the remainder in each case is the same. Which of the following is NOT a factor of x?

A. 6

B. 8

C. 4

D. 16

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick When a number x divides a, b, and c with the same remainder, x is the HCF of the differences |a−b|, |b−c|, and |c−a|. Difference 1: 1027 − 955 = 72 Difference 2: 1075 − 1027 = 48 x = HCF(72, 48) = 24 Factors of 24 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. Comparing with options, 16 is not a factor of 24. ∴ The correct answer is 16. Alternate Method Given: Numbers are 955, 1027, and 1075. Formula Used: For equal remainders, Divisor x = HCF(|Number1 − Number2|, |Number2 − Number3|) ⇒ Let x be the HCF of (1027 − 955) and (1075 − 1027). ⇒ x = HCF(72, 48) ⇒ Prime factors of 72 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 = 23 × 32 ⇒ Prime factors of 48 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 = 24 × 31 ⇒ HCF(72, 48) = 23 × 31 = 8 × 3 = 24 ⇒ Now, check the options to find which is NOT a factor of 24: ⇒ 24 ÷ 6 = 4 (Factor) ⇒ 24 ÷ 8 = 3 (Factor) ⇒ 24 ÷ 4 = 6 (Factor) ⇒ 24 ÷ 16 = 1.5 (Not an integer, hence NOT a factor) ∴ The correct answer is 16. Additional Information HCF of Differences Property The greatest number that divides a, b, and c leaving the same remainder r is the HCF of |a−b|, |b−c|, and |c−a|. Definition of a Factor A factor is a number that divides another number completely without leaving a remainder. For any number x, all its divisors are its factors. Finding the Remainder Once x is found, the common remainder r can be calculated as r = a mod x. In this case, 955 ÷ 24 leaves a remainder of 19.

7. The denominator of a fraction is 4 more than the double of its numerator. When 3 is added to the numerator and 3 is subtracted from denominator the fraction becomes 2/3. Then find the difference between denominator and numerator of the original fraction.

A. 12

B. 13

C. 11

D. 10

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Let the numerator be N. Denominator D = 2N + 4. New Numerator = N + 3, New Denominator = (2N + 4) − 3 = 2N + 1. New fraction = (N + 3) / (2N + 1) = 2/3. By cross-multiplication: 3N + 9 = 4N + 2 ⇒ N = 7. Original Denominator = 2(7) + 4 = 18. Difference = 18 − 7 = 11. ∴ The correct answer is 11. Alternate Method Given: Relationship between numerator (N) and denominator (D): D = 2N + 4 Condition: (N + 3) / (D − 3) = 2/3 Calculations: ⇒ Substitute D = 2N + 4 into the condition: ⇒ (N + 3) / ((2N + 4) − 3) = 2/3 ⇒ (N + 3) / (2N + 1) = 2/3 ⇒ 3 × (N + 3) = 2 × (2N + 1) ⇒ 3N + 9 = 4N + 2 ⇒ 4N − 3N = 9 − 2 ⇒ N = 7 ⇒ D = 2 × 7 + 4 = 14 + 4 = 18 ⇒ Difference = D − N = 18 − 7 = 11 ∴ The correct answer is 11. Additional Information Proper and Improper Fractions A fraction where the numerator is less than the denominator is a proper fraction (e.g., 7/18), while one where the numerator is greater is an improper fraction. Equivalent Fractions Fractions that represent the same value even though they look different (e.g., 2/3 = 10/15), obtained by multiplying/dividing N and D by the same non-zero number. Reciprocal of a Fraction The reciprocal of a fraction a/b is b/a. The product of a fraction and its reciprocal is always 1.

8. The graphs of the linear equations 3x - 2y = 8 and 4x + 3y = 5 intersect at the point P(α, β). What is the value of (2α - β)?

A. 5

B. 6

C. 3

D. 4

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Using the cross-multiplication formula for intersection (x, y): x = [(−2 × −5) − (3 × −8)] ÷ [3 × 3 − 4 × (−2)] = [10 + 24] ÷ [9 + 8] = 2 y = [(−8 × 4) − (−5 × 3)] ÷ 17 = [−32 + 15] ÷ 17 = −1 Therefore, α = 2 and β = −1. Required value (2α − β) = 2(2) − (−1) = 4 + 1 = 5. ∴ The correct answer is 5. Alternate Method Given Equations: 3x − 2y = 8 ... (i) 4x + 3y = 5 ... (ii) ⇒ To eliminate y, multiply (i) by 3 and (ii) by 2: ⇒ 9x − 6y = 24 ⇒ 8x + 6y = 10 ⇒ Adding both equations: 17x = 34 ⇒ x = 2 ∴ α = 2 ⇒ Substitute x = 2 in (i): 3(2) − 2y = 8 ⇒ 6 − 2y = 8 ⇒ −2y = 2 ⇒ y = −1 ∴ β = −1 ⇒ Value of (2α − β) = 2(2) − (−1) = 4 + 1 = 5. ∴ The correct answer is 5. Additional Information Nature of Solutions For equations a1x + b1y = c1 and a2x + b2y = c2, a unique solution exists if a1/a2 ≠ b1/b2. Inconsistent Equations Lines are parallel (no solution) if a1/a2 = b1/b2 ≠ c1/c2. Dependent Equations Lines are coincident (infinite solutions) if a1/a2 = b1/b2 = c1/c2.

9. In E is a point inside the quadrilateral ABCD where DE and CE is the angle bisector of ∠D and ∠C respectively. If ∠B is 82° and ∠DEC is 80°, then find the ∠A.

A. 84°

B. 78°

C. 81°

D. 75°

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Property: The angle between the bisectors of two adjacent angles of a quadrilateral is equal to half the sum of the remaining two angles. ∠DEC = (∠A + ∠B) ÷ 2 80° = (∠A + 82°) ÷ 2 160° = ∠A + 82° ∠A = 160° − 82° = 78° ∴ The correct answer is 78°. Alternate Method Given: ∠B = 82°, ∠DEC = 80°. DE and CE are bisectors of ∠D and ∠C respectively. Let ∠EDC = x and ∠ECD = y. Since DE and CE are bisectors: ⇒ ∠D = 2x and ∠C = 2y In ▵DEC: ⇒ ∠EDC + ∠ECD + ∠DEC = 180° ⇒ x + y + 80° = 180° ⇒ x + y = 100° Multiplying by 2: ⇒ 2x + 2y = 200° → ∠D + ∠C = 200° In quadrilateral ABCD: ⇒ ∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360° ⇒ ∠A + 82° + 200° = 360° ⇒ ∠A + 282° = 360° ⇒ ∠A = 360° − 282° ⇒ ∠A = 78° ∴ The correct answer is 78°. Additional Information Angle Sum Property of Quadrilateral The sum of all interior angles of a quadrilateral is always 360°. Angle Bisector Relation In any quadrilateral, the angle between the bisectors of two adjacent angles is half the sum of the other two angles: ∠E = (∠A + ∠B) ÷ 2 if bisectors of ∠C and ∠D meet at E. 12. If the five-digit number 235 xy is divisible by 3, 7 and 11, then what is the value of (3x - 4y)?

A. 9

B. 5

C. 8

D. 10

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick LCM of 3, 7, and 11 is 231. Assume the number is the maximum possible 23599. 23599 ÷ 231 ⇒ Remainder = 37. Exact divisible number = 23599 − 37 = 23562. This gives x = 6 and y = 2. Required value of 3x − 4y = 3(6) − 4(2) = 18 − 8 = 10. ∴ The correct answer is 10. Alternate Method Given: A five-digit number 235xy is divisible by 3, 7, and 11. Formula: If a number is divisible by coprime factors, it is divisible by their LCM. ⇒ LCM of 3, 7, and 11 = 3 × 7 × 11 = 231. ⇒ For the number to be of the form 235xy, we divide 23599 by 231. ⇒ 23599 = 231 × 102 + 37. ⇒ The required number = 23599 − 37 = 23562. ⇒ By comparing 235xy with 23562, we find x = 6 and y = 2. ⇒ Value = 3x − 4y = 3 × 6 − 4 × 2. ⇒ Value = 18 − 8 = 10. ∴ The correct answer is 10. Additional Information Divisibility Rule of 11 A number is divisible by 11 if the difference between the sum of digits at odd positions and the sum of digits at even positions is either 0 or a multiple of 11. Divisibility Rule of 3 A number is divisible by 3 if the sum of all its digits is divisible by 3. For 23562, 2+3+5+6+2 = 18, which is divisible by 3. LCM of Prime Numbers If a set of numbers are all prime (like 3, 7, and 11), their Least Common Multiple (LCM) is simply the product of those numbers.

17. If a + b + c = 7 and a3 + b3 + c3 - 3abc = 175, then what is the value of (ab + bc + ca)?

B. 7

C. 6

D. 9

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Use the direct identity: a3 + b3 + c3 − 3abc = (a + b + c) × [(a + b + c)2 − 3(ab + bc + ca)] Substitute the given values into the formula: 175 = 7 × [72 − 3(ab + bc + ca)] ⇒ 175 ÷ 7 = 49 − 3(ab + bc + ca) ⇒ 25 = 49 − 3(ab + bc + ca) ⇒ 3(ab + bc + ca) = 49 − 25 = 24 ⇒ ab + bc + ca = 8 ∴ The correct answer is 8.Alternate Method Given: a + b + c = 7 a3 + b3 + c3 − 3abc = 175 Formula Used: (i) (a + b + c)2 = a2 + b2 + c2 + 2(ab + bc + ca) (ii) a3 + b3 + c3 − 3abc = (a + b + c)(a2 + b2 + c2 − (ab + bc + ca)) Calculations: ⇒ From (i): a2 + b2 + c2 = (a + b + c)2 − 2(ab + bc + ca) ⇒ a2 + b2 + c2 = 72 − 2(ab + bc + ca) = 49 − 2(ab + bc + ca) ⇒ Substitute this into (ii): 175 = 7 × [(49 − 2(ab + bc + ca)) − (ab + bc + ca)] ⇒ 175 ÷ 7 = 49 − 3(ab + bc + ca) ⇒ 25 = 49 − 3(ab + bc + ca) ⇒ 3(ab + bc + ca) = 49 − 25 ⇒ 3(ab + bc + ca) = 24 ⇒ ab + bc + ca = 24 ÷ 3 ⇒ ab + bc + ca = 8 ∴ The correct answer is 8.Additional Information The Factorized Identity The expression a3 + b3 + c3 − 3abc is always equal to (a + b + c)(a2 + b2 + c2 − ab − bc − ca).
