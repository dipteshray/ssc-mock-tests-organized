19. In the following question, a sentence has been given in Active/Passive voice. Out of the four alternatives suggested, select the one which best expresses the same sentence in Passive/Active voice. This is the second time I have caught you cheating.

A. You have been caught cheating by me for the second time.

B. You were cheating for a second time and I have caught you.

C. This is the second time you have cheated and were caught by myself.

D. Catching you cheating for the second time has been done by me.

Answer A

Solution

Here the correct answer is You have been caught cheating by me for the second time. Key Points:- Thus, the same sentence in the Passive voice is You have been caught cheating by me for the second time. Additional Information

• The given sentence is in the Active voice, therefore, it must be changed to the Passive voice.

• In order to do that, we need to follow the given steps:-

• The subject of the active voice 'I' will become the object of the passive voice 'me'.

• The object of the active voice 'you' will become the subject of the passive voice.

• The active voice is in present perfect tense.

• Since the active voice is in the present perfect tense, therefore, it will follow the given structure:- Active Voice - has/have + V3

• Passive Voice - has/have + been + V3

• Thus, 'have caught' (has/have + V3) will become 'have been caught' (has/have + been + V3).

• When we convert active voice to passive voice, three things need to be kept in mind:- Change object into subject.

• Change subject into object.

• Always use third form of verb in passive voice.

• Use 'by' before the new object.

B. Up the hill pulling of the trains is being done by the two engines.

C. The train is being pulled up the hill by two engines.

D. Two engines are needed for pulling the train up the hill.

Answer C

Solution

The given sentence is in the Active voice, as the form of the verb (‘pulling’) shows that its subject (‘two engines’) was doing something and was ‘active’. Therefore, it must be changed to the Passive voice, where something could be done by the subject, and it was ‘passive’. In order to do that, 1. The object of the transitive verb in the Active voice should become the subject of the verb in the Passive voice (‘the train’ becomes the subject in place of ‘two engines’). 2. Add the appropriate form of the verb 'be' to the sentence and use the past participle of the verb (‘are pulling’ is converted to 'is being pulled'). 3. Add a 'by' followed by the doer of the action. Thus, the same sentence in the Passive voice is ‘The train is being pulled up the hill by two engines’.
