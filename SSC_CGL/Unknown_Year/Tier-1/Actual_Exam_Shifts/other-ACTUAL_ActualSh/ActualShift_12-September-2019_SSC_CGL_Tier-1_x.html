1. When an article is sold at its marked price, it gives a profit of 25% If a discount of 9.6% is allowed on the marked price, then the profit percent will be:

A. 16.6

B. 13

C. 15.4

D. 15

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Let Cost Price (CP) = 100. Profit = 25% when sold at MP → Marked Price (MP) = 125. New Selling Price (SP) after 9.6% discount = 125 × (100 − 9.6)% = 125 × 90.4%. New SP = 1.25 × 90.4 = 113. Profit % = New SP − CP = 113 − 100 = 13%. ∴ The correct answer is 13%. Alternate Method Given: Profit at Marked Price = 25% Discount offered on Marked Price = 9.6% Formula Used: Profit % = [(SP − CP) ÷ CP] × 100 SP = MP − Discount Calculations: ⇒ Let the Cost Price (CP) of the article be 100. ⇒ Since selling at MP gives 25% profit, MP = 100 + 25% of 100 = 125. ⇒ Discount amount = 9.6% of MP = (9.6 ÷ 100) × 125. ⇒ Discount amount = (9.6 × 1.25) = 12. ⇒ Selling Price (SP) = MP − Discount = 125 − 12 = 113. ⇒ Profit = SP − CP = 113 − 100 = 13. ⇒ Profit % = (13 ÷ 100) × 100 = 13%. ∴ The correct answer is 13%. Additional Information Relationship between CP and MP The ratio of Cost Price to Marked Price is given by CP ÷ MP = (100 − Discount%) ÷ (100 + Profit%). Successive Discounts If two successive discounts of a% and b% are allowed, the equivalent single discount is (a + b − ab/100)%. Effective Profit Percentage Effective Profit % = Mark up % − Discount % − (Mark up % × Discount % ÷ 100).

2. The bisector of ∠B in ΔABC meets AC at D. If AB = 10 cm, BC = 11 cm and AC = 14 cm, then the length of AD is∶

A. 7 cm

B. 22/3 cm

C. 20/3 cm

D. 6 cm

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick According to the Angle Bisector Theorem, the ratio AD : DC is equal to the ratio AB : BC. Ratio AD : DC = 10 : 11 Total parts in AC = 10 + 11 = 21 units Value of 21 units = 14 cm Value of 10 units (AD) = (14 ÷ 21) × 10 = (2/3) × 10 = 20/3 cm ∴ The correct answer is 20/3 cm. Alternate Method Given: AB = 10 cm, BC = 11 cm, AC = 14 cm. In ΔABC, BD is the internal bisector of ∠B. Formula Used: Angle Bisector Theorem states AD/DC = AB/BC. ⇒ Let AD = x cm, then DC = (14 − x) cm. ⇒ AD / DC = AB / BC ⇒ x / (14 − x) = 10 / 11 ⇒ 11x = 10 × (14 − x) ⇒ 11x = 140 − 10x ⇒ 11x + 10x = 140 ⇒ 21x = 140 ⇒ x = 140 / 21 ⇒ x = 20 / 3 ∴ The correct answer is 20/3 cm. Additional Information Length of Internal Angle Bisector The length of the angle bisector BD can be calculated using the formula: BD2 = AB × BC − AD × DC. External Angle Bisector Theorem If the bisector of an external angle of a triangle intersects the extended opposite side at a point D', then AD' / CD' = AB / BC. Incenter Property The internal angle bisectors of a triangle are concurrent at a point called the incenter, which is equidistant from all sides.

3. If the curved surface area of a solid cylinder is one-third of its total surface area, then what is the ratio of its diameter to its height?

A. 4 : 1

B. 2 : 1

C. 5 : 2

D. 1 : 1

Answer A

Solution

As we know, Curved surface area of cylinder = 2 πrh Total surface area of cylinder = 2 πr(h + r) According to the question [2 πrh/2 πr (r + h)] = 1/3 ⇒ 3h = h + r ⇒ 3h – h = r ⇒ 2h = r ⇒ h/r = 1/2 ⇒ h/2r = 1/4 ⇒ 4/1 = 2r/h So, ratio of diameter to height of cylinder = 4 : 1

5. The speed of a boat in still water is 18 km/h and the speed of the current is 6 km/h. In how much time (in hours) will the boat travel a distance of 90 km upstream and the same distance downstream?

A. 12

B. 11(1/4)

C. 10

D. 9(1/2)

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Downstream Speed (v + u) = 18 + 6 = 24 km/h Upstream Speed (v − u) = 18 − 6 = 12 km/h Total Time = (Distance ÷ Downstream Speed) + (Distance ÷ Upstream Speed) Total Time = (90 ÷ 24) + (90 ÷ 12) = 3.75 + 7.5 = 11.25 hours ∴ The correct answer is 11¼ hours. Alternate Method Given: Speed of boat (B) = 18 km/h Speed of current (C) = 6 km/h Distance (D) = 90 km Formula Used: Time = Distance ÷ Speed Downstream Speed = Speed of boat + Speed of current Upstream Speed = Speed of boat − Speed of current Calculations: ⇒ Downstream speed = 18 + 6 = 24 km/h ⇒ Upstream speed = 18 − 6 = 12 km/h ⇒ Time for downstream trip = 90 ÷ 24 = 3.75 hours ⇒ Time for upstream trip = 90 ÷ 12 = 7.5 hours ⇒ Total time = 3.75 + 7.5 = 11.25 hours ⇒ 11.25 hours = 11 + 0.25 = 11 + (1/4) = 11¼ hours ∴ The correct answer is 11¼ hours. Additional Information Speed in Still Water If downstream speed (D) and upstream speed (U) are known, Speed of boat = (D + U) ÷ 2. Speed of Current If downstream speed (D) and upstream speed (U) are known, Speed of current = (D − U) ÷ 2. Average Speed for Round Trip Average Speed = (Downstream Speed × Upstream Speed) ÷ Still Water Speed = (D × U) ÷ B.

9. A sum lent out at simple interest amounts to Rs. 6076 in 1 year and Rs. 7504 in 3 years. The sum and the rate of interest (approx) p.a are respectively:

A. Rs. 5,600 and 9%

B. Rs. 5,400 and 9%

C. Rs. 5,400 and 10%

D. Rs. 5,362 and 13.32%

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Interest for 2 years (3 yr − 1 yr) = 7504 − 6076 = Rs. 1428 Interest for 1 year = 1428 ÷ 2 = Rs. 714 Principal (Sum) = Amount (1 yr) − Interest (1 yr) = 6076 − 714 = Rs. 5362 Rate % = (Interest ÷ Principal) × 100 = (714 ÷ 5362) × 100 ≈ 13.32% ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 5,362 and 13.32%. Alternate Method Given: Amount after 1 year (A1) = Rs. 6076 Amount after 3 years (A3) = Rs. 7504 Formula Used: Simple Interest (SI) = Principal × Rate × Time ÷ 100 Amount (A) = Principal (P) + Simple Interest (SI) Calculations: ⇒ SI for 2 years (3 − 1 years) = A3 − A1 ⇒ SI for 2 years = Rs. 7504 − Rs. 6076 = Rs. 1428 ⇒ SI for 1 year = 1428 ÷ 2 = Rs. 714 ⇒ Principal (Sum) = Amount after 1 year − SI for 1 year ⇒ Principal = 6076 − 714 = Rs. 5362 ⇒ Rate of Interest (R) = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × T) ⇒ R = (714 × 100) ÷ (5362 × 1) ⇒ R ≈ 13.3159% → 13.32% ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 5,362 and 13.32%. Additional Information Nature of Simple Interest In Simple Interest, the interest earned remains constant every year because it is always calculated on the initial principal (P). Amount and Principal Relation Amount after T years is A = P + (P × R × T) ÷ 100. If we know the amounts at different years, the difference in amounts gives the interest for that specific time gap. Effective Rate of Interest For SI, the effective rate for T years is simply R × T. In Compound Interest, the effective rate is calculated using the formula [ (1 + R/100)T − 1 ] × 100.

13. A circle is inscribed in quadrilateral ABCD touching AB, BC, CD and AD at the points P, Q, R and S, respectively, and ∠B = 90°. If AD = 24 cm, AB = 27 cm and DR = 6 cm, then what is the circumference of the circle?

A. 18π

B. 20π

C. 15π

D. 12π

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Tangents from an external point are equal: DR = DS = 6 cm. AS = AD − DS = 24 − 6 = 18 cm, and AP = AS = 18 cm. PB = AB − AP = 27 − 18 = 9 cm. Since angle B = 900, the radius r of the inscribed circle is equal to PB = 9 cm. Circumference = 2 × π × r = 2 × π × 9 = 18π. ∴ The correct answer is 18π cm. Alternate Method Given: ∠B = 90°, AD = 24 cm, AB = 27 cm, DR = 6 cm Circle touches AB at P, BC at Q, CD at R, and AD at S. Formula Used: Tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal. B. 5 ∶ 4

C. 7 ∶ 8

D. 9 ∶ 10

Solution

Alternate Method Given: Numbers for third proportional: a = 0.4, b = 0.8 Numbers for mean proportional: x = 13.5, y = 0.24 Formula Used: Third Proportional (c) of a and b is given by a : b = b : c ⇒ c = b2/a Mean Proportional (m) of x and y is given by x : m = m : y ⇒ m = √(xy) Calculations: ⇒ Let the third proportional be P1. ⇒ P1 = (0.8 × 0.8) ÷ 0.4 ⇒ P1 = 0.64 ÷ 0.4 = 1.6 ⇒ Let the mean proportional be P2. ⇒ P2 = √(13.5 × 0.24) ⇒ P2 = √(3.24) ⇒ P2 = 1.8 ⇒ Required Ratio = P1 : P2 ⇒ Required Ratio = 1.6 : 1.8 ⇒ Multiply both by 10: 16 : 18 ⇒ Divide both by 2: 8 : 9 ∴ The correct answer is 8 : 9. 18. A, B and C start a business. A invests 33(1/3)\% of the total capital, B invests 25% of the remaining and C invests the rest. If the total profit at the end of a year is Rs. 1,62,000 then A’s share in profit is:

A. Rs. 90,000

B. Rs. 81,000

C. Rs.60, 000

D. Rs. 54,000

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick A's investment is 33 1/3% (or 1/3) of the total capital. Since the time period for all partners is the same, the profit sharing ratio is identical to the investment ratio. A's share of profit = 33 1/3% of total profit → (1/3) × 1,62,000. A's share = Rs. 54,000. ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 54,000. Alternate Method Given: Total Profit = Rs. 1,62,000 A's investment = 33 1/3% of total capital B's investment = 25% of the remaining capital Formula Used: Profit Share = Investment × Time period Calculations: ⇒ Let total capital = 1 unit ⇒ A's investment = 33 1/3% = 1/3 ⇒ Remaining capital = 1 − 1/3 = 2/3 ⇒ B's investment = 25% of 2/3 = 1/4 × 2/3 = 1/6 ⇒ C's investment = 2/3 − 1/6 = (4 − 1)/6 = 3/6 = 1/2 ⇒ Investment ratio A : B : C = 1/3 : 1/6 : 1/2 = 2 : 1 : 3 ⇒ Total ratio parts = 2 + 1 + 3 = 6 units ⇒ 6 units = Rs. 1,62,000 ⇒ 1 unit = 1,62,000 ÷ 6 = Rs. 27,000 ⇒ A's share (2 units) = 2 × 27,000 = Rs. 54,000 ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 54,000. Additional Information Profit Sharing Rule In any partnership, if the time period of investment is the same for all partners, the profit is distributed in the ratio of their capitals: P1 : P2 = C1 : C2. Compound Partnership If partners invest for different time periods, the profit is divided in the ratio of the product of capital and time: P1 : P2 = (C1 × T1) : (C2 × T2). Active and Sleeping Partners An active partner manages the business and may receive a salary or fixed percentage of profit before the remaining profit is distributed among all partners in their investment ratio.
