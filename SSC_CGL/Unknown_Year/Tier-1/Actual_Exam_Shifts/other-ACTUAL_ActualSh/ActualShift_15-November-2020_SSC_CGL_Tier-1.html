Solution

9. In ΔPQR, O is the in-center and ∠R = 42°. Find the measure of ∠QOP.

D. 132°

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick In any triangle, the angle formed at the in-center by two vertices is 90° + half of the third vertex angle. Formula: ∠QOP = 90° + (∠R ÷ 2) Given ∠R = 42° ∠QOP = 90° + (42° ÷ 2) = 90° + 21° = 111° ∴ The correct answer is 111°. Alternate Method Given: O is the in-center of ΔPQR and ∠R = 42°. 12. A takes 2 hours more than B to cover a distance of 40 km. If A doubles his speed, he takes 1(1/2) hours more than B to cover 80 km. To cover a distance of 90 km, how much time will B take travelling at his same speed?

C. 1 (1/8)hours

D. 1 (1/6) hours

Solution

New difference for 80 km: Time A (for 40 km) − Time B (for 80 km) = 1.5 hours. Difference in B's times for 80 km and 40 km = 2 − 1.5 = 0.5 hours. B covers the extra 40 km in 0.5 hours ⇒ Speed of B = 40 ÷ 0.5 = 80 kmph. (ii) ⇒ From (i), 40/SA = 40/SB + 2 ⇒ Substitute this in (ii): (40/SB + 2) − 80/SB = 1.5 ⇒ 2 − 1.5 = 80/SB − 40/SB ⇒ 0.5 = 40/SB ⇒ SB = 40 ÷ 0.5 = 80 kmph ⇒ Time taken by B to cover 90 km = 90 ÷ 80 ⇒ Time = 9/8 = 1 1/8 hours. ∴ The correct answer is 1 1/8 hours.Additional Information Basic Speed-Distance Formula Speed = Distance ÷ Time | Distance = Speed × Time | Time = Distance ÷ Speed Relative Speed When two objects move in the same direction, relative speed = S1 − S2. 92. In ΔABC, ∠A - ∠B = 33°, ∠B - ∠C = 18° What is the sum of the smallest and the largest angles of the triangle?

A. 92°

B. 143°

C. 125°

D. 108°

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Let ∠B = x. Given ∠A − ∠B = 33° and ∠B − ∠C = 18°. ⇒ ∠A = x + 33° and ∠C = x − 18°. Sum of angles: (x + 33°) + x + (x − 18°) = 180° ⇒ 3x + 15° = 180° ⇒ x = 55°. Largest ∠A = 55° + 33° = 88° and Smallest ∠C = 55° − 18° = 37°. Sum of Smallest and Largest = 88° + 37° = 125°. ∴ The correct answer is 125°. Alternate Method Given: ∠A − ∠B = 33° ∠B − ∠C = 18° Formula: In any triangle ABC, ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180° Step-by-step Calculation: ⇒ ∠A = ∠B + 33° ⇒ ∠C = ∠B − 18° Substituting values in the angle sum formula: ⇒ (∠B + 33°) + ∠B + (∠B − 18°) = 180° ⇒ 3 × ∠B + 15° = 180° ⇒ 3 × ∠B = 180° − 15° ⇒ 3 × ∠B = 165° ⇒ ∠B = 165° ÷ 3 = 55° Finding all individual angles: ⇒ ∠A = 55° + 33° = 88° (Largest angle) ⇒ ∠C = 55° − 18° = 37° (Smallest angle) Calculation of the required sum: ⇒ Sum = ∠A + ∠C = 88° + 37° = 125° ∴ The correct answer is 125°. Additional Information Angle Sum Property The sum of all interior angles in any Euclidean triangle is always 180°. Angle-Side Relationship In a triangle, the side opposite the largest angle is the longest side, and the side opposite the smallest angle is the shortest side. Exterior Angle Theorem An exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the two opposite interior angles (e.g., Exterior ∠A = ∠B + ∠C). Classification by Angles Triangles are classified as Acute (all angles 90°).

93. Three men and 4 women can do a piece of work in 7 days, whereas 2 men and 1 woman can do it in 14 days. Seven women will complete the same work in:

A. 8 days

B. 9 days

C. 10 days

D. 12 days

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Equate total work: (3M + 4W) × 7 = (2M + 1W) × 14 ⇒ 3M + 4W = 2(2M + 1W) ⇒ 3M + 4W = 4M + 2W ⇒ 1M = 2W Total work in terms of women = (3 × 2W + 4W) × 7 = 70W Number of days for 7 women = 70W ÷ 7W = 10 days ∴ The correct answer is 10 days. Alternate Method Given: (3M + 4W) complete work in 7 days; (2M + 1W) complete work in 14 days. Formula Used: Total Work = Efficiency × Time; Efficiency × Time = Constant Group Days Total Work (LCM) Efficiency 3M + 4W 7 14 units 2 2M + 1W 14 1 ⇒ Efficiency of 3M + 4W = 2 −−− (i) ⇒ Efficiency of 2M + 1W = 1 −−− (ii) ⇒ From (i) and (ii): 3M + 4W = 2(2M + 1W) ⇒ 3M + 4W = 4M + 2W ⇒ 1M = 2W ⇒ Substitute M = 2W in (ii): 2(2W) + 1W = 1 ⇒ 5W = 1 ⇒ W = 1/5 unit/day ⇒ Efficiency of 7 women = 7 × W = 7 × (1/5) = 7/5 units/day ⇒ Time taken by 7 women = Total Work ÷ Efficiency = 14 ÷ (7/5) ⇒ Time = 14 × (5/7) = 10 days ∴ The correct answer is 10 days. Additional Information M-D-H Relation For group work, use (M1×D1×H1)/W1 = (M2×D2×H2)/W2 to compare different sets of workers and work done. Efficiency and Time Efficiency is inversely proportional to the time taken to complete the same amount of work. If Time ratio is x:y, Efficiency ratio is y:x. Work and Wages Wages are always distributed in the ratio of the work done by individuals, which is (Efficiency × Time).
