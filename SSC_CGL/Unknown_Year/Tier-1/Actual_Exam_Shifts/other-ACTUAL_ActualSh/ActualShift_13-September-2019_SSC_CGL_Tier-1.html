Answer C

Solution

• The correct answer is option 3.

• Hence, 'don't' will be changed to 'didn't' and 'will' will be changed to 'would'.

Teens who hit puberty late may end up with weaker bones, a new study finds. C. However, they continue to have lower bone strength even after they finish growing and may be at greater risk for osteoporosis in adulthood. D. These later maturing teens do catch up with early maturing teens in terms of bone strength.

A. BCDA

B. ADCB

C. BADC

D. ACDB

Answer C

Solution

• The correct answer is Option 3.

• The correct answer begins with the sentence B as it is introducing the subject 'a new study' and all other sentences are talking about what the study says.

• Sentence B will be followed by A which begins with 'in the study', the article 'the' shows that the study has already been introduced i.e. in sentence A.

• Sentence D will be followed by C. The adverb 'however' is used to introduce a statement that contrasts with something that has just been said. The sentences mean 'the later maturing teens try to reach somebody who is ahead however they continue to have lower bone strength'.

• Therefore, the correct sequence is given in option 3 'BADC'.

200. Choose the most appropriate option to change the voice (active / passive) form of the given sentence. An interesting discussion is being held on environmental degradation.

A. They will be holding an interesting discussion on environmental degradation.

B. They are holding an interesting discussion on environmental degradation.

C. An interesting discussion had been held on environmental degradation.

D. An interesting discussion is to be held on environmental degradation.

Answer B

Solution

The given sentence is in the Passive voice. We must convert it into Active voice: In our case of the Present Continuous Tense:("is being held") Thus, the correct answer is option 2.

• Thus in the Active voice, The Subject of the Passive voice ('An interesting discussion') is changed into the Object of the Active voice.

• Similarly, The Object of the Passive voice is implicit(hidden) i.e the discussion must be held by some people which can be denoted by the pronoun 'them'. [An interesting discussion is being held on environmental degradation(by them)]. This Object of the Passive Voice is changed into the Subject (Subject1) of the Active voice.

• The construction of Passive Voice here is – Subject+ am/is/are+ being+ Verb3+ (by+agent) An interesting discussion is being held on environmental degradation. (by them)

• Therefore, the construction of Active Voice here will be: Subject1+ am/is/are+ Verb(+ing)+ Object They are holding an interesting discussion on environmental degradation.
