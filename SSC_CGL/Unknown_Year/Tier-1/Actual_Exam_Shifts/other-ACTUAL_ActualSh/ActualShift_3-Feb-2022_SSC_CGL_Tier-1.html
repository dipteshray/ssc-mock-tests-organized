5. The ratio of the incomes of A and B in 2020 was 5 ∶ 4. The ratios of their individual incomes in 2020 and 2021 were 4 ∶ 5 and 2 ∶ 3, respectively. If the total income A and B in 2021 was Rs.7,05,600, then what was the income (in Rs.) of B in 2021?

A. 3,45,600

B. 2,79,700

C. 3,60,000

D. 4,25,900

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Income A : B (2020) = 5 : 4 Change ratios: A (4 : 5) → A (2021) = 5 × (5 ÷ 4) = 6.25 units Change ratios: B (2 : 3) → B (2021) = 4 × (3 ÷ 2) = 6 units Income A : B (2021) = 6.25 : 6 = 25 : 24 (Total = 49 units) B's 2021 income = (24 ÷ 49) × 7,05,600 = 24 × 14,400 = Rs. 3,45,600 ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 3,45,600. Alternate Method Given: Income ratio A : B (2020) = 5 : 4 Individual income ratio A (2020 : 2021) = 4 : 5 Individual income ratio B (2020 : 2021) = 2 : 3 Total income of A and B in 2021 = Rs. 7,05,600 Formula Used: New Income = Old Income × (New Ratio Part ÷ Old Ratio Part) Calculations: ⇒ To make the 2020 income of A (5) divisible by 4 and B (4) divisible by 2, multiply the A:B (2020) ratio by 4. ⇒ New A:B (2020) = (5 × 4) : (4 × 4) = 20 : 16 ⇒ A's income in 2021 = (20 ÷ 4) × 5 = 25 units ⇒ B's income in 2021 = (16 ÷ 2) × 3 = 24 units ⇒ Total income in 2021 = 25 + 24 = 49 units ⇒ Given 49 units = 7,05,600 ⇒ 1 unit = 7,05,600 ÷ 49 = 14,400 ⇒ Income of B in 2021 = 24 × 14,400 = 3,45,600 ∴ The correct answer is Rs. 3,45,600. Compound Ratios The final ratio of two variables after changes can be found by multiplying the initial ratio parts by their respective fractional change factors. 9. The driver of a car, which is travelling at a speed of 75 km/h, locates a bus 80 m ahead of him, travelling in the same direction. After 15 seconds, he finds that the bus is 40 m behind the car. What is the speed of the bus (in km/h)?

A. 44.2

B. 42.5

C. 47.5

D. 46.2

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total relative distance covered by car = Initial distance ahead + Final distance behind = 80 + 40 = 120 m. Relative speed in m/s = Distance ÷ Time = 120 ÷ 15 = 8 m/s. Convert relative speed to km/h = 8 × (18/5) = 28.8 km/h. Speed of the bus = Car speed − Relative speed = 75 − 28.8 = 46.2 km/h. ∴ The correct answer is 46.2 km/h. Alternate Method Given: Speed of car (Vc) = 75 km/h Initial distance of bus ahead = 80 m Final distance of bus behind = 40 m Time taken (t) = 15 seconds Formula Used: Relative Speed (Same direction) = V1 − V2 Distance = Speed × Time Calculations: ⇒ Let the speed of the bus be Vb km/h. ⇒ Total relative distance to be covered by the car = 80 + 40 = 120 m. ⇒ Relative speed = (Vc − Vb) × (5/18) m/s. ⇒ Relative speed = Distance ÷ Time = 120 ÷ 15 = 8 m/s. ⇒ (75 − Vb) × (5/18) = 8 ⇒ 75 − Vb = 8 × (18/5) ⇒ 75 − Vb = 144 ÷ 5 ⇒ 75 − Vb = 28.8 ⇒ Vb = 75 − 28.8 ⇒ Vb = 46.2 km/h. ∴ The correct answer is 46.2 km/h. Additional Information Relative Speed Concepts When two objects move in the same direction, relative speed = V1 − V2. When they move in opposite directions, relative speed = V1 + V2. Unit Conversion To convert speed from km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18. To convert from m/s to km/h, multiply by 18/5. General Formula Distance = Speed × Time. In overtaking problems, the distance is the total gap closed between the two objects.
