1. In an examination, 92% of the students passed and 480 students failed. If so, how many students appeared in the examination?

A. 6000

B. 5000

C. 5800

D. 6200

Answer A

Solution

Calculation: Let, total appeared students 100% In the examination 92% students passed that means (100 - 92) = 8% student failed Accordingly, 8% → 480 ⇒ 100% → (480/8) × 100 ⇒ 100% → 6000 ∴ 6000 students appeared in the examination.

3. A and B can do a work together in 18 days. A is three times as efficient as B. In how many days can B alone complete the work?

A. 54 days

B. 64 days

C. 72 days

D. 60 days

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Efficiency Ratio (A : B) = 3 : 1 Combined Efficiency (A + B) = 3 + 1 = 4 units/day Total Work = Combined Efficiency × Combined Time = 4 × 18 = 72 units Days taken by B alone = Total Work ÷ Efficiency of B = 72 ÷ 1 = 72 days ∴ The correct answer is 72 days. Alternate Method Given: Time taken by A and B together = 18 days. A is three times as efficient as B. 6. Anil bought two articles A and B at a total cost of Rs. 10,000. He sold the article A at 15% profit and the article B at 10% loss. In the whole deal, he made no profit or no loss. Find the selling price of the article A.

A. Rs. 4,200

B. Rs. 5,400

C. Rs. 4,500

D. Rs. 4,600

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Ratio of CP of A to CP of B by Alligation = |0 − (−10)| : |15 − 0| = 10 : 15 = 2 : 3 Total units = 2 + 3 = 5 units → ₹10,000 CP of article A = 2 units = (10,000 ÷ 5) × 2 = ₹4,000 SP of article A = 115% of CP = 1.15 × 4,000 = ₹4,600 ∴ The correct answer is ₹4,600. Alternate Method Given: Total Cost Price (CP) of A and B = ₹10,000 Profit on article A = 15% Loss on article B = 10% Net Profit/Loss = 0% Formula Used: Selling Price (SP) = CP × (100 + Profit%)/100 Using Alligation to find the ratio of CP of two articles: Calculations: ⇒ Let CP of A be 2x and CP of B be 3x. ⇒ 2x + 3x = 10,000 ⇒ 5x = 10,000 ⇒ x = 2,000 ⇒ Cost Price of A (CPA) = 2 × 2,000 = ₹4,000 ⇒ Selling Price of A (SPA) = CPA + 15% of CPA ⇒ SPA = 4,000 + (15/100) × 4,000 ⇒ SPA = 4,000 + 600 = ₹4,600 ∴ The correct answer is ₹4,600. Additional Information Alligation Rule in Profit and Loss When two articles are sold, the ratio of their Cost Prices is given by the ratio of the differences between the individual profit/loss percentages and the overall profit/loss percentage. No Profit No Loss Condition In a "no profit no loss" scenario, the absolute value of the profit earned on one article must be exactly equal to the absolute value of the loss incurred on the other article: Profit on A = Loss on B. Selling Price Formula SP = CP × (100 ± P/L)% where '+' is used for profit and '−' is used for loss.

8. The interior angle of a regular polygon exceeds its exterior angle by 90°.The number of sides of the polygon is:

A. 6

B. 10

C. 8

D. 12

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Interior Angle (I) + Exterior Angle (E) = 180° Given: I − E = 90° Adding the two: 2I = 270° ⇒ I = 135° Subtracting the two: 2E = 90° ⇒ E = 45° Number of sides (n) = 360° ÷ Exterior Angle = 360° ÷ 45° = 8 ∴ The correct answer is 8. Alternate Method Given: Interior angle (I) − Exterior angle (E) = 90° Formula Used: 1. Interior angle + Exterior angle at any vertex = 180° 2. Number of sides of a regular polygon (n) = 360° ÷ Exterior angle Calculations: ⇒ Let the interior angle be I and the exterior angle be E. ⇒ I − E = 90° --- (Equation 1) ⇒ I + E = 180° --- (Equation 2) ⇒ Subtracting (1) from (2): ⇒ (I + E) − (I − E) = 180° − 90° ⇒ 2E = 90° ⇒ E = 45° ⇒ Number of sides (n) = 360° ÷ E ⇒ n = 360° ÷ 45° ⇒ n = 8 ∴ The correct answer is 8. Additional Information Sum of Interior Angles The sum of all interior angles of a polygon with n sides is given by (n − 2) × 180°. 9. The radii of two cylinders are in the ratio 3 : 4 and their heights are in the ratio 8 : 5. The ratio of their volumes is equal to:

A. 7 : 10

B. 9 : 11

C. 9 : 10

D. 8 : 9

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Volume of a cylinder (V) ∝ r2h Ratio of volumes V1 : V2 = (r1/r2)2 × (h1/h2) V1 : V2 = (3/4)2 × (8/5) = (9/16) × (8/5) V1 : V2 = (9 × 1) : (2 × 5) = 9 : 10 ∴ The correct answer is 9 : 10. Alternate Method Given: Ratio of radii (r1 : r2) = 3 : 4 Ratio of heights (h1 : h2) = 8 : 5 Formula Used: Volume of a cylinder (V) = πr2h Calculations: Let the radii be 3x and 4x, and the heights be 8y and 5y. ⇒ Volume of first cylinder (V1) = π × (3x)2 × 8y ⇒ V1 = π × 9x2 × 8y = 72πx2y ⇒ Volume of second cylinder (V2) = π × (4x)2 × 5y ⇒ V2 = π × 16x2 × 5y = 80πx2y ⇒ Ratio V1 : V2 = 72πx2y : 80πx2y ⇒ Ratio V1 : V2 = 72 : 80 ⇒ Ratio V1 : V2 = 9 : 10 ∴ The correct answer is 9 : 10. B. 634

Solution

Option 1: 366 ÷ 7 → Remainder = 2 Option 2: 634 ÷ 7 → Remainder = 4 Option 3: 384 ÷ 7 → Remainder = 6 Option 4: 364 ÷ 7 = 52 → Remainder = 0 Since 364 is the only option divisible by 7, it is the required number. ∴ The correct answer is 364. Alternate Method Given: Divisors = 12, 18, 24, 30; Common Remainder = 4; Special Divisor = 7. Formula Used: Number = LCM(Divisors) × k + Remainder ⇒ Prime factors of 12 = 22 × 3 ⇒ Prime factors of 18 = 2 × 32 ⇒ Prime factors of 24 = 23 × 3 ⇒ Prime factors of 30 = 2 × 3 × 5 ⇒ LCM (12, 18, 24, 30) = 23 × 32 × 5 = 8 × 9 × 5 = 360 ⇒ General form of the number = 360k + 4 ⇒ To satisfy divisibility by 7: (360k + 4) ÷ 7 must have remainder 0 ⇒ (360k + 4) = (7 × 51k) + (3k + 4) ⇒ For the number to be divisible by 7, (3k + 4) must be divisible by 7. ⇒ If k = 1: 3(1) + 4 = 7 (Divisible by 7) ⇒ Least number = 360(1) + 4 = 364 ∴ The correct answer is 364. 15. On selling 38 balls at Rs. 2,240, there is a loss equal to the cost price of 6 balls. The cost price of a ball is equal to:

A. Rs. 50

B. Rs. 60

C. Rs. 70

D. Rs. 80

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Loss = CP − SP ⇒ CP of 6 balls = CP of 38 balls − SP of 38 balls. Rearranging: SP of 38 balls = CP of (38 − 6) balls. ⇒ SP of 38 balls = CP of 32 balls. ⇒ ₹ 2,240 = 32 × CP of 1 ball. ⇒ CP of 1 ball = 2240 ÷ 32 = 70. ∴ The correct answer is ₹ 70.
