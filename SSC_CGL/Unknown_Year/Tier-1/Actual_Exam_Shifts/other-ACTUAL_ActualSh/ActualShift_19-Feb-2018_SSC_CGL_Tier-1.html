1. If N = 1 + 11 + 111 + 1111 + …… + 111111111, then what is the sum of the digits of N?

A. 45

B. 18

C. 36

D. 5

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick The sum of the series 1 + 11 + 111 + ... up to n terms (where n ≤ 9) results in the number 123...n. Here, the series has 9 terms (ending at 111,111,111), so N = 123456789. Sum of digits of N = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 + 9. Using the formula for sum of first n natural numbers: [n × (n + 1)] ÷ 2. Sum = (9 × 10) ÷ 2 = 45. ∴ The correct answer is 45. Alternate Method Given: N = 1 + 11 + 111 + 1111 + 11111 + 111111 + 1111111 + 11111111 + 111111111 Let us align the numbers vertically to find the sum N: ⇒ Total terms = 9 ⇒ Sum at units place = 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 9 ⇒ Sum at tens place = 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 8 ⇒ Sum at hundreds place = 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 7 ⇒ Continuing this logic, the number N = 123456789 ⇒ Sum of digits of N = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 + 9 ⇒ Sum = (1 + 9) + (2 + 8) + (3 + 7) + (4 + 6) + 5 ⇒ Sum = 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 5 = 45 ∴ The correct answer is 45. Additional Information Sum of first n natural numbers The sum of the first n natural numbers is given by the formula S = [n(n + 1)] ÷ 2. 2. What is the sum of first 40 terms of 1 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 7 + 7 + 10 + 9 + ….?

A. 1010

B. 1115

C. 1030

D. 1031

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick The series can be grouped into pairs: (1 + 3) + (4 + 5) + (7 + 7) + (10 + 9) + ... New paired series: 4 + 9 + 14 + 19 + ... which forms an Arithmetic Progression (AP). For this AP, first term (a) = 4, common difference (d) = 5, and number of terms (n) = 40 ÷ 2 = 20. Sum = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d] ⇒ 20/2 [2 × 4 + (20 − 1) × 5] Sum = 10 [8 + 95] = 10 × 103 = 1030. ∴ The correct answer is 1030. Alternate Method Given series: 1 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 7 + 7 + 10 + 9 + ... up to 40 terms. This series is a combination of two alternating Arithmetic Progressions (APs). Formula Used: Sum of n terms (Sn) = (n/2) × [2a + (n − 1)d] ⇒ Total terms = 40. Therefore, each AP has 40 ÷ 2 = 20 terms. ⇒ For Series 1 (1, 4, 7...): a1 = 1, d1 = 3, n = 20 ⇒ S20(1) = (20/2) × [2(1) + (20 − 1)3] = 10 × [2 + 57] = 590 ⇒ For Series 2 (3, 5, 7...): a2 = 3, d2 = 2, n = 20 ⇒ S20(2) = (20/2) × [2(3) + (20 − 1)2] = 10 × [6 + 38] = 440 ⇒ Total Sum = 590 + 440 = 1030 ∴ The correct answer is 1030. Additional Information General Term of an AP The n-th term of an arithmetic progression is given by Tn = a + (n − 1)d, where 'a' is the first term and 'd' is the common difference. Arithmetic Progression (AP) Sum Formula The sum can also be calculated as Sn = (n/2) × (a + l), where 'l' is the last term of the series. Solution
