Solution

Alternate Method Given: Divisors: 4, 5, 6, 7 Corresponding Remainders: 2, 3, 4, 5 Formula Used: Number (M) = LCM(Divisors) × n − k (where k is the constant difference between divisor and remainder) Calculations: ⇒ Common difference (k) = 4 − 2 = 2, 5 − 3 = 2, 6 − 4 = 2, 7 − 5 = 2. ⇒ LCM(4, 5, 6, 7): ⇒ 4 = 22 ⇒ 5 = 5 ⇒ 6 = 2 × 3 ⇒ 7 = 7 ⇒ LCM = 22 × 3 × 5 × 7 = 420. ⇒ To find the largest 4-digit number, divide 9999 by 420: ⇒ 9999 ÷ 420 = 23 with remainder 339. ⇒ Largest multiple of 420 below 9999 = 9999 − 339 = 9660. ⇒ Therefore, M = 9660 − 2 = 9658. ⇒ Remainder when M is divided by 9: ⇒ 9658 ÷ 9 ⇒ Remainder = 1. ∴ The correct answer is 1. Additional Information LCM Remainder Theorem (Constant Difference) If a number leaves remainders r1, r2... when divided by d1, d2... and (d1 − r1) = (d2 − r2) = k, the number is of the form [LCM(d1, d2...) × n] − k. Solution
