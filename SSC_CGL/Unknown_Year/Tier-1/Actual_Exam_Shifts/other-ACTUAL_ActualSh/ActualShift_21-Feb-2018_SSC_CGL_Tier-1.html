1. If A = 1 - 10 + 3 - 12 + 5 - 14 + 7 + … up to 60 terms, then what is the value of A?

A. -360

B. -310

C. -240

D. -270

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Group the series into pairs: (1 − 10) + (3 − 12) + (5 − 14) + … Sum of each pair = −9 Total terms = 60 ⇒ Total pairs = 60 ÷ 2 = 30 pairs Total sum A = 30 × (−9) = −270 ∴ The correct answer is -270. Alternate Method Given: A = 1 − 10 + 3 − 12 + 5 − 14 + … up to 60 terms Separate the series into two Arithmetic Progressions (APs) of 30 terms each: AP1: 1, 3, 5, 7, … (Odd positioned terms) AP2: −10, −12, −14, −16, … (Even positioned terms) Formula for Sum of AP: Sn = (n ÷ 2) × [2a + (n − 1)d] ⇒ For AP1: a = 1, d = 2, n = 30 ⇒ S1 = (30 ÷ 2) × [2(1) + (30 − 1) × 2] = 15 × [2 + 58] = 15 × 60 = 900 ⇒ For AP2: a = −10, d = −2, n = 30 ⇒ S2 = (30 ÷ 2) × [2(−10) + (30 − 1) × (−2)] = 15 × [−20 − 58] = 15 × (−78) = −1170 ⇒ Total Sum A = S1 + S2 = 900 + (−1170) = −270 ∴ The correct answer is -270. General Term of AP The n-th term of an AP is given by an = a + (n − 1)d. Sum of First n Odd Numbers The sum of the first n odd natural numbers (1, 3, 5, …) is always n2. 95. What is the difference (in Rs) in Compound interest earned in 1 year on a sum of Rs. 25,000 at 20% per annum compounded semi-annually and annually?

A. 125

B. 250

C. 500

D. 375

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Effective rate for annual compounding (1 year) = 20% For semi-annual compounding, rate = 20% ÷ 2 = 10% and periods = 2. Effective rate (Semi-annual) = 10 + 10 + (10 × 10) ÷ 100 = 21% Difference in effective rates = 21% − 20% = 1% Difference in Compound Interest = 1% of ₹25,000 = ₹250 ∴ The correct answer is ₹250. Alternate Method Given: Principal (P) = ₹25,000 Rate (R) = 20% p.a. Time (T) = 1 year Formula: Amount (A) = P(1 + r/100)n ⇒ Case 1: Compounded Annually ⇒ Interest (CI1) = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 ⇒ CI1 = (25,000 × 20 × 1) ÷ 100 = ₹5,000 ⇒ Case 2: Compounded Semi-annually ⇒ New rate (r) = 20% ÷ 2 = 10% per 6 months ⇒ Number of compounding periods (n) = 2 ⇒ Amount (A) = 25,000 × (1 + 10/100)2 ⇒ A = 25,000 × (1.1)2 = 25,000 × 1.21 = ₹30,250 ⇒ Interest (CI2) = 30,250 − 25,000 = ₹5,250 ⇒ Difference = CI2 − CI1 = 5,250 − 5,000 = ₹250 ∴ The correct answer is ₹250. Additional Information Effective Interest Rate Formula For two successive interest periods with rates x% and y%, the net effective rate is calculated as x + y + (x × y / 100)%. Compounding Frequency Rules If interest is compounded 'k' times a year, the periodic rate becomes R/k and the number of periods becomes T × k.
