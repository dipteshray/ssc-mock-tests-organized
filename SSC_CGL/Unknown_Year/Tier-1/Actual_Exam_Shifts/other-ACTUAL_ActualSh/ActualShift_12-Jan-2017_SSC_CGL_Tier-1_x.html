2. Two pipes of length 1.5 m and 1.2 m are to be cut into equal pieces without leaving any extra length of pipes. The greatest length of the pipe pieces of the same size which can be cut from these two lengths will be

A. 0.13 m

B. 0.4 m

C. 0.3 m

D. 0.41 m

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick To find the greatest equal piece size without leaving any extra pipe, calculate the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of their lengths. Convert lengths to centimeters for easy integer calculation: 1.5 m = 150 cm and 1.2 m = 120 cm. The HCF of 150 and 120 is clearly 30. Convert the result back to meters: 30 cm = 0.3 m. ∴ The correct answer is 0.3 m. Alternate Method Given: Length of the first pipe = 1.5 m Length of the second pipe = 1.2 m Formula Used: The greatest possible length of equal pieces cut from both pipes = HCF of their lengths. HCF of Fractions = (HCF of Numerators) ÷ (LCM of Denominators) Pipe 1: 1.5 m 0.3 m 0.3 m 0.3 m 0.3 m 0.3 m Pipe 2: 1.2 m 0.3 m 0.3 m 0.3 m 0.3 m Maximum equal piece cut = HCF(1.5, 1.2) = 0.3 m Calculations: ⇒ First, express the given decimal lengths as fractions to use the formula. ⇒ 1.5 = 15/10 and 1.2 = 12/10 ⇒ HCF of numerators (15, 12) = 3 ⇒ LCM of denominators (10, 10) = 10 ⇒ HCF of fractions = 3 / 10 = 0.3 m ∴ The correct answer is 0.3 m. Additional Information LCM of Fractions LCM of Fractions = (LCM of Numerators) ÷ (HCF of Denominators) HCF of Decimals Make the number of decimal places equal in all given numbers. Find the HCF treating them as regular integers, then place the decimal point backward accordingly. Application of HCF HCF is applied when a practical problem asks to split things into smaller identical sections, equally distribute items, or find the maximum possible unit size without leaving a remainder.

4. The smallest number, which should be added to 756896 so as to obtain a multiple of 11, is

A. 1

B. 2

C. 3

D. 5

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Perform direct division of 756896 by 11 to find the remainder. 756896 ÷ 11 ⇒ Remainder = 8. Smallest number to be added = Divisor − Remainder = 11 − 8 = 3. ∴ The correct answer is 3. Alternate Method Given Number: 756896 Formula: Divisibility rule of 11 states that (Sum of digits at odd places) − (Sum of digits at even places) must be 0 or a multiple of 11. ⇒ Sum of digits at odd positions (from right: 6, 8, 5) = 6 + 8 + 5 = 19 ⇒ Sum of digits at even positions (from right: 9, 6, 7) = 9 + 6 + 7 = 22 ⇒ Current Difference = 22 − 19 = 3 ⇒ To make the number divisible by 11, we need to add a number x to the total sum such that the new difference becomes 0. ⇒ (19 + x) − 22 = 0 ⇒ x − 3 = 0 ⇒ x = 3. ∴ The correct answer is 3. Additional Information Divisibility Rule of 11 A number is divisible by 11 if the difference between the sum of its digits at odd places and the sum of its digits at even places is either 0 or a multiple of 11. Finding Number to Add/Subtract To make N divisible by D: the smallest number to subtract is Remainder(N÷D), and the smallest number to add is [D − Remainder(N÷D)]. Divisibility Rule of 7 Double the last digit of the number and subtract it from the remaining number. If the result is divisible by 7, the original number is also divisible by 7.

9. Three electronic devices make a beep after every 48 sec, 72 sec and 108 sec respectively. They beeped together at 10 a.m. The time when they will next make a beep together at the earliest is

A. 10:07:12 hrs

B. 10:07:24 hrs

C. 10:07:36 hrs

D. 10:07:48 hrs

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick LCM of 48, 72, and 108 determines the common interval. LCM(48, 72, 108) = 432 seconds. Convert 432 seconds to minutes ⇒ 432 ÷ 60 = 7 minutes 12 seconds. Next beep time = 10:00:00 + 00:07:12 = 10:07:12 hrs. ∴ The correct answer is 10:07:12 hrs. Alternate Method Given: Beep intervals: 48 sec, 72 sec, 108 sec. Starting time: 10:00:00 a.m. Formula Used: Next simultaneous event = Initial time + LCM of intervals. Calculations: Find LCM using prime factorization: ⇒ 48 = 24 × 31 ⇒ 72 = 23 × 32 ⇒ 108 = 22 × 33 ⇒ LCM = 24 × 33 = 16 × 27 = 432 seconds. Convert 432 seconds into minutes: ⇒ 432 seconds = 420 seconds + 12 seconds ⇒ (420 ÷ 60) minutes + 12 seconds = 7 minutes 12 seconds. Add the interval to the starting time: ⇒ 10:00:00 + 0:07:12 = 10:07:12 hrs. ∴ The correct answer is 10:07:12 hrs. Additional Information LCM for Recurring Events LCM (Least Common Multiple) is used to find the first time multiple events with different periodic intervals will coincide again. Time Unit Conversion To convert seconds into minutes, divide by 60. The quotient represents minutes and the remainder represents the remaining seconds. HCF vs LCM Use LCM for "When will they meet next" or "Minimum distance to cover". Use HCF for "Maximum size of a container" or "Longest tape to measure".
