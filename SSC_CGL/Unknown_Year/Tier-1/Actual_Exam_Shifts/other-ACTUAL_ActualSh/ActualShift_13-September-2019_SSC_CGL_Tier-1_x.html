9. A can do one - third of a work in 15 days, B can do 75% of the same work in 18 days and C can do the same work in 36 days. B and C work together for 8 days. In how many days will A alone complete the remaining work?

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total time for A = 15 × 3 = 45 days; Total time for B = 18 ÷ 0.75 = 24 days. Total Work = LCM(45, 24, 36) = 360 units. Efficiencies: A = 8, B = 15, C = 10. Work done by B and C in 8 days = (15 + 10) × 8 = 200 units. Remaining work = 360 − 200 = 160 units. Time for A to complete remaining work = 160 ÷ 8 = 20 days. ∴ The correct answer is 20 days. Alternate Method Given: A completes 1/3 work in 15 days. B completes 75% work in 18 days. C completes full work in 36 days. B. 9 : 55

C. 8 : 13

D. 25 : 39

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Given AD : AB = 3 : 8, let AB = 8 units and AD = 3 units. BD = AB − AD = 8 − 3 = 5 units. Since DE || AC, ΔBDE ≈ ΔBAC. Area(quadrilateral DECA) = Area(ΔBAC) − Area(ΔBDE) = 64 − 25 = 39. Required Ratio = Area(ΔBDE) : Area(quadrilateral DECA) = 25 : 39. ∴ The correct answer is 25 : 39. Alternate Method Given: AD : AB = 3 : 8 and DE || AC. Formula Used: Ratio of areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.
