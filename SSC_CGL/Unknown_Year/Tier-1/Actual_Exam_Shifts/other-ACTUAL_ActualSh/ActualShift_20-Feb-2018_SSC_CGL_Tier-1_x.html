Solution

The correct answer is Option 2 i.e. confused activity and uproar. Key Points Additional Information

• Alarums and Excursion means Frantic activity that causes a clamor

• For example: The French armies were not far off, and there were alarums and excursions all along the border.

• The noun alarum is an archaic form of alarm, meaning a call to arms (the vowel between -r- and -m- in alarum perhaps arose from rolling of the -r- in the final syllable of the call).
