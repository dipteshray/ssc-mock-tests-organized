3. In what ratio, sugar costing Rs. 60 per kg be mixed with sugar costing Rs. 42 per kg such that by selling the mixture at Rs. 56 per kg there is a gain of 12%?

A. 5 : 6

B. 5 : 7

C. 8 : 9

D. 4 : 5

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Step 1: Calculate the Cost Price (CP) of the mixture from Selling Price (SP) and Profit%. CP of mixture = (SP × 100) / (100 + Profit%) = (56 × 100) / 112 = ₹50/kg. Step 2: Apply Alligation Rule: Ratio = (Mean CP − Lower CP) : (Higher CP − Mean CP). Ratio = (50 − 42) : (60 − 50) = 8 : 10 = 4 : 5. ∴ The correct answer is 4 : 5. Alternate Method Given: Cost of sugar 1 (CP1) = ₹60/kg Cost of sugar 2 (CP2) = ₹42/kg Selling price of mixture (SPm) = ₹56/kg Profit percentage = 12% Formula Used: Cost Price (CP) = SP × 100 / (100 + Gain%) Alligation Rule: (Quantity of Cheaper) / (Quantity of Dearer) = (CP of Dearer − Mean CP) / (Mean CP − CP of Cheaper) Calculations: ⇒ Mean Cost Price = 56 × 100 / (100 + 12) ⇒ Mean Cost Price = 5600 / 112 = ₹50/kg ⇒ Applying Alligation: (Sugar 1 Quantity) : (Sugar 2 Quantity) ⇒ Ratio = (Mean CP − CP2) : (CP1 − Mean CP) ⇒ Ratio = (50 − 42) : (60 − 50) ⇒ Ratio = 8 : 10 ⇒ Ratio = 4 : 5 ∴ The correct answer is 4 : 5. Additional Information Rule of Alligation It is used to find the ratio in which two or more ingredients at their respective prices must be mixed to produce a mixture of a desired price. Ratio = (Price of dearer − Mean price) : (Mean price − Price of cheaper). Cost Price vs Selling Price Always ensure all values used in the alligation cross are of the same type (all Cost Prices or all Selling Prices). If a profit is given on the mixture, convert SP to CP first. Applications of Alligation This method can be applied to mixtures of liquids, averages of marks, speed-time-distance problems, and calculating interest rates on parts of a principal.

5. The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 1/7 of the number. The units digit is 4 less than the tens digit. If the number obtained on reversing its digits is divided by 7, the remainder will be:

A. 4

B. 1

C. 6

D. 5

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Ratio of number to sum of digits is 7 : 1 ⇒ 10x + y = 7(x + y) ⇒ 3x = 6y ⇒ x = 2y. Given that units digit (y) is 4 less than tens digit (x), so x − y = 4. Substituting x = 2y: 2y − y = 4 ⇒ y = 4 and x = 8. The number is 84. Reversing 84 gives 48. Now divide 48 by 7: 48 = (7 × 6) + 6. ∴ The correct answer is 6. Alternate Method Given: Sum of digits = 1/7 × (Two-digit number) Units digit = Tens digit − 4 Formula Used: A two-digit number with tens digit x and units digit y is expressed as 10x + y. ⇒ Let the tens digit be x and the units digit be y. ⇒ According to the first condition: x + y = (10x + y) ÷ 7 ⇒ 7(x + y) = 10x + y ⇒ 7x + 7y = 10x + y ⇒ 3x = 6y ⇒ x = 2y ... (Eq. 1) ⇒ According to the second condition: y = x − 4 ⇒ x − y = 4 ... (Eq. 2) ⇒ Substitute Eq. 1 into Eq. 2: 2y − y = 4 ⇒ y = 4 ⇒ x = 2 × 4 = 8 ⇒ The original number = 10(8) + 4 = 84 ⇒ The reversed number = 10(4) + 8 = 48 ⇒ Dividing reversed number by 7: 48 ÷ 7 = 6 with a remainder of 6. ∴ The correct answer is 6. Additional Information Two-Digit Number Properties A 2-digit number 10x+y and its reverse 10y+x always have a difference that is a multiple of 9, i.e., |(10x+y) − (10y+x)| = 9|x−y|. Divisibility and Remainders For any number N divided by d, N = dq + r, where q is the quotient and 0 ≤ r Sum of Digits Rule If a number is a multiple of its digit sum, such as 84 being 7 × 12, it often relates to divisibility rules of 3 or 9.

6. In ΔABC, AB = 7, BC = 10 cm, and AC = 8 cm. If AD is the angle bisector of ∠BAC, where D is a point on BC, then BD is equal to:

A. 16/3 cm

B. 17/4 cm

C. 15/4 cm

D. 14/3 cm

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick According to the Angle Bisector Theorem, the ratio of the segments formed on the opposite side is equal to the ratio of the other two sides. Ratio BD : DC = AB : AC = 7 : 8 Total ratio parts = 7 + 8 = 15 units Given total length BC = 10 cm BD = (7 ÷ 15) × 10 = 14/3 cm ∴ The correct answer is 14/3 cm. Alternate Method Given: AB = 7 cm, AC = 8 cm, BC = 10 cm. AD is the internal bisector of ∠BAC. Formula Used: In ΔABC, if AD bisects ∠A, then BD / DC = AB / AC. Let BD = x cm. Then DC = (10 − x) cm. ⇒ BD / DC = AB / AC ⇒ x / (10 − x) = 7 / 8 ⇒ 8 × x = 7 × (10 − x) ⇒ 8x = 70 − 7x ⇒ 8x + 7x = 70 ⇒ 15x = 70 ⇒ x = 70 ÷ 15 ⇒ x = 14/3 cm ∴ The correct answer is 14/3 cm. Additional Information Internal Angle Bisector Theorem The bisector of an interior angle of a triangle divides the opposite side internally in the ratio of the sides containing the angle. BD/DC = AB/AC. External Angle Bisector Theorem The bisector of an exterior angle of a triangle divides the opposite side externally in the ratio of the sides containing the angle. BE/CE = AB/AC. Length of the Bisector The length of the internal angle bisector AD can be calculated using the formula AD2 = AB × AC − BD × DC. Incenter of a Triangle The intersection point of all three internal angle bisectors is called the incenter, which is the center of the circle inscribed in the triangle.

8. PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral in which PQ = 14.4 cm, QR = 12.8 cm and SR = 9.6 cm. If PR bisects QS, what is the length of PS?

A. 16.4 cm

B. 13.6 cm

C. 19.2 cm

D. 15.8 cm

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick In a cyclic quadrilateral, if one diagonal bisects the other, the product of the adjacent sides of the bisecting diagonal is equal. If PR bisects QS, then the property is: PQ × QR = PS × SR Given: PQ = 14.4 cm, QR = 12.8 cm, SR = 9.6 cm ⇒ 14.4 × 12.8 = PS × 9.6 ⇒ PS = (14.4 × 12.8) ÷ 9.6 = 1.5 × 12.8 = 19.2 cm ∴ The correct answer is 19.2 cm. Alternate Method Given: PQ = 14.4 cm, QR = 12.8 cm, SR = 9.6 cm Diagonal PR bisects diagonal QS at point O (QO = OS). Formula Used: In a cyclic quadrilateral PQRS, if diagonal PR bisects QS, then Area(ΔPQR) = Area(ΔPSR). ⇒ 1/2 × PQ × QR × sin(Q) = 1/2 × PS × SR × sin(S) Since ∠Q + ∠S = 180° (Cyclic quadrilateral property), sin(Q) = sin(S). ⇒ PQ × QR = PS × SR Calculations: ⇒ 14.4 × 12.8 = PS × 9.6 ⇒ PS = (14.4 × 12.8) ÷ 9.6 ⇒ PS = 184.32 ÷ 9.6 ⇒ PS = 19.2 cm ∴ The correct answer is 19.2 cm. Additional Information Cyclic Quadrilateral Definition A quadrilateral whose all four vertices lie on the circumference of a circle. Sum of opposite angles is always 180° (∠P + ∠R = 180°). Ptolemy's Theorem In a cyclic quadrilateral, the product of the diagonals is equal to the sum of the products of opposite sides: (PR × QS) = (PQ × RS) + (PS × QR). Area of Cyclic Quadrilateral Area = √((s−a)(s−b)(s−c)(s−d)), where s is the semi-perimeter (a+b+c+d)/2. This is known as Brahmagupta's Formula.

9. The ratio of copper to zinc in alloys A and B are 3 : 4 and 5 : 9 respectively. A and B are taken in the ratio 2 : 3 and melted to form a new alloy C. What is the ratio of copper to zinc in C?

A. 8 : 13

B. 9 : 11

C. 27 : 43

D. 3 : 5

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick Total parts in A = 3 + 4 = 7; Total parts in B = 5 + 9 = 14. Equalize parts by multiplying Alloy A ratio by 2: A → 6 : 8 (Total 14). Now multiply by mixing ratio 2 : 3: A → (6 × 2) : (8 × 2) = 12 : 16; B → (5 × 3) : (9 × 3) = 15 : 27. New Copper = 12 + 15 = 27; New Zinc = 16 + 27 = 43. ∴ The correct answer is 27 : 43. Alternate Method Given: Ratio of Cu:Zn in A = 3 : 4 Ratio of Cu:Zn in B = 5 : 9 Mixing ratio A : B = 2 : 3 Calculations: ⇒ Fraction of Copper in A = 3/7 and Zinc in A = 4/7 ⇒ Fraction of Copper in B = 5/14 and Zinc in B = 9/14 ⇒ Total Copper in C = (2 × 3/7) + (3 × 5/14) ⇒ Total Copper in C = 6/7 + 15/14 = (12 + 15)/14 = 27/14 ⇒ Total Zinc in C = (2 × 4/7) + (3 × 9/14) ⇒ Total Zinc in C = 8/7 + 27/14 = (16 + 27)/14 = 43/14 ⇒ Required Ratio = (27/14) : (43/14) = 27 : 43 ∴ The correct answer is 27 : 43. Additional Information Alligation Rule for Mixtures Quantity of cheaper / Quantity of dearer = (Rate of dearer − Mean rate) / (Mean rate − Rate of cheaper). Weighted Average Formula Average = (n1x1 + n2x2) / (n1 + n2), where n represents quantities and x represents values/ratios. Ratio Balancing To combine ratios of different totals, find the LCM of the sums of the parts and multiply each ratio accordingly to equalize the base.

11. A and B started their journeys from X to Y and Y to X, respectively. After crossing each other, A and B completed the remaining parts of their journeys in 6(1/8) h and 8 h respectively. If the speed of B is 28 km/h, then the speed (in km/h) of A is:

A. 42

B. 40

C. 32

D. 36

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick For two objects starting from opposite ends and moving towards each other, if they meet and then take times T1 and T2 to reach their respective destinations, the ratio of their speeds is S1/S2 = √(T2/T1). Given: T1 = 6 (1/8) h = 49/8 h, T2 = 8 h, and S2 (Speed of B) = 28 km/h. Ratio of speeds SA/SB = √(8 / (49/8)) = √(64/49) = 8/7. Speed of A = (8/7) × Speed of B = (8/7) × 28 = 8 × 4 = 32 km/h. ∴ The correct answer is 32 km/h. Alternate Method Given: Speed of B (SB) = 28 km/h Time taken by A after crossing (TA) = 6 (1/8) = 49/8 hours Time taken by B after crossing (TB) = 8 hours Formula: SA / SB = √(TB / TA) Calculations: ⇒ SA / 28 = √(8 / (49/8)) ⇒ SA / 28 = √(8 × 8 / 49) ⇒ SA / 28 = √(64 / 49) ⇒ SA / 28 = 8 / 7 ⇒ SA = (8 / 7) × 28 ⇒ SA = 8 × 4 ⇒ SA = 32 km/h ∴ The correct answer is 32 km/h. Additional Information Meeting Time Formula In cases where two objects start simultaneously and meet, then take T1 and T2 times to reach the ends, the time from the start until they meet is given by T = √(T1 × T2). Relative Speed When two bodies move towards each other, their relative speed is the sum of their individual speeds (S1 + S2). Speed-Time Relation For a fixed distance, speed is inversely proportional to time (S ∝ 1/T), but in these specific meeting-point problems, the square root relationship is derived from equating the time taken to reach the meeting point.

17. When 7897, 8110 and 8536 are divided by the greatest number x, then the remainder in each case is the same. The sum of the digits of x is:

A. 9

B. 14

C. 5

D. 6

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick When the remainder is the same, the greatest number x is the HCF of the differences between the numbers. Difference 1 = 8110 − 7897 = 213 Difference 2 = 8536 − 8110 = 426 x = HCF(213, 426) = 213 Sum of digits of x = 2 + 1 + 3 = 6 ∴ The correct answer is 6. Alternate Method Given: Numbers: 7897, 8110, 8536 Formula used: If numbers a, b, and c leave the same remainder when divided by x, then x = HCF(|a−b|, |b−c|, |c−a|). ⇒ Difference 1 = 8110 − 7897 = 213 ⇒ Difference 2 = 8536 − 8110 = 426 ⇒ Difference 3 = 8536 − 7897 = 639 ⇒ x = HCF(213, 426, 639) ⇒ 213 = 213 × 1 ⇒ 426 = 213 × 2 ⇒ 639 = 213 × 3 ⇒ x = 213 ⇒ Sum of digits of x = 2 + 1 + 3 = 6 ∴ The correct answer is 6.
