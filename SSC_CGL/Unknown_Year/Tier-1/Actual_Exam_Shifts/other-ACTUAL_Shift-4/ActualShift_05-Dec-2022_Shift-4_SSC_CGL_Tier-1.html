30. Which theory discusses the relationship between ligand bonding and degradation of d orbitals in metal complexes and the geometry of metal complexes and the splitting of d orbitals?

A. Crystal field theory (CFT)

B. Ligand field theory (LFT)

C. Valence bond theory (VBT)

D. Molecular orbital theory (MOT)

Answer A

Solution

Crystal field theory (CFT) explains the relationship between ligand bonding and degradation of d orbitals in metal complexes, the geometry of metal complexes, and the splitting of d orbitals. It governs how ligands interact with the central metal atom or ion and views the interaction as an electrostatic phenomenon. CFT considers the partially covalent nature of bonding between the ligand and metal atom. The theory explains how the d orbitals split apart and become non-degenerate due to the varied symmetries of the d orbitals and the inductive influence of the ligands on the electrons.

36. Which of the following is caused by exogenic force?

A. Erosion

B. Landslide

C. Earthquake

D. Volcano

Answer A

Solution

Erosion is caused by exogenic forces which originate from outside the earth's surface, such as water, wind, and waves. These forces wear away the surface of the earth, leading to the removal of soil, rocks, and other materials. This process is known as denudation, and it is a significant factor in shaping the earth's landscape. Endogenic forces, on the other hand, originate from within the earth and can cause events like earthquakes and volcanoes. Therefore, the correct answer is erosion, which is caused by exogenic forces.
