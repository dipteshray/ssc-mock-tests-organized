28. The upper portion of the mantle is called .

A. Lithosphere

B. Stratosphere

C. Mesosphere

D. Asthenosphere

Answer D

Solution

The asthenosphere is a denser and weaker layer located between 100 to 410 km beneath Earth's surface. Its high temperature and pressure cause rocks to soften and partially melt, making it more viscous than the lithosphere. The lithosphere-asthenosphere boundary marks the difference in ductility between the two layers.

41. In organic chemistry, which of the following is an empirical rule used to predict the regioselectivity of electrophilic addition reactions of alkanes and alkynes?

Answer C

Solution
