32. Where in India are the Sundari trees that provide durable and hard timber found?

C. Montane Forests

D. Tropical Evergreen Forests

Solution

The Sundari trees, scientifically known as Heritiera fomes, are found in India's mangrove forests, particularly in the Sundarbans, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. 33. Which of the following is NOT included in domestic income?

A. Income earned by the residents of the country

B. Income earned by the non-residents outside the country

C. Income earned by all the employees of the country

D. Profit of all the industries of the country

Answer B

Solution

Domestic income, also known as Gross Domestic Product, is the total income earned by all sectors (households, firms, and the government) within the geographical boundaries of a country, irrespective of whether they are residents or non-residents. However, income earned by non-residents outside the country is NOT included in the domestic income. Such income is part of the national income, not domestic income.
