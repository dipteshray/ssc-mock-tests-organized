1. Select the correct combination of mathematical signs to sequentially replace the * signs and balance the given equation. 16 * 3 * 2 * 11 * 9 = 22

A. × ÷ + -

B. × ÷ − +

C. ÷ × + +

D. ÷ + × ÷

Answer B

Solution

The pattern followed here is: We have to put the mathematical signs to sequentially replace the * signs and balance the given equation. Let's check each option one by one. Option 1) × ÷ + - Given: 16 * 3 * 2 * 11 * 9 = 22 ⇒ 16 × 3 ÷ 2 + 11 - 9 = 22 ⇒ 16 × 1.5 + 11 - 9 = 22 ⇒ 24 + 11 - 9 = 22 ⇒ 35 - 9 = 22 ⇒ 26 ≠ 22 ⇒ LHS ≠ RHS Option 2) × ÷ − + Given: 16 * 3 * 2 * 11 * 9 = 22 ⇒ 16 × 3 ÷ 2 - 11 + 9 = 22 ⇒ 16 × 1.5 - 11 + 9 = 22 ⇒ 24 - 11 + 9 = 22 ⇒ 33 - 11 = 22 ⇒ 22 = 22 ⇒ LHS = RHS Option 3) ÷ × + + Given: 16 * 3 * 2 * 11 * 9 = 22 ⇒ 16 ÷ 3 × 2 + 11 + 9 = 22 ⇒ 5.33 × 2 + 11 + 9 = 22 ⇒ 10.6 + 11 + 9 = 22 ⇒ 30.6 ≠ 22 ⇒ LHS ≠ RHS Option 4) ÷ + × ÷ Given: 16 * 3 * 2 * 11 * 9 = 22 ⇒ 16 ÷ 3 + 2 × 11 ÷ 9 = 22 ⇒ 5.33 + 2 × 1.22 = 22 ⇒ 5.33 + 2.44 = 22 ⇒ 7.77 ≠ 22 ⇒ LHS ≠ RHS Hence, the correct answer is "Option 2".
