A. Platyhelminthes

B. Ctenophores

C. Mollusca

D. Aschelminthes

Answer D

Solution

Aschelminthes, also known as roundworms, have a circular cross-section and can be free-living or parasitic in plants and animals. They are distinguished by having a pseudocoelom and are found in freshwater and soil. As endoparasites, Ascaris is discovered to exist in human intestines. 94. Select the most appropriate option to fill in blank number 5.

A. satisfied

B. empty

C. lively

D. honoured

Answer B

Solution

The correct answer is empty. Key Points Correct Paragraph: We believe our value is in direct proportion to the value of our accomplishments. If we cannot accomplish certain results, we tend to feel (1) low about ourselves. Some of us try too hard and become workaholics and over-achievers. With a few genuine feelings of self-worth, we try to create some and prove that we are somebody by our successes and (2) achievements. Because our desire for perfection is so great, we (3) tend to set unrealistic goals and place unreasonable demands on ourselves. Failing, rather than encouraging us to have realistic aspirations, only leads to a mere punishing round of self-blame and a resolve to drive ourselves harder next time. If we do not finally achieve our goals, we are (4) disappointed; despite everything we have done, we still feel (5) empty inside. Additional Information

• Empty: having no value or purpose. (जिसका कोई मूल्य या उद्देश्य न हो) Example: He set the empty glass down.

• Thus, the most appropriate option to fill in the blank number 5 is option 2.

• Satisfied: pleased because you have had or done what you wanted. (अभीष् ट काम को संपन् न करने या वस् तु को प्राप् त करने पर प्रसन् न; संतुष् ट)

• Lively: full of energy, interest, excitement, etc. (चुस् ती, रुचि)

• Honoured: regarded with great respect. (सम्मानित)
