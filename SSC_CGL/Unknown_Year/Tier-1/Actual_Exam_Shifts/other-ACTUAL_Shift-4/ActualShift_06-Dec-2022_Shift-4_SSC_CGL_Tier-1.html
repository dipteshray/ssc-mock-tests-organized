25. ______ time graph shows speed of an object.

B. Distance

C. Displacement

D. Acceleration

Answer B

Solution

A distance-time graph shows the speed of an object. The gradient of the line is equal to the object's speed, with steeper lines indicating faster movement. Distance is the sum of an object's movements regardless of direction, and can be defined as the amount of space covered. Velocity is the direction an object is moving, displacement is the shortest distance between two points, and acceleration is the rate of change of velocity over time.
