Solution

• The given sentence is written in an interrogative form as the question tag is there, and we need to convert it into the passive form.

• Active Voice describes a sentence where the subject performs the action stated by the verb. While in the Passive Voice subject is acted upon by the verb.

• We follow the following structure in case of an interrogative sentence: Active voice: Do/Does/Did + Doer + Verb 1 + Noun/Pronoun + Rest of the sentence Example - Do they beat you every day? (Active)

• Passive voice: Am/is/are/was/were + Noun/Pronoun + Verb 3 + By + Doer + Rest of the sentence Example - Are you beaten by them every day? (Passive)

• While converting Interrogative Active into Interrogative Passive Voice. The helping verb comes before the object in the passive voice, (as the passive voice starts with the object of the active voice)

• Convert the verb into past participle or 3rd form of the verb.

• Use the preposition ‘by’.

• Complete the sentence with the subject and sign of Interrogation (?).

• Thus, the correct answer is Option 2.
