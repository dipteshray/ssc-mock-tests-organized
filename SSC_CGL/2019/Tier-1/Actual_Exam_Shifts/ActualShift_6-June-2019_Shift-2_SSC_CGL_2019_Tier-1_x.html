47. If sin θ = 4 cos θ, then what is the value of sin θ cos θ?

A. 4/17

B. 2/9

C. 3/4

D. 3/10

Answer A

Solution

Shortcut Trick Given: sin θ = 4 cos θ ⇒ tan θ = 4/1 Consider a right-angled triangle where Perpendicular (P) = 4 and Base (B) = 1. Hypotenuse (H) = √(42 + 12) = √17 sin θ cos θ = (P/H) × (B/H) = (4/√17) × (1/√17) = 4/17 ∴ The correct answer is 4/17. Alternate Method Given: sin θ = 4 cos θ Formula Used: tan θ = sin θ / cos θ; sin θ cos θ = tan θ / sec2θ; sec2θ = 1 + tan2θ ⇒ sin θ / cos θ = 4 ⇒ tan θ = 4 ⇒ sin θ cos θ = (sin θ / cos θ) × cos2θ ⇒ sin θ cos θ = tan θ × (1 / sec2θ) ⇒ sin θ cos θ = tan θ / (1 + tan2θ) ⇒ Substituting tan θ = 4: 4 / (1 + 42) ⇒ 4 / (1 + 16) = 4/17 ∴ The correct answer is 4/17. Additional Information Trigonometric Identities Fundamental identity: sin2θ + cos2θ = 1. Also, sec2θ − tan2θ = 1 and cosec2θ − cot2θ = 1. Double Angle Formula sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ = 2 tan θ / (1 + tan2θ). This shows sin θ cos θ is exactly half of the sin 2θ tan-formula.

93. Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank. Catholics and Protestants have a long history of ______ in Northern Ireland but volunteers from both communities came together to build the monument of Peace

A. truce

B. agreement

C. contest

D. conflict

Answer D

Solution

Here are the meanings of the given options: We can understand from the context of the sentence that there must have been some form of negative sentiment i.e. disagreement between the Catholics and Protestants . This is because the word in blank is then contrasted with 'coming together' which is a positive sentiment. The only word that fits this context is 'conflict'. Hence, the correct answer is option 4.

• Truce - an agreement to stop fighting or arguing for a period of time

• Agreement - a situation in which people share the same opinion

• Contest - an event in which people compete for supremacy in a sport or other activity

• Conflict - an active disagreement between people with opposing opinions
