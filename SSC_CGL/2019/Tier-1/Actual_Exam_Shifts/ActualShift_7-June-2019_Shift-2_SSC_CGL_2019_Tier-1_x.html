44. A person sold 25 articles for Rs. 2500 and incurred a loss of 10%. How many articles should he sell for Rs. 2400 to make a profit of 20%?

A. 20

B. 18

C. 16

D. 15

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick Use the direct relation: (Total Price1 / (Quantity1 × (100 − Loss%))) = (Total Price2 / (Quantity2 × (100 + Profit%))) ⇒ 2500 / (25 × 90) = 2400 / (n × 120) ⇒ 100 / 90 = 20 / n ⇒ 10 / 9 = 20 / n ⇒ n = (20 × 9) / 10 = 18 ∴ The correct answer is 18. Alternate Method Given: Quantity1 = 25 articles, Total SP1 = ₹2500, Loss = 10% Desired Profit = 20%, Total SP2 = ₹2400 Formula: Cost Price (CP) = (100 × SP) / (100 ± P/L%) ⇒ Selling Price (SP) of 1 article = 2500 ÷ 25 = ₹100 ⇒ Since loss is 10%, 90% of CP = ₹100 ⇒ CP of 1 article = 100 ÷ 0.9 = ₹1000/9 ⇒ New SP required for 20% profit = 120% of CP ⇒ New SP = (120/100) × (1000/9) = ₹400/3 ⇒ Number of articles for ₹2400 = Total SP ÷ New SP per article ⇒ Number of articles = 2400 ÷ (400/3) = (2400 × 3) ÷ 400 = 6 × 3 = 18 ∴ The correct answer is 18. Additional Information Cost Price and Selling Price Relationship Cost Price (CP) is always considered 100%. If there is a profit of P%, SP = (100 + P)% of CP. If there is a loss of L%, SP = (100 − L)% of CP. Unitary Method in Profit and Loss When the number of articles changes, it is easiest to find the CP or SP of one single unit before calculating the total for the new quantity. Profit/Loss Percentage Profit or Loss percentage is always calculated on the Cost Price unless stated otherwise in the question. Profit % = (Profit/CP) × 100.
