52. If a sum of money on compound interest becomes three times in 4 years, then at the same interest rate, the sum will become 27 times in:

A. 15 years

B. 12 years

C. 10 years

D. 16 years

Answer B

Solution

Shortcut Trick If a sum becomes n times in t years at compound interest, it becomes nx times in x × t years. Given, sum becomes 3 times (31) in 4 years. 95. Select the correct active form of the given sentence. The results will be known to us by the end of the next month.

A. We will know the results by the end of the next month.

B. We have known the results by the end of the next month.

C. We are knowing the results by the end of the next month.

D. We know the results by the end of the next month.

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is We will know the results by the end of the next month. Key Points

• In Active Voice, a sentence emphasizes the subject, performing an action.

• In Passive Voice, a sentence emphasizes the action or the object of the sentence.

• The given sentence is in the passive voice and 'The results' is the subject and 'us' is the object.

• When we convert this sentence into active voice, the subject 'The results' of the passive voice becomes the object, the object 'us' becomes the subject ( subjective case of pronoun for us is 'we').

• The passive format "will + be + V3" should be changed into the active format "will + V1 ".

• This is the active and passive voice rule for the future simple tense.

• Hence, the correct answer is option 1.
