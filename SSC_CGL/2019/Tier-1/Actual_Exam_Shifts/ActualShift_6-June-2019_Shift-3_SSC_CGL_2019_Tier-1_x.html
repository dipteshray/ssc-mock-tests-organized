44. In ΔABC, D is a point on AC such that AB = BD = DC, if ∠BAD = 70°, then the measure of ∠B is:

A. 80°

B. 75°

C. 85°

D. 70°

Answer B

Solution

Given, AB = BD = DC And ∠BAD = 70° In ΔABD, ⇒ ∠BAD = ∠BDA = 70° [∵ AB = BD] ⇒ ∠ABD + ∠BAD + ∠BDA = 180° ⇒ ∠ABD = 180° – 70° – 70° = 40° ⇒ ∠BDC = 180° – ∠BDA = 180° – 70° = 110° In ΔBDC, ⇒ ∠DBC = ∠DCB [∵ BD = DC] ⇒ ∠DBC + ∠BDC + ∠DCB = 180° ⇒ 2∠DBC = 180° – 110° = 70° ⇒ ∠DBC = 35° ⇒ ∠ABC = ∠ABD + ∠DBC = 40° + 35° = 75°

46. A train travelling at 44 km/h crosses a man walking with a speed of 8 km/h, in the same direction, in 15 seconds. If the train crosses a woman coming from the opposite direction in 10 seconds, then what is the speed (in km/h) of the woman?

A. 8.5

B. 9

C. 10.5

D. 10

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick Relative Speed (Same direction) = 44 − 8 = 36 km/h = 10 m/s. Length of Train = 10 × 15 = 150 m. Relative Speed (Opposite direction) = 150 ÷ 10 = 15 m/s = 54 km/h. Woman Speed = 54 − 44 = 10 km/h. ∴ The correct answer is 10 km/h. Alternate Method Given: Speed of Train (ST) = 44 km/h, Speed of Man (SM) = 8 km/h, Time to cross man (t1) = 15 s, Time to cross Woman (t2) = 10 s. Formula Used: Distance = Relative Speed × Time; 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s. ⇒ Relative speed with man = 44 − 8 = 36 km/h ⇒ Speed in m/s = 36 × 5/18 = 10 m/s ⇒ Length of train (L) = Speed × Time = 10 × 15 = 150 m ⇒ Let the speed of woman be x km/h. Relative speed (opposite) = (44 + x) km/h ⇒ Length = Relative Speed × Time ⇒ 150 = (44 + x) × (5/18) × 10 ⇒ 15 = (44 + x) × 5/18 ⇒ 270 = 220 + 5x → No, simpler: 15 × 18 / 5 = 44 + x ⇒ 54 = 44 + x ⇒ x = 10 km/h ∴ The correct answer is 10 km/h. Additional Information Relative Speed Concept When two objects move in the same direction, relative speed is S1 − S2. In the opposite direction, it is S1 + S2. Unit Conversion To convert km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18. To convert m/s to km/h, multiply by 18/5. Crossing a Moving Object The distance covered while crossing a person is equal to the length of the train itself, regardless of the person's speed.
