53. ΔABC ∼ ΔEDF and ar(ΔABC) ∶ ar(ΔEDF) = 1 ∶ 4. If AB = 7 cm, BC = 8 cm and CA = 9 cm, then DF is equal to∶

A. 14 cm

B. 18 cm

C. 16 cm

D. 8 cm

Answer C

Solution

Shortcut Trick The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides. Given: ar(ΔABC) : ar(ΔEDF) = 1 : 4 ⇒ Ratio of corresponding sides = √1 : √4 = 1 : 2 Since ΔABC ∼ ΔEDF, the side BC corresponds to the side DF. ⇒ BC : DF = 1 : 2 ⇒ 8 : DF = 1 : 2 ⇒ DF = 8 × 2 = 16 cm. ∴ The correct answer is 16 cm. Alternate Method Given: ΔABC ∼ ΔEDF ar(ΔABC) : ar(ΔEDF) = 1 : 4 AB = 7 cm, BC = 8 cm, CA = 9 cm Formula Used: For two similar triangles, ar(Δ1) / ar(Δ2) = (Side1 / Side2)2 Calculations: Since ΔABC ∼ ΔEDF, the ratio of their areas is: ⇒ ar(ΔABC) / ar(ΔEDF) = (BC / DF)2 ⇒ 1 / 4 = (8 / DF)2 Taking square root on both sides: ⇒ √(1 / 4) = 8 / DF ⇒ 1 / 2 = 8 / DF ⇒ DF = 8 × 2 ⇒ DF = 16 cm ∴ The correct answer is 16 cm. Additional Information Area-Side Relationship in Similar Triangles If two triangles are similar, the ratio of their areas is equal to the square of the ratio of any two corresponding sides: Area1/Area2 = (s1/s2)2. Perimeter of Similar Triangles The ratio of the perimeters of two similar triangles is equal to the ratio of their corresponding sides: P1/P2 = s1/s2. 66. Six cubes, each of edge 2 cm, are joined end to end. What is the total surface area of the resulting cuboid in cm2?

A. 144

B. 96

C. 128

D. 104

Answer D

Solution

Shortcut Trick 6 individual cubes have a total of 6 × 6 = 36 faces. Joining them end-to-end forms 5 joints between the cubes. Each joint hides 2 faces, leaving 36 − (5 × 2) = 26 visible faces. Area of each square face = a2 = 22 = 4 cm2. Total surface area = 26 × 4 = 104 cm2. ∴ The correct answer is 104 cm2. Alternate Method Given: Number of cubes = 6 Edge of each cube (a) = 2 cm Formula Used: Total Surface Area of a Cuboid = 2(lb + bh + hl) Calculations: When 6 uniform cubes are joined end to end, a cuboid is formed where the length increases but breadth and height remain unchanged. ⇒ Length of the resulting cuboid (l) = 6 × 2 = 12 cm ⇒ Breadth of the resulting cuboid (b) = 2 cm ⇒ Height of the resulting cuboid (h) = 2 cm ⇒ Total Surface Area = 2 × (l × b + b × h + h × l) ⇒ Total Surface Area = 2 × (12 × 2 + 2 × 2 + 2 × 12) ⇒ Total Surface Area = 2 × (24 + 4 + 24) ⇒ Total Surface Area = 2 × 52 = 104 cm2 ∴ The correct answer is 104 cm2. Additional Information Total Surface Area of a Cube The total surface area of a single cube of edge length 'a' is given by 6a2, as it is composed of 6 identical square faces. Joining 'n' Cubes When 'n' cubes of side 'a' are joined end-to-end, the resulting shape is a cuboid with length 'n × a', while the breadth and height remain 'a'. Lateral Surface Area The lateral (or curved) surface area of a cuboid excludes the top and bottom faces. It is given by the formula LSA = 2h(l + b).
