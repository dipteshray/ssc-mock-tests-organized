92. Select the correct passive form of the given sentence. The enemy will have seized the fort before nightfall.

A. The fort will have been seized by the enemy before nightfall.

B. The fort would have been seized by the enemy before nightfall.

C. The enemy will seize the fort before nightfall

D. The enemy will be seized by the fort before nightfall

Answer A

Solution

The correct answer is option 1. The fort will have been seized by the enemy before nightfall. The given sentence is in future perfect tense identified by will have. Firstly, we interchange the subject and the object while converting it to the passive form. The fort will become the subject and the enemy will become an object in the passive voice. Also, been will be added according to the rules. So, options 2,3 and 4 are incorrect.
